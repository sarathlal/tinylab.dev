Every "it can't connect", "it's slow" and "the certificate is wrong" comes back to **networking**: the layers that carry a request from a browser across the internet to a server and bring the answer back.

This subject follows one request through every layer, then goes deeper into each: addresses and subnets, TCP, DNS, HTTP, TLS and certificates, NAT and firewalls, load balancers. It ends with a debugging toolkit and a lab where you capture real traffic and break things on purpose.

## How to use this subject

- **Read in order** — each page builds on the one before.
- **Run the commands** — every page has a hands-on part; that is where the understanding comes from.
- **Check yourself at the end** — each page closes with questions whose answers are in the page.
- **Stop at the depth you need** — pages are tagged <span class="badge badge-foundation">foundation</span>, <span class="badge badge-intermediate">intermediate</span> and <span class="badge badge-advanced">advanced</span>.
