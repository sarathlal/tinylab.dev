TinyLab is a small, independent site with two things on it:

- **Learning tracks** — long-form tutorial series written for people who want to understand a topic properly, not just copy commands. Each page starts with an everyday analogy, then explains the real mechanism, then gives you something to try and a few questions to check your understanding.
- **AI news** — a short daily brief on model releases, policy, funding and research.

The site is built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and its source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/{{ site.repository }}). Found a mistake? Every tutorial page has an "Edit this page" link, or open an issue.
