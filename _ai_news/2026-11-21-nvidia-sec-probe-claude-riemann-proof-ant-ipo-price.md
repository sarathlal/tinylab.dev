Friday was the worst day for AI stocks in a long time. On the same day, two mathematicians posted rival claims to one of the oldest open problems in math, and one of them did it with Claude.

## The AI trade cracks on Wall Street

### Nasdaq erases its 2026 gains as Nvidia falls 14%
The Nasdaq Composite fell about 5.3% on Friday and is now flat for the year. Nvidia dropped roughly 14% to $125.53. It has lost about $2.5 trillion in value since its October 6 peak. Axios says the Nasdaq is now down 17.9% from that peak, as investors question whether AI spending plans can hold up.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/20/anthropic-ipo-nvidia-ai-tech-selloff) · [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/20/stock-market-today-nov-20-nvidia-s-usd2-5-trillion-rout-deepens-as-sec-accounting-probe-adds-to-wall-street-s-ai-sell-off/)*

### Reuters: SEC enforcement staff are looking at Nvidia's accounting
Two people briefed on the matter told Reuters the SEC is probing Nvidia's accounting practices. It's not clear when the inquiry started or which issues it covers. The Wall Street Journal adds that the SEC is checking whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors. The SEC declined to comment, and it lands days after Jensen Huang's exit as CEO.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/wall-street-regulator-probing-nvidia-s-accounting-reuters-says) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidia-stock-extends-25-trillion-rout-as-sec-probe-reportedly-investigates-its-accounting-194945274.html)*

### Broadcom loses $191B after a report that Google cut TPU orders
Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo says Google trimmed 2027 orders for its TPU 8i chips by about 1 million units, or roughly 20%. Most of the cut hits the pricier 8i Max. Broadcom fell about 12% and some volume may shift to MediaTek. Neither Google nor Broadcom has confirmed Kuo's numbers.

*Sources: [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4675350-broadcom-sinks-12-as-google-trims-2027-tpu-orders-tech-stocks-drop) · [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/broadcom-stock-plunges-12-and-loses-191-billion-after-a-report-that-google-cut-its-plans-for-broadcom-ai-chips/)*

### Anthropic's stock comes within 5 cents of its IPO price on day two
ANT traded as low as $83.05 on Friday, a nickel above its $83 offer price. It bounced 6% to close at $88.30, down 9% from Thursday's $97.17. For comparison, Facebook closed its own second day 10% below its IPO price.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-stock-came-within-5-211710460.html) · [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/20/why-anthropic-stock-is-falling-today/)*

## Two claimed proofs of the Riemann hypothesis in one day

### Anthropic's Levent Alpöge says he and Claude proved the Riemann hypothesis
Alpöge posted a four-page note on his personal site, plus a proof written in the Lean formal language. Reports say Claude's full proof runs past 250,000 pages, and he claims it also covers the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. Nobody outside has verified it yet, and Anthropic hasn't made an official statement. Number theorist Brian Conrey told AFP it would be "the biggest thing I think that's ever happened in mathematics."

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/11/20/using-anthropic-ai-mathematician-claims-have-solved-riemann-hypothesis/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/20/anthropic-researcher-claims-to-have-proven-riemann-hypothesis-using-claude/) · [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-anthropic-claude-math-greatest-riddle.html)*

### Hours later, Stanford's Ravi Vakil posts an "all human" rival proof
AMS president Ravi Vakil released a draft by Alessandro Masullo that claims to prove the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. Vakil says Stanford's Brian Conrad has checked it for months and it's "so far looking good." The plan was a year of polishing first, but Vakil chose to release it now. Neither proof has passed independent review.

*Sources: [Vakil's blog](https://math216.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/hurried-announcement-alessandro-masullo-grh/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)*

## AI gets hands on real computers, for good and bad

### European Commission confirms an "AI-generated cyber campaign" took down staff systems
The Commission says an AI-assisted attack knocked out payroll, leave requests, medical reimbursements and translation tools for more than 10 days. Spokesperson Thomas Regnier said the first signs showed up October 22. He found no evidence of a broad data breach or a ransom demand. Politico reports CERT-EU flagged ARTEX, a public AI hacking framework, though the Commission hasn't named the tool.

*Sources: [The Record](https://therecord.media/european-commission-confirms-cyberattack-using-ai) · [Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/ai-artex-framework-european-commission-cyberattack-601595)*

### Gemini on Mac can now click, type and fill forms for you
Google rolled out computer use in the Gemini app for Mac. It works in a hidden background window, so your screen and cursor stay free. You trigger it by adding "@Computer use" to a prompt, and it pauses for logins and payments. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac and a paid Google AI plan, and it's US-only in English for now.

*Sources: [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/google-gives-gemini-the-ability-to-use-your-mac-for-you/) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/19/gemini-mac-computer-use/)*
