OpenAI's latest reasoning model disproved a math conjecture that had stood since 1946, while Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark told Oxford the first Nobel-winning AI collaboration is months away. Two of the week's biggest talent moves confirmed the lab-to-lab war is fully underway.

## AI Cracks Serious Mathematics

### OpenAI Model Disproves an 80-Year Erdős Conjecture

An OpenAI general-purpose reasoning model autonomously disproved Paul Erdős's 1946 planar unit-distance conjecture — the belief that the densest point arrangements for maximizing unit-distance pairs look like square grids. The model found an infinite family of constructions that outperform grids, using tools from algebraic number theory (specifically the Golod-Shafarevich theorem) that had never been applied to this problem. Fields medalist Tim Gowers, who checked the proof, called it "a milestone in AI mathematics."

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/openai-claims-it-solved-an-80-year-old-math-problem-for-real-this-time/) · [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-just-solved-an-80-year-old-erdos-problem-and-mathematicians-are-amazed/)*

### Anthropic Co-Founder Says AI Will Win a Nobel Within 12 Months

Jack Clark told an Oxford University audience on May 21 that AI will partner with humans on a Nobel-worthy discovery by early 2027. His other timelines: AI-run companies generating millions in revenue within 18 months, bipedal robots assisting tradespeople in two years, and AI designing its own successors by 2028. He still holds that the technology carries "a non-zero chance of killing everyone on the planet."

*Sources: [ResultSense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-05-21-jack-clark-anthropic-ai-nobel-prize-prediction/) · [TechCentral.ie](https://www.techcentral.ie/anthropic-co-founder-predicts-that-ai-will-within-a-year/) · [Time](https://time.com/article/2026/05/22/anthropic-claude-code-jack-clark-ai-safety/)*

## Big Talent Moves Reshape the Labs

### DeepMind Pays $100M to Hire Contextual AI's Team

Google DeepMind agreed to bring more than 20 researchers from startup Contextual AI into its ranks and license their technology for roughly $100 million. Contextual co-founder and CEO Douwe Kiela is among those joining. The deal skips a formal acquisition — and the DOJ is watching: it flagged such acquihire structures as a potential antitrust "red flag" back in March.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-19/google-hires-staff-from-bezos-backed-contextual-ai-in-licensing-deal) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52681185/google-deepmind-contextual-ai-talent-antitrust-scrutiny) · [NewsBytesApp](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/deepmind-to-hire-contextual-ai-researchers-license-tech-for-90-million/tldr)*

### Andrej Karpathy Joins Anthropic's Pre-Training Team

Karpathy — OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI director — is moving to Anthropic to work on pre-training research. He'll also help build a team that uses Claude to accelerate Anthropic's own model training. For OpenAI, it's a symbolic sting: one of its founding architects is now working to make a rival's models better.

*Sources: [Medium / AI Analytics Diaries](https://medium.com/ai-analytics-diaries/openai-co-founder-joins-anthropic-what-andrej-karpathys-move-tells-us-about-the-real-ai-war-02c3bacb0bf1) · [AI Flash Report](https://aiflashreport.com/model-releases.html)*

## Developer Tools and Enterprise Deployment Race

### Anthropic Ships Managed Agents and Private Infrastructure Support for Claude Code

At Code with Claude events across London and other cities (May 19–22), Anthropic unveiled Managed Agents — pre-built agentic workflows companies can deploy without standing up their own stack. New sandboxes let enterprises run agents on their own servers. MCP tunnels let those agents reach internal systems without touching the public internet. Claude Code shipped a May 22 update improving background sessions and the code-review flow.

*Sources: [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/code-with-claude/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/21/claude-code-london-anthropic-ai-software-engineering/) · [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/05/21/1137735/anthropics-code-with-claude-showed-off-codings-future-whether-you-like-it-or-not/)*

### Google Cuts Gemini Ultra to $200, Adds $100 Developer Tier

Google trimmed its top Gemini subscription from $250 to $200 per month and introduced a new $100/month Developer tier for engineers who don't need the full plan. Combined with Gemini 3.5 Flash becoming the default model, the move reads as a direct response to enterprise customers pushing back on premium AI pricing.

*Sources: [HeyGoTrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/google-io-2026-gemini-deepmind-contextual-ai/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/21/google-ai-anthropic-openai-war)*

### Southeast Asia Shows Enterprise AI Has Moved Past the Pilot Phase

ATxEnterprise 2026 in Singapore on May 22 made the case that regional enterprise AI adoption is accelerating fast. VIP enterprise buyers in supply chain and logistics surged 500% year-on-year; healthcare and pharma rose 320%. A McKinsey figure shared at the conference captures the gap still to close: 62% of firms are experimenting with AI agents, but only 23% have scaled one.

*Sources: [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/05/22/tmt-newswire/pr-newswire/atxenterprise-2026-signals-southeast-asias-shift-from-ai-experimentation-to-enterprise-deployment/2349956)*
