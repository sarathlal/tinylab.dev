Today's headlines span the largest IPO in history, Anthropic's most capable model going public, and a quiet but significant overhaul of how ChatGPT remembers you.

## The AI IPO Wave Accelerates

### SpaceX Begins Trading on Nasdaq — Largest IPO in History at $1.77 Trillion

SpaceX opened on Nasdaq this morning under the ticker SPCX, priced at $135 per share after trading closed on June 11. The offering raised roughly $75 billion and values the company at $1.77 trillion — past Saudi Aramco's 2019 record for the largest IPO ever. Investor demand hit $250 billion before pricing, leaving the deal about 3.5 times oversubscribed. Senator Elizabeth Warren asked the SEC to delay the offering over governance and valuation concerns; the agency went ahead.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/09/spacex-ipo-explained-stock-price-date.html) · [Capital.com](https://capital.com/en-int/market-updates/spacex-ipo-targets-11-06-2026) · [HeyGoTrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/spacex-ipo-177-trillion-valuation-2026-ipo-wave/)*

### OpenAI Files S-1 at $852B — Anthropic Already In at $965B

OpenAI confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC on June 8 at an $852 billion valuation, with Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan leading. Anthropic filed at $965 billion on June 1 — it would surpass OpenAI's valuation if that number holds at pricing. The order of listing now looks like: SpaceX first, Anthropic second, OpenAI third. Three of the most-watched names in tech going public in sequence puts real pressure on market absorption.

*Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/01/nx-s1-5843199/anthropic-ipo-filing-ai-large) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/news/)*

## Frontier Models Make Their Move

### Anthropic Opens Mythos-Class Access with Claude Fable 5

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 on June 9 — the first version of its secretive Mythos tier that the general public can access. It scores 95% on SWE-bench Verified and tops the FrontierCode leaderboard, putting it 11 points above Claude Opus 4.8 on SWE-bench Pro. Pricing is $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output, double Opus 4.8 but well under Mythos Preview's $25/$125 rate. Requests touching cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, or model distillation fall back to Opus 4.8 via a safety classifier. Fable 5 runs free for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise customers through June 22 before usage credits kick in.

*Sources: [TruFoundry](https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/claude-fable-5-api-benchmarks-pricing-how-to-use-it) · [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/anthropic-claude-fable-5-on-aws-mythos-class-capabilities-with-built-in-safeguards-now-available/) · [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/blog/research/claude-fable-5-review)*

### Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro Is Weeks Away — 2M Tokens and Deep Think Mode

Google confirmed at I/O 2026 that Gemini 3.5 Pro would ship "next month," landing the window squarely in June. The model targets a 2-million-token context window and a "Deep Think" reasoning mode — Google's direct answer to Anthropic's extended thinking. As of June 12 it remains in limited Vertex enterprise preview. Gemini 3.5 Flash, which launched in May, already outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on several agentic benchmarks at under half the cost.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-june-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm) · [WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/gemini-3-5-pro-coming-next-month/) · [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/)*

### ChatGPT's Memory Got a Complete Rewrite — Factual Recall Jumps to 82.8%

OpenAI shipped Dreaming V3 on June 4, replacing ChatGPT's manual memory list with a background synthesis engine. The system reads across years of past conversations and updates what it knows about you without prompting — "you're going to Singapore in July" becomes "you went to Singapore" once the trip ends. Factual recall on OpenAI's internal eval rose from 41.5% in 2024 to 82.8% today. US Plus and Pro subscribers got first access; free-tier and international rollout follows in the coming weeks.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2187811/chatgpt-s-memory-is-getting-better-especially-if-you-re-on-the-free-tier/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317840/20260605/chatgpt-memory-dreaming-update-openai-rewrites-personalization-engine-limits-audit-trail.htm)*

## Enterprise AI Deals Close

### OpenAI Models Now Billable Through Existing Oracle Cloud Credits

Announced June 11: enterprise customers with Oracle Universal Credits can apply those credits toward OpenAI models and Codex — no separate vendor contract needed. For companies already carrying multi-year, eight- or nine-figure Oracle commitments, this removes a real procurement barrier. The integration sits inside the broader $300 billion Stargate infrastructure partnership. Codex is specifically included, giving Oracle clients direct access to OpenAI's code-generation tool through their existing spending.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-on-oracle-cloud/) · [StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-teams-up-with-oracle-cloud) · [Data Center Frontier](https://www.datacenterfrontier.com/machine-learning/article/55316610/openai-and-oracles-300b-stargate-deal-building-ais-national-scale-infrastructure)*

### KPMG and Microsoft Scale AI Agents Globally with Agent 365

On June 9, KPMG and Microsoft expanded their global relationship to deploy Microsoft's Agent 365 across KPMG's client base. KPMG wraps the framework in its "Trusted AI" compliance layer — letting large enterprises monitor, audit, and manage AI agents running on their systems. The deal targets the gap between what autonomous agents can do and what corporate governance currently allows them to do unsupervised.

*Sources: [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/06/09/kpmg-and-microsoft-scale-trusted-enterprise-ai-agents-globally-through-deployment-of-agent-365-and-copilot/)*
