Google's worst week in the AI talent war landed in full on Monday as the market absorbed back-to-back exits by two of the company's most consequential scientists.

## Google's AI Talent Exodus Costs $250 Billion

### Noam Shazeer Leaves for OpenAI, Taking the Transformer Legacy With Him

Google paid $2.7 billion two years ago to bring Shazeer back after his stint at Character.AI. He's now OpenAI's Lead for Architecture Research, responsible for exploring next-generation model architectures. Shazeer co-authored the 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper that invented the transformer — the architecture inside every major model today, including Gemini and ChatGPT. Sam Altman said Shazeer was "one of the people I have most wanted to work with since the very beginning of OpenAI."

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html) · [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/star-google-ai-researcher-shazeer-joins-openai) · [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91562193/google-ai-leader-noam-shazeer-leaves-company-for-openai)*

### Nobel Laureate John Jumper Heads to Anthropic After Nine Years at DeepMind

One day after Shazeer's exit, John Jumper announced on X he's leaving DeepMind for Anthropic. Jumper shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for building AlphaFold2, which cracked protein structure prediction. He said he'd take time to recharge before starting. Alphabet's stock fell roughly 7% on June 22 — erasing about $250 billion in market cap, the company's worst single session in over a year.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/19/john-jumper-to-leave-google-deepmind-for-anthropic.html) · [CNBC Alphabet](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/alphabet-goog-stock-ai-departures.html)*

## Frontier Model Race

### GPT-5.6 Is Expected to Drop Tomorrow

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki told staff GPT-5.6 is a "meaningful improvement" over GPT-5.5. The model carries a 1.5 million token context window — up 43% — and a larger reasoning budget. Prediction markets on June 15 gave an 83% probability to a June 22-28 launch window, with June 25 the sharpest estimate. No official announcement exists yet; the evidence comes from internal logs and leaks.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318492/20260616/gpt-56-openai-chief-scientist-calls-it-meaningful-leap-june-launch-nears.htm) · [ChatForest](https://chatforest.com/builders-log/openai-gpt-5-6-june-2026-pre-release-builder-guide/) · [WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/gpt-5-6-canary-leak-what-we-know/)*

### Gemini 3.5 Pro Remains Stuck in Limited Preview

Six weeks after Sundar Pichai promised the model at Google I/O, Gemini 3.5 Pro is still only available to a handful of Vertex AI enterprise customers. When it ships, it targets a 2 million token context window and a "Deep Think" reasoning mode. Prediction markets put the odds of a public launch before June 30 at 50-55%.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-june-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm) · [FindSkill.ai](https://findskill.ai/blog/gemini-3-5-pro-release-date/) · [WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/gemini-3-5-pro-coming-next-month/)*

## AI Export Controls and the Global Sovereignty Scramble

### Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Remain Blocked Globally

On June 12, the US Commerce Department ordered Anthropic to cut off foreign nationals from Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5, citing a narrow jailbreak that could assist with cybersecurity tasks. Anthropic disagreed with the government's reasoning but disabled both models for every user, unable to segment by nationality in real time. Both models had launched just three days earlier.

*Sources: [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/ai-company-anthropic-suspends-access-claude-fable-5-claude-mythos-5-following-us) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/19/us-export-ban-on-anthropics-ai-models-further-strains-alliances) · [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/6/ai-company-anthropic-suspends-access-to-claude-fable-5-claude-mythos-5-following-us-export-control-directive)*

### EU Schedules Emergency AI Board Session Over the Anthropic Ban

The Anthropic shutdown is the first time a US government directive has pulled a frontier AI model from the global market entirely. It's accelerating calls across US allies for AI self-reliance. The European Union has scheduled an emergency session of its AI Board to consider countermeasures, with data localization mandates among the options on the table.

*Sources: [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/anthropic-export-controls-ai-sovereignty/) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/14/us-asks-anthropic-to-block-global-access-to-top-ai-models-why-it-matters)*

## AI Labs Bet on Machine Welfare

### DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta All Expand Consciousness Research

The Financial Times reported that all three labs are now funding dedicated machine consciousness and AI welfare research, hiring philosophers, psychologists, and ethicists to assess whether AI systems have morally relevant inner states. Anthropic says it has been testing models for behaviors resembling "panic" and "anxiety," noting the question is "serious enough to study carefully as AI systems get more capable." On June 23, Demis Hassabis told Semafor that DeepMind "remains deeply committed" to understanding consciousness — while also insisting the company is "still winning" the talent war.

*Sources: [Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-deemind-ai-consciousness) · [Reuters/TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L4N42A0BV:0-google-deepmind-anthropic-and-meta-expand-research-into-machine-consciousness-and-ai-welfare-ft/) · [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/23/2026/deepmind-chief-demis-hassabis-says-googles-still-winning-ai-talent)*

### Congress Drafts the First Comprehensive US Federal AI Law

Representatives Jay Obernolte and Lori Trahan released a discussion draft of the Great American Artificial Intelligence Act on June 4, covering frontier model governance, workforce, cybersecurity, and international cooperation. The bill would require frontier developers to file disclosures and get third-party audits — the first bipartisan attempt at a federal framework of this scope. It's still a draft, but it's the furthest any such effort has progressed in the US.

*Sources: [Gunderson Dettmer](https://www.gunder.com/en/news-insights/insights/2026-ai-laws-update-key-regulations-and-practical-guidance) · [Verifywise](https://verifywise.ai/blog/state-of-ai-governance-regulations-united-states-2026)*
