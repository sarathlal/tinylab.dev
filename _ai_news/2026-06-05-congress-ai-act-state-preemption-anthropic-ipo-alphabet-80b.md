Yesterday, Congress dropped a 269-page bill that would prevent states from regulating AI development for three years. The same day, legislatures in five states pushed their own AI bills forward — and Anthropic filed to go public at a near-trillion-dollar valuation.

## The Federal vs. State AI Regulation Collision

### Bipartisan House Bill Would Freeze State AI Development Laws for Three Years

The Great American AI Act — a 269-page discussion draft unveiled June 4 — would preempt any state law "specifically regulating the development" of an AI model for three years. Reps. Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) wrote it with four other co-sponsors. States would keep the right to regulate how AI is used within their borders, but not how it's built.

*Sources: [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/06/04/bipartisan-ai-draft-proposes-three-year-preemption-of-state-laws/) · [Nextgov/FCW](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/06/lawmakers-propose-ai-framework-would-preempt-state-laws-3-years/413975/) · [Rep. Trahan](https://trahan.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=3783)*

### Trump Signed an Order Asking AI Labs for 30-Day Government Preview Before Release

President Trump signed an executive order on June 2 asking frontier AI developers to give the government voluntary early access to new models for up to 30 days before public release. An earlier draft required 90 days; the final version cut that window to 30 and explicitly bans any mandatory licensing or pre-clearance regime. The order also creates an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse for sharing vulnerability data across federal agencies.

*Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/trump-executive-order-ai.html) · [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-promotes-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/)*

## State Legislatures Won't Wait

### Vermont, Illinois, and California All Moved AI Bills in the Same 24 Hours

Vermont enacted a ban on AI therapy chatbots on June 5; Illinois lawmakers sent five AI bills to Governor Pritzker before adjourning; and California advanced 30 AI bills through second-chamber committees. New York and Rhode Island also moved bills forward after months of stalling. All of this landed one day after Congress introduced legislation that would strip states of the power to regulate AI development.

*Sources: [Transparency Coalition AI](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-june5-2026)*

### Colorado Repealed Its Own AI Act Before the Federal Fight Could Decide It

Governor Jared Polis signed SB 26-189 on May 14, repealing SB 24-205 — one of the country's first algorithmic fairness laws — and replacing it with a narrower automated-decision-making disclosure framework that doesn't take effect until January 1, 2027. The original law required AI developers to assess high-risk uses and notify consumers; the replacement focuses on transparency rights for users rather than obligations on builders.

*Sources: [CO-AIMS](https://co-aims.com/blog/is-colorado-ai-act-delayed-2026-timeline) · [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-04-24-state-ai-laws-where-are-they-now)*

## AI Labs Race to Public Markets

### Anthropic Files Confidential S-1 at a $965B Valuation

Anthropic submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC on June 1, four days after closing a $65B Series H led by Altimeter, Sequoia, and Dragoneer at a $965B post-money valuation. Its revenue run rate hit $47B in May — up from $14B in February — with most coming from enterprise API and cloud deals. The company could go public as early as October 2026 in one of the largest US market debuts on record.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-ipo-s1-prospectus.html) · [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/tv-shows/business/20260602-ai-startup-anthropic-files-for-ipo-after-reaching-965-billion-valuation)*

### Alphabet Raising $80B with Berkshire Hathaway as the Anchor Buyer

Google's parent announced an $80B equity raise on June 1: $30B in underwritten public offerings and a $40B at-the-market program starting in Q3. Berkshire Hathaway separately agreed to buy $10B of stock in a private placement at roughly $350/share. Alphabet has guided 2026 capex at $180-190B, doubling last year's $91.4B.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/alphabet-to-raise-80-billion-in-equity-capital-for-ai-spending) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-to-raise-80-billion-from-stock-sales-to-fund-ai-buildout.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-80-billion-ai-buildout)*

## New Model Capabilities

### OpenAI Codex Now Connects to 66 New Tools Across More User Tiers

OpenAI expanded Codex on June 3 with 66 single-app plugins covering Databricks, Salesforce, Hex, and Clay, along with role-specific integrations for teams across more workflows. The update also extends access beyond Pro subscribers, reaching more plan levels with agentic coding capabilities. OpenAI called it the broadest push yet to bring Codex to "every role, tool, and workflow."

*Sources: [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/codex) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-upgrades-to-codex/)*

### Claude Opus 4.8 Adds Effort Control and Dynamic Workflows in Claude Code

Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 4.8 this week with effort control in claude.ai — a new slider that lets users tune how hard the model works on a given task. Dynamic workflows in Claude Code let agents chain multi-step tasks without manual handoffs. The update also improves honesty and reasoning benchmarks, and a cheaper fast mode reduces cost for lighter workloads.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)*
