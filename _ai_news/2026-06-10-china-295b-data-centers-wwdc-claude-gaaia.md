China's $295 billion AI data center plan led June 9's news, joined by Apple WWDC developer announcements that put Claude and Gemini inside the iOS toolchain for the first time — and a congressional fight over who gets to write America's AI rules.

## The AI Compute Race

### China Plans $295 Billion to Build a Nationwide AI Data Center Network

China is preparing to spend 2 trillion yuan ($295 billion) over five years on a network of interconnected AI computing hubs across the country. The National Development and Reform Commission is drafting the blueprint; state carriers China Mobile and China Telecom will operate the facilities. At least 80% of the chips must come from domestic suppliers — primarily Huawei — effectively shutting Nvidia and AMD out. The goal is a unified national compute environment by 2028, not counting additional spending by private firms like Alibaba and Tencent.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-09/china-prepares-295-billion-plan-to-fund-nationwide-ai-buildout) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/china-ai-data-center-buildout-295-billion-huawei-chips-060926) · [Techstrong.ai](https://techstrong.ai/articles/china-plans-295-billion-ai-investment-focused-on-interconnected-network/)*

### Applied Digital Locks In a $5.2 Billion, 210 MW Hyperscaler Lease

Applied Digital signed a 15-year take-or-pay lease at its Delta Forge 2 AI Factory campus with an unnamed investment-grade hyperscaler, covering 210 megawatts of compute capacity. Base revenue is $5.2 billion; all renewal options push the total to $12.7 billion over 30 years. The deal is the company's fifth AI Factory campus and brings its total contracted portfolio to $36 billion.

*Sources: [Applied Digital IR](https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/154/applied-digital-signs-210-mw-lease-at-delta-forge-2) · [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/08/3308459/0/en/Applied-Digital-Signs-210-MW-Lease-at-Delta-Forge-2-Expanding-Its-AI-Factory-Franchise-Model-to-a-Fifth-Campus.html)*

## WWDC's AI Developer Upgrades

### Apple Foundation Models Now Swaps Claude, Gemini, or On-Device AI with One Package Change

Apple's updated Foundation Models framework — announced at WWDC developer sessions on June 9 — lets developers switch between Apple's on-device model, Google's Gemini, and Anthropic's Claude by changing a single Swift Package Manager dependency. No changes required in session logic or app code. Claude usage is billed directly to your Anthropic API account. A team can prototype with on-device Apple Intelligence and route complex queries to Claude or Gemini without restructuring anything.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318039/20260609/wwdc-2026-developer-tools-foundation-models-now-swaps-ai-providers-without-code-changes.htm) · [ByteIota](https://byteiota.com/apple-foundation-models-wwdc-2026-multimodal-python-sdk/) · [Claude API Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/cli-sdks-libraries/libraries/apple-foundation-models)*

### Xcode 27 Ships a Dual-Engine AI Coding System Built on Claude and Gemini

Xcode 27 pairs a local Neural Engine model for real-time Swift suggestions with a cloud routing layer for heavier analysis via Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini, or OpenAI's agents. Developers decide which model handles which task; the routing is handled inside the IDE. It's the first version of Xcode where multi-provider AI is a first-class part of the coding workflow, not a plugin.

*Sources: [Pokde.net](https://pokde.net/system/software/ai/apple-ai-framework-xcode-27) · [ByteIota](https://byteiota.com/apple-foundation-models-wwdc-2026-multimodal-python-sdk/)*

## OpenAI's June Platform Moves

### ChatGPT's "Dreaming" Update Gives Memory That Stays Current Over Time

OpenAI rolled out a new memory architecture to ChatGPT Plus and Pro users in the US on June 8. Called "Dreaming," it automatically synthesizes and keeps your personal context current — so "you're traveling to Singapore in July" becomes "you went to Singapore in July 2026" after the trip ends. Pro users get twice the previous memory capacity. Free and Go plan users get it in the coming weeks.

*Sources: [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-is-rolling-out-a-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-memory/) · [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-upgrades-chatgpt-memory-architecture-for-fresher-pers-b26b51d5)*

### OpenAI Invites Outside Researchers to Study AI's Economic Impact

OpenAI launched the Economic Research Exchange on June 8 — a platform inviting external academics to study how AI affects workers, firms, and institutions, with privacy-protected access to OpenAI tools. Applications close July 5; selected researchers hear back by July 31. The Exchange comes as OpenAI's confidential S-1 filing sits with the SEC, and the lab is under pressure to show it takes independent scrutiny seriously.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/economic-research-exchange/) · [StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-launches-ai-economic-research-program)*

## Washington's AI Governance Standoff

### House Bipartisan Bill Would Freeze State AI Laws for Three Years

Reps. Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) dropped a 269-page discussion draft on June 4 called the Great American Artificial Intelligence Act. It would preempt any state law specifically targeting AI model development for three years — suspending transparency rules in California, New York, and Illinois. Opposition came fast: labor unions, consumer advocates, and a House Democratic commission all pushed back. Americans for Responsible Innovation president Brad Carson called it "a generational mistake."

*Sources: [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/06/04/bipartisan-ai-draft-proposes-three-year-preemption-of-state-laws/) · [NextGov](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/06/lawmakers-propose-ai-framework-would-preempt-state-laws-3-years/413975/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317903/20260606/federal-ai-regulation-bill-freezes-state-consumer-protections-three-years-sparks-revolt.htm)*

### The White House Is Running a Separate Deal to Block State Rules

The White House and Sen. Marsha Blackburn (R-TN) are separately negotiating to pair federal preemption of state AI laws with the Kids Online Safety Act and deepfake legislation — trading one tech priority for another. Both tracks point the same direction: pull AI governance to Washington before states move further. Colorado's new AI consumer protection law takes effect June 30.

*Sources: [Benton Institute](https://www.benton.org/headlines/white-house-hill-relaunch-effort-block-state-ai-laws) · [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/)*
