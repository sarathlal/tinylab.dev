Anthropic spent the week chasing history's biggest IPO while dodging a billion-dollar lawsuit, and Elon Musk's SpaceX just cost Cursor its favorite AI model. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last two days.

## Anthropic's IPO Chase Collides With a Billion-Dollar Copyright Fight

### Sony and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic Over "Tens of Thousands" of Stolen Songs

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sued Anthropic on Friday, accusing it of "illegally torrenting, scraping, and downloading" copyrighted songs to train Claude. The suit names co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann directly and cites specific tracks, including "Eye of the Tiger" and Taylor Swift's "Paper Rings." The publishers say Claude can reproduce copyrighted lyrics on request, which could let it replace licensed lyric services outright.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/sony-music-warner-sue-anthropic-alleging-a-brazen-campaign-of-intellectual-property-theft/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/29/anthropic-sony-warner-music-copyright)*

### Anthropic's $45B Nscale Deal Fuels an IPO Bid Bigger Than SpaceX's

Anthropic agreed to pay Nscale $45 billion over six years for AI computing power out of a West Virginia data center running Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips. The deal adds to a growing list of infrastructure bets as Anthropic preps an IPO that Polymarket bettors now expect to beat SpaceX's record $85.7 billion raise from June. Anthropic raised $65 billion in May at a $965 billion valuation, even after losing roughly $42 billion in 2025.

*Sources: [MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/anthropic-to-rent-ai-computing-power-from-nscale-for-45-billion-source-says-ce7858d9de8af121) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/antoniopequenoiv/2026/08/26/anthropic-overtakes-spacex-in-betting-odds-for-2026s-biggest-ipo/)*

## Musk's AI Empire Grows as OpenAI Cuts Cursor Loose

### OpenAI Will Stop Supplying Models to Cursor After SpaceX's $60B Buyout

OpenAI told Cursor it's winding down their model contract, with access cutting off November 12 — the maximum notice period the deal allows after a change of ownership. The move follows SpaceX's $60 billion acquisition of the coding startup in April, and OpenAI says it can't trust Musk's companies to follow its terms of service given past violations at Twitter and xAI. Cursor CEO Michael Truell says OpenAI models make up only 5% of Cursor's traffic and that talks are underway to find a fix.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-cuts-off-cursor-after-spacex-acquisition-citing-musks-history-of-breaking-contracts/)*

### Grok 4.6 Lands on Microsoft Foundry With a 500K-Token Context Window

xAI added Grok 4.6 to Microsoft Foundry, built for long-running agents and visual work with configurable reasoning levels from low to xhigh. It's the fourth major platform to pick up the model this month, after GitHub Copilot, Amazon Bedrock, and Google's Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Grok is spreading across every major cloud right as its parent company tightens its grip on Cursor.

*Sources: [xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6-microsoft-foundry) · [Big Hat Group](https://www.bighatgroup.com/blog/xai-weekly-2026-08-30/)*

## Washington Can't Agree on Who Regulates AI, Brussels Presses Ahead

### A Three-Way Turf War Is Paralyzing US AI Oversight

The Commerce Department, national security officials, and pro-industry White House aides are stuck in a standoff over who gets to test AI models before release, CNN reported. The fight escalated after the Center for AI Standards and Innovation Security quietly deleted a May announcement about new testing deals with Google, Microsoft, and xAI, after the White House said it conflicted with a planned executive order. CAISI was then told to stop talking to the public entirely — even interagency meetings got shut down.

*Sources: [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/08/30/business/the-scramble-us-government-to-regulate-ai) · [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/the-white-house-is-at-war-with-itself-over-who-gets-to-regulate-ai)*

### The EU's AI Act Now Has Teeth: Fines Up to 3% of Global Revenue

Brussels started enforcing its AI Act's transparency rules this month, requiring chatbots to identify themselves and AI-generated content to carry machine-readable marks. Companies that skip the disclosures risk fines up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide revenue, whichever is bigger. Anthropic and OpenAI are both now under the EU AI Office's direct scrutiny, even as Washington can't decide who's in charge of the same job at home.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/eu-ai-act-enforcement-powers.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/28/eu-ai-act-gets-real)*
