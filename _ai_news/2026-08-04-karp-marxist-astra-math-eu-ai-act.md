A record Palantir quarter turned into a broadside against OpenAI and Anthropic, an OpenAI model quietly solved math problems that stumped humans for decades, and Brussels started fining companies over chatbot disclosure. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## AI Labs Face a Reckoning on Trust, Rules, and Jobs

### Palantir's Karp Calls the AI Industry 'Marxist' After a Record Quarter

Palantir posted $1.9 billion in revenue on August 3, up 93% year over year, with $1.1 billion in profit. Karp used the moment to attack the labs building the models: "There are Marxist overtones and undertones to our business," he wrote in the shareholder letter, accusing large language model makers of trying to "capture the means of production of their purported partners." His point: enterprises hand over sensitive data to frontier labs and get little measurable value back.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/after-killer-quarter-palantir-ceo-alex-karp-calls-ai-industry-marxist/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/03/palantir-earnings-guidance-beat-revenue-profit-ai-demand/)*

### EU Starts Fining Companies Over AI Transparency, Delays the Hard Rules

Article 50 of the EU AI Act became enforceable on August 2, requiring chatbots to disclose they're not human and deepfakes to carry visible labels. Violators face fines up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover, and the EU AI Office now has full penalty power over general-purpose model providers for the first time. The tougher high-risk rules, covering things like hiring and credit-scoring AI, got pushed back to December 2027 under a separate "AI Omnibus" deal.

*Sources: [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august) · [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026)*

### Over Half of 2026's Layoffs Now Cite AI as the Reason

A tracker update as of August 3 counts 322 layoff events this year affecting 205,832 workers, and 54% of those events explicitly cite AI, automation, or machine learning as a driver, touching 170,945 workers across 173 companies. Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff said the company used AI agents to cut its support staff from 9,000 to 5,000. Not everyone buys the causal story: Gallup data shows 62% of laid-off workers weren't even using AI themselves.

*Sources: [FounderReports](https://founderreports.com/ai-layoffs-tracker/) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/ai-adoption-job-security)*

## Frontier Models Push Into New Territory

### OpenAI's Astra Solves 10 Math Problems That Stumped Experts for Decades

An internal version of OpenAI's next model, Astra, cracked ten open problems in math and theoretical computer science, then published machine-checked Lean 4 proofs on GitHub with zero unproven steps. The results include the first explicit construction of a non-sofic group, a question open since 1999, and a disproof of Connes's rigidity conjecture. OpenAI says the whole run cost about $2,000, and because Lean's kernel either verifies a proof or rejects it, there's no need to trust the model's word for it.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322710/20260802/openais-astra-solves-ten-decade-old-math-problems-machine-checkable-lean-proofs.htm) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/02/openais-astra-solves-10-long-open-math-problems-publishes-proofs/)*

### DeepSeek's V4-Flash Undercuts Everyone on Price

DeepSeek's V4-Flash-0731 model prices in at $0.14 per million input tokens and $0.28 per million output tokens, the cheapest of any major model tracked by Artificial Analysis. Running it through a full benchmark suite costs roughly 3 cents, versus 86 cents for Moonshot's comparable model. DeepSeek closed its first outside funding round this year, over $7 billion, giving it room to keep undercutting rivals as OpenAI has already started cutting GPT-5.6 prices in response.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/01/deepseek-model-cheap-ai-price-war) · [Qz](https://qz.com/deepseek-v4-flash-cheapest-ai-model-benchmark-080326)*

### Microsoft's Unannounced Voice Model Surfaces in a Hidden Preview

MAI-Realtime appeared inside Microsoft's MAI Playground on August 2 with no blog post, no model card, and no pricing page. It's a full-duplex voice model, meaning it can listen and talk at the same time instead of trading turns, putting it in the same category as OpenAI's realtime voice models. Early testers found two voices, 17 supported languages, and a debug panel showing live latency, with a small group of partners already trying it out.

*Sources: [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/exclusive-microsoft-tests-new-mai-realtime-voice-model/)*

## Ai4 2026 Opens in Vegas With a Fight Over Extinction Risk

### America's Largest AI Conference Opens With 12,000 Attendees

Ai4 2026 kicked off August 4 at the Venetian in Las Vegas, drawing more than 12,000 attendees from over 90 countries, 1,000-plus speakers, and nearly 400 exhibitors including AMD, Nvidia, Cisco, Google Cloud, and Mistral AI. The exhibit hall grew from about 225 exhibitors last year, and the three-day agenda runs through August 6 with tracks on enterprise deployment, governance, and applied AI.

*Sources: [AccessNewswire](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/computers-technology-and-internet/ai4-2026-unites-the-worlds-most-influential-ai-leaders-for-an-unp-1191330) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/ai4-2026-unveils-expanded-exhibit-191000645.html)*

### Hinton and Ng Will Share a Stage to Argue About Whether AI Ends Humanity

Geoffrey Hinton, Andrew Ng, and Fei-Fei Li headline Ai4's main stage on August 5 in a rare joint appearance built around their opposing views. Hinton has warned the industry may be building something that ends humanity as it exists today. Ng calls that framing "harmful nonsense" that distracts from real, fixable harms. Neither has backed down in public before, which is what makes sharing a stage notable.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322674/20260802/ai4-2026-opens-tuesday-hinton-ng-face-off-ais-existential-stakes.htm)*
