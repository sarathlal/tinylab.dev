Four days after Jensen Huang quit, his biggest backers want the door left open for a comeback. Meanwhile the Fed says almost everyone it talks to now sees AI as the top threat to markets, three days before Anthropic starts trading.

## Nvidia's leadership fight and the chip rules around it

### Two big Nvidia shareholders ask the board to keep Huang's return on the table
Altimeter's Brad Gerstner and Atreides' Gavin Baker wrote to Nvidia's board asking it not to rush a successor. They didn't demand Huang's reinstatement. They asked that it stay possible. The FT reports Huang has talked with allies about coming back, while Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent wants the board to look outside the company. A separate petition backed by Elon Musk and Tim Sweeney asks for Huang's return while the board's independent investigation runs. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and Huang has no controlling stake, so the call is the board's.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/nvidia-investors-jensen-huang-return-board-letter.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/zacharyfolk/2026/11/15/jensen-huang-is-already-considering-returning-to-nvidia-report-says/) · [Yahoo Finance (FT)](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/breaking-news/article/jensen-huang-weighs-comeback-as-nvidia-ceo-as-allies-lobby-board-ft-reports-bessent-calls-for-dramatic-shakeup-204839950.html) · [Nvidia 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm)*

### NYT: Lutnick pushed softer chip rules that touch his family firm's clients
A Malaysian data center called A201 is set to open next year full of Nvidia's top AI servers. It's run by Bitdeer, a client of Cantor Fitzgerald, the firm Howard Lutnick ran for decades and his sons now lead. The New York Times reports Lutnick wants the new export rule to cover only future chip shipments. Data centers already stocked with US hardware would be exempt. The Times found no evidence Cantor swayed him, and Commerce says he followed his ethics agreement. Sen. Ruben Gallego called it "shady" and wants Lutnick, his sons and Huang to testify under oath.

*Sources: [Philadelphia Inquirer (NYT)](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/howard-lutnick-commerce-secretary-rules-data-centers-nvidia-chips-china-southeast-asia-cantor-fitzgerald-20261115.html) · [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4672784-lutnick-backs-narrower-ai-chip-controls-as-family-firm-s-business-draws-scrutiny)*

### Seven countries' data centers may soon need a US license to buy AI chips
The draft rule covers non-US-run data centers in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Norway, Brazil and South Africa. Commerce's export bureau plans to send it for interagency review, with Federal Register publication expected in late November. The gap it's chasing is remote access. The Institute for Progress says renting is about half of the US-made compute Washington could still cut off from China by the end of 2027. Malaysia is moving too. It plans to make operators disclose GPU counts, locations and power use.

*Sources: [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/regulation/55398) · [DigiTimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261113VL214/ai-chip-data-center-usa-policy-nvidia-2027.html) · [The Epoch Times](https://www.theepochtimes.com/world/malaysia-probes-ai-chip-shipments-as-us-scrutiny-turns-to-southeast-asia-data-centers-6124881)*

## Wall Street counts AI's risks as Anthropic heads to Nasdaq

### The Fed says 87% of its market contacts now name AI as a stability risk
The Fed surveyed 23 market contacts for its November 13 Financial Stability Report, and 87% named AI. In the spring it was 50%. Debt tied to advanced computing hit $680 billion by October. That's five times the total for all of 2023. The Fed compared the buildout to the late-1990s broadband boom and said this cycle may soon pass it. A broad sell-off in risky assets came second on the worry list, at 52%.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/fed-sees-risks-tied-to-elevated-asset-prices-partly-driven-by-ai) · [AOL (Reuters)](https://www.aol.com/articles/ai-rockets-top-worries-financial-210222000.html)*

### Anthropic's bankers reportedly eye pricing above the $70-$78 range
The FT, citing five people, says bankers expect to price Anthropic's 825 million shares above the range. That could push the valuation to $1.5 trillion or more, up from about $1.4 trillion at the top of the range. It's still well below the $2 trillion some investors hoped for in September. Pricing is set for Wednesday, November 18, with Nasdaq trading as "ANT" on Thursday.

*Sources: [RuntimeWire (FT)](https://runtimewire.com/article/anthropic-bankers-lift-ipo-valuation-1-5tn) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382939/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-at-70-to-78-valuing-company-at-up-to-1-4-trillion)*

### Anthropic's filing shows $121.3B promised to Google Cloud through 2033
The amended IPO filing raises Anthropic's minimum Google commitment from $114.5 billion. Its Microsoft deal now runs up to $49.2 billion, and most of it can't be cancelled. One deal moved the other way. A $34.9 billion agreement with Lambda is now terminable by either side because Lambda didn't lock in a facility lease on time. Investors buying this week are buying those bills too.

*Sources: [DatacenterDynamics](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/anthropic-signs-another-43bn-in-compute-agreements-with-microsoft) · [Magica](https://magica.com/news/anthropic-adds-microsoft-google-cloud-commitments) · [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-anthropic-raises-its-google-spending-floor-to-121-3b/)*

## Allies and states write new AI rules

### The US and eight partners sign the "San Francisco Declaration" on AI supply chains
Japan, Australia, Israel, the UK, South Korea, India, Qatar and the UAE signed on. The pact expands Pax Silica, the State Department push led by Under Secretary Jacob Helberg. Signers will draw up a joint list of chips, minerals, energy and factory capacity that need protection from China. Germany, Sweden, the Netherlands and the European Commission are reportedly set to join next week. The timing matters, since Trump meets Xi at APEC in Shenzhen soon after.

*Sources: [Yahoo News (FT)](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/report-us-allies-sign-accord-181215730.html) · [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/ai-security/india-signs-us-led-declaration-to-safeguard-ai-supply-chains-report)*

### California's AI kill-switch recommendations are due today
Gov. Gavin Newsom's September 18 order gave state officials until November 16 to recommend changes to California's AI safety laws. On the list: outside safety evaluators at AI labs, independent checks of the safety frameworks labs already file under SB 53, and a possible "kill switch" for frontier models. The order doesn't require a kill switch yet. Watch for whether the state publishes the recommendations or keeps them internal.

*Sources: [Office of the Governor](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026-09-18/governor-newsom-issues-executive-order-to-accelerate-independent-oversight-and-advance-the-creation-of-an-ai-kill-switch) · [CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/politics/2026/09/ai-rules-newsom-state-directive)*
