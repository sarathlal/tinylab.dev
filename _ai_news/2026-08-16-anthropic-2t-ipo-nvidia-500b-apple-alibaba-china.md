Wall Street spent the last day and a half figuring out how to finance the AI buildout, while the model race kept shipping and the legal bills kept coming due. Here's what actually mattered.

## Wall Street Bets Bigger on the AI Buildout

### Anthropic Investors Eye a $2 Trillion IPO in October

Investors briefed on Anthropic's plans expect the company to go public in October at a valuation of $2 trillion or more, which would make it the largest IPO ever, surpassing SpaceX's $1.77 trillion debut in June. The math requires Anthropic's annualized revenue to hit $100-120 billion by year-end, up from $47 billion in May. Even the company's own executives haven't settled on a target valuation, and Jim Cramer has already dismissed the number as unrealistic.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-ipo-2-trillion-october-largest-ever-spacex/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/13/anthropic-eyes-2-trillion-in-october-ipo-a-record-breaking-debut/)*

### Nvidia Lines Up $500B to Turn GPUs Into a Wall Street Asset Class

Nvidia struck agreements with six of the world's biggest asset managers, including BlackRock, Blackstone, Apollo, KKR, Brookfield and Goldman Sachs, to assemble a $500 billion financing pipeline for data centers and GPU clusters. The pitch: treat compute like commercial real estate, something companies without top credit ratings can borrow against instead of buying chips outright. Jensen Huang calls Nvidia GPUs an "investable asset," but China's fast-growing domestic chip supply could undercut the whole thesis by flooding the market with cheaper alternatives.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/nvidia-wall-street-asset-managers-500-billion-ai-push.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-ai-funding-jensen-huang-china-risk.html)*

### Big Tech's AI Purchase Commitments Near $1.5 Trillion

Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Nvidia, Oracle and Meta have racked up close to $1.5 trillion in purchase commitments for chips, data center capacity and energy, according to a Financial Times analysis out this week. That sits on top of another roughly $1.5 trillion in lease commitments tracked separately by Goldman Sachs. None of it counts as debt on a balance sheet, but it's all cash the companies have contractually promised to spend.

*Sources: [Being Guru](https://beingguru.com/big-tech-ai-purchase-commitments-approach-1-5-trillion-as-infrastructure-obligations-surge/)*

## The Model Race Keeps Shipping

### Grok 4.6 Ships, and Musk Says 4.7 Could Beat Every Model on Intelligence

xAI launched Grok 4.6 on August 12, matching GPT-5.6 Sol Max on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at the same $2/$6 per million token price as Grok 4.5, with context expanded to 500K tokens. On August 15, Musk posted that Grok 4.6 is the pick for speed and cost, then said Grok 4.7 "has a good chance of exceeding all current models in intelligence." No release date yet.

*Sources: [Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/grok-4-6-vs-4-7-what-elon-musk-just-said-about-xais-ai) · [FelloAI](https://felloai.com/all-we-know-so-far-about-grok-5/)*

### DeepSeek Hikes API Prices Up to 1,100% as V4-Pro Goes GA

DeepSeek's bargain era is ending. Starting 16:00 UTC on August 16, peak-hour output pricing for V4-Pro jumps to $3.96 per million tokens, up from $0.87, with V4-Flash rising from $0.28 to $1.32. The move lands alongside the general release of V4-Pro-0813, tuned for agentic tasks like tool use and multi-step workflows. Even after the hike, DeepSeek stays far cheaper than GPT-5.6 Sol's $30 or Claude Fable 5's $50 per million output tokens.

*Sources: [Quartz](https://qz.com/deepseek-api-price-increase-v4-peak-off-peak-081326) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/deepseek-increases-prices-for-ai-services-by-multiple-times/)*

### Meta Open-Sources Muse Glimmer, a 30B Model Built for Laptops

Meta released Muse Glimmer under an Apache 2.0 license, its first return to a fully permissive license after the more restrictive Llama Community License. The 30-billion-parameter model runs on a single GPU and is tuned for local agentic work: tool use, long task trajectories, and coding harnesses. Weights are live on Hugging Face now.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meta-returns-to-open-source-with-muse-glimmer-an-apache-2-0-licensed-30b-parameter-ai-model-optimized-for-agents-available-now) · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/meta-muse-glimmer/)*

### OpenAI Previews a Cerebras-Powered Tier That Runs Sol 14x Faster

OpenAI's new Ultrafast API tier, built on Cerebras chips, pushes GPT-5.6 Sol to up to 750 output tokens per second, 14 times the standard speed, with no drop in intelligence. On GDP-Val, a benchmark of tasks like legal briefs and financial models, it delivered a 5.6x end-to-end speedup. It's in early preview for a select group of API customers.

*Sources: [Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/accelerating-gpt-5-6-sol-ultrafast-with-openai) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/14/openais-gpt-5-6-sol-runs-up-to-14x-faster-with-ultrafast-mode/)*

## China Strategy Shifts While Legal Bills Come Due

### Apple Trains Its Own China LLM With Alibaba, a Beijing First

Apple built a proprietary large language model for the China market with help from Alibaba, rather than leaning solely on a third-party model as originally planned. That makes Apple the first foreign company Chinese regulators have cleared to offer a proprietary AI model in the country. Apple will still fold in Alibaba's Qwen model too, running a dual-track setup as Apple Intelligence rolls out in China over the coming months.

*Sources: [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/08/14/apple-ai-model-china-alibaba/) · [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4633040-apple-partners-with-alibaba-on-custom-ai-model-for-china-reuters)*

### A Sixth Grader Joins the CSAM Lawsuit Pile-Up Against xAI

A woman identified as Jane Doe 4 joined an ongoing lawsuit against xAI, alleging her stepfather used Grok to turn a childhood photo of her, taken at age 11, into more than 7,000 sexualized images. It's at least the fourth such case filed against the company since July. xAI says it suspended over 52,000 accounts and filed more than 73,000 reports to NCMEC this year, but the lawsuits keep stacking up even as Grok's model lineup keeps shipping.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/woman-claims-her-stepfather-used-grok-to-transform-childhood-photo-into-explicit-imagery/) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/potts-law-firm-announces-third-lawsuit-against-xai-in-relation-to-alleged-ai-generated-child-sexual-abuse-material-302846165.html)*
