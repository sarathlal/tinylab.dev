Washington made AI regulatory history last week by ordering the first-ever export restrictions on a commercially available AI model — and Anthropic is now in the middle of a political, diplomatic, and commercial fight over who gets to use its best work.

## Anthropic's Export Control Crisis

### U.S. Blocks Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for All Foreign Nationals

On June 12, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick invoked the Export Control Reform Act to immediately suspend access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for any foreign national — inside or outside the U.S., including Anthropic's own foreign-born employees. Officials cited fears the models could be jailbroken to identify software vulnerabilities and diverted to Chinese or Russian military intelligence. As of June 17, Trump told reporters the negotiations are "going fine," but a personal appeal from UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer for a British exemption was rejected — leaving allies locked out alongside adversaries. It's the first time any government has used national security export controls to restrict a commercial AI product already in widespread public use.

*Sources: [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/ai-company-anthropic-suspends-access-claude-fable-5-claude-mythos-5-following-us) · [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/claude-mythos-5-and-fable-5-export/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318573/20260617/anthropic-fable-5-export-ban-trump-calls-g7-talks-fine-uk-exemption-dies.htm)*

### Anthropic Opens Seoul Office Anyway — NAVER, Samsung, LG, Nexon All Sign On

Five days after the export directive, Anthropic opened its Seoul office on June 17 — its third Asia-Pacific location after Tokyo and Bengaluru. KiYoung Choi, former GM of Snowflake Korea, leads it as Representative Director. NAVER is deploying Claude Code across its full engineering organization; Samsung SDS is rolling out Claude Cowork and Claude Code across Samsung Electronics; LG CNS is extending Claude to the LG Group; Nexon is using Claude Code for live-service game development. Anthropic also signed an MOU with Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT on AI safety. The timing is deliberate: Anthropic's most capable models are blocked for foreign nationals, yet its Asian enterprise reach keeps growing.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/seoul-office-partnerships-korean-ai-ecosystem) · [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/06/18/korea-Anthropic-Seoul-office-Korea-partnerships-Washington-AI-export-controls/4641781769900/) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/06/53267847/anthropic-eyes-south-korea-expansion-ahead-of-ipo-with-seoul-office-and-partnerships)*

## OpenAI's Health and Enterprise Push

### GPT-5.5 Instant Health AI Goes Free for All 230 Million Weekly ChatGPT Users

OpenAI announced on June 18 that GPT-5.5 Instant now powers health intelligence for all free ChatGPT users — the 230 million people who ask health questions on the platform each week. Factuality errors fell 71% versus its predecessor, and the model matched frontier-level performance on HealthBench Professional, a benchmark where OpenAI had doctors review 3,500 model outputs against answers physicians wrote with unlimited time and internet access. ChatGPT already helps users interpret lab results, prepare for appointments, and navigate insurance; making it free puts that capability in the hands of anyone who can't afford a paid subscription.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/improving-health-intelligence-in-chatgpt/) · [Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/openai-says-new-chatgpt-model-tops-physician-written-health-answers/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/05/openai-releases-gpt-5-5-instant-a-new-default-model-for-chatgpt/)*

### OpenAI's $150M Partner Network Targets 300,000 Certified Consultants by Year-End

OpenAI launched its Partner Network on June 14, putting $150M behind a program to drive enterprise AI deployment through consulting firms. Founding partners include Accenture, Bain, BCG, McKinsey, and PwC. Partners move through three tiers — Select, Advanced, Elite — based on certifications, live deployments, and co-sell activity, with specialty credentials in Codex, cybersecurity, and AI agents. A new Forward Deployed Experts program pairs certified partner staff directly with OpenAI engineers on client engagements. The network is a bet that selling AI to enterprises is a people business, not just a model comparison.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-partner-network/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318436/20260615/openai-launches-partner-network-150m-bet-that-implementation-beats-model-power.htm) · [Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/news-and-trends/openai-debuts-partner-network-backed-by-150m-investment/)*

## The Labs Line Up for IPOs — and Pay the Compute Bill

### Both Anthropic and OpenAI Filed Confidential S-1s Eight Days Apart

Anthropic filed a confidential draft S-1 with the SEC on June 1; OpenAI did the same on June 8. Neither is public yet, but both signal intent to tap public markets. Anthropic's latest private valuation reached $965 billion after its $65 billion Series H in late May — the first time it has passed OpenAI's private marks since ChatGPT launched. Two labs that together raised more than $150 billion in private capital in 2026 alone are now preparing to let public investors in.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/) · [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/)*

### Anthropic Pays xAI $1.25 Billion a Month to Rent 220,000 NVIDIA Chips

SpaceX's IPO filing last month revealed that Anthropic is paying $1.25 billion per month — $15 billion per year through May 2029 — to rent xAI's Colossus 1 data center in Memphis. The facility runs more than 220,000 NVIDIA processors drawing 300 megawatts of power. Either party can exit with 90 days' notice. That a safety-focused lab pays Elon Musk's AI company more than a billion dollars monthly for compute is the clearest signal yet of how scarce high-end GPU capacity has become.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-will-pay-xai-1-25-billion-per-month-for-compute/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-spacex-compute) · [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/spacex-ipo-filing-reveals-anthropic-set-to-pay-musks-firm-125bn-a-month-to-rent-xai-data-center-space/)*

## AI Safety and Science Get Serious Funding

### Google DeepMind Opens a $10M Research Fund for Multi-Agent AI Safety

Google DeepMind, Schmidt Sciences, and the Cooperative AI Foundation announced a $10M research grant call on June 11 focused on the safety risks of large-scale agent-to-agent interaction. The concern is concrete: millions of AI agents built by different organizations will soon communicate, negotiate, and transact across digital environments — and what they do when they interact is largely unstudied. Applications are open until August 8, with awards announced in autumn 2026. It's a rare case of safety research funding arriving before the deployment problem fully materializes.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/investing-in-multi-agent-ai-safety-research/) · [Digital Watch Observatory](https://dig.watch/updates/google-deepmind-partners-with-doe-for-ai-driven-science)*

### Google DeepMind Expands Genesis With AlphaEvolve, AlphaGenome, and WeatherNext

Google DeepMind is adding three new AI tools — AlphaEvolve for algorithm design, AlphaGenome for genomic research, and WeatherNext for weather forecasting — to the Genesis mission, its joint program with the U.S. Department of Energy spanning all 17 national laboratories. Early results include drug-repurposing candidates and antimicrobial resistance predictions already validated in lab experiments. Gemini's AI co-scientist is also available to researchers to synthesize large datasets and generate novel hypotheses. Genesis is the clearest example so far of frontier AI accelerating peer-reviewed science rather than just threatening to replace it.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/google-deepmind-supports-us-department-of-energy-on-genesis/) · [BNL Newsroom](https://www.bnl.gov/newsroom/news.php?a=222774) · [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/google-deepmind-supports-doe-genesis-a-national-mission-to-accelerate-innovation-and-scientific-discovery/)*
