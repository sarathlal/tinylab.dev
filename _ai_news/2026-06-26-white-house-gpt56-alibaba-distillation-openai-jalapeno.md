The past 48 hours pushed security to the center of every major AI story: a frontier model cracked US government systems, a foreign lab quietly stole from Claude at massive scale, and the White House stepped in to decide who gets the next GPT.

## AI, Security, and the Government's Tightening Grip

### Anthropic's Mythos Broke Into Nearly Every Classified US System It Tested

Under Project Glasswing — a joint testing program with US intelligence agencies — Anthropic's Mythos model identified vulnerabilities across almost all the classified government systems it was run against, often within hours. Sen. Mark Warner disclosed the results at a June 11 Senate banking hearing: "This tool broke into almost all of our classified systems, not in weeks but in hours." The findings add context to why Fable 5 and Mythos 5 remain offline under an export order Anthropic is still contesting.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/anthropics-mythos-model-found-vulnerabilities-in-classified-us-government-systems-official-says.html) · [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/06/anthropics-mythos-model-found-vulnerabilities-in-classified-us-government-systems-official-says/) · [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/06/24/anthropics-mythos-ai-found-flaws-in-classified-us-systems-within-hours-officials-say)*

### Alibaba Used 25,000 Fake Accounts to Extract 28.8 Million Claude Conversations

Anthropic wrote to US Senators and the White House accusing Alibaba and its Qwen AI lab of running "the largest known distillation attack" against Claude. The operation ran from April 22 through June 5, using roughly 25,000 fraudulent accounts to generate 28.8 million exchanges — targeting capabilities like agentic reasoning, software engineering, and long-horizon tasks. Anthropic is asking Congress to pass laws penalizing AI developers caught running such operations and urging stricter semiconductor export restrictions.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/anthropic-alibaba-distillation-campaign.html) · [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/ai-cloud/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-large-scale-distillation-attack/) · [Bleuken](https://www.bleuken.com/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-running-the-largest-known-ai-distillation-attack-28-8-million-claude-interactions-via-25000-fake-accounts/)*

### White House Asks OpenAI to Gate GPT-5.6 Access Customer by Customer

OpenAI's newest model won't get a public launch: the Trump administration's Office of the National Cyber Director asked the company to limit GPT-5.6 to a select group of approved partners while the government builds a security evaluation framework for frontier models. CEO Sam Altman told staff the government would be "approving access customer by customer." He said in a memo this isn't OpenAI's preferred approach long-term, and the company hopes to follow with a broader release "a couple of weeks later."

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/25/the-white-house-is-asking-openai-to-slow-roll-the-release-of-its-new-model-over-safety-concerns/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/25/trump-administration-openai-gpt-model-release) · [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/25/tech/openai-limit-release-white-house)*

## OpenAI's Hardware and Enterprise Push

### OpenAI's First Custom Chip Is Called Jalapeño and It Was Built in Nine Months

OpenAI and Broadcom on June 24 unveiled Jalapeño, an inference accelerator designed from scratch for LLM workloads — not adapted from general-purpose predecessors. The chip went from initial design to manufacturing tape-out in nine months, with OpenAI's own models helping speed parts of the design process. Performance per watt is expected to beat current state-of-the-art; deployment with Microsoft and other partners is planned for end of 2026 at gigawatt scale.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/openai-unveils-its-first-custom-chip-built-by-broadcom/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/openai-and-broadcom-reveal-jalapeno-first-ai-chip-in-partnership.html) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/openai-unveils-first-custom-ai-inference-chip-jalapeno-with-broadcom-and-its-development-was-sped-up-with-openais-own-models)*

### Samsung Reversed Its ChatGPT Ban and Signed One of OpenAI's Largest Enterprise Deals

Samsung Electronics is rolling out ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex to all staff in Korea and across its global Device eXperience division — reversing a company-wide ban it put in place in 2023. The deal covers software development, marketing, and manufacturing functions, and is OpenAI's largest enterprise rollout to date. Samsung SDS is also co-developing AI data centers with OpenAI and supplying HBM4 memory for OpenAI's custom Titan chip, with mass production targeted for late 2026.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/samsung-electronics-chatgpt-codex-deployment/) · [Samsung Newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-and-openai-announce-strategic-partnership-to-accelerate-advancements-in-global-ai-infrastructure)*

## The Consumer Market Is Shifting

### Claude's Paid Users Up 75% This Year as ChatGPT Falls Below 50% Market Share

Anthropic's Claude has grown its paying consumer base roughly 75% since January 2026, according to transaction data covering subscriptions and API tokens. "Claude" is now the most searched term on DataCamp — ahead of "AI" itself. ChatGPT's overall market share dropped below 50% for the first time ever, a threshold that shows how fast the field has fragmented since 2023.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/25/anthropics-claude-is-winning-over-paid-consumers-a-market-owned-by-chatgpt/)*

### Notion Mail Shuts Down Because More Than Half Its Users Stopped Opening Their Inbox

Notion is killing its email app after finding that over 50% of Notion Mail users manage email entirely through agents — never manually opening the inbox themselves. The company said it's "going all in on using agents to run your inbox" instead of building a traditional email client. It's a clean data point on where the consumer productivity market is heading.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/25/notion-mail-shuts-down-amid-agent-takeover/)*
