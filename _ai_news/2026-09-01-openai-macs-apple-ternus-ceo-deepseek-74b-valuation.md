Apple got a new CEO today, and it turns out the AI labs care about its old hardware more than anyone expected. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## Apple's AI Moment: New CEO, and Everyone Wants Its Macs

### OpenAI Is Buying So Many Mac Minis That Apple Can't Keep Up

OpenAI has purchased tens of thousands of Mac mini and Mac Studio units — no display, no keyboard, just dedicated compute — to run reinforcement learning for its computer-use agents. Anthropic is chasing the same trick but renting Mac mini capacity through AWS instead of buying outright. Training an agent to click around a screen and follow feedback loops millions of times is memory-heavy and light on parallelism, which makes Apple silicon a surprisingly good fit compared to the chips built for pretraining giant transformers.

*Sources: [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-openai-mac-mini-mac-studio-ai-agents/) · [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/31/openai-buys-apple-macs-ai-training/)*

### John Ternus Takes Over as Apple CEO, Inheriting a Broken Siri

John Ternus officially became Apple's CEO today, ending Tim Cook's 15-year run; Cook moves to executive chairman. Ternus, 51, joined Apple in 2001 and ran hardware engineering before the promotion, and his first big test lands September 9, when Apple is expected to show its first foldable iPhone and a rebuilt Siri running on Google's Gemini. Apple also dropped its long-standing "net cash neutral" target, freeing up room for bigger AI R&D spending and acquisitions.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/apple-s-new-ceo-john-ternus-takes-reins-from-tim-cook-focusing-on-ai) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/john-ternus-becomes-apple-ceo-111908957.html)*

## Nvidia and DeepSeek Chase the Next Round of AI Money

### Nvidia Puts $3.5 Billion Into MediaTek, With Alphabet Riding Along

Nvidia bought $3.5 billion of MediaTek convertible bonds on August 31, part of a $3.9 billion offering that Alphabet also bought into. The deal deepens work on three fronts: MediaTek is adopting Nvidia's NVLink Fusion so its custom chips can plug into Nvidia's rack-scale data centers, the two keep building RTX Spark and DGX Spark processors together, and they're extending an automotive tie-up around MediaTek's Dimensity Auto chips. Nvidia's real goal is to keep its interconnects and architecture central to data centers even as its biggest customers design their own silicon.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/31/nvidia-invests-3-5b-in-mediatek-as-part-of-expanded-ai-chip-partnership/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/nvidia-to-invest-3-5-billion-in-chipmaker-mediatek)*

### DeepSeek Nears a $7.4 Billion Round at a $74 Billion Valuation

DeepSeek is closing in on roughly 50 billion yuan ($7.4 billion) in fresh funding at a 500 billion yuan (~$74 billion) pre-money valuation, with existing backers Monolith, Shixiang Capital and battery giant CATL putting money in. The round had been paused in July after founder Liang Wenfeng's comments went viral, but it's now targeting a close around the end of August. DeepSeek says it's running at $500 million in annual revenue and is lining up a Shanghai STAR Market IPO for 2027.

*Sources: [China Money Network](https://www.chinamoneynetwork.com/2026/08/29/deepseek-nears-7-4-billion-funding-round-at-74-billion-valuation-ahead-of-2027-ipo) · [Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/deepseek-reportedly-seeks-7-4-billion-at-74-billion-valuation-for-compute-buildout)*

## Washington Fights Over Who Gets to Use AI Compute

### Commerce Moves to Close the Cloud Loophole China Used to Rent Nvidia Chips

The Commerce Department is drafting a rule to stop Chinese firms from renting Nvidia GPU power through data centers in third countries like Thailand and Singapore, with a draft expected to reach trade groups as early as September. The loophole let chips stay physically outside China while customers rented access remotely — no border crossing required. One known case: Shanghai's INF Tech reportedly got access to 2,300 banned Nvidia GPUs by renting 32 GB200 servers worth about $100 million from an Indonesian telecom.

*Sources: [Vision Times](https://www.visiontimes.com/2026/08/31/china-finds-new-routes-around-us-ai-chip-controls-as-washington-moves-to-close-cloud-loophole.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/viviantoh/2026/08/31/the-ai-chip-wars-new-front-control-the-cloud-not-the-silicon/)*

### AI Data Centers Become a Top Issue in the 2026 Midterms

More than 99% of political ad spending on data centers this cycle is running against them, and a pro-AI nonprofit called Build American AI just launched a multimillion-dollar counter-campaign in Kansas, Ohio and Wisconsin. In Ohio, former Sen. Sherrod Brown has spent millions branding GOP Sen. Jon Husted "the face of data centers," and Pennsylvania's Gov. Josh Shapiro signed an executive order tightening standards on new builds. Google, Microsoft, Meta, Oracle, xAI, OpenAI and Amazon have all signed onto Trump's pledge to make companies cover the power and grid costs instead of ratepayers.

*Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/31/politics/data-centers-backlash-midterms-invs-vis) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/-ai-group-jumps-data-center-fight-new-ads-aimed-reshaping-public-opini-rcna595198)*
