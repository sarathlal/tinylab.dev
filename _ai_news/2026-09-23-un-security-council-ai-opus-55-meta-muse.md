DeepSeek, OpenAI and Anthropic sit before the UN Security Council together for the first time today, while back on Earth, Anthropic and OpenAI both slashed model prices and Meta's new AI agent spooked half of Wall Street. Here's what mattered in the last day.

## World's AI Powers Meet the UN Over Loss-of-Control Risk

### DeepSeek, OpenAI and Anthropic Brief the UN Security Council for the First Time

The UN Security Council meets Wednesday with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, senior Anthropic staff, and China's DeepSeek and Moonshot AI all briefing the same 15-member room. France holds the Council's September presidency and called the session to examine loss-of-control risks and AI's role in actions that threaten international peace and security. DeepSeek founder Liang Wenfeng isn't expected to attend, but it's still the first time Chinese and US frontier labs have faced the Council together.

*Sources: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/deepseek-openai-and-anthropic-to-brief-un-security-council-on-ai-this-week-126092201553_1.html) · [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/deepseek-un-security-council-ai-risks/)*

### OpenAI Will Let Outside Groups Watch Its Models Train, Not Just Test Them After

OpenAI said it will give external evaluators access to its models during training and deployment, not just after release, a shift from how safety reviews have worked so far. The company named four priority areas: safety cases, safeguard evaluations, Preparedness Framework capability tests, and independent probes of misalignment incidents. OpenAI is reportedly in talks with METR and Redwood Research, groups that already audit its models, to take on the deeper access.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/priorities-principles-third-party-assessments/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-evaluators-training-phase)*

## Anthropic and OpenAI Both Cut Frontier Model Prices the Same Week

### Claude Opus 5.5 Matches Fable 5.1's Performance at 40% Lower Cost

Anthropic's new Opus 5.5 beats its predecessor on agentic coding, computer use and knowledge work, and costs 40% less to run — $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output, down from $5 and $25. Cache reads drop 60% to $0.20 per million tokens. One early tester finished a 680,000-line code migration in under a day, work Anthropic says would normally take an engineering team weeks.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-releases-claude-opus-5-5-beating-fable-5-1-on-key-agentic-benchmarks-at-60-cheaper-api-price) · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-claude-opus-5-5/)*

### OpenAI Halves API Prices With GPT-6 Sol and Luna

OpenAI shipped two new models a day earlier: GPT-6 Sol at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens, half of GPT-5.6 Sol's $4/$20, and GPT-6 Luna at $0.10/$0.50, down from $0.20/$1.20. An OpenAI spokesperson confirmed the prices are permanent, not a launch promotion. Both models slot in below flagship GPT-6 Astra, landing the same week Anthropic and Meta also shipped new AI products.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/openai-launches-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-releases-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-models-slashing-api-costs-50-or-more)*

## AI Agents Start Breaking Old Business Models

### Meta's Muse Agent Tops the App Store and Tanks Bank, Insurance and Travel Stocks

Muse, Meta's AI agent that can send emails, book travel, sell your car and haggle down a bill, became the No. 1 free app on Apple's US App Store this week, ahead of ChatGPT. Wall Street reacted fast: the S&P 500 Financials Index fell as much as 2.4% to its lowest since July, Charles Schwab dropped 6.7%, and insurers Allstate and Progressive slid on fears of AI agents automatically re-shopping policies. Travel sites Expedia and Booking Holdings each fell more than 3.5% on worries agents will skip booking portals entirely.

*Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/meta-ai-agent-triggers-heavy-selloff-in-banks-insurers-and-travel-stocks-4911491) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/metas-muse-becomes-app-stores-hottest-download)*

### Software Stocks Have Lost $2 Trillion in 2026 as AI Agents Erode SaaS Demand

Software licensing is taking a separate hit: the iShares Expanded Tech-Software ETF is down more than 21% this year, roughly 30% off its September 2025 peak, as slowing SaaS growth collides with AI agents cutting into seat-based pricing. The bet from investors is that AI agents doing what used to take a whole team means fewer software licenses, period. Customer service, marketing and accounting software are flagged as most exposed to agents doing the job directly.

*Sources: [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/saas-stock-crash-ai-agents-2-trillion-2026/) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/ai-disruption-looms-over-markets-with-us-jobs-data-on-tap-4530856)*

## Money and Open Source Keep Building Out AI Infrastructure

### Verda Raises $189M, Becomes Europe's Newest AI Cloud Unicorn

Helsinki-based Verda, formerly DataCrunch, raised a $189M Series B led by Emergence Capital, pushing its valuation past $1B. The company's annualized revenue run rate hit $165M in July, up from $60M in April, and it's planning more than 250 MW of AI compute capacity across Europe, the UK, the US and Asia by 2027. Total funding now tops $450M across equity and debt.

*Sources: [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/verda-189m-series-b-unicorn-ai-cloud-helsinki/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/european-neocloud-verda-raises-189m-to-build-the-ai-infrastructure-of-tomorrow/)*

### Go.AI Raises $85M to Put Auditable AI Inside Banks and Hospitals

Chicago's Go.AI closed an $85M Series A led by Updata Partners to expand on-premises AI hardware built for regulated industries like banking, healthcare and defense. The company says it now serves 200-plus customers handling 12.5 million queries a day, with annual recurring revenue up more than eightfold in a year while staying profitable. Go.AI rebranded from Go Abacus earlier this year after launching The Go1, its first on-prem AI appliance.

*Sources: [Finovate](https://finovate.com/go-ai-secures-85-million-in-series-a-funding/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/fintech-deals/2026/09/22/go-ai-85m-series-a-bank-ai-appliances)*

### Alphabet's Intrinsic Open-Sources the Core of Its Robotics Platform

Google's robotics unit Intrinsic released Intrinsic Core under the permissive Apache 2.0 license at ROSCon 2026 in Toronto, giving away the real-time control, motion planning and pose-estimation software it built for industrial robots. The stack is compatible with the widely used Robot Operating System and includes Nvidia FoundationPose-based pose estimation plus drivers for Universal Robots and FANUC arms. It's a bet that free infrastructure wins more developers than a walled garden does.

*Sources: [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/intrinsic-open-sources-key-parts-platform-easier-development/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/googles-robotics-unit-intrinsic-open-sources-its-foundational-infrastructure-for-intelligent-robots/)*
