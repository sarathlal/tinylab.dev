OpenAI shipped its most dangerous model yet and then spent a billion dollars trying to contain the fallout. Here's what else moved in AI in the last day.

## OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra and a $1B Plan to Defend Against It

### GPT-6 Astra Goes Live After a Chaotic Rollout

OpenAI officially launched GPT-6 Astra on September 3, its most capable model yet for coding, research, and multi-step computer use. The launch got messy first: CNBC, Reuters, and The Verge published leaked details before OpenAI's own blog post went live, and the company's page briefly vanished around 2:40 p.m. ET before reappearing an hour later. Access starts limited — a small group of organizations, then ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, plus the API and AWS.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/openai-rolls-out-gpt-6-astra-model-with-added-cyber-guardrails) · [Yahoo News](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/openai-announces-gpt-6-astra-202113897.html)*

### OpenAI Commits $1B to Protect Water Utilities and Small Banks From AI Attacks

OpenAI says it "cannot rule out" that Astra has crossed the "Critical" threshold on its Preparedness Framework — the first OpenAI model to do so, after acing its ExploitBench test and finding two zero-day flaws with no human help. In response, president Greg Brockman told 300 security leaders at OpenAI's headquarters that the company is putting $1 billion into subsidized cyber tools, training, and support for water systems, community banks, and local governments through a new Daybreak for America program.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/03/openai-critical-infrastructure-cyber-ai-models) · [CSO Online](https://www.csoonline.com/article/4218373/openai-targets-small-utilities-with-1-billion-cyber-defense-initiative.html)*

## Washington Picks a Side in AI's Legal Fights

### DOJ Tells a Judge That Training AI on News Articles Is Fair Use

The Justice Department filed a Statement of Interest on September 1 urging a federal judge to rule that training large language models on copyrighted text is fair use. It's the first time the federal government has staked out a position in the wave of AI copyright suits, and it argues a loss for OpenAI and Microsoft would "distort copyright law" and threaten U.S. national security. The New York Times, which brought the underlying suit, blasted the move as the administration doing Silicon Valley's bidding.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/02/doj-urges-judge-rule-openai-microsoft-ny-times-lawsuit/) · [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/09/new-york-times-justice-department-openai-1237066310/)*

### Texas Opens Its AI Complaint Portal, Giving the State's AI Law Teeth

The Texas Attorney General's complaint portal for the Texas Responsible AI Governance Act went live on its September 1 statutory deadline. TRAIGA bans AI systems built for behavioral manipulation, discrimination, and unlawful deepfakes, and the AG holds sole enforcement power with no private right to sue. As of late June, zero complaints had been logged — the portal opening is the first real test of whether Texas will actually use the law.

*Sources: [Texas AI Report](https://www.texasaireport.com/tracker) · [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/c6c60e0c/the-texas-responsible-ai-governance-act)*

## Enterprise AI Deals Keep Getting Bigger and Faster

### AfterQuery Becomes YC's Fastest-Ever Unicorn, Jumping to $3.2B in 5 Months

AfterQuery, which pays doctors, lawyers, and other specialists to help train AI models, just closed a round valuing it at $3.2 billion — up from $300 million at its Series A in April. Y Combinator partner Gustaf Alströmer called it the fastest jump from launch to unicorn in the accelerator's history. The founders are 22 and 23 years old, and were in YC's Winter 2025 batch just 18 months ago.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/afterquery-reportedly-becomes-y-combinators-fastest-ever-unicorn-now-valued-at-3-2b/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com.au/news/innovation/afterquery-becomes-ycs-fastest-unicorn-at-us3-2-billion/)*

### Palo Alto Networks Pays $500M for an AI Helpdesk Startup Its Own CEO Backed

Palo Alto Networks bought Console, a two-year-old startup whose AI agents handle password resets and app-access requests without a human in the loop, for $500 million in cash and stock. Console had raised just $29 million and was valued at $157 million before the sale — and CEO Nikesh Arora was an early angel investor in it himself. The company will fold into Cortex, Palo Alto's AI-driven threat detection platform.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/02/palo-alto-networks-paid-500m-for-thrive-backed-console-sources-say/) · [Network World](https://www.networkworld.com/article/4217170/palo-alto-networks-buys-console-to-boost-agentic-security.html)*

### Cisco Gives All 90,000 Employees Their Own AI Agent

Cisco's MyAgent is now live company-wide, backed by more than 800 specialized subagents that handle tasks across Outlook, Webex, Jira, and SharePoint. To keep costs down, 50-60% of requests route to open-weight models and 20-30% to plain software automation, with only a small slice going to a frontier model. Any action outside the chat window still needs a human's explicit sign-off.

*Sources: [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/cisco-deploys-custom-ai-agent-to-entire-90000-person-workforce) · [Cisco Blogs](https://blogs.cisco.com/news/my-agent-and-the-rise-of-ambient-intelligence-ciscos-next-step-in-enterprise-ai)*
