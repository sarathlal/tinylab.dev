SpaceX's record-breaking IPO debut and OpenAI's confidential S-1 filing anchored June 12-13, while a fresh wave of model launches, a massive enterprise AI rollout, and a state-level policy reversal rounded out a dense 36 hours.

## The AI IPO Wave

### SpaceX Raises $75B in the Biggest IPO Ever, Closes Up 19%

SpaceX opened at $135 on June 12, raised $75 billion, and closed the day at $161.11 — a 19.3% gain that breaks Saudi Aramco's 2019 record of $35.4 billion. MSCI cleared the stock for early index inclusion starting June 13; ETFs benchmarked to $15-20 trillion in AUM are expected to push roughly $35 billion into SPCX as they rebalance. SpaceX is the first of three major AI-adjacent companies — alongside Anthropic and OpenAI — expected to list this year.

*Sources: [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261960721-spacex-ipo-is-live-at-135-bull-base-and-bear-cases-for-the-first-90-days-tradingkey) · [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-june-12-2026) · [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/spacex-record-breaking-ipo-2023-1802289)*

### OpenAI Files a Confidential S-1 Targeting a $1 Trillion Valuation

OpenAI submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC on June 8-9 and immediately announced it publicly — "We expect it to leak so we're just announcing it." The company is targeting a roughly $1 trillion valuation and a Wall Street debut by Q4 2026, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley managing the deal. Anthropic had submitted its own S-1 roughly one week earlier, making this the first time two direct AI competitors have simultaneously been in the IPO pipeline.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/08/openai-confidentially-files-for-ipo-prepping-wall-street-for-ai-debut.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/09/openai-files-confidential-s-1-sec-ipo/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/)*

## New Models and Developer Tools

### Google Ships Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite and a New TTS Model

Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite as the fastest and cheapest model in the Gemini 3 series, built for high-volume, latency-sensitive tasks like translation and classification. Alongside it, Google shipped Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS — a text-to-speech model with support for 70+ languages, audio tags for granular vocal-style control, and SynthID watermarking on all output. Both target developers who need speed and cost efficiency over raw benchmark performance.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/flash-lite/) · [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)*

### xAI Opens the Grok Build Plugin Marketplace

xAI launched its Grok Build Plugin Marketplace in beta on June 11, shipping with six integrations at launch: MongoDB, Vercel, Sentry, Chrome DevTools, Cloudflare, and Superpowers. A plugin bundles skills, slash commands, agents, hooks, and MCP servers into a single installable package you can browse and install without leaving your terminal. The catalog is open — developers can submit their own plugins via pull request to xai-org/plugin-marketplace on GitHub.

*Sources: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/11/xai-ships-grok-build-plugin-marketplace-with-mongodb-vercel-sentry-chrome-devtools-cloudflare-and-superpowers-plugins-at-launch/) · [xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-plugin-marketplace)*

### Meta's Muse Spark Replaces Llama 4 on Smart Glasses

Meta is now running Muse Spark — the first model from its Superintelligence Labs — on most of its smart glasses, swapping out Llama 4. The model matches Llama 4 Maverick on benchmarks while using one-tenth the compute, making it fast enough for real-time on-device responses in the physical world. It's closed source for now; Meta says it "hopes to open-source future versions of the model" as the Muse series matures.

*Sources: [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/meta-muse-spark-ai-model-replaces-llama-on-smart-glasses/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/08/meta-debuts-first-major-ai-model-since-14-billion-deal-to-bring-in-alexandr-wang.html)*

## Enterprise AI at Scale

### KPMG Rolls Microsoft Agent 365 to All 276,000 Staff

KPMG announced on June 9 that it will deploy Microsoft Agent 365 — a governance and orchestration layer for AI agents — across its entire global workforce, alongside Microsoft 365 Copilot at the same scale. Agent 365 reached general availability in May 2026. The deal also covers a joint UNESCO program called AI EmpowerED, with a goal of credentialing 500,000 teachers and students by end of 2026.

*Sources: [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/06/09/kpmg-and-microsoft-scale-trusted-enterprise-ai-agents-globally-through-deployment-of-agent-365-and-copilot/) · [EnterpriseDNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/kpmg-microsoft-agent-365-enterprise-ai-agents-2026/) · [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/media/press-releases/2026/06/kpmg-and-microsoft-scale-trusted-enterprise-ai-agents-globally.html)*

### Boston Dynamics Upgrades Atlas with Gemini Robotics ER 1.6

Boston Dynamics integrated Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics ER 1.6 into its Orbit AIVI-Learning platform, letting Spot robots continuously learn facility environments and reason through complex industrial tasks. The partnership was announced at CES 2026 and targets automotive manufacturing as the first commercial application. Boston Dynamics says the upgrade allows Spot and Orbit to do higher-order visual reasoning that wasn't possible with earlier Llama-based models.

*Sources: [Boston Dynamics](https://bostondynamics.com/blog/aivi-learning-now-powered-google-gemini-robotics/) · [Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260106VL212/boston-dynamics-google-deepmind-gemini-partnership-humanoid-robot.html)*

## AI Policy in Flux

### Colorado Replaces Its AI Act Before It Ever Took Effect

Colorado's Governor signed SB 26-189 on May 14, repealing the original CO AI Act just weeks before its June 30 effective date. A federal court had already stayed the original law in April. The replacement narrows the scope from a broad "high-risk AI system" framework to automated decision-making in consequential decisions, with the new rules kicking in January 1, 2027. Companies that had been preparing for June 30 need to shift their compliance timelines by six months.

*Sources: [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=0430a8e5-f229-4eef-a2f1-157d8f96fba1) · [Law and the Workplace](https://www.lawandtheworkplace.com/2026/05/major-developments-put-colorados-ai-law-on-ice-ahead-of-implementation/) · [Seyfarth Shaw](https://www.seyfarth.com/news-insights/colorado-enacts-artificial-intelligence-replacement-law.html)*

### EU Publishes a Code of Practice on AI-Generated Content Labels

The European Commission published a Code of Practice on marking and labeling AI-generated content on June 10. It specifies how platforms and developers must label synthetic text, images, and audio — complementing AI Act obligations already in force since August 2025. The code isn't binding law, but regulators can weigh non-compliance when assessing whether a company meets its broader AI Act duties.

*Sources: [EU Digital Strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai) · [MindFoundry](https://www.mindfoundry.ai/blog/ai-regulations-around-the-world)*
