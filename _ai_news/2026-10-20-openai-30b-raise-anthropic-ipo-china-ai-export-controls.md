Anthropic's IPO is pulling the whole industry's money plans forward. Meanwhile, Beijing wants a say in who gets its models, and one hacker showed how cheap agent-driven break-ins have become.

## The Race to Fund AI Before Anthropic Lists

### OpenAI is rushing to raise $30B before Anthropic's IPO
OpenAI wants to close a round of about $30B at a valuation above $1.4T, Reuters reported Monday. Two sources said SoftBank and Abu Dhabi's MGX are in talks. The goal is to lock in a private price before Anthropic's expected mid-November listing gives investors a public number to compare against.

*Sources: [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusiveopenai-seeks-to-accelerate-fundraising-ahead-of-anthropic-ipo-sources-say-4958511) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-fundraising-ahead-anthropic-ipo/)*

### Anthropic's Q3 revenue hit $16.8B, up 45% from Q2
Anthropic told investors its third-quarter revenue reached $16.8B, the Financial Times reported mid-month. It was also profitable on an adjusted basis for a second straight quarter. Adjusted numbers leave out stock pay, so they're not directly comparable to GAAP.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-q3-revenue-climbs-to-168-billion-report-221619202.html) · [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/15/openais-revenue-estimate-just-got-cut-by-20-billio/)*

### Broadcom may lend Anthropic up to $42B to rent its chips
Anthropic's IPO filing shows Broadcom agreed to lend up to $42B as convertible notes. That covers about a third of a $125.2B, five-year commitment to lease Broadcom-linked TPU capacity. The filing flags "potential conflicts of interest," since Broadcom is both supplier and lender.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/01/broadcom-lending-anthropic-42-billion-chips-reuters.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/broadcom-to-lend-anthropic-up-to-42-billion-to-lease-chips-in-latest-circular-investing-deal-121617505.html)*

### Tenstorrent raises $1.5B at a $6.2B valuation
Jim Keller's chip startup closed a $1.5B Series E on October 19, more than doubling its valuation from December 2024. Trace Capital and Innovation X led. The money goes to Galaxy server production and international expansion.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/19/chip-legend-jim-kellers-tenstorrent-raises-1-5b-to-expand-its-open-compute-ecosystem/) · [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/ai-chip-startup-tenstorrent-more-than-doubles-its-valuation-to-62-billion-in-funding-round-4957179)*

## Borders and Break-Ins: Policy and Security

### China proposes export controls on 23 AI technologies
China's commerce ministry put 23 AI technologies on a draft export control list, covering model weights, agents and chip designs. Models of roughly 2 trillion parameters or more are the main target. Open-source tech is exempt, overseas users can still reach models through apps and APIs, and comments run until November 15.

*Sources: [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/china-says-it-will-curb-tech-exports-including-ai-citing-national-security-risks) · [TechNode](https://technode.global/2026/10/18/china-ai-export-controls-23-technologies/)*

### A hacker used AI agents to breach 100+ company sites for about $5,000
One Chinese-speaking attacker aimed commercial AI agents at more than 1,200 targets and got into sites tied to over 100 companies, including Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins. That works out to roughly $50 per successful break-in. Researcher Snorre Fagerland of NorForge found it through exposed instances of the open-source Cairn agent platform. Deere says it sees no sign sensitive data was accessed.

*Sources: [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/a-chinese-hacker-used-ai-agents-to-break-into-more-than-100-company-websites-deere-and-sharkninja-among-websites-hit-by-open-source-ai-powered-problem-solving-platform-demo) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ai-agents-hacker-cairn-100-sites-deere-norforge)*

## New Models: DeepSeek Updates, Meta Opens Its Weights

### DeepSeek ships V4.1-Flash-1017 after a nearly five-hour outage
DeepSeek's update scores 77% on DeepSWE v1.1, up from 74.2%, and 42.6 on Humanity's Last Exam without tools, up from 36.8. The scores are DeepSeek's own. Claude Opus 5 still leads on most of the 11 benchmarks DeepSeek published. The release followed an API outage on October 15 that ended in an apology and user credits.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/18/deepseek-releases-updated-v4-1-flash-model-it-says-completes-tasks-faster/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/deepseek-pushes-updated-ai-model-134714989.html)*

### Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter open-weight model
Muse Spark Open has 816B total and 42B active parameters, plus a 1M-token context window. Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model to date, built for coding and agent work. TechCrunch notes a version runs on Macs.

*Sources: [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/meta-launches-an-open-weight-model-that-runs-on-macs/)*
