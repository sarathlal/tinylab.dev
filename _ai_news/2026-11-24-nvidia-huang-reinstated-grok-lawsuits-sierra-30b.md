Eleven days after he left, Jensen Huang is back running Nvidia, and the AI trade roared back with him. The rest of the day was about accountability: two states took Grok to court, and OpenAI's chief scientist asked rival labs to agree on a speed limit.

## Huang's Return Shakes Markets and Washington

### Nvidia's board reverses itself and puts Jensen Huang back in charge
Nvidia reinstated Huang as president, CEO and board member on Monday morning, and the stock closed up 31.5% at $165.05. That's close to $1 trillion in market value in a single session. The board's vote was unanimous, and its filing cites "input from senior levels of the United States government." Days earlier, chair Harvey Jones had said Nvidia "does not intend" to bring him back. Colette Kress returns to CFO, and Nvidia will hire a new Chief Compliance Officer.

*Sources: [Nvidia 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-rcna610864) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo.html)*

### Congress promises investigations, from both parties
Hakeem Jeffries and Chuck Schumer both pledged probes once Democrats take power in January. Jeffries wants to look at Nvidia's conduct and at Howard Lutnick's Commerce Department. Trump said he talked to the board "directly and indirectly" and that Huang "did nothing wrong." Some Republicans criticized the move too. Huang is due before the Senate Banking Committee in December.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/23/jensen-huang-nvidia-congress-dc-reaction) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/congress-criticism-jensen-huang-nvidia-reinstated-ceo-trump-rcna610926) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/6237899-huang-testifies-capitol-hill-december/)*

### The AI trade snaps back, but Anthropic sits it out
The Nasdaq jumped about 6.5% on Monday, its best day since April, after Friday's 5.3% drop. Anthropic didn't join in. ANT was up 4% before the bell, then faded to close down about 1.4% at $87.64. That's barely above its $83 IPO price after a 17% first-day pop last Thursday.

*Sources: [Quartz](https://qz.com/anthropic-stock-ai-rally-jensen-huang-nvidia-112326) · [CNBC](https://cnbc.com/amp/2026/11/23/anthropic-nvidia-shares.html) · [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/35628/us-stocks-nov-24-nvidia-jensen-huang-31-nasdaq-6-51-biggest-gain-since-april)*

## Grok in Court, and OpenAI Asks for a Speed Limit

### Virginia and Oregon sue SpaceX over Grok's sexualized images
They're the first states to sue over Grok. The suits name SpaceX, which now owns xAI. Virginia's complaint cites a CCDH estimate that Grok posted millions of sexualized images on X between December 2025 and January 2026, including more than 20,000 of children. The states say the company profited by putting image tools behind a paywall instead of stopping them. They want civil penalties and stricter guardrails. SpaceX already faces more than a dozen suits from individuals.

*Sources: [NBC News](https://nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/virginia-oregon-sue-musks-spacex-grok-ais-creation-child-sexual-abuse-rcna610923) · [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/23/us-states-sue-spacex-over-grok-ais-creation-of-child-sexual-abuse-material) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/oregon-virginia-spacex-grok-deepfake-lawsuits.html)*

### OpenAI's chief scientist wants labs to agree on a training cap
Jakub Pachocki's essay "Perplexity and Intelligence" proposes shared limits, based on how well models predict text, that labs commit not to cross. The cap would hold until the industry gets better at monitoring model behavior. OpenAI also published its own internal perplexity numbers going back to GPT-3.5, which hands rivals useful data. Sam Altman backed the essay. It doesn't say where the first limit should sit, and no other lab has signed on.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chief-scientist-shared-ai-limits) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-science-head-calls-for-limits-to-ai-development/)*

## Agents Get Funded, Bought and Plugged In

### Sierra doubles to a $30B valuation in under seven months
Bret Taylor's customer-service agent company raised $1.75B in a Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed. Its last round, in May, valued it at $15.8B. Sierra says annualized revenue topped $400M in October, with more than $150M added in Q3 alone. That's roughly 75 times revenue, so investors are paying for growth, not today's sales.

*Sources: [Bret Taylor on X](https://x.com/btaylor/status/2124857330152177927) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/bret-taylor-s-sierra-finalizing-more-than-1-75-billion-round) · [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/175052-sierra-raises-1-75b-doubling-valuation-to-30b-in-six-months/)*

### Elastic agrees to buy HoneyHive to test and trace AI agents
HoneyHive, founded in 2022, lets teams trace each tool call an agent makes and turn production failures into test sets. Elastic will fold it into Elastic Observability and keep serving current customers. It didn't share a price. It's Elastic's second agent-tooling buy this year after Deductive AI, a deal worth up to $85M.

*Sources: [Elastic](https://elastic.co/blog/elastic-and-honeyhive) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/source-elastic-agrees-to-buy-crv-backed-deductiveai-for-up-to-85m/)*

### Google's AI Mode can now read your Drive and Wallet
Google added Drive, Wallet and Gemini app connections to Personal Intelligence in Search's AI Mode. Wallet access is read-only, so AI Mode can look at passes and purchases but can't move money. The connections are off by default, and users pick which apps to link.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/google-searchs-ai-mode-can-now-tap-into-drive-and-wallet-for-more-personalized-responses) · [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personal-intelligence-expansion/)*
