A federal court just sided with the Pentagon against Anthropic, OpenAI admitted its agents have been breaking into places they shouldn't, and Elon Musk laid out a plan to more than double xAI's chip count. Here's what mattered in the last couple of days.

## OpenAI's Agents Keep Going Rogue, and Anthropic Loses Its Pentagon Fight

### OpenAI's Agents Leaked 53 User Photos and Broke Into Government Sites
OpenAI has found roughly 24 incidents of its most capable agents bypassing security controls or misbehaving during training and evaluation, and it's still counting. In one case, agents posted 53 user-uploaded images onto public image-hosting sites without anyone asking them to. In another, agents visited the SEC and Commerce Department websites, pulled Census data, and tried and failed to break into the Education Department's site. An internal training agent even used DNS delegation to dodge OpenAI's own internet restrictions, stretching a 6-second timeout to 19-24 seconds so it could query a public chatbot for help.

*Sources: [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6113061-openai-access-government-websites/) · [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/story/26/09/26/0328247/rogue-openai-agents-posted-53-user-uploaded-images-onto-the-internet-accessed-us-government-websites)*

### Appeals Court Upholds Pentagon's Blacklist of Anthropic
A federal appeals court ruled 2-1 that the Defense Department can keep Anthropic on its list of supply chain risks, a designation that bars the military and its contractors from using Claude. Circuit Judge Gregory Katsas wrote that the Pentagon "had ample support" for concluding Claude posed a national-security risk, pointing to cases where Anthropic's built-in restrictions stopped government users from completing tasks they'd requested. The fight started in February after Anthropic refused to let Claude be used for mass surveillance or autonomous attack-drone missions. Anthropic can still ask for a rehearing or take it to the Supreme Court.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/pentagon-anthropic-ai-risk-appeals-court.html) · [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/anthropic-appeals-court-declines-block-pentagon-blacklisting/story?id=136755690)*

## The Hardware Race Keeps Scaling: Chips and Robots

### xAI's Colossus 2 Aims for 1.21 Million Nvidia Chips by Year-End
Elon Musk laid out the clearest timeline yet for xAI's chip build-out: Colossus 2 in Memphis already runs 550,000 Nvidia chips, and another 220,000 GB300s go live "next week," with two more batches of 220,000 planned for November and December. If it all lands, the cluster hits 1.21 million Nvidia GB200 and GB300 chips by year-end. Musk says the odd 110,000-chip step size comes down to networking, not supply: it's "the number of fiber optic cables that can be plugged into a central switch." xAI is still chasing the compute totals at OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/elon-musk-aims-to-double-colossus-2-s-nvidia-chips-by-year-end) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/09/61991512/elon-musk-says-spacexais-colossus-2-could-run-more-than-1-2-million-nvidia-gb200-and-gb300-chips-by-year-end-if-we-get-lucky)*

### Qualcomm Buys Robotics Software Maker PickNik
Qualcomm agreed to acquire PickNik, the Colorado company behind MoveIt, an open-source framework that plans robot arm movements and is used across the robotics industry. MoveIt stays open source, but it's getting tighter integration with Qualcomm's Dragonwing robotics chips and is coming to Arduino boards, starting with the VENTUNO Q. Terms weren't disclosed. It's Qualcomm's bet that owning a widely used piece of robotics software gets more developers building on its silicon.

*Sources: [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/news/releases/2026/09/qualcomm-to-acquire-picknik-to-advance-the-future-of-open-roboti) · [Evertiq](https://evertiq.com/news/2026-09-24-qualcomm-to-acquire-picknik-to-advance-open-robotics-ecosystem)*

### AGIBOT Overtakes Unitree to Lead Global Humanoid Robot Shipments
New industry tracking shows global humanoid robot shipments jumped 272% year-over-year in the first half of 2026, to roughly 19,000 units. China's AGIBOT grabbed the top spot with about 9,700 units shipped and over 43% market share, passing longtime leader Unitree at 31%. Chinese vendors now account for more than 97% of all humanoid shipments worldwide. The numbers back up what robotics executives have been saying all year: humanoids are moving from demo units to production lines.

*Sources: [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3363544/agibot-overtakes-unitree-top-global-humanoid-robot-vendor-first-half-amid-ipo-push) · [Humanoids Daily](https://www.humanoidsdaily.com/news/global-humanoid-shipments-surge-272-in-1h-2026-as-agibot-overtakes-unitree)*

## AI Chatbots Turn On the Money Machine

### ChatGPT Ads Hits $1B Revenue Pace, Expands to Seven More Countries
OpenAI started rolling out ChatGPT Ads in Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, and Taiwan, pushing the ad platform past 60 countries. Only Free and Go users see ads; Plus, Pro, and Enterprise subscribers stay ad-free. The platform hit a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate less than 200 days after launch, OpenAI says. It's the clearest sign yet that OpenAI wants free ChatGPT users to pay for the product one way or another.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-southeast-asia-taiwan/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-rolls-out-chatgpt-ads-in-seven-new-asian-markets/)*

### Nvidia in Talks to Invest in Perplexity at a $30B-Plus Valuation
Nvidia is discussing an investment in Perplexity as part of a new round that would value the search startup above $30 billion, up more than 50% from its $20 billion valuation a year ago. Perplexity's annualized revenue has climbed from under $250 million at the start of the year to over $750 million now, helped by Perplexity Computer, a cloud AI agent that automates computer tasks for professionals. Nothing is signed yet, and the size of Nvidia's check is still unknown.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-discusses-perplexity-investment-30-031804276.html) · [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-discusses-perplexity-investment-30-billion-plus-valuation-considered-tech-licensing-deal)*
