Apple's WWDC keynote today confirmed what the leaks promised: Siri is rebuilt on Google's Gemini, at a reported $1 billion per year. That's the day's headline — but the week also brought Anthropic's S-1 filing at a near-trillion-dollar valuation, Alphabet's $84.75 billion equity raise, and OpenAI turning ChatGPT into a proper ad platform.

## Apple and Google Double Down on Gemini

### Apple Officially Launches Siri 2.0 on a $1B-Per-Year Gemini Deal at WWDC

Apple's June 8 WWDC keynote made it official: Siri now runs on a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Google Gemini model, licensed for roughly $1 billion per year. The rebuilt assistant sees your screen, reads personal context from emails, photos, and files, and executes actions across apps through a new system-wide "Search or Ask" gesture tied to Dynamic Island. A new Extensions system lets users switch between Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude as the underlying AI — turning Siri into a multi-model router. iOS 27 also ships three on-device generative photo tools: Extend (generative fill), Enhance (smart auto-adjust), and Reframe (perspective shift). This was Tim Cook's final WWDC keynote; John Ternus takes over as CEO on September 1.

*Sources: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/wwdc-2026-what-to-expect/) · [RedShark News](https://www.redsharknews.com/apple-wwdc-2026-siri-gemini-ios-27) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317902/20260606/wwdc-2026-opens-monday-gemini-powers-rebuilt-siri-iphone-11-faces-ios-27-cut.htm)*

### Gemini 3.5 Flash Becomes the Mandatory Enterprise Baseline Starting June 8

Google locked Gemini 3.5 Flash in as the non-optional default for its Enterprise app today. The model scores 76.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and beats Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding, agentic tasks, and multimodal reasoning. DeepMind's chief technologist described it as "an incredible combination of quality and low latency." It's already the default for billions of users in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Google Search globally.

*Sources: [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/) · [DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/flash/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/with-gemini-3-5-flash-google-bets-its-next-ai-wave-on-agents-not-chatbots/)*

## OpenAI Goes After Revenue

### ChatGPT Ads Go Self-Serve with CPC Bidding and Conversion Tracking

OpenAI opened ChatGPT advertising to any U.S. business this week, dropping the $50,000 minimum spend entirely. Advertisers can now buy on a cost-per-click basis (not just CPM), set daily budgets, and target by state, DMA, or ZIP code. Conversion-optimized campaigns started rolling out June 5. OpenAI's stated goal is $2.5 billion in ad revenue this year and $100 billion by 2030.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/new-ways-to-buy-chatgpt-ads/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/05/openai-self-serve-ad-platform) · [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/openai-launches-self-serve-ads-manager-for-chatgpt/573971/)*

### ChatGPT "Dreaming V3" Doubles Memory Capacity for Plus and Pro Users

OpenAI shipped a memory overhaul called "Dreaming V3" to Plus and Pro subscribers in the U.S. It doubles memory capacity, makes context update automatically with no manual saves, and adds time-aware relationship tracking so responses stay fresher over time. A new memory summary page lets users review exactly what ChatGPT knows about them. Free and Go plans get the upgrade next.

*Sources: [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/04/openai-says-chatgpts-memory-feature-is-getting-smarter-and-coming-to-free-users/) · [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-is-rolling-out-a-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-memory/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317840/20260605/chatgpt-memory-dreaming-update-openai-rewrites-personalization-engine-limits-audit-trail.htm)*

## AI Capital Markets Accelerate

### Anthropic Files a Confidential S-1 at a $965B Valuation with $47B ARR

Anthropic submitted a draft Form S-1 to the SEC on June 1, signaling a potential IPO. The filing came weeks after the company closed a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion post-money valuation — ahead of OpenAI's $852 billion. ARR reached roughly $47 billion in May, up from about $10 billion a year prior. Timing, share price, and share count are all undisclosed.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-ipo-s1-prospectus.html)*

### Alphabet Raises $84.75B — Berkshire Hathaway Puts in $10B at $351 Per Share

Google's parent upsized its equity raise to $84.75 billion on June 2, from an initial $80 billion announced June 1. The package includes $30 billion in underwritten public offerings, a $40 billion at-the-market program starting Q3 2026, and a $10 billion private placement to Berkshire Hathaway at $351.81 per Class A share. All proceeds fund AI infrastructure and compute.

*Sources: [Alphabet IR](https://abc.xyz/investor/news/news-details/2026/Alphabet-Announces-Proposed-80-Billion-Equity-Capital-Raise-to-Expand-AI-Infrastructure-and-Compute-2026-b0myAMewCa/default.aspx) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/alphabet-to-raise-80-billion-in-equity-capital-for-ai-spending) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-to-raise-80-billion-from-stock-sales-to-fund-ai-buildout.html)*

## Labs Price Up for the Agent Era

### xAI Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Tops the AI Video Arena Leaderboard at 720p

xAI released grok-imagine-video-1.5-preview on June 3 via its API, and it debuted at number one on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena image-to-video leaderboard. The model turns a still image and a text prompt into a cinematic clip at up to 720p, preserving source lighting and look. Pricing starts at $0.08 per second at 480p and $0.14 per second at 720p. Consumer rollout to X Premium is still in progress.

*Sources: [xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-1-5) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/xai-updates-grok-imagine-to-1-5-with-image-to-video-generation-at-720p-resolution/)*

### Anthropic Splits Claude Subscriptions: Agent Usage Gets Its Own Credit Pool June 15

Starting June 15, programmatic use of Claude through consumer plans (Pro, Team) moves to a separate monthly credit pool, walling off agent and developer usage from the standard user quota. Anthropic also launched a Partner Hub and Services Track for enterprise customers needing qualified implementation partners — infrastructure that looks designed to support an IPO roadshow narrative about enterprise penetration.

*Sources: [DevToolPicks](https://devtoolpicks.com/blog/anthropic-splits-claude-subscriptions-agent-sdk-credit-june-2026) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news)*
