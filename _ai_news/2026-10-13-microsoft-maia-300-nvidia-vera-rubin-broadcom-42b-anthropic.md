It was a quiet 36 hours, so this brief is shorter than usual. It covers six stories, a few from the past week that are still playing out.

## Chip wars and who pays for compute

### Microsoft unveils Maia 300 and calls it its "biggest bet yet" on custom silicon
Nvidia shares fell about 1.6% on Monday after Microsoft showed Maia 300, its third-generation AI chip. Microsoft says it delivers 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU systems. Large deployments start in 2027, so Nvidia has time, but the direction is clear.

*Sources: [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/10/12/why-nvidia-stock-is-down-around-1-6-on-monday) · [GeekWire](https://geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-makes-biggest-bet-yet-on-its-own-silicon-unveils-new-ai-chip-to-reduce-reliance-on-nvidia)*

### Nvidia opens its Vera Rubin design at the OCP summit
Nvidia put out Open Data Center Specifications v0.7 on Oct. 12. It covers power supply, cooling and facility layout. It also showed a pluggable optics form factor built with Cisco. Opening the spec helps Nvidia stay the default even as Microsoft builds its own chips.

*Sources: [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/latest)*

### Broadcom will lend Anthropic up to $42B for chips
Anthropic's IPO filing shows Broadcom could cover about a third of a $125.2B five-year compute commitment. The debt can convert to Anthropic equity. Bloomberg reported on Oct. 2 that Broadcom's banks are raising around $60B in outside debt to fund chips for Anthropic and others.

*Sources: [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/private-markets/26/10/62118633/anthropics-ipo-filing-reveals-a-42-billion-broadcom-lending-deal) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/broadcom-ceo-flags-capital-ai-074152257.html)*

## Agents and the money behind them

### Google's new Gemini agent runs on Claude too
Google Cloud unveiled the agent on Oct. 8 at its Gemini at Work event. It splits jobs across temporary sub-agents, each with its own identity, and can run for hours or days. It picks between Gemini and Anthropic's Claude models. It's in private preview with no price or release date.

*Sources: [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/10/google-launches-universal-gemini-ai-agent-enterprise-work.html) · [Unite.AI](https://unite.ai/google-cloud-unveils-gemini-its-universal-agent-for-work)*

### Infinigence AI raises RMB 2.3B in Shanghai
The Series C is worth about $344M. Chinese reporting calls it a record for an AI-native infrastructure company in the country. It shows Chinese investors still want to pay for the picks and shovels, not just model labs.

*Sources: [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/12/startup-funding-news-today-october-12-2026-infinigence-ai-mosi-intelligence-fulian-integrated-more)*

### Nous Research hits a $1.5B valuation and ships business agents
Nous raised a $90M Series B led by Robot Ventures. It launched AI agents for business users the same week. The company is best known for open-weight models, so this is a push into paid products.

*Sources: [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/12/startup-funding-news-today-october-12-2026-infinigence-ai-mosi-intelligence-fulian-integrated-more) · [AF.net](https://af.net/realtime/ai-startups-raise-1-81-billion-across-46-funding-rounds-on-october-8-2026/)*
