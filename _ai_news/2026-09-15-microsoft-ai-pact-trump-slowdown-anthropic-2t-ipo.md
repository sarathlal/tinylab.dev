Microsoft just joined every major AI lab's pledge to slow down, and the White House said no thanks. Here's what else moved in AI over the last day.

## Microsoft Joins the AI Slowdown Pact, and Wall Street Flinches

### Nadella Publishes Microsoft's AI "Code of Conduct" and Backs the Pacing Push

Satya Nadella said Microsoft will open a 37-page rulebook for its own MAI models to six weeks of public comment. The draft bars Microsoft's AI from helping build weapons, resisting shutdown, setting its own goals, or hiding its reasoning from human auditors. Nadella's move joins Dario Amodei, Sam Altman, and Google DeepMind's working group, which has met since July on an industry-run alternative to waiting for Congress.

*Sources: [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-floats-rules-for-its-own-ai-models-as-industry-debates-a-slowdown/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/14/microsofts-new-ai-code-of-conduct-tells-models-not-to-hack-systems-or-trick-humans/)*

### White House Adviser Tells Labs: Pace All You Want, Just Don't Call It a Cartel

David Sacks, the PCAST chair and former White House AI czar, told Anthropic and OpenAI to go ahead and slow down if they want to. But he drew a line: "stop pretending antitrust law has to be suspended so you can form a cartel." The comment signals Washington will watch the labs' new standards body for coordinated market behavior, not just safety intent.

*Sources: [CDM Press](https://cdm.press/news/business/2026/09/13/amodei-altman-and-musk-all-want-to-slow-down-ai-david-sacks-says-they-are-building-a-cartel/) · [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/david-sacks-blasts-openai-and-anthropic-over-slowdown/)*

### Chip Stocks Tumble as Investors Price In a Slower AI Race

Nvidia fell more than 3% Monday, while Intel, AMD, and Marvell dropped 5% to 6% after AI leaders backed slowing development. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index sank almost 6% on the day. Meta, Microsoft, and Alphabet rose instead, as investors start separating companies that need endless AI capex from those that could profit by spending less.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/14/ai-slowdown-stocks-nvidia-meta/) · [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/09/14/chip-stocks-tumble-as-ai-pacing-call-reaches-beyond-memory-intel-drops-7-amd-sinks-6-nvidia-pulls-back/)*

## Governments Split on Whether to Force the Issue

### Trump Rejects the AI Slowdown: "Whoever Wins AI Wins"

President Trump dismissed calls from Amodei, Altman, and Musk to slow AI development, telling reporters at the Irish Open, "We're leading China in AI, we're the most sophisticated country in the world, and frankly I want to keep it that way." He argued regulation would hand the race to Beijing, which itself rejected calls to slow down. Democrats are turning his resistance into a midterm attack line, pointing to polls showing 52% of Americans worry about AI in daily life.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/13/trump-rejects-calls-so-slow-ai-development-citing-chinese-competition/) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/14/tech/china-trump-ai-explainer-intl-hnk)*

### UK Panel Demands a Real AI Law, Not Industry Pledges

The UK's Joint Committee on Human Rights said in a September 14 report that existing law can't handle the "scale and seriousness" of AI's risks, citing sexualized deepfakes of women and girls and unconsented facial scanning. It called for one statutory regulator and a risk-tiered law with tougher duties on high-risk systems. Committee chair Alex Sobel said no country, the UK included, has an AI approach that's "fit for purpose."

*Sources: [MyJoyOnline](https://www.myjoyonline.com/uk-lawmakers-demand-new-law-to-address-ai-threat-to-human-rights/) · [The News](https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1416153-uk-parliament-urges-comprehensive-ai-bill-to-tackle-human-rights-threats-and-regulatory-gaps)*

## Anthropic's Big Financial Week

### Anthropic Targets a $2 Trillion Nasdaq Listing in October

Anthropic told investors it expects a second straight profitable quarter, after Q2 revenue hit $11.5 billion, more than 14 times what it made a year earlier. The company is now pointing at an October Nasdaq debut at a $2 trillion valuation, up sharply from the $965 billion private round it closed in May. Annualized revenue reached $65 billion by the end of July, up from $9 billion at the end of last year.

*Sources: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/anthropic-expects-profit-this-quarter-ahead-of-potential-2-trillion-ipo-126091400290_1.html) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/09/61756404/anthropic-nasdaq-2-trillion-ipo-second-consecutive-profitable-quarter)*

### Claude Code's "25% Increase" Works Out to a 17% Cut for Regular Users

Anthropic raised standard weekly Claude Code limits by a permanent 25% starting September 14, but the math only holds against the original baseline. A temporary 50% boost that had run since May expired the day before, on September 13, so anyone on that boosted tier saw real capacity drop by about 17%. Users called out the gap between the announcement's framing and what actually landed in their accounts.

*Sources: [MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/claude-code-weekly-rate-limit-changes) · [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/13403/claude-code-weekly-limits-25-percent-17-cut)*

## Fresh Money for AI Infrastructure and Healthcare

### Fortaegis Raises $50M to Sell Silicon-Rooted Security to AI and Defense Buyers

The Amsterdam startup closed an oversubscribed $50 million Series A led by Singapore's Serendipity Capital, with Tokyo Electron's venture arm also investing. Fortaegis uses the physical properties of chips to generate encryption keys that are never stored on the device, and its connections run 200 times faster than conventional approaches in its own testing. It's already working with more than 25 companies and governments across defense, AI infrastructure, and telecom.

*Sources: [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/09/14/fortaegis-raises-50-million-to-commercialize-chip-based-security-technology/) · [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/infrastructure/144251/dutch-company-fortaegis-raises-50-million-for-chip-security/)*

### Tandem Health Raises $100M to Build an AI Operating System for European Clinics

The Stockholm company's Series B was led by EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund, bringing its total funding to $160 million. Tandem's AI scribe already runs in 10,000 care organizations across 14 countries, and a July study found it cut clinicians' admin time by 29%. The round is Scaleup Europe Fund's first healthtech bet, sitting next to Mistral in its portfolio of European tech champions.

*Sources: [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/tandem-health-raises-100m-series-b-to-build-ai-clinic-operating-system/) · [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/tandem-health-raises-100m-series-b-eqt-scaleup-europe-fund/)*
