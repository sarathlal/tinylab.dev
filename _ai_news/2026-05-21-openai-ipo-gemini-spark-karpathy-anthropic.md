OpenAI is on its way to the stock market. Google turned Gemini into an always-on background agent, and Anthropic landed one of AI research's most recognized names — all within the same 48-hour window.

## OpenAI Clears Its Last Legal Hurdle and Eyes the Stock Market

### Jury Throws Out Musk's Claims Against OpenAI in Under Two Hours

A federal jury on May 18 unanimously rejected Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI and its co-founders in less than two hours of deliberation. The jury found his claims were barred by California's statute of limitations, dismissing allegations that OpenAI abandoned its nonprofit mission. The ruling removes what lawyers described as a critical risk to OpenAI's planned public offering.

*Sources: [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/05/20/business/foreign-business/openai-defeats-elon-musks-lawsuit-removes-obstacle-to-ipo/2347435) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-20/openai-faces-ipo-unknowns-even-after-victory-over-elon-musk)*

### OpenAI Prepares Confidential S-1 with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley

Days after the verdict, OpenAI is moving to file a confidential S-1 as early as May 22. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are lead underwriters. The company's March 2026 funding round put its post-money valuation at $852 billion, and a Q4 2026 public listing could push the figure past $1 trillion, which would make it one of the largest IPOs ever.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/openai-ipo-filing.html) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-files-confidential-ipo-with-goldman-morgan-stanley)*

## Google I/O 2026: Gemini Goes Agentic

### Gemini 3.5 Flash Is 4× Faster, Globally Available Today at $1.50 per Million Tokens

Google launched Gemini 3.5 Flash at I/O on May 19 with a claim of 4× speed over comparable models. It has a 1 million-token context window and is priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $9 per million output tokens. It's available now in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Google Search worldwide.

*Sources: [HotHardware](https://hothardware.com/news/google-io-2026-gemini-35-flash-ai-search-and-more) · [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/google-i-o-2026-all-about-gemini-3-5-spark-omni-models-and-revamped-app-126052000494_1.html)*

### Gemini Spark Runs on Cloud VMs So It Can Finish Tasks After You Close Your Laptop

Gemini Spark is built on dedicated Google Cloud virtual machines — not your device — which means it keeps running long-horizon tasks after you lock your screen. It integrates natively with Gmail and Workspace and will later operate directly inside Chrome. The beta rolls out to US Google AI Ultra subscribers next week, with Google cutting that plan from $250 to $200 per month.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-gemini-spark-agentic-assistant-gmail-io-2026) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/google-io-2026-was-about-turning-every-google-app-into-an-agent/)*

### Gemini App Doubles to 900 Million Monthly Users in One Year

Google reported 900 million monthly active Gemini users at I/O, up from 400 million at last year's conference. Daily requests grew 7× over the same period. The numbers signal that Gemini is no longer chasing ChatGPT — it's competing on raw user scale.

*Sources: [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/sundar-pichai-io-2026/) · [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/google-io-2026-new-gemini-app-flash-model-and-agentic-ai-push-heres-everything-google-announced-532395-2026-05-20)*

## Anthropic's Talent and Enterprise Offensive

### Andrej Karpathy Joins Anthropic to Use Claude to Train Claude

Andrej Karpathy — OpenAI co-founder, former Tesla AI director, and founder of the AI education startup Eureka Labs — announced May 19 he's joining Anthropic's pre-training team. His mandate is to build a new unit focused on using Claude to accelerate the research behind Claude's own training runs. He reports to pre-training head Nick Joseph.

*Sources: [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/prominent-ai-researcher-andrej-karpathy-picks-anthropic-over-former-home-openai-to-get-back-into-frontier-llm-research/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/openai-co-founder-andrej-karpathy-joins-anthropics-pre-training-team/)*

### KPMG Deploys Claude Across All 276,000 Employees Globally

KPMG announced a global alliance with Anthropic on May 19 to bring Claude into Digital Gateway, the platform KPMG's staff and clients use for day-to-day work. The rollout starts with Tax & Legal and expands across advisory services, with full Azure integration targeted for September 2026. Anthropic also named KPMG a preferred partner for private equity clients as part of the deal.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-kpmg) · [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/media/news/kpmg-anthropic-global-alliance.html)*
