Money and security dominate today. Anthropic's IPO is close, and attackers are running AI tools at scale. Public opinion keeps sliding the other way.

## The IPO and mega-round race

### Anthropic could file its IPO prospectus as early as Friday
Anthropic's draft prospectus shows Q3 revenue of $16.8B, up from $11.6B in Q2 and $4.7B in Q1. It also shows an $80.7B net loss in the first half of 2026. Yahoo Finance, citing three people familiar, says a public S-1 could land October 23. Bloomberg reports a listing as soon as mid-November, at a valuation somewhere between $1.5T and $2T. I couldn't confirm the filing happened.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropic-to-publicly-file-for-ipo-as-early-as-friday-sources-say-135343841.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/19/anthropic-ipo-ai-trade.html)*

### OpenAI is still chasing $30B at a $1.4T valuation
Bloomberg reports OpenAI wants at least $30B at a $1.4T pre-money valuation. UAE funds including MGX may put in up to $10B, and BlackRock is in talks. OpenAI reportedly wants the round done before Anthropic's IPO. Sam Altman has ruled out an OpenAI listing this year.

*Sources: [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/30/openai-reportedly-seeks-30-billion-at-1-4-trillion-valuation/) · [Arise](https://www.arise.tv/openai-eyes-30bn-funding-round-as-investors-push-valuation-toward-1-4tn/)*

### DeepSeek's first outside round could reach about $15B
Bloomberg said on October 6 that DeepSeek was close to at least 80B yuan ($11.9B). Signed term sheets could push it toward 100B yuan. Tencent and CATL are among the biggest backers. It's the largest private AI raise in China's history if it closes at that size.

*Sources: [SCMP](https://scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3355818/deepseek-nears-us7b-haul-first-ever-funding-round-backing-tencent-catl) · [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/deepseek-to-raise-at-least-12-billion-in-tencent-backed-funding)*

## AI as an attack tool, and governments reacting

### Microsoft ties a ConnectWise hack to a China-linked group using an open-source AI tool
Microsoft says a group it tracks as Storm-0940 used ARTEX, an open-source agentic pentesting framework, against hundreds of organizations. The group compromised IT services firm ConnectWise along the way. Cyberscoop reports it reached more than 2,250 tenants at 2,900 sign-in attempts per minute. Most attempts failed, but the group got privileged cloud access at many targets.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/artex-ai-pentesting-tool-used-in-data.html) · [Reuters via KFGO](https://kfgo.com/2026/10/21/chinese-linked-hackers-compromise-connectwise-target-hundreds-in-global-attack-microsoft-says/) · [Cyberscoop](https://cyberscoop.com/?p=91224)*

### CrowdStrike found an attacker's Claude Code history on an open server
A separate ARTEX campaign hit South Korean financial firms, including Shinhan Bank, from late September to early October. CrowdStrike found it through exposed directories on a Hong Kong IP address. They held the operator's Claude Code session histories and ARTEX config files. The operator looks financially motivated and isn't tied to a known group.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/artex-ai-pentesting-tool-used-in-data.html)*

### The US told civilian agencies to rip out DeepSeek
DHS signed the order on July 24, and the Federal Register published it October 20. It bars civilian agencies from buying IT products or services from DeepSeek or its affiliates. That includes third-party tools built on DeepSeek technology, though agencies can request exceptions. China's Foreign Ministry objected. Private and consumer use isn't covered.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/us-orders-federal-agencies-to-remove-chinas-deepseek-ai-from-their-systems/) · [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/us/article/3371431/us-orders-deepseek-removed-federal-systems-citing-national-security-risks)*

## Public and market mood turns cautious

### 57% of Americans say AI is bad for society
An ABC News/Washington Post/Ipsos poll, fielded October 9-13, found 57% call AI bad for society and 42% call it good. Under-30s are the most negative at 65%. About two-thirds oppose new US data centers, rising to 72% when it's in their own community.

*Sources: [ABC30](https://abc30.com/story/most-americans-say-ai-is-bad-society-oppose-more-data-centers-abcpostipsos-poll/19964027/) · [Good Morning America](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/americans-ai-bad-society-oppose-data-centers-abcpostipsos-poll-137414085)*

### AI stocks calmed on Friday after a rough Thursday
US News, running Reuters, says trading in big AI shares was quieter on October 23 after the previous day's slump. The Nasdaq already had a rough October, with inflation data pushing up rate-hike expectations. The Fed meets Wednesday, October 28, and Anthropic's IPO will test whether investors still want AI risk.

*Sources: [US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-10-23/after-previous-days-slump-trading-is-quieter-among-ai-shares) · [Kitco/Reuters](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-10-15/nasdaq-slides-near-one-month-low-ai-rout-deepens)*
