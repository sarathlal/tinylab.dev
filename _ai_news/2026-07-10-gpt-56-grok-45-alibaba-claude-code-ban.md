Three labs shipped new frontier models in the space of two days, Meta stepped on a privacy landmine, and the US-China AI fight opened new fronts on chips, code, and courtrooms. Here's what actually mattered.

## The Frontier Model Race Sprints Ahead

### GPT-5.6 Goes Fully Public After a Government Review

OpenAI released GPT-5.6 to every ChatGPT user, ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API on July 9, after the Commerce Department's AI standards office ran extra tests and cleared a wider rollout. The family splits into three tiers: Sol for frontier reasoning at $5/$30 per million tokens, Terra for GPT-5.5-level work at half the cost, and Luna at $1/$6 for high-volume jobs. Sol also adds "ultra mode," which farms out complex tasks to subagents instead of running as one model.

*Sources: [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/08/openai-gpt-5-6-launch-july-9/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2210308/openai-rolls-out-gpt5-6-july-9/)*

### Grok 4.5 Launches as xAI's First Model Built for Coding

xAI shipped Grok 4.5 on July 8, its first model trained specifically for coding and agentic work, developed alongside Cursor. Elon Musk called it "an Opus-class model, but faster, more token-efficient and lower cost," pricing it at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. It's live now in Grok Build, Cursor, and the xAI console.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-grok-new-model) · [x.ai](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5)*

### Google Delays Gemini 3.5 Pro to July 17 for a Ground-Up Rebuild

Google scrapped its Gemini 2.5 Pro-based architecture and pushed Gemini 3.5 Pro's launch to July 17, according to reports citing internal testing. Enterprise testers reportedly flagged that early builds burned far more tokens on long agentic tasks than benchmarks suggested, pushing real-world costs above what the scores implied. The rebuild targets a 2-million-token context window and a "Deep Think" reasoning layer to compete with GPT-5.6 and Fable 5 — though Google hasn't confirmed the date with an official model card yet.

*Sources: [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319877/20260708/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-deepseeks-july-24-deadline-hits-developers-now.htm)*

## Meta's Muse Push Meets a Privacy Backlash

### Meta Opens Muse Spark 1.1 to Developers

Meta gave developers public preview access to Muse Spark 1.1 on July 9, pitching it as its most capable model yet for real coding and multi-step agentic tasks. It's part of Meta's "personal superintelligence" push and is available now on the Meta Model API, with $20 in free credits before pay-as-you-go pricing kicks in.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/meta-debuts-muse-spark-1-140113992.html)*

### Muse Image Lets Strangers Remix Your Instagram Photos — and Users Are Furious

Meta's new Muse Image tool lets anyone tag a public Instagram profile with @ and generate AI images built from that person's photos, and accounts were opted in by default. Talent agency CAA and SAG-AFTRA both pushed Meta to make protection the default instead of the exception, warning that people have no way to know when their public photos get reused this way.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/how-to-stop-metas-ai-image-generator-from-using-your-instagram-photos/) · [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/07/09/muse-spark-meta-image-instagram-opt-out/)*

## US-China AI Friction Escalates on Chips, Code, and Courts

### Alibaba Bans Claude Code for Employees Starting Today

Alibaba's internal ban on Anthropic's Claude models — Sonnet, Opus, Fable, and the Claude Code agent tool — takes effect today, July 10, with staff told to switch to Alibaba's own Qoder platform. Alibaba cited security concerns after developers found Claude Code inspecting user timezone and proxy data. The ban follows a June 10 letter Anthropic sent the Senate Banking Committee accusing Alibaba of running "the largest known distillation attack" against it: 25,000 fake accounts and 28.8 million exchanges between April and June.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/06/alibaba-anthropic-ai-ban-claude-china.html) · [Pandaily](https://pandaily.com/alibaba-bans-anthropic-claude-products-jul2026) · [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-claims-that-chinas-alibaba-illicitly-distilled-its-models-from-april-to-june-2026-says-effort-involved-25-000-fake-accounts-and-28-8-million-exchanges-on-claude)*

### China Will Let Alibaba, ByteDance, and DeepSeek Buy Limited Nvidia H200 Chips

Beijing plans to let its top AI firms buy a capped number of Nvidia H200 chips, easing a compute crunch domestic chipmakers can't fill, The Information reported July 8. The total is expected to land below 200,000 chips — less than half of what companies requested — with each firm capped around 75,000 units, and the chips restricted to training rather than inference. China wants inference work to run on domestic hardware, mainly Huawei's chips, instead.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/china-to-let-ai-firms-buy-nvidia-h200-chips-information-says) · [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/07/09/news-china-reportedly-to-allow-nvidia-h200-imports-but-approvals-may-fall-below-200k-chips-less-than-half-requested/)*

### News Outlets Ask a Judge to Sanction OpenAI Over Hidden Evidence

The New York Times, the New York Daily News, and other papers filed a sanctions motion against OpenAI in Manhattan federal court on July 9, in their copyright case over ChatGPT's training data. The outlets say OpenAI told the court it couldn't search its models for their copyrighted articles — while it had already run that search before the lawsuit was even filed — and that OpenAI deleted or hid billions of ChatGPT conversations relevant to the case.

*Sources: [US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-07-09/new-york-times-led-group-asks-court-to-sanction-openai-in-us-copyright-dispute) · [Las Vegas Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2026/jul/09/news-outlets-urge-a-judge-to-sanction-openai-in-a-/)*
