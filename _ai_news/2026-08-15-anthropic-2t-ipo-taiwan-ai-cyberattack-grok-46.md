Anthropic's bankers are chasing history's biggest IPO, hackers just proved AI agents can run a cyberattack on their own, and two frontier labs moved their prices in opposite directions. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## Anthropic Eyes History's Biggest IPO as Nvidia Lines Up $500B

### Anthropic CFO Starts IPO Meetings as Investors Float a $2 Trillion Price Tag

Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao began early investor meetings this week for an IPO that backers think could value the company at $2 trillion or more. That would make it the largest IPO ever, bigger than SpaceX's $1.77 trillion debut in June. Anthropic's run-rate revenue passed $47 billion in May, up from about $10 billion for all of 2025, and some investors expect it to hit $100-120 billion by year-end.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-cfo-early-ipo-meetings-valuation.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/13/anthropic-eyes-2-trillion-in-october-ipo-a-record-breaking-debut/)*

### Nvidia Lines Up $500 Billion to Turn GPUs Into an Asset Class

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang recruited Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to help finance $500 billion worth of AI data centers and GPU clusters. "This is really the first time that technology chips have become an investable asset class," Huang told CNBC. The plan lets companies without Nvidia's balance sheet borrow against chips the way they'd borrow against real estate, though analysts warn cheap Chinese silicon could undercut that collateral fast.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/nvidia-wall-street-asset-managers-500-billion-ai-push.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/nvidia-s-500-billion-plan-envelops-wall-street-in-its-ai-frenzy)*

### IBM Trains Its Consulting Army on OpenAI's Models

IBM and OpenAI announced a partnership Aug 13 that puts GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work inside IBM Consulting Advantage, the AI platform IBM's roughly 150,000 consultants use with clients. IBM will build a dedicated OpenAI practice and certify tens of thousands of consultants on OpenAI's tools over the coming months. IBM's generative AI revenue already passed $12.5 billion in 2025, and this deal bets that number keeps climbing.

*Sources: [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-08-13-ibm-partners-with-openai-to-accelerate-secure-ai-deployment-for-enterprises-across-core-operations) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/ibm-partners-with-openai-to-bolster-enterprise-ai-push/)*

## AI Agents Get More Autonomy, and More Risk

### Hackers Ran the First Fully Autonomous AI Cyberattack on a Government

Suspected China-linked hackers used AI agents to run what researchers call the first end-to-end autonomous cyberattack on a government target, hitting Taiwan over four days in July. The attackers ran up to eight agents at once, built on open-source frameworks called Hermes and OpenClaw, breaching 85 government accounts and stealing more than 2,500 personnel records before moving on to Taiwan's nuclear safety agency and seven energy companies. "I've never seen an attack like this," Dream's chief strategy officer Amir Becker said, calling it truly end-to-end autonomous.

*Sources: [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/near-autonomous-ai-attack-government-target-taiwan/) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/13/tech/china-taiwan-ai-agent-cyberattack-intl-hnk)*

### Anthropic Turns On Auto Mode by Default in Claude Code

Anthropic made Auto Mode the default in Claude Code for Pro, Max, and Team plans on Aug 14, handing routine approval decisions to a classifier instead of the developer. In Anthropic's own study, human testers caught a planted dangerous command only 13.6% of the time; the classifier caught 89%. Enterprise, API, and cloud-platform deployments stay opt-in while admins review the change.

*Sources: [Claude by Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/auto-mode-default-in-claude-code) · [InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4207959/anthropic-makes-claude-codes-auto-mode-default-for-paid-users.html)*

### Critical Flaw in Microsoft Copilot Cowork Let Attackers Skip Login

Microsoft's August Patch Tuesday disclosed CVE-2026-59118, a 9.3-severity flaw in Copilot Cowork that let an unauthenticated attacker elevate privileges over the network. It's one of 415 vulnerabilities Microsoft patched this month, including one already-exploited zero-day and 62 other critical bugs. Researchers are telling enterprises to confirm they're on the patched Cowork build now, not later.

*Sources: [CrowdStrike](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/patch-tuesday-analysis-august-2026/) · [Talos Intelligence](https://blog.talosintelligence.com/microsoft-patch-tuesday-for-august-2026/)*

## Frontier Labs Split on Pricing

### Grok 4.6 Ties GPT-5.6 Sol at Half the Price

xAI's Grok 4.6 landed Aug 12 with an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score of 61, tying OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Max and trailing Fable 5 Max by just one point. Pricing held flat at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, more than 60% cheaper than GPT-5.6 Sol. The model is now the default in Grok Build and ships inside Cursor on every plan.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/spacexai-debuts-grok-4-6-overtaking-kimi-k3s-performance-and-matching-gpt-5-6-sol-for-worlds-third-best-on-artificial-analysis) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/12/spacexai-releases-grok-4-6/)*

### DeepSeek Raises V4 Pro Prices as Much as 14x

DeepSeek moved the opposite direction, launching V4 Pro at $1.32 per million input tokens and $3.96 per million output tokens, nine and fourteen times pricier than its own V4 Flash. The company says V4 Pro brings a real jump in agentic capability, not just a bigger number. It's a rare move against the grain: OpenAI and Anthropic have both been cutting prices as Chinese rivals like DeepSeek and Moonshot chase enterprise inference budgets.

*Sources: [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/08/14/deepseek-v4-pro-official-release-agent-benchmarks/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/01/deepseek-model-cheap-ai-price-war)*

## Nvidia and Meta Both Open-Source Their Newest Models

### Nvidia Ships Its First Open-Source Model, a 30B Agent Built for One GPU

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning on Aug 11, its first open-weight model since Jensen Huang started publicly backing open source in July. It's a 30-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model that activates only 3 billion parameters per token, so it runs on a single consumer GPU and finishes agentic tasks about 30% faster than comparable open models. Nvidia also released NeMo Switchyard, a free tool that routes agent requests to whichever model handles them most efficiently.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-releases-nemotron-3point5-lightning-open-source-ai-model-.html) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-ai-releases-nemotron-3-5-lightning-and-nemo-switchyard/)*

### Meta Open-Sources Muse Glimmer, a 30B Model That Runs on a Laptop GPU

Meta released Muse Glimmer under an Apache 2.0 license on Aug 10, a 30-billion-parameter agentic model quantized down to under 20GB so it runs on a single 24GB consumer GPU or a Mac. It's Meta Superintelligence Labs' clearest signal yet that the company plans to keep shipping open weights even as rivals lean toward closed models. Early benchmarks put it ahead of similarly-sized open models on agentic tool-use tasks.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meta-returns-to-open-source-with-muse-glimmer-an-apache-2-0-licensed-30b-parameter-ai-model-optimized-for-agents-available-now) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/10/meta-releases-open-source-muse-glimmer-model-30b-parameters/)*
