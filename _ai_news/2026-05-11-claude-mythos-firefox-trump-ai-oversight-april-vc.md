Anthropic's Claude Mythos model found 271 Firefox vulnerabilities in a single month — and the downstream effects are already reshaping US AI policy. Elsewhere, capital keeps breaking records, with April venture funding hitting $56B globally.

## AI Security Takes Center Stage

### Claude Mythos Finds 271 Firefox Zero-Days in a Month

Mozilla patched 423 Firefox security bugs in April — nearly 20 times its typical monthly count. Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview found 271 of them. Of those 271, 180 were high-severity, meaning a normal user could trigger them just by visiting a malicious webpage. One patched flaw had sat undetected in the codebase for 20 years. The AI pipeline goes further than earlier static-analysis attempts: it generates working proof-of-concept exploits to confirm each bug is real. That step eliminates the false positives that made GPT-4 and Claude 3.5 experiments impractical at scale.

*Sources: [Mozilla Hacks](https://hacks.mozilla.org/2026/05/behind-the-scenes-hardening-firefox/) · [Schneier on Security](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/04/claude-mythos-has-found-271-zero-days-in-firefox.html) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/claude-mythos-finds-271-firefox-vulnerabilities/)*

### Trump Administration Reverses on AI Pre-Deployment Testing

CAISI — the Center for AI Standards and Innovation, a NIST division — signed pre-deployment evaluation agreements with Google DeepMind, Microsoft, and xAI this week. The White House is separately studying mandatory pre-release reviews for frontier models. Security officials cited Claude Mythos's ability to find and exploit vulnerabilities at scale as a key factor in the policy shift. CAISI has already run 40 evaluations, including on models not yet publicly released. Existing agreements with OpenAI and Anthropic were renegotiated under Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's new directives.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/05/ai-oversight-trump-google-microsoft-xai.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/05/us-frontier-ai-testing-white-house-pivots-safety) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/trump-administration-embraces-ai-oversight-policies-it-once-rejected-anthropic-mythos-caisi/)*

## Model Releases and Agent Upgrades

### Anthropic Gives Claude Agents a "Dreaming" System

Anthropic released a research preview of "dreaming" — an overnight process that reviews an agent's past sessions, merges duplicate memories, prunes stale entries, and surfaces recurring patterns. Legal AI firm Harvey saw a 6x jump in task completion rates after implementing it. Medical document company Wisedocs cut review time by 50%. The feature is live now for Claude Managed Agents and can run automatically or with human review before changes apply.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-introduces-dreaming-a-system-that-lets-ai-agents-learn-from-their-own-mistakes) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/07/anthropic-updates-claude-managed-agents-with-three-new-features/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/06/anthropic-letting-claude-agents-dream-dont-sleep-job/)*

### Google's Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Hits General Availability

Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite went GA on May 7. It's 2.5x faster to first token than Gemini 2.5 Flash and costs $0.25 per million input tokens — the cheapest multimodal model in the Gemini line. Google built it for high-volume agentic pipelines: tool calling, orchestration, and automated workflows where latency and price matter more than peak reasoning. Output generation is 45% faster than its predecessor.

*Sources: [Google Cloud Blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-3-1-flash-lite-is-now-generally-available) · [Google AI Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-1-flash-lite/)*

## Capital Keeps Flowing at Record Pace

### April Global VC Hits $56B — AI Takes 66%

Global venture funding reached $56B in April, the third-largest monthly total ever — up 100% year-over-year. AI alone pulled in $37B, or 66% of all venture dollars. AI model companies took the largest slice at $26.7B. Q1 2026 had already set a record at $300B, and April's pace suggests no slowdown.

*Sources: [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/global-startup-funding-april-2026-anthropic-jeff-bezos-project-prometheus-biggest-deals/)*

### Moonshot AI Closes $2B Round at $20B Valuation

Beijing-based Moonshot AI, maker of the Kimi chatbot, closed a $2B round led by Meituan's Long-Z Investments. The $20B valuation makes it China's top-funded LLM startup. Kimi K2.6, a 1-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 262,000-token context window, is now the second-most-used LLM on OpenRouter. The company's ARR crossed $200M in April, and total capital raised since January has reached $3.9B.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-07/kimi-chatbot-maker-moonshot-ai-valued-at-20-billion-in-meituan-led-round) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/07/chinas-moonshot-ai-raises-2b-at-20b-valuation-as-demand-for-open-source-ai-skyrockets/)*

### Google's Drug Discovery Spinout Isomorphic Labs Eyes $2B+

Isomorphic Labs, the AlphaFold-based drug discovery company DeepMind spun out in 2021, is in advanced talks to raise more than $2B. Thrive Capital — which led its $600M Series A in March 2025 — is set to lead again, with Alphabet also participating. Proceeds will expand its drug design engine and support partnerships with J&J, Eli Lilly, and Novartis. Demis Hassabis runs Isomorphic alongside his role as DeepMind CEO.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-08/google-s-isomorphic-labs-to-raise-over-2-billion-in-new-funding) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/08/alphabets-isomorphic-labs-reportedly-raising-2b-medical-research-ai/)*

### Sierra Raises $950M at $15B for Enterprise AI Agents

Bret Taylor's Sierra raised $950M led by Tiger Global and GV, pushing its valuation above $15B — up from $10B last fall. The startup builds AI customer service agents and hit $150M ARR in eight quarters. It claims 40%+ of the Fortune 50 as customers. Taylor, who also chairs OpenAI, said Sierra now has "more than $1 billion to invest" toward becoming the global standard for AI-driven customer experience.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/04/bret-taylor-sierra-fundraise-openai.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/)*
