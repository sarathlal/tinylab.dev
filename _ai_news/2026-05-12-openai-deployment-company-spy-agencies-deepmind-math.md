OpenAI made its biggest move into professional services, Washington's intelligence agencies made their play for AI oversight, and DeepMind published research that changes what "frontier AI" means in mathematics.

## OpenAI Goes All-In on Enterprise Deployment

### OpenAI Launches a $4B Consulting Arm to Embed Engineers Inside Businesses

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Deployment Company on May 11 — a $4 billion venture valued at $14 billion, backed by 19 partners including TPG, Bain Capital, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, McKinsey & Company, and Capgemini. At launch, it acquired Tomoro, a Scottish applied AI firm whose ~150 Forward Deployed Engineers have built real-time systems for Tesco, Virgin Atlantic, and Supercell. The new company's FDEs will embed inside client organizations to build and run production AI systems connected directly to each company's data and workflows.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-11/openai-to-buy-consulting-firm-for-private-equity-joint-venture) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/11/openai-deployco-private-equity)*

### Windows 11 May 12 Update Opens the Taskbar to Third-Party AI Agents

Microsoft's May 12 security update ships new AI agent APIs that let third-party agents sit in the Windows 11 taskbar and display real-time reasoning updates on hover. Users invoke them via "@" in the search box; agents are off by default. It's the first time Microsoft has given external AI products a persistent hook inside the Windows shell.

*Sources: [MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/windows-11-may-2026-update-pairs-new-features-with-ai-rethink/gm-GMCB6A6D01) · [Windows Latest](https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/04/18/microsoft-confirms-ai-agents-are-still-coming-to-the-windows-11-taskbar-as-it-prepares-for-public-rollout/)*

### IBM: 76% of Companies Now Have a Chief AI Officer — Up from 26% a Year Ago

An IBM survey of 2,000+ business leaders, published May 11, found three-quarters of organizations have created a chief AI officer role — up from one in four a year ago. The same report found 59% of respondents expect HR chiefs to gain influence as AI agents reshape how work gets done. McKinsey, one of the OpenAI Deployment Company's launch partners, called it "the largest organizational shift since the industrial and digital revolutions."

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/11/heres-how-artificial-intelligence-is-changing-boardrooms.html)*

## Anthropic's Mythos Model Triggers a Washington Power Struggle

### US Spy Agencies Want Formal Oversight of Advanced AI — Trump White House Is Split

The Washington Post reported Monday that senior intelligence officials — from the NSA, the Director of National Intelligence, and the Office of the National Cyber Director — are pushing for formal authority over advanced AI models. The Trump administration, until recently explicitly anti-regulation, is now sharply divided over the proposal. The fight traces directly to Anthropic's Mythos model, which found cybersecurity vulnerabilities across every major operating system and browser at a scale that alarmed national security officials.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/05/11/trump-ai-regulation-commerce-intelligence/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/trump-administration-embraces-ai-oversight-policies-it-once-rejected-anthropic-mythos-caisi/)*

### Anthropic CEO: 6-12 Months to Patch What Mythos Found Before Chinese AI Catches Up

Mythos Preview found nearly 300 vulnerabilities in Firefox — versus roughly 20 found by an earlier Claude model — and autonomously exploited a 17-year-old FreeBSD remote code execution bug that grants root access over NFS. Dario Amodei said there's a six- to 12-month window to patch tens of thousands of software vulnerabilities before Chinese labs reach comparable capability. Anthropic initially gave access only to Apple, Amazon, JPMorgan Chase, and Palo Alto Networks.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/05/anthropic-ceo-cyber-moment-of-danger-mythos-vulnerabilities.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/08/anthropic-mythos-ai-cybersecurity-banks.html)*

## DeepMind Puts AI on Science's Frontier

### DeepMind's AI Co-Mathematician Hits 48% on the Hardest Math Benchmark — and Helps Crack an Open Problem

Google DeepMind's AI co-mathematician — a multi-agent system built on Gemini 3.1 — scored 48% on FrontierMath Tier 4, a benchmark Epoch AI designed to be "potentially unsolvable by AI for decades." Oxford professor Marc Lackenby used it to resolve problem 21.10 from the Kourovka Notebook, an open problem in group theory that had gone unsolved for years. A reviewer agent flagged a flaw in the AI's first proof attempt; Lackenby spotted the fix.

*Sources: [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06651) · [The Rundown AI](https://www.therundown.ai/p/google-deepmind-powerful-ai-co-mathematician) · [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/google-deepmind-releases-ai-co-mathematician-that-creates-new-high-score-on-frontiermath-benchmark/)*

### AlphaEvolve's First Year: Power Grid Feasibility From 14% to 88%, DNA Errors Down 30%

AlphaEvolve lifted a power grid optimization model's feasibility rate from 14% to 88% in its first year in production, according to DeepMind's May 2026 impact report. The Gemini-powered algorithm discovery system also cut DNA sequencing error rates by 30% at PacBio and found quantum circuits with 10x lower error for molecular simulations on Google's Willow processor. It continuously recovers 0.7% of Google's global compute through better data center scheduling.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-impact/) · [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/54172/google-deepmind-releases-one-year-results-for-alphaevolve-dna-variant-detection-errors-cut-30-percent)*
