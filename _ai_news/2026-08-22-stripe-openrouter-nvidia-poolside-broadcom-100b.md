Two AI companies got bought for north of $6 billion apiece this week, and that wasn't even the biggest financing story. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## Big Tech Goes on an AI Buying Spree

### Stripe Pays $7.5B for OpenRouter, Its Largest Deal Ever

Stripe agreed to buy AI model-routing startup OpenRouter for about $7.5 billion, with $1.5 billion going to its founders and $6 billion to investors. OpenRouter lets developers route requests across roughly 400 models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others, picking by price, speed, and reliability. The price is nearly six times the $1.3 billion valuation OpenRouter raised at just three months ago.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/)*

### Nvidia Pays Poolside $6B to License Its Model Factory, Hires 100+ Staff

Nvidia struck a non-exclusive $6 billion licensing deal with AI coding startup Poolside for access to its "Model Factory," plus a $1 billion investment at a $12 billion valuation. Nvidia is also extending job offers to more than 100 Poolside employees, while the startup keeps operating independently. It's the same acquihire-style playbook Google and Microsoft used on Character.AI and Inflection.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/nvidia-to-pay-ai-startup-poolside-a-6-billion-license-newcomer-says) · [Newcomer](https://www.newcomer.co/p/sources-poolside-strikes-6-billion)*

### Nvidia in Early Talks to Invest in or Buy Korean Chip Rival Rebellions

Jensen Huang met Rebellions co-founder Sunghyun Park in Santa Clara this week to discuss a technical partnership, investment, or outright acquisition. Rebellions builds neural processing units for AI inference and has raised about $850 million from SK Hynix, Samsung Ventures, and Arm at a $2.3 billion valuation. Talks are described as preliminary and may not lead to a deal.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/nvidia-in-talks-with-chip-startup-rebellions-for-potential-deal) · [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/21/nvidia-explores-potential-partnership-or-acquisition-with/)*

## AI's Capital Markets Moment

### Broadcom Seeks Up to $100B in Debt to Bankroll Anthropic's Chips

Broadcom is in talks with lenders, including Blackstone and Apollo, for $70-80 billion in debt that could grow to $100 billion, funding custom AI chips for Anthropic and other customers. Under the structure, investors buy the chips and lease them to Anthropic rather than Anthropic buying them outright. It builds on a $35 billion financing platform Broadcom set up with Apollo and Blackstone back in June.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/broadcom-seeks-more-than-60-billion-in-latest-ai-debt-deal) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/broadcom-debt-deal-expected-to-reach-upwards-of-70-billion-sources.html)*

### Anthropic Schedules IPO Investor Meetings, Eyes October Listing

Anthropic is lining up meetings with prospective investors as it moves toward a possible IPO as soon as October, following a confidential S-1 filing in June. The company raised $65 billion in a Series H round at a $965 billion valuation and is projecting annualized revenue to nearly triple to around $26 billion, with more than 300,000 business customers.

*Sources: [Financial Times via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/anthropic-plans-ipo-early-2026-004854547.html)*

### Rillet Becomes a $1B AI-Native ERP Unicorn in 14 Months

Accounting startup Rillet raised a $100 million Series C led by ICONIQ at a $1 billion valuation, its third round in about 14 months. New annual recurring revenue doubled in the last three months and its customer base topped 600 companies, including Mercor and Function Health. Rillet calls its pitch "Accounting Superintelligence" — AI agents doing complex bookkeeping directly inside a live general ledger.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/rillet-raises-100m-series-c-at-1b-valuation-2-years-after-emerging-from-stealth/) · [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/rillet-raises-100-million-series-c-at-1-billion-valuation-as-ai-native-erp-tops-600-customers/)*

## AI Moves Deeper Into Daily Life

### ChatGPT Can Now Read and Send Your iMessages on Mac

OpenAI shipped a ChatGPT plugin for Apple Messages on Apple silicon Macs that can read, search, summarize, and send iMessage, SMS, and RCS conversations on a user's behalf. It's available to all ChatGPT tiers, including free, but requires Full Disk Access and approves every outgoing message before it sends. OpenAI says it processes messages locally rather than building a searchable index of them.

*Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2241390/openai-chatgpt-imessage-integration/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/gabrielalinzainescu/2026/08/21/chatgpt-can-now-send-your-imessages---and-test-apples-privacy-brand/)*

### Apple Music Will Start Labeling AI-Generated Songs

Apple Music will require record labels and distributors to tag tracks with "Made With AI" labels when a material portion of a song was AI-generated, with the rule arriving later this year. Nearly 40% of July's music releases reportedly involved AI in some form, and Deezer says AI tracks now make up about 44% of daily uploads, though they're still under 3% of total streams.

*Sources: [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/20/apple-music-will-soon-get-visible-labels-for-ai-generated-content/) · [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2509039/apple-music-will-also-now-label-ai-generated-songs/news)*

### Google Gives Publishers a "Preferred Sources" Button to Fight AI Traffic Loss

Google launched a button publishers can add to their sites letting readers mark them as a favorite source, boosting how often their content shows up in Top Stories, AI Overviews, and AI Mode. It's a response to steep referral-traffic declines, with some publishers reporting click-through drops of up to 58% as AI Overviews answer queries directly instead of sending readers out.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/google-gives-publishers-a-new-way-to-fight-ai-driven-traffic-losses/) · [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/google-ai-overviews-linked-to-25-drop-in-publisher-referral-traffic-new-data-shows/)*
