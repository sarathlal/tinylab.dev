Anthropic kicked off the biggest tech IPO roadshow in memory and shipped an office suite on the same day. Meanwhile, two governments moved on frontier-lab rules, and the money behind AI chips is starting to look shaky.

## Anthropic's IPO Week

### Anthropic prices IPO at $70–$78 a share, aiming to raise up to $64.4B

Anthropic will sell 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each, which raises about $64.4B at the top of the range. That implies a valuation between roughly $1.26T and $1.41T. It plans to price on November 18 and start trading on Nasdaq as "ANT" on November 19. About 20% of shares are set aside for retail buyers, far above the norm. The launch wasn't clean: a leaked prospectus briefly showed a $69.3B target before the company called it outdated.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-ipo-roadshow-with-64b-fundraising-goal)*

### Claude Sheets, Meetings, Whiteboards and Data go after Microsoft Office

Anthropic launched four office tools the same day its roadshow began. Claude Sheets is a spreadsheet with Claude built in, and Anthropic admits it's basic for now. Claude Data is generally available, while Sheets and Whiteboards are in beta for paid plans. Microsoft shares fell about 2–3%. The awkward part: Anthropic models also power parts of Microsoft's Copilot.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/anthropic-unveils-office-tools-ahead-of-ipo-taking-on-microsoft) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-office-knowledge-worker-product-launch-ahead-of-ipo/)*

## New Models and Products From the Other Big Players

### Google opens Gemini 4 Carbon to all paying developers

Gemini 4 Carbon, Google's strongest model, is now open to paying API developers and AI Ultra subscribers. The intro price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. It doubles to $4/$20 on December 1. Google first gave Carbon only to a small group of cyber defenders when it announced the model on October 28.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/google-gemini-4-carbon-is-now-publicly-available-and-it-undercuts-claude-fable-5-2-and-opus-5-5-on-api-price) · [eesel AI](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/gemini-4-carbon-pricing)*

### OpenAI lets businesses claim their ChatGPT profiles and run their own agents

Businesses can now claim a verified profile in ChatGPT, with a blue check, and see how often ChatGPT mentions them. The new Business Hub shows click-throughs and common topics, and lets firms update pricing and stock. US ecommerce and online-service companies can also launch a labeled Business Agent. OpenAI says paying for ads won't change how often ChatGPT recommends a business.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-businesses-in-chatgpt-business-agents) · [Search Engine Watch](https://searchenginewatch.com/chatgpt-business-hub-claim-profile-mentions-clicks/)*

### Nvidia's GR00T 2 robot model learned mostly from human video

Only about 5.4% of GR00T 2's 436,414 training hours came from robots. The rest is human video. Nvidia showed the model at CoRL in Austin. It hit 95% average success across 50 tasks with five demonstrations each, in Nvidia's own tests. Weights and code are due in December.

*Sources: [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/nvidia-introduces-gr00t-2-for-robots-learning-from-human-video/) · [Pebblous](https://blog.pebblous.ai/blog/nvidia-groot-2-human-video-data-provenance/en/)*

## Governments Write Rules for Frontier Labs

### Australia will create an AI safety regulator with binding rules

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said frontier AI companies will need independent model evaluations, published safety frameworks and fast incident reporting to operate in Australia. The push follows a June incident where an OpenAI agent got into a government Medicare reporting portal. The government is also weighing criminal penalties for the worst breaches. Penalty amounts, a legal definition of "frontier" and a timeline are still missing.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/australia-to-create-ai-safety-regulator-mandatory-safety-rules) · [Australian Government](https://www.minister.industry.gov.au/t-ayres/media/new-australian-ai-safety-and-security-protections) · [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/australia-rolls-out-new-ai-regulators-security-obligations)*

### New York opens RAISE Act registration for OpenAI, Anthropic and other big labs

New York opened a registration portal for frontier developers with more than $500M in annual revenue. They have until January 1, 2027 to sign up. Covered labs must publish safety frameworks and report critical incidents within 72 hours. Fines run up to $1M for a first violation and $3M for repeats.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/) · [Pillsbury](https://consumer-protection-dispatch.pillsburylaw.com/new-york-raise-act-what-ai-developers-need-know/)*

## Cracks in AI's Money and Plumbing

### Lenders try to unload AI chip and data-center loans at steep discounts

Some investors are selling whole portfolios of data-center loans below face value. The worry is simple: the loans may outlast the GPUs backing them. ING has a $500M loan package on the block, with bids due November 19. Ares's CEO said the firm will pass on GPU financing deals.

*Sources: [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/09/2026/ai-chip-financing-hits-a-wall) · [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/bond-investors-are-rethinking-ai-data-center-debt)*

### Fireworks AI says hackers reached customer datasets and unfinished model weights

Fireworks widened its disclosure of an October breach. Attackers got in through a third-party cloud provider and reached billing data, secrets, datasets and partial model weights for some customers. The company blames "LLMjacking" gangs that steal and resell AI access, and is working with Palo Alto's Unit 42. Fireworks says zero-retention inference wasn't affected.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/annatong/2026/11/09/fireworks-cyberattack-exposes-customer-data-and-ai-models-as-fallout-widens/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/fireworks-ai-breach-widens-to-expose.html)*

### OpenAI closes $11B first tranche at a $1.25T valuation

OpenAI reportedly secured $11B at a $1.25T valuation, the first piece of a round expected to reach up to $20B. That's well short of its original goal of at least $30B at about $1.4T. OpenAI hasn't confirmed the numbers. The gap looks sharper next to Anthropic's IPO, which targets a similar valuation.

*Sources: [Bestmediainfo](https://bestmediainfo.com/mediainfo/mediainfo-digital/openai-raises-11-billion-in-latest-funding-round-at-125-trillion-valuation-report-12798201) · [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-funding-news-nov-7-8-2026/)*
