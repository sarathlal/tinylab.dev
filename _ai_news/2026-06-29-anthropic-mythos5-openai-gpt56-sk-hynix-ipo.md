Two of the most powerful AI systems yet released both cleared Washington before going live this week. The informal gatekeeping is now the story.

## Washington Grabs the Frontier AI Release Trigger

### US Commerce Clears Anthropic's Mythos 5 for 100+ Vetted Institutions — Fable 5 Still on Hold

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick wrote to Anthropic's chief compute officer Tom Brown on Friday, saying "I have determined that appropriate safeguards are in place to permit certain trusted partners to access the Claude Mythos 5 Model." That ended a roughly two-week block. The administration had pulled both Mythos and the consumer-facing Fable 5 after Amazon and others warned they could be jailbroken for harmful uses. The letter doesn't mention Fable 5. People close to the negotiations say a separate deal for Fable is in progress, with no timeline set.

*Sources: [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/27/2026/us-releases-powerful-anthropic-model-mythos-to-some-us-companies) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/27/anthropic-mythos-5-ai-model-us-commerce-department-clearance-fable/)*

### OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Also Released Under the Same Informal Arrangement

OpenAI previewed its GPT-5.6 family on Thursday — Sol, Terra, and Luna — but made them available only to roughly 20 organizations after sharing plans with Washington first. Sol is the flagship for hard problems like complex coding and security research, priced at $5 input / $30 output per million tokens. Terra matches GPT-5.5 performance at half the price. Luna is the cheapest option at $1/$6. Two frontier launches in four days, both routed through DC. The White House AI executive order signed June 2 established a voluntary review framework that explicitly bars mandatory licensing — but both labs are clearly treating coordination with government as non-optional.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/26/openai-gpt-sol-terra-luna-trump) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)*

## Custom Silicon and the AI Infrastructure Bet

### OpenAI and Broadcom Unveiled Jalapeño — First Custom Chip, Built in Nine Months

OpenAI and Broadcom announced Jalapeño on Wednesday: a reticle-sized ASIC built for LLM inference. The chip went from initial design to tape-out in nine months — a development pace that may be the fastest ever for a high-performance ASIC. OpenAI used its own models to accelerate the engineering work. The chip is less flexible than an Nvidia GPU but purpose-built around OpenAI's model kernels, memory movement, and serving patterns. Deployment targets end of 2026, with plans for gigawatt-scale data centers with Microsoft and others beyond that.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/openai-and-broadcom-reveal-jalapeno-first-ai-chip-in-partnership.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/openai-unveils-its-first-custom-chip-built-by-broadcom/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/openai-unveils-first-custom-ai-inference-chip-jalapeno-with-broadcom-and-its-development-was-sped-up-with-openais-own-models/)*

### SK Hynix Eyes a $29B Nasdaq Listing — Possibly the Biggest Tech IPO Ever

SK Hynix, NVIDIA's main HBM memory supplier, plans to begin trading on Nasdaq on July 10 at roughly $166 per ADR, raising $29.6 billion. That would top Saudi Aramco's $25.6 billion 2019 offering as the largest global tech listing on record. The company is building a new chip campus in South Korea and a $4 billion HBM packaging plant in Indiana. HSBC pegs the IPO price 20% below fair value on AI memory demand. The timing is sharp: the Nasdaq has fallen more than 6% from its AI-driven all-time high set June 2, as public markets take a harder look at AI valuations.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/sk-hynix-nasdaq-adr-listing-south-korea.html) · [CNBC HSBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/sk-hynix-us-listing-ipo-hsbc-price-target.html) · [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/26/investing/tech-stocks-nasdaq-kospi)*

## Regulation Softens on Both Sides of the Atlantic

### EU Gives High-Risk AI Systems an Extra 16 Months

The European Parliament voted June 16 to delay core AI Act obligations for high-risk systems by 16 months. AI deployed in employment, education, healthcare, and other sensitive sectors now have until December 2, 2027 to comply — pushed from August 2, 2026. The Parliament simplified other requirements at the same time. European businesses argued the original timeline was unworkable. The delay looks like a deliberate pivot: the EU is now treating industrial AI competitiveness as a first-order concern alongside precaution.

*Sources: [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/06/eu-approves-delays-and-other-amendments-to-certain-eu-ai-act-obligations-what-businesses-should-know)*

### Colorado Replaced Its Landmark AI Law — Arizona Vetoed All Three AI Bills

Colorado Governor Jared Polis signed SB 26-189 in May, repealing the state's 2024 AI Act — the first comprehensive state AI law in the US. The replacement drops risk management programs, impact assessments, and algorithmic discrimination duties. What's left: notify users when AI is involved, disclose adverse outcomes within 30 days, let people correct inaccurate data, and allow human review. It takes effect January 1, 2027. Arizona went further — Governor Hobbs vetoed all three AI bills her legislature passed this session. Strict AI frameworks enacted in 2024-25 are being scaled back before they take effect.

*Sources: [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-us/knowledge/publications/18733d31/colorado-enacts-revised-ai-law) · [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/colorado-hits-reset-on-ai-regulation-sb-26-189-repeals-and-reenacts-the-colorado-ai-act) · [Davis Wright Tremaine](https://www.dwt.com/blogs/privacy--security-law-blog/2026/05/colorado-ai-act-repeal-new-transparency-law)*
