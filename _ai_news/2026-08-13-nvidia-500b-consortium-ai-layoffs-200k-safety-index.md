Nvidia just bought itself $500 billion in Wall Street goodwill to keep the AI buildout financed. Layoffs crossed 200,000 for the year and every frontier lab flunked its own safety report card. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last two days.

## Nvidia's $500 Billion Wall Street Deal Calms Jittery AI Credit Markets

### Nvidia Enlists Six Wall Street Giants for $500 Billion AI Financing Push

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang says he approached six firms — Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR — to help finance $500 billion of AI infrastructure. None turned him down. The move came after the cost of insuring Nvidia's debt against default nearly doubled in three weeks, hitting $82,000 a year per $10 million of principal, as investors fretted over circular financing deals. Nvidia's own exposure stays limited: it's guaranteeing up to 25% of some projects, while the six firms independently pick which deals to fund.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/nvidia-s-show-of-financial-force-soothes-jittery-credit-markets) · [Bloomberg Newsletter](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-12/nvidia-calms-credit-markets-with-ai-capital-consortium)*

### AI Stocks Now Make Up 35% of the S&P 500, Reviving Bubble Talk

The top 10 S&P 500 stocks now account for 35% of the index, well above the 25% concentration seen at the dot-com bubble's peak. The index is up 13.4% year to date, largely on the back of AI names. Nvidia alone pulled in $215.94 billion in revenue for fiscal 2026, up 65% year over year — the kind of cash flow that separates this rally from 2000's, but not enough to quiet comparisons.

*Sources: [The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/11/is-the-artificial-intelligence-ai-bubble-about-to/)*

## US AI-Linked Job Cuts Cross 200,000 for the Year

### Oracle Plans Another Layoff Round to Fund Its AI Buildout, Aiming to Cut Before September

Oracle has drawn up plans for a fresh round of job cuts this month, an internal document viewed by Business Insider on August 11 shows. Some teams could see double-digit percentage reductions. Oracle already cut 21,000 roles earlier in 2026 and booked up to $2.1 billion in restructuring charges, while its AI data-center capex jumped to $55.7 billion for the year from $21.2 billion — a gap it's covering by borrowing $43 billion and selling $5 billion in stock.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/oracle-planning-round-layoffs-august-134527039.html)*

### AI-Attributed Layoffs Hit 205,832 Workers in 2026, Already Matching Last Year's Full Total

US layoffs tied to AI and automation reached 205,832 workers across 322 events as of August 12 — about 919 job losses a day. Oracle, Amazon (16,000 cut), Meta (8,000), Microsoft (4,800), and Cisco (roughly 4,000, despite record revenue) lead the list. The cuts concentrate in customer service, data operations, entry-level software roles, and finance back offices.

*Sources: [Martin Cid](https://www.martincid.com/business/tech-layoffs-2026-record-ai-blame/)*

## AI Cybersecurity Cuts Both Ways

### OpenAI Expands Daybreak With GPT-5.6-Cyber for Vetted Security Teams

OpenAI expanded its Daybreak cybersecurity initiative on August 10, adding GPT-5.6-Cyber, a model built for authorized security work. New Daybreak Blue and Daybreak Red tiers give approved defenders access to frontier models for vulnerability research, code review, incident response, and security testing.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/news/company-announcements/)*

### No Frontier AI Lab Scores Above a C+ on Safety, New Index Finds

The Future of Life Institute's Summer 2026 AI Safety Index gave its highest grade — a C+ worth 2.66 out of roughly 4.0 — to Anthropic. xAI failed outright with a 0.65, down from a D-level 1.17 six months earlier, dropping it from 4th to 7th place among labs ranked. DeepSeek and Mistral also received failing grades.

*Sources: [Future of Life Institute](https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/)*

## Enterprise AI Deals Keep Stacking Up

### AI Code-Testing Startup Blacksmith Raises $45M, Valuation Jumps 10x to $550M

Blacksmith closed a $45 million Series B led by Peak XV Partners on August 12, pushing its valuation to $550 million — up roughly tenfold from its Series A less than a year ago. The bet: as AI writes code faster, validating and testing that code becomes the bottleneck, not writing it.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/blacksmiths-valuation-jumps-10x-to-550m-as-ai-coding-fuels-software-validation/)*

### LivePerson Pushes Shareholders to Approve SoundHound's Takeover Before August 20 Vote

LivePerson urged its shareholders on August 11 to back SoundHound AI's acquisition of the company, first announced back in April at about $3.33 a share. The combined company would serve 25 Fortune 100 customers and targets $350-400 million in 2027 revenue, plus a $500 million cross-sell opportunity. The vote lands August 20.

*Sources: [Foreign Policy Journal](https://www.foreignpolicyjournal.com/2026/08/11/liveperson-nasdaq-lpsn-pushes-stockholders-to-back-soundhound-ai-nasdaq-soun-takeover-before-august-20-vote/) · [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/LPSN/live-person-sends-letter-to-stockholders-highlighting-benefits-of-vdx79zyhr1iv.html)*
