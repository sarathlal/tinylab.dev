A banned Nvidia rack turned up in a Shanghai data center, and a warranty database gave it away. Elsewhere, governments set the terms for military AI and smart glasses, and shoppers leaned on chatbots over Thanksgiving.

## Chips and Attack Tools Slip Past the Guardrails

### Bloomberg finds a banned Blackwell rack in a Shanghai data center

Filings with Chinese regulators show a GB300 NVL72 rack, one of Nvidia's most powerful systems, at a Shanghai data center. Bloomberg traced it to Taiwan's Gigabyte through a serial number in a public warranty database. The operator, Shanghai Shuoyao, pledged the hardware for a 96 million yuan ($14M) loan. Shuoyao serves Megaspeed, the Singapore firm founded by Alice Huang that the US blacklisted this month. Bloomberg says it found no proof Megaspeed itself moved the gear, and Megaspeed denies wrongdoing.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-27/nvidia-blackwell-chips-turn-up-at-china-site-tied-to-alice-huang) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/banned-nvidia-blackwell-chips-turn-up-in-a-shanghai-data-center-linked-to-alice-huang-bloomberg-reports/) · [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/world/2026/11/28/nvidias-blackwell-chips-spotted-at-shanghai-data-center)*

### Microsoft counts six state-linked groups using the ARTEX AI attack tool

Microsoft now tracks six groups using ARTEX, including China's Storm-0940 and Salt Typhoon and North Korea's Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet. ARTEX started in July as an open-source, LLM-driven pen-testing tool. It went closed-source in October, but public forks are still out there. Storm-0940 used it to try sign-ins at more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants. Some intrusions reached privileged access in under three hours.

*Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/china-apt-uses-ai-pentest-tool-artex-in-connectwise-attacks/) · [ComplexDiscovery](https://complexdiscovery.com/microsoft-salt-typhoon-artex-no-campaign-link/)*

## Governments Write the Terms for AI

### Poland signs a $300M, five-year deal for Palantir's battlefield AI

Poland's defense ministry signed a deal on Friday worth about $300 million over five years. It buys Palantir software that helps army commanders make decisions. Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said Polish forces keep the keys, the servers and the data. Palantir shares rose almost 5% on the news. The deal puts Warsaw out of step with European allies who are hunting for homegrown defense software.

*Sources: [Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/11/27/poland-signs-300m-deal-with-palantir-for-ai-powered-battlefield-software-platform/) · [The Star / Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/27/poland-signs-contract-with-palantir-worth-300-million-over-5-years) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/contracts/26/11/63678543/palantirs-polish-ai-deal-comes-as-peter-thiel-buys-shares-and-cathie-wood-sells)*

### China drafts property rights for the data that trains AI

China's National Data Administration published a draft on November 26 that would create property rights over data used to train and run AI models. It's open for public comment. Beijing hasn't set a date or final details yet. If it sticks, Chinese labs would need clear ownership terms for their training sets, which is something US courts are still fighting over.

*Sources: [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/world/2026/11/27/china-moves-to-create-property-rights-for-ai-training-data)*

### Dutch consumer group takes Meta's camera glasses to court

The Consumentenbond argued its case in Amsterdam District Court on November 26. It wants Meta to change the Ray-Ban glasses and their default settings. If Meta can't, the group wants sales banned, with an exception for blind and low-vision users. It says covert footage of Dutch women is circulating on creepshot sites and Telegram. Meta says it will keep selling the glasses. No ruling yet.

*Sources: [Mixed News](https://mixed-news.com/en/consumentenbond-court-case-meta-camera-glasses-sales-ban/) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/news/meta-glasses-privacy-lawsuit/) · [MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2537670)*

## AI Shoppers Spend More, ChatGPT Users Get Less

### Thanksgiving online sales hit $7.1B as AI-referred traffic jumps 162%

Americans spent a record $7.1 billion online on Thanksgiving, up 10.4% and above Adobe's $6.9 billion forecast. Visits to retail sites from AI tools rose 162% year over year. Shoppers who came from AI tools bought 74% more often than everyone else. Adobe expects $12.9 billion in online spending today, on Black Friday.

*Sources: [TipRanks / The Fly](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/adobe-data-shows-ai-traffic-soaring-as-thanksgiving-online-sales-reach-7-1b-thefly-news) · [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/11/25/adobe-online-holiday-spending-data/) · [Adobe](https://business.adobe.com/resources/holiday-shopping-report.html)*

### OpenAI fixes a billing bug, so GPT-6.2 limits now hit 40% sooner

A launch bug had undercounted GPT-6.2 usage in ChatGPT. Now that it's fixed, users hit limits 40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra and 25% faster on Sol. OpenAI won't charge for past usage. To say sorry, it's adding "one banked reset for everyone on Go, Plus, Pro, and Business," said OpenAI's Tibo Sottiaux.

*Sources: [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/11/27/openai-fixes-a-billing-bug-and-chatgpt-users-will-now-hit-gpt-6-2-limits-40-faster/) · [Super Power Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/openai-fixes-billing-error-leaving-gpt-6-2-users-hitting-limits-faster)*
