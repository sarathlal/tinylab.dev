Two storylines ran through the last few days: how far AI labs will bend to Washington's demands, and how little the export ban is actually slowing China down. Here's what happened.

## Washington's New Grip on the AI Labs

### Pentagon Emails Show Anthropic's Weapons Redlines Almost Got Erased

Court documents unsealed July 2 show Pentagon Under Secretary Emil Michael telling Dario Amodei the two sides were "very close" on a contract — one day after his own department finalized a supply-chain risk designation against Anthropic. Amodei pushed back that the proposed language would "completely remove" Anthropic's redlines on autonomous weapons and domestic surveillance. Michael didn't deny it. He just called the guardrails "not workable."

*Sources: [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/read-the-tense-emails-between-the-pentagon-former-uber-exec-and-anthropic-dario-amodei-2000780849) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319713/20260704/pentagon-blacklisted-anthropic-over-autonomous-weapons-limits-emails-reveal-very-close-talks.htm)*

### OpenAI Offers Washington a 5% Stake Worth $42.6 Billion

OpenAI pitched the US government a 5% equity stake modeled on Alaska's Permanent Fund, the Financial Times reports. At OpenAI's $852B valuation, that slice is worth roughly $42.6B. Sam Altman wants Anthropic, Google, and Meta to hand over similar stakes too, though none have agreed, and Senator Bernie Sanders says the public should get 50%, not 5%.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/openai-proposes-us-government-own-5percent-stake-to-address-political-blowback.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/07/02/openai-reportedly-pitches-granting-us-government-5-stake/)*

## China's AI Machine Keeps Running Despite the Export Ban

### Anthropic Cracks Down on Ant Group and ByteDance's Backdoor Claude Access

Anthropic is closing gaps that let Chinese firms use Claude despite its ban on China-linked accounts, the Financial Times reports. Ant Group reportedly gave staff Claude accounts tied to a Singapore subsidiary, while ByteDance engineers expensed personal Claude subscriptions bought over VPN. Anthropic now says it will flag accounts by time zone and usage pattern to catch these "transfer stations" — after Amodei said in February the company had already forgone "several hundred million dollars" in revenue to keep CCP-linked firms out.

*Sources: [BanklessTimes](https://www.banklesstimes.com/articles/2026/07/03/anthropic-moves-to-block-chinese-firms-using-claude-via-offshore-workarounds/) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-targets-loopholes-used-by-chinese-firms-to-access-claude-ft-reports-4774998)*

### Meituan's 1.6-Trillion-Parameter Model Was Secretly Topping OpenRouter for Weeks

Meituan open-sourced LongCat-2.0, a 1.6-trillion-parameter model trained entirely on domestic Chinese chips, under an MIT license. It had already been climbing OpenRouter's usage charts under the alias "Owl Alpha" before Meituan revealed it built the thing. The model trained on a cluster of 50,000 Chinese ASICs instead of Nvidia GPUs — proof Beijing's chip industry can now support near-frontier training runs without US hardware.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meituan-open-sources-longcat-2-0-the-1-6t-near-frontier-agentic-coding-model-thats-been-leading-openrouter-trained-entirely-on-chinese-chips) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/30/chinas-meituan-open-sources-massive-longcat-2-0-ai-model-saying-trained-domestic-chips/)*

## The Frontier Model Race

### Meta's Unreleased Watermelon Model Claims It Caught Up to GPT-5.5

Meta AI chief Alexandr Wang told staff at a town hall that Watermelon, the successor to April's Muse Spark, now matches GPT-5.5 on benchmarks he didn't name. Watermelon is still training, using what Wang called "an order of magnitude more" compute than its predecessor. Meta declined to comment and OpenAI didn't respond, so treat this as a town-hall claim, not a published result.

*Sources: [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60264651/metas-upcoming-watermelon-ai-model-matches-openais-gpt-5-5-on-key-benchmarks-alexandr-wang-reportedly-tells-employees) · [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/07/03/meta-ai-chief-says-watermelon-model-has-caught-up-to-gpt-5-5-484022/)*

### Grok 4.5 Is Already Driving Tesla and SpaceX, Musk Says

Elon Musk says Grok 4.5, built on xAI's 1.5-trillion-parameter V9 base model, is now running in private beta inside Tesla and SpaceX. He says internal evals put it "close to, perhaps exceeding" Claude Opus, though no outside lab has verified that claim. The model adds Cursor coding data in supplemental training, and Musk says SpaceX will ship a new model every month this year.

*Sources: [CyberNews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/musk-grok-4-5-private-beta-superior-claude-opus/) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319314/20260629/grok-45-enters-private-beta-spacex-tesla-no-public-access-no-independent-benchmark.htm)*

## AI Labs Chase the Enterprise Deployment Business

### Microsoft Puts $2.5 Billion and 6,000 Engineers Into a New AI Deployment Unit

Microsoft launched Frontier Company on July 2, a unit that embeds 6,000 engineers directly at customer sites to build and run AI systems. The London Stock Exchange Group, Unilever, Land O'Lakes, and Accenture are the first partners. It's Microsoft's answer to OpenAI's own deployment arm, DeployCo, and to AWS's $1B push into the same business two days earlier.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/microsoft-launches-its-own-ai-deployment-company-with-2-5-billion-commitment/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/microsoft-commits-2point5-billion-6000-employees-ai-implementation-unit.html)*

### Meta May Start Renting Out Its AI Compute, Following xAI's Lead

Meta is weighing whether to sell access to its data-center compute, an internal effort called Meta Compute, as its own models keep lagging rivals. The plan mirrors what SpaceX's xAI already did in May, when it sold Anthropic all the compute capacity at its Colossus 1 data center. Meta's stock jumped 9% the day the plan leaked.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/meta-like-spacex-looks-to-turn-excess-ai-compute-into-cash/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/01/meta-stock-cloud-ai-compute.html)*
