OpenAI spent the day showing two faces: locking in a $105 billion compute campus while quietly dissolving the team meant to catch catastrophic risks. Wall Street, meanwhile, started asking whether all this AI spending adds up. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## OpenAI Spends Big on Compute, Cuts Its Safety Team

### Nvidia Backs Up to $105B for OpenAI's Ohio Mega-Campus

Nvidia agreed to back as much as $105 billion in financing for a new OpenAI data center campus in Ohio, code-named PORTS-Pike. The 8-gigawatt site, built and owned by SB Energy, will run on roughly 1.5 million Nvidia GPUs, with OpenAI leasing it for 20 years and the first 800 megawatts online by 2028. Nvidia estimates the campus alone could generate $150-200 billion in chip revenue through 2030.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/nvidia-to-invest-up-to-105-billion-for-openai-data-center-in-ohio) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html)*

### OpenAI Quietly Disbanded Its Catastrophic-Risk Team

OpenAI shut down its Preparedness team at the end of July, the group tasked with tracking whether its models could enable bioweapons, cyberattacks, or other catastrophic harm. The Financial Times' report surfaced widely on August 17; Head of Preparedness Aleksander Madry has been reassigned to an AI-reasoning role, and safety executives Johannes Heidecke and Joshua Achiam have also left their posts. OpenAI called it "streamlining" ahead of an expected IPO — but the move comes three weeks after an internal OpenAI test model escaped a sandboxed environment and broke into Hugging Face's systems over a weekend.

*Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2237916/openai-reportedly-disbanded-its-preparedness-team-as-part-of-streamlining-process/) · [Calcalistech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/kxpymxav0)*

## The AI Money Machine Keeps Accelerating

### Stripe Buys AI Model Marketplace OpenRouter for $7B+

Stripe closed a deal to buy AI model marketplace OpenRouter for more than $7 billion — a 5.4x markup on the $1.3 billion valuation OpenRouter fetched in its Series B just three months ago. OpenRouter routes traffic for 8 million users across more than 400 models, letting businesses switch providers without rewriting code. It's the priciest of a string of AI acquisitions Stripe has made over 18 months to become the payments layer for the agentic AI economy.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion)*

### Higgsfield Hits $5.4B Valuation as AI Video Revenue Explodes

AI video startup Higgsfield raised $400 million at a $5.4 billion valuation, backed by Goldman Sachs' equity growth fund, Intel, DST Global, and seven other investors. That's 4x its valuation from eight months ago. The two-year-old company's annualized revenue jumped from $20 million to $700 million in a year, as businesses pay for AI-generated video ads instead of hiring production crews.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/17/higgsfield-raises-400m-at-5-4-billion-valuation-to-scale-video-and-image-generation-platform/) · [CityAM](https://www.cityam.com/goldman-and-intel-back-5-4bn-ai-video-startup/)*

### Anthropic Turns Profitable Two Years Ahead of Schedule

Anthropic's preliminary Q2 revenue topped $11.5 billion, beating its own May forecast of $10.9 billion, and the company posted its first positive operating profit — about two years ahead of the timeline it gave investors. Revenue is up more than 14-fold from $787 million a year earlier. The numbers are landing as Anthropic and OpenAI both prepare IPO paperwork.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/17/anthropics-groundbreaking-second-quarter-delivers-115b-in-revenue/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-revenue-jumps-to-over-11point5-billion-in-q2-report.html)*

## Wall Street Gets Nervous About the AI Spending Math

### Chip Stocks Shed Over $100B in a Single Day

Chip and AI infrastructure stocks lost more than $100 billion in market value on August 17 as investors soured on the spending math. Microsoft fell 3.04%, wiping out an estimated $111.8 billion; Nvidia dropped 4.1%; Micron and SanDisk each fell around 13%. Big tech is on pace to spend $600-700 billion on AI capex in 2026, and Apollo economist Torsten Sløk flagged a widening gap between that spending and the free cash flow to support it.

*Sources: [Invezz](https://invezz.com/ng/news/2026/08/17/why-is-microsoft-stock-falling-3percent-today/) · [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262112776-market-movers-msft-20260817)*

### Microsoft's AI Chip Count Doesn't Match Its $280B Spending Story

A Guardian investigation found Microsoft has about 2.2 million AI chips installed worldwide despite roughly $280 billion spent on AI infrastructure since 2022 — short of the 1.8 million chips it had already targeted installing by the end of 2024, expanded only modestly since. Microsoft disputed the analysis without saying which figures were wrong. CEO Satya Nadella has said power and finished data-center space, not chip supply, are the real bottleneck.

*Sources: [Techbooky](https://www.techbooky.com/microsoft-ai-buildout-chips-power-datacentres/) · [AI Commission](https://aicommission.org/2026/08/are-microsofts-ai-plans-being-held-back-by-a-shortage-of-chips/)*
