OpenAI just called this the start of the "AGI era," Nvidia locked up the internet's biggest open-model hub, and regulators moved fast on Tesla's driverless taxis. Here's what mattered in AI over the last two days.

## OpenAI Ships GPT-6 Astra and Bets $1B on Cyber Defense

### OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra, Calls It the Start of the "AGI Era"

Astra scores 97.6% on FrontierMath Tier 4 and 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3, marks OpenAI says saturate both benchmarks. It also hits 100% on ExploitBench, the company's test for turning known bugs into working exploits. The model costs $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output — 2.5x GPT-5.6 Sol's price — and rolls out first to select organizations before reaching ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) · [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gpt-6-astra)*

### OpenAI Pledges $1B in Free AI Tools for Water Utilities and Small Banks

The new Daybreak for Frontline Defenders program gives water utilities, electric grid operators, community banks, and open-source maintainers free access to OpenAI's cyber models for about six months. It starts in the US before expanding to partner countries. The goal: let teams with no security budget use AI to review old code, find bugs, and patch them before attackers do.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/daybreak-for-frontline-defenders/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/04/openai-commits-1b-in-ai-credits-to-frontline-cyber-defenders/5294382)*

## AI Infrastructure Money Keeps Breaking Records

### Nvidia Confirms $12.9B Deal to Buy Hugging Face

Nvidia will pay about $12.9 billion for the platform that hosts 3 million AI models and serves 18 million developers, CEO Jensen Huang confirmed. The deal includes up to $1 billion in retention bonuses for Hugging Face staff who join Nvidia. Huang says Hugging Face will stay an "open platform for the entire AI ecosystem" — a promise open-source developers will be watching closely.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/nvidia-agrees-to-13-billion-deal-for-ai-platform-hugging-face) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/)*

### Crusoe Triples Its Valuation to $30B in Under a Year

The AI data center builder closed a $3 billion round co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners, with Abu Dhabi's Mubadala joining in. The jump comes 10 months after Crusoe raised at a $10 billion valuation, fueled by a new $13 billion, five-year deal to supply trading firm Jane Street with GPUs. Crusoe started in 2018 as a crypto miner burning flared natural gas.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/crusoe-reportedly-raises-3b-at-a-30b-valuation/) · [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/crusoe-raises-3b-at-30b-valuation-after-landing-13b-jane-street-deal/)*

### Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab Is Talking to Investors About a $40B Valuation

Accel is in talks to lead a $1 billion round for the startup, quadrupling its valuation from $10 billion just over a year ago. Nvidia has also discussed joining. Thinking Machines sells tools for customizing AI models, gives away its base models for free, and already pulls in a few hundred million dollars a year in revenue.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/accel-reportedly-in-talks-to-lead-1b-round-for-thinking-machines-at-40b-valuation/) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/thinking-machines-lab-seeks-40-billion-dollar-valuation)*

### Gimlet Labs Raises $300M to Mix Chips From Different Makers in One AI Job

The startup's Series B, led by Andreessen Horowitz, values it at $3 billion — just six months after an $80 million Series A. Gimlet splits AI workloads across GPUs, CPUs, and other chip types in the same data center, claiming up to 10x better throughput in the same power budget. Arm and Microsoft's M12 fund joined as new backers.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/andreessen-backed-ai-startup-gimlet-is-valued-at-3-billion-in-new-round) · [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/andreessen-backed-gimlet-labs-hits-3b-valuation-with-300m-round-as-ai-goes-multi-chip/)*

## Robotaxi Wars Turn Into a Federal Safety Fight

### Feds Open a Probe Into Tesla's Cybercab Hours After It Hit the Road

NHTSA launched an audit of nearly 1,000 Cybercabs the same day Tesla began commercial rides in Austin. The agency wants to know how Tesla certified a car with no steering wheel, brake pedal, or mirrors as meeting federal safety standards. It's the fastest a regulator has moved against a Tesla robotaxi rollout yet.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/us-auto-safety-regulator-opens-probe-into-nearly-1000-tesla-cybercabs.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/feds-launch-investigation-into-teslas-cybercab-deployment/)*

### Waymo Attacked Tesla's Sensor-Free Approach Days Before the Cybercab Launched

Waymo argued in a blog post and an Axios interview that "pure end-to-end" AI without extra sensors like lidar isn't safe enough for driverless cars — a direct shot at Tesla's camera-only Cybercab. Waymo backed it up with an IIHS study showing its rider-only cars get into 76% fewer crashes than human drivers. The company also opened service in Denver, San Diego, and Tampa this week and is closing in on a $3 billion debt deal with Pimco and Blackstone.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/waymo-goes-on-offense-ahead-of-teslas-cybercab-launch/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/waymo-taps-pimco-blackstone-for-3-billion-in-first-debt-deal)*

## Big Tech's Race to the Bottom on Transcription Pricing

### Microsoft Undercuts Everyone With 10-Cent-an-Hour Transcription

MAI-Transcribe-2 runs about 10 times faster than OpenAI's transcription model and beats Google and ElevenLabs on speed too, Microsoft says. It covers 60 languages, adds speaker diarization and word-level timestamps, and costs $0.10 per audio hour through the end of 2026 — 72% below Microsoft's own prior model. It tops the FLEURS benchmark with a 5.2% average error rate.

*Sources: [Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/mai-transcribe-2-is-the-fastest-most-accurate-and-cheapest-speech-recognition-model-in-the-world/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/microsoft-ais-mai-transcribe-2-undercuts-openai-google-and-elevenlabs-on-price-and-speed)*

### Meta's New Voice Model Tracks 20+ Speakers Talking at Once

Muse Voice Transcribe, from Meta Superintelligence Labs, transcribes speech in real time, tells speakers apart, and switches languages mid-sentence without missing a beat. It was trained on more than 70 languages, with 25 validated at launch, and costs $3 per 1,000 audio minutes — about 18 cents an hour. Meta says it beats OpenAI and Google on real-time transcription benchmarks.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meta-prices-muse-voice-transcribe-at-0-18-an-hour-with-real-time-diarization-for-20-speakers) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2249112/meta-new-ai-transcription-model-can-distinguist-between-multiple-speakers-and-languages-in-real-time/)*
