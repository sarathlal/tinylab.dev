It was a quiet weekend for launches and a loud one for consequences. The AI selloff reached emerging markets, the prompt-leak story got its first real fallout, and states told Washington they want a say in the rules.

## The AI selloff spreads past Wall Street

### Emerging-market stocks are in a bear market as the AI trade cracks
An MSCI gauge of developing-market stocks is down more than 20% from its June peak, Bloomberg reports. It's heading for its longest losing streak since 2023 and is on track to erase all of 2026's gains. Copper is caught in it too: it fell more than 9% last week on AI demand worries before bouncing 1% to $12,287 a ton on Monday.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/emerging-market-stocks-set-to-erase-2026-gains-on-ai-selloff) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/copper-rises-after-falling-more-than-9-last-week-on-ai-gloom)*

### US futures bounce after Friday's 5.3% Nasdaq drop
Nasdaq futures jumped 2.89% and S&P 500 futures rose 2.1% on Sunday, with Dow futures up nearly 700 points. That follows a Friday where the Nasdaq lost 1,261 points to close at 22,618 and all three major indexes landed in correction territory. One bounce doesn't end a correction, but it shows buyers haven't given up on the AI trade.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/22/stock-market-today-dow-futures-sp500-nasdaq-correction-ai-bust/) · [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/stocks-enter-correction-territory-ai-fears/)*

## Who gets paid, and on what terms, in AI's mega-raises

### Anthropic told its IPO bankers to stay away from OpenAI for six months
Anthropic asked senior officers at some of its 22 underwriting banks to sign papers barring core deal-team members from working with OpenAI, Cursor, DeepSeek and Hugging Face for six months after the listing, Reuters reports. It also capped each bank at four senior bankers. The banks split $550 million, or 0.676% of the deal, and got nothing on the $12.2 billion greenshoe. At least one banker refused to sign.

*Sources: [Reuters via Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261123/like-spacex-anthropic-ran-an-unusual-ipo-process-chafing-some-bankers) · [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/ai-news-markets-correction-11-22-26)*

### DeepSeek's giant round lands with 31 investors and more state money
DeepSeek's second funding round is now on the business registry, with 31 new partners joining through a holding vehicle called Hangzhou Chengyan. Reports put the round at roughly 100 billion yuan (about $15B), with Tencent and CATL as the largest backers. State-owned investors supplied about 27% of the money, up from about 2% in the first round. Founder Liang Wenfeng keeps control through a 99% stake in the vehicle's manager.

*Sources: [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/128603/chinas-state-backed-capital-piles-into-ai-champion-deepseek-as-ai-robot-ipos-face-curbs) · [Recode China AI](https://www.recodechinaai.com/p/new-chip-restrictions-deepseeks-funding)*

## AI security: leaky providers and hacking agents

### OpenRouter drops 3 inference providers and pulls 125 endpoints from its privacy tier
OpenRouter says it cut three inference providers and removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility. The move follows Dragos's canary research, which caught providers keeping and reading prompts they had promised not to store. OpenRouter hasn't named the three providers. It also dropped the ZDR label from some MiniMax endpoints after hidden test links in prompts were opened 40 to 130 hours later.

*Sources: [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/) · [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/frontier/ai/openrouter-drops-zero-data-retention-labels-from-affected-minimax-endpoints)*

### Amazon says China-linked AI agents found a zero-day and got root in 11 minutes
Amazon's threat intelligence team says a China-linked group used autonomous agents to map and exploit a path-traversal bug in MONAI Label, an open-source medical imaging tool. The agents went from probing the API to writing files on a university server in about 11 minutes, then got root. Versions 0.8.5 and earlier are exposed in the default setup, which has no login.

*Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/amazon-china-linked-hackers-ai-agents-exploited-medical-software-zero-day/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/20/china-attackers-deployed-ai-agents-that-autonomously-found-and-exploited-a-0-day-to-take-over-a-university-server/5311382)*

## States and Brussels write the next rules

### Governors ask Congress for one AI rulebook that leaves states standing
The National Governors Association released bipartisan AI principles on Sunday. Washington would handle frontier-model safety reviews and incident reporting. States would keep consumer, discrimination and jobs rules. "AI poses both opportunities and risks," said Maryland Gov. Wes Moore, who chairs the NGA. It's a direct answer to the White House push to preempt state AI laws.

*Sources: [NGA](https://www.nga.org/news/press-releases/governors-call-for-ai-action/) · [Bloomberg](https://bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-22/governors-call-for-federal-ai-rules-without-sidelining-states)*

### The EU's December 2 AI labeling deadline is nine days out
Generative AI systems already on the EU market must mark their output as AI-made under Article 50(2) by December 2. New bans on AI-generated non-consensual intimate images and child abuse material also apply that day. OpenAI's answer is textGrain, an invisible watermark for ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU. In its own test, swapping 10% of words cut detection from about 92% to 66%.

*Sources: [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/eu-ai-act-2-december-2026-obligations-article-50) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/openai-will-start-watermarking-chatgpts-text-in-the-eu/)*
