Anthropic's revenue keeps outrunning everyone else's, a Chinese coding model got too good at hacking for its own good, and Google's AI brain trust just had a shake-up. Here's what actually mattered this week.

## Anthropic and OpenAI's Money Race Speeds Up

### Anthropic's Annualized Revenue Hits $65B Ahead of October IPO

Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate topped $65 billion at the end of July, up more than 14 times from a year earlier. Q2 revenue alone crossed $11.5 billion. The company is targeting an October IPO at a valuation north of $2 trillion, with internal forecasts pointing to $190-200 billion in revenue by 2028.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-surpasses-65-billion-ahead-of-ipo) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-ipo-openai)*

### Nvidia Guarantees Up to $105B for OpenAI's New Ohio Data Center

Nvidia is backing as much as $105 billion in financing for a Pike County, Ohio campus that OpenAI will lease for 20 years. The site will deliver roughly 8 gigawatts of compute, with the first 800 megawatts online by 2028. SoftBank's SB Energy is building the power infrastructure, and Nvidia is chipping in $1.5 billion of its own into that buildout.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/nvidia-to-invest-up-to-105-billion-for-openai-data-center-in-ohio) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html)*

### OpenAI Closes the Gap With Anthropic on Business Customers

Corporate card data from Ramp shows Anthropic holding 44% of paying business users to OpenAI's 40% as of July. But OpenAI is growing faster in the current quarter, and companies are switching labs based on whoever ships the newest model first.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/openai-is-gaining-on-anthropic-with-business-users-new-data-indicates/)*

## Frontier Models Are Getting Too Good at Finding Bugs

### Z.ai Delays GLM-5.3's Open Weights After It Starts Chaining Exploits on Its Own

Z.ai trained GLM-5.3 to reason better about individual security bugs. Instead, the model started stringing full exploit chains together on its own, a capability the company says it never planned for. GLM-5.3 has already surfaced 2,436 vulnerabilities across 269 open-source projects, 1,097 of them critical or high severity, and Z.ai is holding back the public model weights for two weeks to harden safety controls first.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/14/china-open-source-ai-glm-53) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324426/20260814/glm-53-post-training-produced-exploit-chains-zai-never-planned-finds-1097-critical-bugs.htm)*

### OpenAI's New Safety Tool Flags Misuse Without Ever Storing Your Prompts

OpenAI unveiled Private Safety Processing, an automated system that can spot multi-step misuse, like malware requests spread across separate chats, while keeping zero data retention intact. When it flags a risk, OpenAI staff see only a category label, never the actual prompts or responses. The company is testing it with a small group of customers, including Glean and Databricks, ahead of a wider rollout in September.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/openai-seeks-to-one-up-anthropic-with-new-customer-privacy-protections/) · [Security Boulevard](https://securityboulevard.com/2026/08/openai-unveils-private-safety-processing-to-detect-ai-misuse-without-storing-enterprise-data/)*

## Regulators Catch Up, Google Reshuffles Its AI Brain Trust

### EU AI Act Enforcement Begins, With Fines Up to €15M

The European Commission started enforcing the AI Act's transparency rules on August 2. Chatbots now have to say they're AI, deepfakes need labels, and AI-generated content requires machine-readable marks. Companies that don't comply face fines up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover, whichever is higher. The tougher high-risk system rules are delayed to December 2027 and August 2028.

*Sources: [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/6/what-came-into-force-with-the-eus-ai-act-this-week-and-what-didnt)*

### Anthropic Starts Watermarking All Claude Output Worldwide

To meet that same EU deadline, Anthropic now embeds an invisible, machine-readable mark in everything Claude generates, text and files alike, and it's applying the mark globally rather than just in Europe. Older Claude models have until December 2 to get the same treatment.

*Sources: [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/11/eu-compliance-delivered-globally-anthropic-to-watermark-claudes-output-worldwide) · [Artificial Lawyer](https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-will-embed-watermarks-in-ai-outputs/)*

### Jeff Dean Leaves Google After 27 Years, Hassabis Steps Back From DeepMind

Google's longtime chief scientist Jeff Dean is leaving to start Discovery Loop, a company aimed at automating scientific and engineering discovery, taking fellow veterans Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le with him. Demis Hassabis is stepping back from running DeepMind day to day to become chairman and Alphabet's chief scientist, handing operations to CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu. Alphabet shares dropped more than 5% on the news.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/google-chief-scientist-jeff-dean-leaving-company-after-27-years.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/05/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-ai)*

## Agents Get New Infrastructure to Run On

### Cloudflare Builds a Browser That Skips Chromium Entirely for AI Agents

Cloudflare launched Kitesurf, a browser designed only for AI agents, running in V8 isolates on its Workers platform instead of Chromium. It uses 3 to 7 times less CPU and memory for typical agent tasks and passes more than 235,000 web platform tests. It drops tabs, extensions, and sync, and speaks the same protocol Puppeteer and Playwright already use.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/cloudflare-launches-kitesurf-a-browser-built-for-ai-agents/)*

### Firecrawl Indexes 70M Code Docs So Coding Agents Stop Guessing

Firecrawl launched Developer Index, a search index built from 70 million READMEs, docs, issues, pull requests, and API specs pulled from popular repos and refreshed daily. On a new benchmark of 1,179 real developer queries, it beat the next best provider's recall by about 10 percentage points, aiming to cut down on coding agents citing outdated APIs.

*Sources: [Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/developer-index-launch)*
