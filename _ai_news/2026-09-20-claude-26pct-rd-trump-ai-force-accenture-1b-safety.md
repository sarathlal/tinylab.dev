AI is now doing a chunk of the work of building more AI, Washington wants to give the whole field a new name, and the infrastructure money hasn't slowed down at all. Here's what mattered in the last day.

## Anthropic Puts Claude in Charge of Building Claude

### Claude Now Leads 26% of the Work That Builds the Next Claude

Anthropic said Claude leads about 26% of its own model research and development as of August, up from close to zero in February. That means Claude can take a high-level prompt and carry a task through to the end largely on its own, while a human still watches. About 90% of Anthropic's R&D now involves Claude as a collaborator or better, and the company runs roughly 30,000 agents at once doing research and engineering work, with monitors blocking about one action in every 47,000.

*Sources: [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6098297-anthropic-claude-leads-ai-development/) · [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/anthropic-model-claude-helping-build-version-136547096)*

### Anthropic and Accenture Will Each Spend $1B Watching Anthropic's Models

Accenture is building a team of "embedded evaluators" who'll sit inside Anthropic with employee-level access, watching models train and talking directly to staff instead of auditing from the outside. Anthropic and Accenture each plan to spend at least $1 billion over five years on the effort, run through Accenture's Faculty AI unit. It's the first outside group to get this kind of access, delivering on Dario Amodei's recent call for independent monitoring of frontier AI labs.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/anthropics-first-embedded-evaluator-is-accenture/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/anthropic-accenture-ai-safety.html)*

## AI Agents Move Into Law, Home, and Code

### OpenAI Built a Legal Researcher That Searches 230 Million Case-Law URLs

OpenAI launched Astra for Law, a version of GPT-6 Astra tuned for legal work with its own search index covering more than 230 million URLs of US case law, statutes, and regulations. The index draws partly on the nonprofit Free Law Project's CourtListener database, covering over 99.9% of published US precedential case law. On OpenAI's own benchmark, Astra for Law answered correctly 54% of the time, versus 38.7% for the base model using plain web search.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/) · [ABA Journal](https://www.abajournal.com/news/article/openai-introduces-new-ai-model-for-law-firms)*

### Google Turns Its CC Assistant Into a Family's Shared Command Center

Google's CC agent, once a solo productivity tool, now supports up to six people in one household. Every morning it emails the whole family a "Your Day Ahead" brief listing who needs to be where and what still needs doing, and it can fill out permission slips, build shopping lists, and plan meals on its own. CC runs on Gemini and Google's Antigravity agent harness on its own isolated cloud computer, and for now it's limited to US users 18 and up with personal Gmail accounts.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/googles-new-cc-is-an-ai-agent-that-helps-families-run-their-households/) · [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/cool-tech/googles-new-ai-agent-wants-to-take-the-stress-out-of-running-a-family/)*

### Claude Code Now Splits Itself Into Parallel Coding Agents That Keep Running After You Log Off

Anthropic's redesigned Claude Code Projects lets one conversation break a goal into several threads, each a full cloud coding session running on its own repo branch. The threads work in parallel, pass context to each other, and keep going after you close your laptop, with every worker running on Opus at high effort by default. It's in beta for select Pro and Max subscribers, capped at 200 new threads a day per project.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/18/claude-code-revamps-projects-so-you-can-work-and-pay-in-parallel/5297532) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/17/anthropic-launches-claude-code-projects-in-beta-parallel-cloud-sessions-that-keep-running-after-you-close-your-laptop/)*

## Washington and Nvidia Wave Off the Calls to Slow Down

### Trump Wants to Rename "AI" and Is Standing Up an "AI Force"

Trump posted a poll on Truth Social asking followers to pick a replacement for the term "Artificial Intelligence," which he called "inaccurate, and very ineloquent." "Superior Intelligence" led with 42% of roughly 30,000 votes cast within the first hour. He also announced he's forming an "AI Force," modeled on the Space Force he created in his first term, and will soon name an AI "czar," joking that "only high I.Q. individuals need apply." The Washington Post reports he's rejecting industry calls for more constraints on AI development at the same time.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/19/trump-form-ai-force-name-ai-czar-rejects-calls-constraints/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/19/trump-suggests-rebranding-ai-with-a-new-name-says-hes-also-creating-an-ai-force/)*

### Nvidia's Huang Predicts Chip Sales Will Double Next Year, Pushes Back on Slowdown Calls

Speaking at a summit with King Charles III in Scotland, Jensen Huang said, "I expect Nvidia to sell twice as many chips next year as we do this year," counting units sold, not revenue. On AI safety, he framed it as an engineering problem, not a political one: "When a product is not safe, we should hold it back and keep engineering it... you should go as fast as you can, but no faster than that." The comments landed the same week Anthropic and OpenAI leaders were publicly calling for the industry to pace itself.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/nvidia-huang-ai-chip-guidance.html) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/fox-news-tech/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-ai-chip-sales-growth)*

## The Infrastructure Money and Power Keep Flowing

### Crusoe Raises $3.9B to Build "AI Factories," Hits $30.9B Valuation

Crusoe closed a $3.9 billion Series F co-led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital, and Valor Equity Partners, with Nvidia, GIC, and Qatar's sovereign fund also participating. The new $30.9 billion valuation funds both large, vertically-integrated data center campuses and smaller modular "Crusoe Spark" units the company can truck to spare power. Redwood Materials CEO JB Straubel and Cloudflare CFO Thomas Seifert are joining Crusoe's board.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/crusoe-raises-3-9b-to-build-massive-data-centers-and-small-modular-ai-factories/) · [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/17/3364326/0/en/crusoe-raises-3-9-billion-series-f-for-its-vertically-integrated-ai-infrastructure-platform.html)*

### Nvidia, Google, and Emerald AI Team Up to Make Data Centers Play Nice With the Grid

The three launched the AI Energy Management Alliance to get AI data centers connected to power grids faster, in exchange for the centers cutting usage during peak demand. Nvidia says US power infrastructure was built for "flat, static electricity demand," not computing loads that can flex up and down. The group wants a shared standard for data centers that can shift workloads and draw on storage instead of forcing costly, taxpayer-funded grid upgrades.

*Sources: [TechBriefly](https://techbriefly.com/2026/09/18/nvidia-and-google-push-new-power-deal-for-ai-data-centers/)*
