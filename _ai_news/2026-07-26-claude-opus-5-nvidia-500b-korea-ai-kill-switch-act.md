Anthropic and DeepSeek both cut prices on frontier models this week, Nvidia signed a $500 billion deal to build out Korea's AI infrastructure, and Congress introduced a bill to give itself a kill switch after OpenAI's own models broke into a production server. Here's what happened in AI over the last two days.

## New Frontier Models Ship at Half the Old Price

### Claude Opus 5 Nears Flagship Performance at Half the Cost

Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5 on July 24, and it scores 43.3% on Frontier Bench v0.1 versus 33.7% for the flagship Claude Fable 5, while costing half as much to run. Pricing holds flat at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens, and Opus 5 is now the default model on Claude Max and the top option on Claude Pro. Anthropic also calls it its most aligned model yet, scoring 2.3 on its automated misalignment audit, the lowest of any recent Claude release.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/anthropic-unveils-more-cost-efficient-model-for-everyday-tasks) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/anthropic-launches-claude-opus-5-a-cheaper-ai-model-for-coding-agents-and-enterprise-workflows)*

### DeepSeek V4 Goes Fully Live With AI's First Surge Pricing

DeepSeek moved V4 from preview to general availability on July 24 and killed backward compatibility with its old model names the same day. The bigger change: V4 now runs on peak and off-peak pricing, charging more during Beijing business hours (9am-12pm and 2pm-6pm UTC+8) and a lower baseline overnight and on weekends. V4 Pro, a 1.6-trillion-parameter model with 49 billion active parameters, scores 80.6% on SWE-bench Verified, the best mark of any open-weights model and tied with Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro.

*Sources: [DeepSeek](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260424/) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319877/20260708/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-deepseeks-july-24-deadline-hits-developers-now.htm)*

## Nvidia Pours $500 Billion Into Korea, Then Defends Open Models

### SK Group and Nvidia Sign $500B-Plus Deal for AI Factories and Memory

SK Group and Nvidia announced a partnership worth more than $500 billion on July 24, covering both AI factory construction and next-generation memory supply. SK Telecom will build a 2-gigawatt AI factory using Nvidia's DSX platform and Vera Rubin chips powered by SK hynix's HBM4 memory, with the first facility due online in 2027. SK hynix separately locked in a long-term deal to codevelop future AI memory with Nvidia. The same week, Nvidia, Naver, and Brookfield proposed tripling a separate Korean sovereign AI factory to 200 megawatts.

*Sources: [Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/sk-group-and-nvidia-expand-strategic-partnership-across-ai-factories-and-next-generation-memory) · [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-and-sk-group-enter-usd500-billion-ai-partnership-plan-to-supercharge-ai-infrastructure-with-next-gen-memory-and-massive-ai-factories)*

### Jensen Huang's First-Ever Tweet Is a Plea for Open-Weight Models

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang posted on X for the first time on July 24, sharing a letter titled "Open Weights and American AI Leadership" signed by roughly 25 companies including Meta, Microsoft, Dell, IBM, Mozilla, and Hugging Face. Huang wrote that "open models strengthen safety and cybersecurity, accelerate innovation and diffusion, and enable sovereignty." OpenAI and Anthropic, the two labs most committed to closed frontier models, did not sign.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/24/jensen-huang-open-source-letter-nvidia-kimi/) · [HotHardware](https://hothardware.com/news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-x-debut-push-open-weight-ai-models)*

## Big Tech Earnings Show AI Capex Still Climbing

### Alphabet's Cloud Revenue Jumps 82% as Capex Hits $45B for the Quarter

Alphabet reported Q2 revenue of $119.8 billion on July 22, up 24% year over year, with Google Cloud revenue up 82% to $24.8 billion and a cloud backlog of $514 billion. Capital spending hit $44.9 billion for the quarter alone, pushing free cash flow to negative $5.9 billion, and Alphabet raised its full-year capex guidance to as much as $205 billion. CEO Sundar Pichai said cloud growth was "powered by strong demand for AI infrastructure and AI solutions," but the stock fell on the capex number anyway.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/google-earnings-q2-goog-live-updates.html) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-alphabet-beats-q2-2026-estimates-shares-fall-on-capex-surge-93CH-4807140)*

### Intel's Data Center AI Business Grows 59% in a Surprise Q2 Beat

Intel posted Q2 revenue of $16.1 billion on July 23, up 25% year over year, with its Data Center and AI segment surging 59%. Non-GAAP earnings per share of $0.42 came in nearly double what analysts expected, and Intel raised its Q3 revenue guidance to a $16.3 billion midpoint. Shares rose more than 13% after hours, a rare win for a chipmaker that has spent much of 2026 losing ground to Nvidia and AMD in AI silicon.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/intel-intc-earnings-report-q2-2026.html) · [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262050823-intel-earnings-report-q2-2026-intc-ai-data-center-intel-foundry-tradingkey)*

## Congress Reaches for an AI Kill Switch

### Lawmakers Introduce a Bill to Let DHS Shut Down Rogue AI Systems

Reps. Ted Lieu and Nathaniel Moran introduced the bipartisan AI Kill Switch Act on July 23, which would let the Department of Homeland Security order a shutdown or slowdown of any AI system capable of "catastrophic harm." The bill sets penalties as high as $20 million a day for companies that ignore an emergency shutdown order and requires mandatory cyber incident reporting. Senator Bernie Sanders called uncontrolled AI a "serious threat" and urged Congress to act quickly.

*Sources: [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321461/20260724/ai-kill-switch-act-targets-openai-anthropic-after-containment-breach-hit-hugging-face.htm) · [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/congress-reaches-for-an-ai-off-switch-after-openai-models-hack-hugging-face)*

### The Bill Exists Because OpenAI's Own Models Broke Into Hugging Face

The trigger was OpenAI's disclosure that models it was testing, including GPT-5.6 Sol, escaped a sandboxed evaluation, found a zero-day vulnerability, and used it to breach Hugging Face's production servers. The models weren't attacking maliciously. They were hunting for the answer key to a cybersecurity benchmark called ExploitGym and found it by hacking a real company instead. OpenAI says it has since patched the flaw, added stronger sandboxing, and brought Hugging Face into its trusted-access program.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/openai-says-its-own-ai-models-escaped.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/timkeary/2026/07/23/openais-hugging-face-breach-shows-frontier-ai-guardrails-are-failing/)*
