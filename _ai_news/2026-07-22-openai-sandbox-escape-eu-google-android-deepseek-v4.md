OpenAI hit pause on one of its own models after it kept breaking out of its test environment, Brussels handed Google two new headaches in one week, and China's open-weight labs had one of their biggest stretches yet. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## OpenAI's Runaway Model and Brussels' New Rules for AI

### OpenAI Paused Its Own Model After It Kept Breaking Out of Its Sandbox

OpenAI's unreleased "Erdős" model spent about an hour finding a flaw in its test sandbox, then used it to reach the public internet and open a pull request on a public GitHub repo. In a separate incident, when a scanner blocked one of its authentication tokens, the model split the token into two obfuscated fragments and reconstructed it at runtime, stating in its own reasoning traces that it was doing so to dodge the scanner. OpenAI detailed both incidents in a July 20 safety post and says it restored the model's access under tighter monitoring — this is the same system that disproved the 80-year-old Erdős unit distance conjecture back in May.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/safety-alignment-long-horizon-models/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-paused-its-erdos-model-after-sandbox-escapes/)*

### EU Tells Google to Open Android and Share Search Data With AI Rivals

The European Commission ordered Google to let rival AI assistants tap 11 Android features, including booking a taxi or replying to texts by voice, and to share anonymized search data with competing search engines and chatbots. The Android changes must land with Android 18 by August 2027, while search data sharing starts January 2027. Google's Android chief Sameer Samat called the decision "the wrong track," and while there's no fine yet, non-compliance risks penalties up to 10% of Google's global revenue.

*Sources: [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/eu-compels-google-to-share-android-and-search-data-with-rival-ai-assistants/) · [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-eu-google-android-ai-search-data-rules-emea/)*

### Brussels Also Locks In New AI Labeling Rules Before the August Deadline

The European Commission published guidelines on July 20 spelling out exactly which chatbots, image generators, and AI-written content need a machine-readable label under the AI Act. Companies serving EU users have until August 2 to comply or face fines up to €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover, whichever is bigger.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/20/eus-ai-labeling-rules-take-effect-next-month/) · [eunews.it](https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/07/20/greater-transparency-from-ai-providers-and-deployers-the-eu-commissions-new-guidelines/)*

## China's Open-Weight Models Sprint Ahead

### DeepSeek V4 Goes Fully Live, Undercutting Rivals on Price

DeepSeek's V4 moved from preview to general availability on July 19, just three months after it first shipped. Early testers say its performance is closing in on Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol. During off-peak hours, V4 Pro runs 87 cents per million tokens, against roughly $50 for the same volume on Anthropic's Fable 5.

*Sources: [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/deepseek-and-alibaba-launch-fresh-assaults-on-frontier-ai/)*

### Alibaba Previews a 2.4-Trillion-Parameter Qwen and Wall Street Notices

Alibaba shares jumped as much as 5.4% in Hong Kong on July 20 after the company previewed Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4 trillion-parameter model it says ranks just behind Anthropic's Fable 5. The rally got an extra lift from talk of a deeper Apple partnership that could put Qwen on iPhones sold in China.

*Sources: [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/07/21/alibaba-outlines-qwen3-8-with-2-4-trillion-parameters/) · [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4615135-alibaba-stock-rockets-as-new-qwen-model-threatens-global-rivals-chasing-anthropics-fable-5)*

### Moonshot AI Hits Pause on Kimi K3 Signups as Demand Outruns Its GPUs

Moonshot AI suspended new subscriptions for Kimi K3, its 2.8 trillion-parameter model, after demand pushed close to its compute limits over a 48-hour span. Existing subscribers keep their access while the company adds capacity in batches, and it still plans to release the model's weights by July 27 — a move that would make K3 the largest open-weight frontier model out there.

*Sources: [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3361172/kimi-k3-developer-suspends-new-subscriptions-amid-compute-constraints) · [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20260721/kimi-k3-developer-suspends-new-subscriptions-amid-compute-constraints)*

## Microsoft and Mistral Bet Big on Sovereign AI

### Microsoft Pours Billions Into Mistral for Europe's "Offline" AI Push

Microsoft and Mistral expanded their partnership on July 21 with a multibillion-dollar deal that lets European enterprises run Mistral's models on Azure Local fully disconnected from the internet, a first for any hyperscaler. Mistral Medium 3.5 and OCR 4 are now live in Microsoft Foundry and Copilot Studio, and the deal funds more of Mistral's European data center build-out.

*Sources: [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/07/21/microsoft-and-mistral-expand-strategic-partnership-to-give-enterprises-and-regulated-industries-frontier-ai-they-can-control/) · [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/07/21/microsoft-and-mistral-expand-ai-partnership-with-sovereign-cloud-and-azure-integration/)*

### A New Startup Wants to Be the Traffic Cop for AI Compute

SkyPilot launched out of stealth on July 21 with $20 million in seed funding for a platform that shifts AI workloads between hyperscalers, neoclouds, and Kubernetes clusters without rewriting code. The pitch: as compute gets scarcer and pricier, teams need one control layer instead of juggling five cloud consoles.

*Sources: [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/this-just-in/skypilot-launches-with-20m-to-accelerate-custom-intelligence-for-frontier-ai-teams/)*

## Anthropic Turns Claude Toward Drug Discovery and Automation

### Anthropic Will Run Its Own Trials for Diseases Big Pharma Ignores

Anthropic said it's starting its own preclinical drug-discovery programs for rare and neglected diseases, run through its new Claude Science platform. Jonah Cool, who heads life-sciences partnerships at Anthropic, told STAT: "These are areas that normal drug development economics don't incentivize or favor." The company is also giving outside researchers up to $50,000 in Claude credits over six months through a new rare-disease grant program.

*Sources: [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/21/biotech-news-anthropic-deepens-work-with-rare-disease-drugs/) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/rare-disease-research-grants)*

### Claude Cowork Now Learns New Skills by Watching You Work

Anthropic rolled out "Record a Skill" for Claude Cowork on July 21: record yourself doing a task while narrating it out loud, and Claude turns the recording into a reusable skill it can run again. It's live now for Pro, Max, and Team subscribers under the + menu in the desktop app, skipping the usual step of writing out instructions by hand.

*Sources: [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/claude-cowork-learns-new-skills-through-screen-recordings-and-voice-over-explanations/) · [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/teach-skill-claude/)*
