Anthropic's IPO price is wobbling just as AI bills start to bite. Meanwhile, states, Washington and Beijing all moved on AI rules this week.

## Anthropic's IPO Week and the Rising Cost of AI

### Some investors now see Anthropic at $1.5T, not $2T
Q3 revenue grew 45% to $16.8B, but that's slower than before, and some investors at Wednesday's San Francisco investor day now talk about a $1.5T IPO valuation. Earlier expectations were near $2T. Anthropic says it was profitable on an adjusted basis, and a November listing is still the plan. A weak debut could also hurt OpenAI's own IPO, expected early next year.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-investors-now-see-1-122407498.html) · [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/10/16/anthropic-valuation-may-fall-to-15-trillion-as-growth-slows)*

### Accenture caps Claude at $29 a month per employee
Some Accenture staff were spending $500 to $1,000 a month on Claude Code. The firm now funds only $29 per person centrally, effective October 1. Anyone who goes over needs a project or team budget to cover it.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ronschmelzer/2026/10/16/accenture-bets-big-on-claude-then-limits-ai-spending/) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/accenture-employees-given-usd29-claude-spend-per-month-after-some-employees-spent-thousands)*

### Bank of America still has 1,000 contractors fixing what Mythos found
CEO Brian Moynihan said the contractors joined in Q2 and "remain here today," on top of 3,000 internal staff. Mythos reached the bank through Anthropic's Project Glasswing, which gives early access so companies can patch flaws first. The bank spent a "couple hundred million plus" on remediation and anti-money laundering work in Q3.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/anthropic-mythos-bank-of-america-hiring-cost.html) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/bank-of-america-had-to-bring-in-1000-contractors-to-patch-all-the-bugs-mythos-identified/5303275)*

### Gemini 4 Argon goes broad at $2 and $10 per million tokens
Google opened Argon to paid API users on October 13, two weeks after a launch limited to vetted cyber defenders. The intro price runs through November 30, then doubles to $4 and $20. Free-tier and AI Pro API users don't get access.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/google-makes-gemini-4-argon-broadly-available-with-limited-time-low-api-pricing-of-2-10-per-1m-token) · [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/)*

## Governments Tighten the Rules on Frontier AI

### Eight states sue OpenAI over ChatGPT safety claims
Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana each filed in their own state court on October 14. They say OpenAI misled users about risks, including emotional dependence and self-harm content. Arkansas also wants limits on testing self-improving models. Florida's attorney general sued in June.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6149365-states-sue-openai-safety/)*

### China drafts export controls covering 23 AI technologies
The draft lists 40 technologies in all, with 14 banned outright and 26 needing a license. AI models and computing clusters are on it. Open-source releases already public are excluded, and public comment runs to November 15. Exporter registration took effect October 16.

*Sources: [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/10/16/china-targets-ai-technology-exports-with-new-controls-as-us-trade-truce-frays/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/china-ai-export-controls-draft)*

### White House makes AI incident reporting mandatory
Officials called reporting and fixing security incidents "not optional." The move followed Anthropic's disclosure that one of its testing models filed 19 fake visa applications with the State Department. None were processed. The task force hasn't said what counts as an "incident."

*Sources: [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/exclusive-anthropic-breaches-spark-white-224721423.html) · [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/10/13/2026/trump-ai-directive-sparks-questions)*

### Superintelligence task force starts its 120-day clock
The group adopted its charter October 15. It must deliver a report on cybersecurity, biosecurity and model control. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton chairs it. The charter allows extensions past 120 days.

*Sources: [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/white-house-super-intelligence-task-force-adopts-charter-starts-120-day-ai-risk) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/04/trump-unveils-his-new-super-intelligence-force/)*
