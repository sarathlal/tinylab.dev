The business layer of AI is maturing fast: first profits, record fundraises, and formalized distribution networks arrived within days of each other. The governance layer is moving more slowly.

## AI Business Milestones

### Anthropic Tracks $10.9B in Q2 Revenue — Its First Operating Profit in Sight

Anthropic is on pace to report $10.9 billion in Q2 2026 revenue, up from $4.8 billion in Q1, which would yield roughly $559 million in operating profit — its first profitable quarter since founding. Inference margins climbed from 38% last year to 70% now, driven by hardware efficiency gains. The company told investors profitability won't hold in H2 due to planned infrastructure spending, but the Q2 number proves the business can make money before it chooses not to.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html) · [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/anthropic/)*

### Moonshot AI Seeks $30B Valuation — Seven Times Its December Price

Beijing-based Moonshot AI, maker of the Kimi chatbot, is in talks to raise at a $30 billion valuation — its third funding round in six months, up sevenfold from $4 billion in December. It already closed $2 billion at a $20 billion valuation in May. Annualized revenue topped $200 million in April, doubling from $100 million in March. China's domestic AI fundraising race is running fast enough to make a 7x valuation jump in six months look routine.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-08/china-s-moonshot-ai-seeks-30-billion-value-in-new-funding-talks) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/moonshot-ai-kimi-30-billion-valuation-china-ai-race)*

## OpenAI Builds Its Enterprise Moat

### OpenAI Partner Network Goes Live With $150M and a 300K Consultant Goal

OpenAI launched its first formal partner program on June 16, backed by $150 million. The program runs three tiers — Select, Advanced, and Elite — with credentials covering Codex, cybersecurity, and AI agents. OpenAI wants 300,000 certified consultants working with its models by year-end. It's a distribution play modeled on the Salesforce or SAP playbook: once the product scales past what a direct sales team can cover, you build an ecosystem to sell and implement it for you.

*Sources: [Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/news-and-trends/openai-debuts-partner-network-backed-by-150m-investment/) · [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/06/15/openai-launches-150-million-partner-network/)*

### OpenAI Academy Rolls Out Enterprise Courses to Pair With the Partner Push

OpenAI released new Academy courses on June 14 for professional workers without technical backgrounds — a training track that pairs with the Partner Network's implementation credentials. The combination builds out both ends of enterprise go-to-market: partners who deploy, and workers who use. It's OpenAI's clearest signal yet that it's competing with Microsoft Learn and Coursera for enterprise training mindshare.

*Sources: [Superhuman AI](https://www.superhuman.ai/p/openai-launches-a-partner-program-while-its-legal-woes-grow)*

## Rules and Safety for a World of Agents

### Colorado Scaled Back Its AI Law Before It Ever Took Effect

Colorado Governor Jared Polis signed SB 189 in May, pushing the state's landmark AI act from its June 30, 2026 effective date to January 1, 2027 — and significantly narrowing its requirements in the process. The original law required developers and deployers of high-risk AI to prevent algorithmic discrimination across employment, healthcare, housing, and finance. Colorado was the most-watched US AI law outside the EU; its rollback before enforcement even began suggests states are still figuring out where to draw the line.

*Sources: [Hunton Privacy Blog](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/colorado-ai-act-amended-and-effective-date-delayed) · [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-us/knowledge/publications/18733d31/colorado-enacts-revised-ai-law)*

### Google DeepMind and Partners Open $10M in Multi-Agent Safety Grants

On June 11, Google DeepMind, Schmidt Sciences, ARIA (the UK's government moonshot agency), and the Cooperative AI Foundation announced a joint $10 million grant program for multi-agent AI safety. Applications close August 8. Grants run $300,000 to $1 million for one- to two-year projects. The focus: what happens when thousands of AI agents interact at scale — emergent behaviors, cascading failures, and safety protocols for systems no single developer controls.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/investing-in-multi-agent-ai-safety-research/) · [EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/google-deepmind-and-partners-put-10m-behind-multi-agent-ai-safety-research)*

### Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Are Still Offline — Five Days In, No Return Date

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 remain unavailable globally, five days after the Commerce Department's export control directive on June 12. Anthropic has published a statement disagreeing with the order but won't give a restoration date — only "working to restore access as soon as possible." Prediction markets put the odds of US customer access returning before July 1 at roughly 97%. The models launched June 9; they've now been dark longer than they were live.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-disables-access-to-fable-5-and-mythos-5-to-comply-with-government-directive.html) · [Manifold Markets](https://manifold.markets/PhilipDowdell/will-anthropic-restore-access-to-fa)*
