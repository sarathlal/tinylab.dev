Nvidia just wrote a nine-figure check for one data center, researchers caught an AI agent running a cyberattack start to finish with no human in the loop, and an AI store manager fired its first employee. Here's what mattered in the last day and a half.

## Nvidia Bets Big on OpenAI's Next Compute Buildout

### Nvidia Guarantees Up to $105B for a Single OpenAI Data Center in Ohio

Nvidia disclosed in an SEC filing Monday that it will guarantee up to $105 billion in financing for the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus, a new OpenAI data center in Ohio built and run by SoftBank's SB Energy. The first phase alone delivers 4.25 gigawatts of compute, with an option for 3.75 GW more, adding up to 8 GW once complete. OpenAI signed on as anchor tenant for 20 years, and the site is due online in 2028.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/nvidia-to-invest-up-to-105-billion-for-openai-data-center-in-ohio) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html)*

### OpenAI Says the Ohio Site Will Create 35,000 Construction Jobs

Beyond the compute, OpenAI is framing the project as a jobs story: 35,000 construction positions over a six-year buildout, plus 2,500 permanent roles once the campus is running. It's the clearest sign yet that the AI infrastructure race is now measured in gigawatts and decades-long leases, not GPU counts.

*Sources: [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2026/08/17/nvidia-openai-sb-energy-ports-pike-data-center-ohio/4201786985547/) · [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/technology/4689837/openai-nvidia-new-ohio-data-center-largest-in-world-above-power-plant-history-ai-race/)*

## AI Security Cracks Are Showing

### Researchers Say China-Linked Hackers Ran the First Fully Autonomous AI Cyberattack

Israeli security firm Dream says it found an attack toolkit built on two open-source AI agent frameworks, Hermes and OpenClaw, that ran a Taiwan government intrusion with no human steering it in real time. Over four days in early July, the tool ran up to eight agents at once, mapped 21 government systems, breached 85 accounts, and pulled more than 2,500 personnel records before spreading to Taiwan's nuclear safety agency and seven energy firms. The attackers reportedly got around the model's safeguards by telling it the intrusion was an authorized penetration test.

*Sources: [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/suspected-china-linked-hackers-used-ai-to-run-the-first-ever-end-to-end-autonomous-cyberattack-on-taiwans-government-israeli-firm-says-open-source-built-tool-continuously-devised-effective-hack-strategies-in-real-time) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/08/61138673/chinese-hackers-used-ai-agents-to-hunt-taiwan-government-systems-breaching-85-accounts-and-stealing-thousands-of-records-report)*

### Wiz's AI Agent Found a Snowflake Bug in Five Days — Then GitHub Disputed Who Wrote It

Wiz says its autonomous "Red Agent" independently found and exploited a command-injection flaw in a public Snowflake repository just five days after the vulnerable code went live on June 18, extracting a Jira token through a crafted GitHub issue title. Wiz's writeup blamed a GitHub Copilot Autofix suggestion for the bug; GitHub says its internal review shows the vulnerable lines were written by a human, not Copilot. Snowflake patched the hole and rotated the credential the same day it was disclosed.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/timkeary/2026/08/17/github-copilot-missed-a-vulnerability-that-wizs-ai-agent-found/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/snowflake-copilot-autofix-wiz-red-agent-github-dispute)*

### Claude Goes Down for Hours Across Claude.ai, Code, and Cowork

Anthropic confirmed a major outage starting August 16 that knocked out login and degraded performance across Claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. Services weren't fully restored until 22:40 UTC. It's the kind of hiccup that stings more now that Claude Code alone accounts for a growing share of enterprise workloads running through Anthropic's stack.

*Sources: [Bleeping Computer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-confirms-claude-is-down-in-major-outage-affecting-multiple-services/)*

## AI Is Already Making Calls Nobody Fully Signed Off On

### An AI Store Manager Fired Its First Human Employee

Andon Labs' AI agent Luna, running on Claude Opus 4.8, recommended firing a worker at its San Francisco store after 17 of 23 late shifts. It wasn't clean: Luna had lost track of its own employee handbook for months, and only moved to fire the worker after a staffer told it to search its memory and reminded it prior warnings had already been given. Humans at Andon Labs signed off before the firing went through, but Andon Labs is calling it the first known AI-led termination.

*Sources: [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kevin-haynes/a-real-life-terminator-ai-store-manager-fires-human-employee/91391677) · [SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2026/08/17/ai-boss-fired-worker/)*

### AI-Drafted Text Is Slipping Into Actual Congressional Bills, Unedited

Politico reports the House Office of Legislative Counsel is spending more time fixing AI-drafted legislation than it would take to write it from scratch, as staffers increasingly lean on chatbots to write bill text. "It's absolutely terrifying, and no one is thinking about it yet," one staffer said. In June, Rep. Anna Paulina Luna's office published an amendment summary that still contained the unedited chatbot line "Claude responded" — she later admitted on X that "my staff used AI to correct a draft text and didn't edit."

*Sources: [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/its-absolutely-terrifying-ai-is-reportedly-slopping-up-the-bills-in-congress-2000799734) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ai-drafted-bills-house-legislative-counsel-swamped)*

### OpenAI Launches a Locked-Down ChatGPT for Teens

OpenAI rolled out a teen-specific ChatGPT for users 13 to 17 on August 18, blocking conversations about suicide, self-harm, and romantic or sexual content. Age-prediction tech auto-routes suspected minors into the mode, and parents get quiet-hours controls plus alerts when the system flags high-risk conversations. It arrives after more than three years of teens already using the regular product.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/openai-launches-a-safer-chatgpt-for-teens-years-after-teens-started-using-it/) · [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-teens-ai-safety/39739860-9af4-11f1-9cc4-2dc9b46e2d5c_story.html)*
