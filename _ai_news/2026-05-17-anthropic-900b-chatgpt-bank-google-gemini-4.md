Two labs may each raise $30 billion within months of their last rounds, and neither thinks it's enough compute. Meanwhile, Google I/O opens Tuesday — and the pre-show leaks point to Gemini 4 as the main act.

## The Funding Arms Race Hits a New Gear

### Anthropic in Talks for Another $30 Billion — at a $900 Billion Valuation

Anthropic is in early talks to raise at least $30 billion at a pre-money valuation above $900 billion. That's nearly a trillion-dollar company — and it would triple Anthropic's worth in three months. The company closed a $30 billion Series G at a $380 billion valuation in February. Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia, and Altimeter are each expected to contribute at least $2 billion. No term sheet has been signed.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/anthropic-in-talks-to-raise-30-billion-at-900-billion-valuation) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/anthropic-talks-raise-30-billion-210804604.html)*

### OpenAI's CFO Says the $122 Billion Round May Not Be Enough

Six weeks after closing the largest private fundraising round ever, OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar said on May 15 the company may raise again. The culprit: "a vertical wall of demand" running into "not a lot of compute in 2026." Friar also mentioned public markets as a future financing option — OpenAI's clearest IPO signal yet. The company raised $122 billion at an $852 billion valuation just last month.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-15/openai-may-raise-more-money-as-compute-crunch-deepens-cfo-says) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-considers-raising-more-capital-meet-ai-demand/)*

### Recursive Superintelligence Raises $650 Million to Build Self-Improving AI

Richard Socher — former chief scientist at Salesforce — came out of stealth this week with $650 million at a $4.65 billion valuation. The startup is backed by Nvidia and Google Ventures, and was built with researchers from Meta AI, Google DeepMind, and OpenAI. The plan: build an AI that identifies its own weaknesses and rewrites itself to fix them. First milestone is a system with the equivalent capabilities of "50,000 doctors" to automate AI scientific research.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/what-happens-when-ai-starts-building-itself/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/recursive-superintelligence-self-improving-ai-funding)*

## OpenAI's Consumer Push: Wallets and Deployment

### ChatGPT Can Now See Your Bank Balance — $100/Month Required

OpenAI launched personal finance tools for ChatGPT Pro on May 15, letting users connect bank accounts, credit cards, and investment portfolios through Plaid. The integration covers 12,000+ institutions — Chase, Fidelity, Schwab, Robinhood, American Express, and Capital One among them. ChatGPT surfaces spending dashboards and portfolio summaries but can't move money or place trades. Intuit support is coming next, which would let it factor in tax implications of investment decisions. US only, Pro subscription ($100/month) required.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/)*

### OpenAI Spins Up a Dedicated Deployment Arm Backed by $4 Billion

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Deployment Company this month to embed AI engineering teams directly inside enterprise clients. Capgemini is among the first investors. It's a direct play for the implementation services market — the layer between buying a frontier model and actually running it in production at scale.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/) · [Capgemini](https://www.capgemini.com/news/press-releases/capgemini-strengthens-its-position-in-enterprise-ai-with-investment-in-the-openai-deployment-company/)*

## Google I/O Countdown: Gemini 4 and the Apple Deal

### Gemini 4 Is Expected to Headline Tuesday's Keynote

Google I/O opens at 10 AM PT on May 19, and nearly every pre-event report points to Gemini 4 as the flagship. Pre-event leaks suggest a 10 million-token context window, native multimodal input without a separate transcription step, and three dedicated agentic coding sessions on the conference schedule. Google hasn't confirmed the "Gemini 4" name, but internal testing on a version codenamed "Remy" — pitched as a "24/7 personal agent for work, school, and daily life" — has reportedly been underway.

*Sources: [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/) · [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/expect-google-o-2026-gemini-090000572.html)*

### Apple Is Reportedly Distilling Its Next Foundation Models from Gemini

Weekend pre-I/O coverage surfaced reports of a multi-year Apple-Google deal: Apple's next generation of Foundation Models will reportedly be built by distilling from Gemini, producing smaller, efficient models that run locally on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Apple gets frontier-level AI without building frontier compute infrastructure from scratch. If confirmed Tuesday, it's the most significant cross-company AI partnership since Microsoft backed OpenAI.

*Sources: [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/google-io-2026-preview)*

## Beyond the Lab: Vatican and Public Backlash

### Pope Leo XIV Forms a Vatican AI Study Group

Pope Leo XIV announced an official Vatican AI study group on May 16, ahead of his first encyclical. The document is expected to argue that AI must remain "subordinate to the human person" and frame the question as a labor-rights issue — a deliberate echo of his namesake's 1891 *Rerum Novarum*, which addressed workers' rights during the Industrial Revolution. The encyclical puts the Church on a direct collision course with the Trump administration's pro-acceleration AI agenda.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/14/pope-leo-xiv-ai-first-encyclical) · [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/05/16/world/pope-creates-artificial-intelligence-study-group/)*

### Gallup: 70% of Americans Oppose AI Data Centers Near Them

A new Gallup poll finds seven out of 10 Americans oppose AI data centers being built near their homes. The concerns center on energy costs, water use, and land impact. Several communities are calling for moratoriums on new construction. The same week labs are raising billions to secure more compute, the infrastructure those labs need is running into organized local resistance.

*Sources: [Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/5/13/astra_taylor_ai_data_centers_backlash)*
