Anthropic filed for an IPO and Alphabet raised $84B in the same 48 hours — the AI industry is entering its public market era at full speed.

## The AI IPO Race Accelerates

### Anthropic Submits Confidential S-1 at a $965 Billion Valuation

Anthropic filed a confidential draft S-1 with the SEC on June 1 — days after closing a $65 billion Series H that pushed its valuation to $965 billion. That puts Anthropic past OpenAI ($852B) in private market value for the first time. Annual revenue has hit $47 billion, mostly from enterprise API deals and cloud-provider partnerships.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-ipo-s1-prospectus.html) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/confidential-draft-s1-sec)*

### Alphabet Raises $84.75 Billion in the Largest Equity Offering in History

Alphabet announced an $80 billion equity raise on June 1, then upsized to $84.75 billion after Berkshire Hathaway added a $10 billion private placement. The money goes straight to AI infrastructure — data centers and chips — because enterprise demand is outrunning available supply. It's the single largest equity raise any company has ever done.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-plans-to-raise-80-billion-to-pay-for-ai-buildout/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/alphabet-to-raise-80-billion-in-equity-capital-for-ai-spending) · [Alphabet IR](https://abc.xyz/investor/news/news-details/2026/Alphabet-Announces-Proposed-80-Billion-Equity-Capital-Raise-to-Expand-AI-Infrastructure-and-Compute-2026-b0myAMewCa/default.aspx)*

## New Models for Developers and Enterprise

### Microsoft Debuts Seven In-House AI Models at Build, Led by a Reasoner That Rivals Anthropic

Microsoft unveiled seven in-house models at Build 2026 (June 2–3), led by MAI-Thinking-1 — a 35-billion active-parameter reasoning model trained from scratch with no distillation from OpenAI or Anthropic. On a blind test it matches Opus 4.6 on SWE Bench Pro. AI chief Mustafa Suleiman told the FT the company "closed an enormous gap in six months." Build also brought computer-using agents to general availability and launched a Windows Agent Store with an 85% developer revenue share.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/microsoft-unveils-new-ai-models-lessen-reliance-on-openai-lower-costs.html) · [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/news/live/microsoft-build-2026) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/06/52966889/microsofts-ai-chief-says-company-is-less-concerned-about-google-meta-and-openai-as-anthropic-battle-heats-up-weve-closed-an-enormous-gap)*

### NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra Ships Today as the Highest-Scoring US Open Model

NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra is available on Hugging Face and build.nvidia.com today as a 550-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 55 billion active parameters. It scores 48 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — the highest of any US open model ever — and delivers 300+ output tokens per second. China's Kimi K2.6 from Moonshot AI leads the same index at 54, making "best US open model" the accurate claim.

*Sources: [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-debuts-nemotron-3-family-of-open-models) · [Memeburn](https://memeburn.com/nvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-americas-best-open-ai-model-2026/) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/369689/nvidia-open-ai-model-nemotron-3-ultra)*

### Anthropic Formalizes Its Partner Program with Three Tiers and a New Hub

Anthropic added a tiered Services Track and Partner Hub to its Claude Partner Network on June 3. The three tiers — Select, Preferred, and Global Premier — rank consulting firms by certified practitioners (10 at the base, 1,000 at the top), active client deployments, and published case studies. Over 40,000 companies have applied since Anthropic backed the program with a $100 million commitment in March.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/anthropic-launches-claude-partner-network-134019200.html) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/anthropic-claude-partner-network-services-track-hub-enterprise-060326) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network)*

## AI Security and Policy

### Trump Signs Narrower AI Order: Voluntary 30-Day Review Replaces 90-Day Draft

President Trump signed an executive order on June 2 asking AI companies to voluntarily give the government up to 30 days of early access to frontier models before public release. A May draft required 90 days, but the White House cut that after industry pushback — former AI czar David Sacks was among the critics. The order explicitly bars mandatory licensing or model preclearance and directs agencies to build AI cybersecurity benchmarks and a shared vulnerability clearinghouse.

*Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/02/trump-ai-executive-order-sets-30-day-frontier-model-review/5250322) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/trump-signs-narrower-executive-order-on-ai-oversight-after-industry-objections/)*

### Anthropic Expands Project Glasswing to 150 New Partners in Critical Infrastructure

Anthropic opened its AI vulnerability detection program to 150 additional organizations in more than 15 countries on June 2, adding power, water, healthcare, and hardware sectors that weren't in the initial launch. Since launch, Glasswing partners have flagged over 10,000 high or critical security flaws using Anthropic's Mythos model. Apple, NVIDIA, Microsoft, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks are among the existing major partners.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-mythos-ai-project-glasswing.html) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/03/anthropic-project-glasswing-expansion/) · [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/anthropic-project-glasswing-expansion-critical-infrastructure-claude-mythos/)*
