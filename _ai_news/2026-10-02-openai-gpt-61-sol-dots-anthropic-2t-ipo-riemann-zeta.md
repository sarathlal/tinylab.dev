OpenAI and Google both shipped new frontier models this week. Anthropic is lining up what could be the biggest IPO ever, and its models are doing real science.

## New models and always-on agents

### OpenAI's new Dots agents keep working after you close ChatGPT
Each Dot gets its own cloud computer and browser, and connects to more than 4,000 apps. OpenAI announced them at DevDay on September 29, along with GPT-6.1 Sol. The model costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output, with a 1.05M-token context window. OpenAI says it gets near GPT-6 Astra quality at about 20% of the cost.

*Sources: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-devday-2026-dots-gpt-6-1-sol-codex-developer-tools-126093000396_1.html) · [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/10/openai-launches-gpt-6-1-sol-and-dots-its-new-always-on-ai-agents-that-work-independently/)*

### Google's Gemini 4 Argon starts with security teams, not the public
Argon can write up to 1 million tokens of output in one go, up from 64,000. Google launched it September 30 at $2 and $10 per million tokens. Access starts with trusted members of its Fairwind Program, who use it to find and fix software flaws first. Google is also working with the US government's voluntary pre-release testing program.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/google-releases-gemini-4-argon-called-its-most-powerful-model-yet/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2274263/google-gemini-4-model-argon/)*

## Anthropic's IPO and enterprise push

### Anthropic wants to raise $100B at a $2T valuation in mid-November
Bloomberg reports marketing could start the week of November 9, so shares could trade before Thanksgiving. The delay from October lets Anthropic show investors its third-quarter numbers. For scale, SpaceX raised a record $75B at a $1.8T valuation. Anthropic expects annualized revenue above $110B by year-end.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/anthropic-said-to-target-mega-ipo-before-thanksgiving-holiday) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/ipo/2026/anthropic-targets-november-ipo-revenue-surges/)*

### Barclays wants half its developers on Claude Code by year-end
The bank's Global Markets unit already runs Claude over 120,000 client emails a day. More than 16,000 UK staff use its Claude-based knowledge assistant. Barclays expects most of its software engineers on Claude Code in 2027.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/barclays-scales-claude) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/barclays-accelerates-ai-rollout-with-anthropic-claude-code/)*

## Claude does lab and math work

### An unreleased Claude lifted the Riemann zeta bound from 41.6% to 67.2%
The number is the share of zeros of the zeta function proven to satisfy the Riemann hypothesis. Claude Code ran 60 subagents and used 31 million output tokens over two sessions. It also produced a Lean proof, and mathematicians Brian Conrey and Dan Goldston reviewed the paper. Anthropic doesn't expect this approach to settle the hypothesis itself.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta)*

### Claude agents found a CRISPR-like enzyme system in phage DNA
They ran about 950 parallel searches over 21 hours and 210 million tokens. One agent flagged "a tandem repeat array" that looked CRISPR-like. Anthropic's new in-house life sciences team in the Bay Area is now testing what the enzymes do. A related program, announced the same day, loosens safeguards on Claude for verified biology researchers and watches for misuse offline instead of blocking in real time.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program)*

### Anthropic study: robots could do 74% of US physical tasks, but only 0.3% pay off
Robots are cost-competitive on just 0.3% of tasks today. At a 3% yearly price drop, reaching 10% would take about 40 years. Taxi drivers rank as the most exposed job, at 2.2 on a 3-point index.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/what-work-can-robots-do)*

## Rules and workarounds

### Connecticut's AI law took effect October 1
Generative AI providers with over 1 million monthly users must now attach tamper-resistant provenance data to content they create. Subscription AI services can't renew without proof the customer agreed. Employers must give written notice when AI shapes employment decisions. Other pieces phase in through 2027.

*Sources: [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/sb-5-preparing-connecticuts-artificial-intelligence-responsibility-and-transparency) · [Daily Campus](https://dailycampus.com/2026/09/30/new-ai-regulation-law-goes-into-effect-oct-1)*

### Oracle reportedly leased Tencent 100,000 AI chips for $7B
The five-year deal puts the chips in Southeast Asian data centers, and Tencent pays 30% upfront. US export rules block China from buying chips of this class. Renting them abroad skips the rule.

*Sources: [Tradingpedia](https://tradingpedia.com/2026/10/01/oracle-shares-advance-as-major-tencent-ai-deal-emerges) · [Blockonomi](https://blockonomi.com/tencent-tcehy-stock-massive-7b-oracle-ai-chip-agreement-explained)*
