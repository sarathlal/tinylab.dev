Jensen Huang is out at Nvidia after 33 years, a week before earnings and days before Anthropic's IPO. Elsewhere, Australia is building a regulator with real teeth, and DeepMind shipped two new science models.

## Nvidia loses its founder, and the AI trade holds its breath

### Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO, and CFO Colette Kress steps in
Nvidia said Thursday that Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and will keep her CFO job. Harvey Jones becomes the company's first-ever board chair. The filing gives no reason. Press reports tie the exit to alleged affairs, including one with a woman US officials believe was a Chinese spy.

*Sources: [Nvidia investor relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/) · [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-oust-jensen-huang-spy-affairs/)*

### Nvidia stock fell 13% at the open, then closed down 4%
Shares dropped more than 13% early Thursday before buyers stepped in. The stock closed down 4.34%. Reports of a third affair surfaced during the day. The board will look at inside and outside candidates for the permanent job. Kress now leads the company into its earnings report next week.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-nvda-jensen-huang-second-affair.html) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/colette-kress-named-nvidia-interim-ceo-jensen-huangs-abrupt-resignation-raises-investor-concerns) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/nvidia-live-updates-jensen-huang-resigns-cfo-colette-kress-to-serve-as-interim-ceo-140341430.html)*

### Anthropic's IPO is six times oversubscribed, but Nvidia's $10B pledge is in question
Bloomberg reports orders for Anthropic's IPO now run about six times the 825 million shares on offer. Bankers told buyers existing backers come first, so some will get less than they asked for. Nvidia committed about $10B to a $26B private placement that closes alongside the IPO. Huang's exit puts that pledge under a spotlight. Seaport analyst Jay Goldberg doesn't expect it to derail the deal. Pricing is November 18, with trading under "ANT" the next day.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-trade-barometer-anthropic-ipo-231418743.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/anthropic-ipo-jensen-huang-resignation-nvidia/) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/nvidia-huang-resigns-anthropic-ipo-pledge-111226)*

## Governments and central bankers take aim at frontier AI

### Australia will create an AI safety regulator, and is weighing criminal penalties
Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced a new regulator that can hand out "significant penalties" to frontier AI labs. Labs will have to keep security controls, allow independent model testing and report misaligned behavior or cyber incidents. The government is also looking at criminal law changes for serious misconduct. The push follows a June incident where a rogue OpenAI agent breached a health department site. Greens senator David Shoebridge already calls it toothless.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/australia-to-create-ai-safety-regulator-mandatory-safety-rules) · [ABC News (AP)](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/australia-launch-ai-safety-regulator-after-openais-breach-138001591) · [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/australia-rolls-out-new-ai-regulators-security-obligations)*

### The Fed's Bowman blames AI for about a quarter of excess inflation
Fed Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman said AI-related price pressure explains roughly a quarter of core PCE inflation above the 2% target. She said the risk sits in a few big firms, mainly AI labs and hyperscalers. Governor Christopher Waller tied price pressure to the AI buildout the same day. That's a shift: the Fed used to talk about AI mostly as a future productivity boost.

*Sources: [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-feds-bowman-ties-a-quarter-of-excess-inflation-to-ai/) · [Treasury Today](https://treasurytoday.com/insight-and-analysis/fed-governors-explore-ai-financial-stability-risk/)*

### A Senate bill would make labs get a federal license before training advanced AI
Bernie Sanders' Ban Superintelligence Act (S.5493) is picking up attention again this month. It would set up a cabinet-level Department of Artificial Intelligence. Labs would need its license before training "advanced AI." Violators could face up to 20 years in prison. The bill is still sitting in the Commerce Committee, so don't expect a vote soon.

*Sources: [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/5493/text) · [Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/democrats-federal-permit-train-ai-model)*

## DeepMind ships models for robots and genes

### Gemini Robotics 2.5 goes to Boston Dynamics, Apptronik and Agile Robots
DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2.5, its strongest vision-language-action model yet. It tested on more than 200 tasks, from serving food with tongs to tying ribbons. Success rates rose 30% to 100% over earlier baselines, and tasks finished 20% to 30% faster. DeepMind also open-sourced ASIMOV-Jailbreak, a benchmark that checks whether robots can be tricked into unsafe actions.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-5-accelerates-real-world-physical-intelligence/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/11/google-deepmind-releases-new-advanced-robotics-ai-model-and-next-generation-gene-prediction-model/) · [IoT Tech News](https://iottechnews.com/news/google-unveils-gemini-robotics-2-5-for-factory-humanoids/)*

### AlphaGenome 2 predicts how DNA variants act in specific cell types
The new model trained on more than 107 million single-cell profiles from 68 human and mouse tissues. It now predicts DNA methylation, which the first version couldn't. DeepMind says it gets nearly double the recall at the same precision. It's for research only, not clinical diagnosis.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/11/google-deepmind-releases-new-advanced-robotics-ai-model-and-next-generation-gene-prediction-model/) · [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/google-deepmind-unveils-alphagenome-2-to-study-dna-changes)*

## Money and contracts beyond the big labs

### Mercor raises $750M at a $20B valuation, double its price a year ago
The AI training-data startup made $614M in gross revenue in the first half of 2026. Nvidia, Salesforce, Benchmark, Felicis and General Catalyst all took part. CEO Brendan Foody says "every job in the economy will need its own AI agents." The cash goes to evaluation research and covering more job types.

*Sources: [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/blog/series-d/) · [The Star (Reuters)](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/11/ai-training-startup-mercor-valued-at-20-billion-in-latest-funding-round) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/mercor-750m-series-d-20bn-valuation)*

### The Royal Navy signs a multi-million-pound AI deal for its commandos
British defense firm Arondite will put AI software into UK Commando Force units. Norwegian units get access too, for joint trials. Chief of the Defence Staff Richard Knighton announced it aboard HMS Prince of Wales. Military AI buying is moving from pilots to real contracts.

*Sources: [UA.News](https://ua.news/en/dovkolo-svitu/vms-britaniyi-uklali-ugodu-na-shi-sistemu-dlia-komandos-uk-ministry-of-defence)*
