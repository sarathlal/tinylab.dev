The AI capital cycle hit new highs this week. Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO, Alphabet priced the largest equity raise in its history, and two AI labs opened cybersecurity models to European governments — all in five days.

## The AI IPO Race Is On

### Anthropic Files at $965B — and Expects Its First Profitable Quarter This Month

Anthropic confidentially filed an S-1 with the SEC on June 1, six weeks after closing a $65B Series H that put its valuation at $965B. The company's revenue run rate hit $47B in May, up from roughly $10B a year ago, and it's telling investors to expect a profitable June quarter. Bankers are targeting an October listing that could value Anthropic above $1 trillion at debut.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-confidentially-files-ipo-965-billion-valuation/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-ipo-s1-prospectus.html)*

### Alphabet Prices $84.75B Equity Package — Berkshire Bets $10B

Google's parent announced an $80B equity raise on June 1 and upsized it to $84.75B the next day, making it one of the largest capital raises in corporate history. Berkshire Hathaway committed $10B in a private placement at $351.81 per Class A share. All proceeds go to AI compute infrastructure.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/alphabet-to-raise-80-billion-in-equity-capital-for-ai-spending) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-plans-to-raise-80-billion-to-pay-for-ai-buildout/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-80-billion-ai-buildout)*

### OpenAI Is Also Preparing a Confidential IPO Filing

OpenAI is expected to file its own S-1 in the coming weeks, according to multiple reports. Q1 2026 saw $300B in global venture funding, with $255.5B flowing to AI alone, and both companies want to capture public market momentum while conditions hold.

*Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/6/1/ai-giant-anthropic-files-for-us-ipo-as-investors-bet-big-on-ai-future) · [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/01/nx-s1-5843199/anthropic-ipo-filing-ai-large)*

## Seven Days, Seven New Models

### Microsoft Ships 7 Proprietary MAI Models to Cut OpenAI Reliance

At Build 2026 on June 2, Microsoft launched seven in-house models under the MAI brand. MAI-Thinking-1 is a 35B-active-parameter MoE with a 256K context window — its first flagship reasoning model. MAI-Code-1-Flash runs at 5B active parameters and is built directly into GitHub Copilot. MAI-Transcribe-1.5 covers 43 languages at 5× the speed of rival models. The set signals Microsoft is building a model portfolio that doesn't depend on OpenAI.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/microsoft-unveils-new-ai-models-lessen-reliance-on-openai-lower-costs.html) · [Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/building-a-hillclimbing-machine-launching-seven-new-mai-models/)*

### Alibaba's Qwen 3.7 Max Matches Frontier Performance at Half the Price

Qwen 3.7 Max scores within striking distance of Claude Opus 4.7 on agentic benchmarks, at roughly half the input cost and a quarter of the output cost. Developers are switching, and the pricing pressure is starting to reach Western labs.

*Sources: [llm-stats.com](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)*

### Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Goes Generally Available

Gemini 3.5 Flash hit GA this week with what Google describes as frontier-level intelligence at 4× the speed of comparable models. It targets agent pipelines and coding workloads where latency is the binding constraint.

*Sources: [Google AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/google-ai-news)*

## Cybersecurity AI Moves Into Government

### OpenAI Opens GPT-5.5-Cyber to EU Defenders Under a New Action Plan

Under its EU Cyber Action Plan, OpenAI extended GPT-5.5-Cyber to vetted European cybersecurity teams, businesses, governments, and EU institutions including the EU AI Office. The model is tuned to handle vulnerability research, malware analysis, and reverse engineering more permissively than standard GPT-5.5. Starting June 1, program members must use phishing-resistant authentication.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-5-with-trusted-access-for-cyber/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317891/20260605/openai-gpt-55-cyber-reaches-eu-anthropic-mythos-opens-enisa-days-later.htm)*

### Anthropic Makes ENISA the First EU Body in Project Glasswing

On June 1, Anthropic gave the EU cybersecurity agency ENISA access to Claude Mythos through Project Glasswing, making it the first EU government institution in the program. Anthropic also expanded Glasswing to 150 new organizations this week. Both moves come as Anthropic builds government relationships ahead of its IPO.

*Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/analysis/anthropic-mythos-expansion-opens-a-new-ai-cybersecurity-market-200681377)*

## A Billion Users and a 640% Growth Gap

### ChatGPT Crosses 1 Billion Monthly Users — Fastest App in History

ChatGPT hit 1 billion monthly active users in May 2026, about three years after launch, beating TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Google Maps to the milestone. OpenAI had reported 900 million weekly active users as recently as February. Enterprise now makes up more than 40% of total OpenAI revenue.

*Sources: [U.S. News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-06-02/chatgpt-app-hits-1-billion-monthly-active-users-in-record-time-data-shows) · [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/06/03/chatgpt-reaches-1-billion-monthly-users-becoming-fastest-app-to-hit-milestone-482055/)*

### Claude Is at 56 Million Users — Growing 640% Year-Over-Year

Anthropic's Claude has 56 million monthly active users, dwarfed by ChatGPT's billion. But its 640% year-over-year growth rate is 10× ChatGPT's 62%. Anthropic's S-1 will almost certainly lead with that number.

*Sources: [Gagadget](https://gagadget.com/en/713398-chatgpt-hits-1-billion-users-but-claude-is-closing-the-gap-fast/)*
