Two announcements from Anthropic on May 28 dominated the week: a $65 billion fundraise that pushed its valuation past OpenAI's, and the same-day release of Claude Opus 4.8. Dell's blowout AI server earnings arrived the same day, while May 29 brought safety warnings from DeepMind's CEO and a burst of regulatory activity.

## Anthropic's Near-Trillion Moment

### Anthropic Raises $65B at $965B Valuation, Overtakes OpenAI

Anthropic closed a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion post-money valuation — passing OpenAI's $852 billion mark from March. The round was co-led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital, with over 20 additional investors including Blackstone, Temasek, and T. Rowe Price. Revenue run-rate hit $47 billion this month and is on pace for $50 billion by June, with Q2 potentially bringing the company's first operating profit.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-raises-65-billion-nears-1t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-ai-fundraising-openai) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h)*

### Claude Opus 4.8 Ships With Parallel Subagents and a 2.5x Speed Mode

Anthropic released Opus 4.8 on May 28, calling it a "modest but tangible improvement" on its predecessor. The main new feature is dynamic workflows in Claude Code: Opus 4.8 can spin up parallel subagents that each plan and execute part of a task simultaneously, rather than working step by step through a long refactor. It scores 88.6% on SWE-bench Verified — up from 87.6% on Opus 4.7 and well ahead of GPT-5.5's 58.6% on agentic coding — and adds an optional fast mode running at 2.5x normal speed.

*Sources: [DigitalApplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/claude-opus-4-8-release-dynamic-workflows-2026/) · [WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/opus-4-8/) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-opus-4-8-model-heres-whats-new/)*

## AI Goes to Work

### Dell's AI Server Revenue Jumps 757% Year-Over-Year

Dell's Q1 AI server revenue hit $16.1 billion — up 757% from a year ago — and total quarterly revenue came in at $43.8 billion, roughly $8 billion above analyst estimates of $35.4 billion. The stock jumped 39% in after-hours trading, its fastest sales growth since returning to public markets in 2018. Dell now targets $60 billion in AI server revenue for fiscal 2027, raised from a $50 billion forecast in February.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/dell-q1-earnings-report-2027.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/dell-boosts-outlook-to-60-billion-in-ai-server-sales-this-year) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/28/ai-server-demand-drives-staggering-revenue-growth-dell-stock-soars/)*

### OpenAI Opens GPT-Rosalind for Biodefense and Pandemic Preparedness

OpenAI launched Rosalind Biodefense on May 29, offering free sponsored access to its GPT-Rosalind life sciences model for vetted developers building public-health tools. Launch partners include Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory, using it for epidemiological modeling, early detection, and vaccine development. OpenAI covers access costs and launch support — it's the clearest move yet into national-security use cases beyond enterprise software.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/29/openai-biodefense-program) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/strengthening-societal-resilience-with-rosalind-biodefense/)*

## Safety Warnings and Policy Acceleration

### DeepMind CEO: AI Is in the "Foothills of the Singularity"

Speaking at Stanford on May 29, Demis Hassabis called AI a "species-level transition" moving about 10 times faster than the Industrial Revolution, with "little margin for error" over the next decade. He said AGI could arrive by 2029 — a year earlier than his previous estimate of 2030 — and described the current moment as being "in the foothills of the singularity." The warning carries weight because it came from the head of one of the labs driving that same progress.

*Sources: [Stanford Daily](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/05/29/deepmind-ceo-warns-about-ai/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/26/deepmind-ceo-demis-hassabis)*

### California's 30 AI Bills Clear the Crossover Deadline

Nearly all of California's 30 remaining AI bills passed their chamber of origin before Friday's crossover deadline. AB 1609, requiring chatbots to disclose they're not human when asked, cleared the full Assembly; SB 1111, which makes using a digital replica to impersonate someone count as fraud, passed the Senate 36-0. Legislators have until July 2 summer adjournment to get bills to the governor's desk.

*Sources: [Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/ai-legislative-update-may29-2026)*

### OpenAI Publishes Its Frontier Governance Framework

OpenAI released the Frontier Governance Framework on May 29, a document mapping its safety practices to California's Transparency in Frontier AI Act and the EU AI Act's Code of Practice. It covers four threat categories — cyber offense, CBRN risks, harmful manipulation, and loss-of-control scenarios — each with tiered risk levels and mitigation requirements. It's partly a compliance exercise, but publishing explicit CBRN risk tiers in a public document is unusually direct.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-frontier-governance-framework/) · [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/scaling-safe-enterprise-ai-openai-governance-frameworks/)*
