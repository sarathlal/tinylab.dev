Two of the three most powerful AI models in the world spent the past 19 days offline because the US government said so. Today's brief covers what happened when Washington started switching things back on — and what else is moving in the meantime.

## Washington Becomes Frontier AI's Gatekeeper

### Anthropic's Mythos 5 Cleared for 100+ US Institutions After a 19-Day Shutdown

On June 12, the Commerce Department issued an emergency export control directive forcing Anthropic to pull Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 from every cloud platform — including access for foreign national employees. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick sent a letter to Anthropic's chief compute officer Tom Brown on June 27, clearing more than 100 US companies and government agencies to access Mythos 5 again through Anthropic's new "Glasswing" program. The letter stated that "a license will no longer be required" for those named entities. The full lift for both models is still pending, but the 19-day shutdown demonstrated that frontier AI access can now be revoked by Washington with almost no warning.

*Sources: [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/27/2026/us-releases-powerful-anthropic-model-mythos-to-some-us-companies) · [GovConWire](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/anthropic-fable-5-mythos-5-access-restored) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-says-trump-admin-has-lifted-export-controls-on-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5.html)*

### OpenAI Limits GPT-5.6 to ~20 Government-Vetted Partners

OpenAI released three new models — Sol (flagship), Terra (everyday), and Luna (fast and cheap) — but only about 20 partners whose names were individually approved by the US government can access Sol. The White House asked for the staggered rollout, citing concerns about advanced cyber capabilities reaching hostile nations. Sol starts at $5 per million input tokens. OpenAI said the arrangement "should not become the long-term norm," but it happened anyway — the first time an American AI lab launched a frontier model under a formal government access list.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/openai-limits-new-ai-models-to-trusted-partners-request-us-government.html) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-gpt-5-6-sol-limited-preview-government-approved-partners) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/conormurray/2026/06/26/openai-rolls-out-powerful-gpt-56-models-to-limited-users-vetted-by-us-government/)*

## AI Infrastructure Investment Accelerates

### Baseten Raises $1.5B at a $13B Valuation

Baseten closed a $1.5 billion Series F on June 22, led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction, and Spark Capital. The AI inference platform now handles more than 1 billion calls per day across 87 clusters on 18 different clouds. Revenue grew 20x year-over-year. The $13B valuation represents a 160% jump from the $5B Series E raised just five months ago — the latest signal that inference infrastructure is where the biggest AI bets are landing.

*Sources: [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260622645563/en/Baseten-Raises-%241.5-Billion-to-Power-the-Next-Era-of-AI-Inference) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/baseten-raises-1-5-billion-130000555.html)*

### IBM Joins OpenAI's Daybreak Cyber Program for Enterprise Vulnerability Detection

IBM launched a new application security service built on OpenAI's models on June 22, joining the Daybreak Cyber Partner Program. The service scans enterprise codebases for exploitable software vulnerabilities using read-only access and bounded execution inside client environments. IBM shares rose 3.6% after hours on the announcement. The partnership connects to Project Lightwell, IBM's and Red Hat's $5 billion commitment to securing open-source software supply chains.

*Sources: [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-06-22-ibm-and-openai-bring-frontier-ai-to-cyber-defense-helping-enterprises-keep-pace-with-machine-speed-threats) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ibm-openai-enterprise-security-partnership)*

## Models, Private Betas, and State Policy

### Grok 4.5 Enters Private Beta at SpaceX and Tesla — No Public Access Yet

Elon Musk confirmed today that Grok 4.5 is running in private beta at SpaceX and Tesla. The model runs on xAI's 1.5-trillion-parameter V9 foundation, trained through May 26 and supplemented with data from the Cursor coding environment. Musk claims it rivals Claude Opus 4.8, but no independent benchmarks exist — both SpaceX and Tesla now sit under the same corporate umbrella as xAI. xAI also disclosed plans to release entirely new foundation models from scratch every month through the end of 2026.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319314/20260629/grok-45-enters-private-beta-spacex-tesla-no-public-access-no-independent-benchmark.htm) · [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/grok-4-5-private-beta-spacex-tesla/) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/musk-says-grok-45-enters-private-beta-at-spacex-and-tesla-93CH-4764089)*

### Colorado Moves to Replace Its AI Law After Federal Court Blocks Enforcement

Colorado's AI Act — one of the most comprehensive state AI laws ever passed — is effectively dead. A federal court paused enforcement in April after xAI sued on First Amendment and Commerce Clause grounds. The Trump DOJ then intervened, marking the first time the federal government has moved to strike down a state AI law under its December 2025 executive order. Colorado's legislature responded by passing SB 26-189, a narrower replacement framework governing automated decision-making. Governor Polis is expected to sign it.

*Sources: [BakerMcKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/colorado-two-step-already-facing-potential-amendments-a-federal-court-pauses-enforcement-of-colorados-forthcoming-ai-law/) · [PrivacyWorld](https://www.privacyworld.blog/2026/05/the-colorado-ai-act-hits-a-wall-litigation-legislative-uncertainty-and-an-enforcement-standstill/) · [Seyfarth](https://www.seyfarth.com/news-insights/artificial-intelligence-legal-roundup-colorado-postpones-implementation-of-ai-law-as-california-finalizes-new-employment-discrimination-regulations-and-illinois-disclosure-law-set-to-take-effect.html)*
