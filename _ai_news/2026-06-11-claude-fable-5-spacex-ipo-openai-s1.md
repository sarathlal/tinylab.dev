SpaceX set a $1.75-trillion price tag on its Nasdaq debut tonight while Anthropic made Claude Fable 5 — its most capable model ever — available to all subscribers. Two landmark events within 48 hours signal the same thing: the AI industry is now in the business of publicly accountable capital.

## AI IPO Supercycle

### SpaceX Prices the Largest IPO in History Tonight

SpaceX set its final offer at $135 per share today, targeting a $75-billion raise at a $1.75-trillion valuation — the largest offering in market history, ahead of Saudi Aramco's 2019 record. Shares begin trading Friday on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX. The roadshow ran June 4-11 across 125 banks plus a dedicated retail investor session today; SpaceX chose a fixed-price structure, so secondary-market demand on Friday is the only place real price discovery can happen.

*Sources: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/05/15/spacex-targets-june-11-ipo-pricing-picks-nasdaq-for-historic-market-debut) · [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261904604-spacex-ipo-spcx-date-set-for-june-12-175-trillion-valuation-tradingkey) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/mark-calendar-spacex-ipo-involves-134921607.html)*

### OpenAI Files Confidential S-1 at $852B

OpenAI submitted its draft prospectus to the SEC on June 8, then immediately announced it: "We expect it to leak so we're just announcing it." The company hasn't locked in a listing window — September to November 2026 is the range — but says it wants the option to move quickly. Anthropic filed its own confidential S-1 on June 1. Two of the most valuable private companies in history are now formally in the IPO queue.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/09/openai-files-confidential-s-1-sec-ipo/) · [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/openai-files-confidential-initial-public-offering/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/)*

## New Model Releases

### Anthropic's Most Powerful Model Is Now Open to Everyone

Anthropic made Claude Fable 5 generally available on June 9 — the first time a Mythos-class model has been publicly accessible. It tops nearly every benchmark Anthropic has published, with particular strength in software engineering, vision, and scientific reasoning. The model runs on a 1-million-token context window with up to 128,000 output tokens and a January 2026 knowledge cutoff. Input costs $10 per million tokens; output costs $50 per million. Through June 22, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise users can access it at no extra charge. A safety layer silently reroutes under 5% of sessions to Claude Opus 4.8. The model is also live inside GitHub Copilot.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-released-claude-fable-5-its-most-powerful-model-publicly-days-after-warning-ai-is-getting-too-dangerous/) · [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-09-claude-fable-5-is-generally-available-for-github-copilot/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/9/claude-fable-5/)*

### Decart's Oasis 3 Simulates Hours of Photorealistic Driving on Demand

Israeli startup Decart released Oasis 3 on June 10, a real-time generative world model that produces multi-camera photorealistic driving environments without pre-recorded footage. Autonomous vehicle teams are the first target: they can generate rare road conditions — black ice, debris, low-visibility intersections — at scale, priced at $0.02 per second via API. Decart raised $300 million at a $4-billion valuation in May, backed by Nvidia, Sequoia, and Benchmark, with Andrej Karpathy and Amazon's Annapurna Labs also on board.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/10/decarts-new-world-model-can-simulate-hours-of-photorealistic-driving-with-some-caveats/) · [Decart](https://decart.ai/publications/decart-raises-300m-tech-leaders-back-the-company-as-both-customers-and-investors) · [SiliconAngle](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/18/decart-raises-300m-ai-optimization-software-world-models/)*

### Grok 4.3 Gains Skills Across All Platforms

xAI rolled out Skills — a framework for extending Grok with custom tools — to grok.com, iOS, and Android on June 10. The company also added a Quality Mode to its Grok Imagine image-generation API, giving enterprise developers higher-realism outputs with better text rendering. Grok 4.3 is the current production model, with deep integration into X's real-time data stream for current-events queries.

*Sources: [xAI Release Notes](https://releasebot.io/updates/xai) · [WaveSpeed Blog](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/june-2026-ai-launch-wave/)*

## Agentic AI in the Wild

### Mastercard Launches a Payment Rail Built for AI Agents

Mastercard released Agent Pay for Machines (AP4M) on June 10, an open protocol that lets AI agents transact directly with each other — including micropayments worth fractions of a cent — without a human initiating each transaction. Credentials and spending limits live on public blockchains including Polygon, Solana, and Base. Thirty-one partners signed on at launch: Coinbase, Stripe, Adyen, and Cloudflare among them. Mastercard frames AP4M as a five-year bet on machine-to-machine commerce rather than a near-term revenue driver.

*Sources: [Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/june/mastercard-launches-agent-pay-for-machines.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/10/mastercard-ai-payments-protocol-launch-agentic-finance/) · [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/404288/mastercard-agent-pay-machines-support-autonomous-ai-transactions-stablecoins)*

### HPE Completes a Private Cloud AI Rollout for Sky in 30 Days

Hewlett Packard Enterprise finished a Private Cloud AI deployment for UK broadcaster Sky on June 10, wrapping in roughly one month. Sky needed to train AI on sensitive broadcast data inside its own infrastructure — public cloud was off the table for governance reasons. HPE's model keeps data on-premises while connecting to the same tooling available through public AI platforms. It's early evidence that AI pilots in regulated industries are graduating to full production deployments.

*Sources: [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8909886/hpe-enhances-ai-capabilities-with-private-cloud-deployment)*
