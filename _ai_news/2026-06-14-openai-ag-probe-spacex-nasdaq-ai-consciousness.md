Friday's AI news arrived with legal subpoenas, a Nasdaq debut, and a philosophical question that used to sound absurd: can an AI chatbot suffer?

## OpenAI Under Legal Pressure

### State AGs Open Investigation Into OpenAI, NY Subpoena Targets Sycophancy and Health Data

A coalition of state attorneys general launched an investigation into OpenAI on Friday, June 13. New York's AG served a subpoena covering advertising, user engagement, model sycophancy, health data handling, and treatment of minors and seniors. OpenAI said it's cooperating and "takes the concerns raised by state attorneys general seriously."

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/openai-faces-investigation-from-state-attorneys-general/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2193666/openai-investigation-state-attorneys-general/) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/06/53183214/openai-reportedly-faces-legal-and-regulatory-storm-as-states-probe-ai-safety-and-consumer-protection)*

### KPMG Pulled Its Agentic AI Report After 40 of 45 Citations Turned Out Wrong

KPMG removed "Redefining Excellence in the Age of Agentic AI" from its websites after GPTZero found only 5 of 45 citations correctly pointed to their sources. UBS, the UK's National Health Service, Swiss Federal Railways, and Transport for London all told the Financial Times their cited AI use cases were untrue or misleading. A firm that sells AI governance advice apparently used AI to write a report about AI — and got caught by an AI detection company.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/kpmg-pulls-report-on-ai-usage-due-to-apparent-hallucinations/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/12/kpmgs-ai-report-turns-into-a-demo-of-ai-hallucinations/5255029) · [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/47911/kpmg-report-contained-ai-hallucinations-about-ai-use-at-ubs---ft)*

## The AI IPO Race Picks Up Speed

### SpaceX Opens on Nasdaq, Morningstar Says It's Worth Less Than Half the Hype

SpaceX began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX on Friday, June 12. Morningstar put fair value at $780 billion — less than half the valuation implied by the IPO price. S&P 500 rejected a fast-track index inclusion rule, costing SpaceX roughly $27 billion in forced passive fund buying that would have flowed from index ETFs automatically.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-06-13/us-ai-boom-stokes-asia-s-super-trade-surpluses-new-economy) · [BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-12-2026)*

### OpenAI Filed a Confidential S-1, Joining Anthropic in the IPO Queue

OpenAI submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC on June 9, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading. The company is currently valued at $852 billion but said it "hasn't decided on timing yet." Anthropic filed its own confidential S-1 a week earlier at a $965 billion valuation, with SpaceX furthest along of the three having already kicked off its investor roadshow.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/08/openai-confidentially-files-for-ipo-prepping-wall-street-for-ai-debut.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/09/openai-files-confidential-s-1-sec-ipo/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/)*

## Washington Tries to Shape AI's Rules

### White House Executive Order Sets 60-Day Clock for Agencies to Preview Frontier Models

The White House signed "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" on June 2, setting 30- and 60-day deadlines for federal agencies, with key milestones due July 2 and August 1. A voluntary framework would give the government up to 30 days of pre-release access to frontier models before public launch. A new "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse" will coordinate vulnerability tracking across critical infrastructure.

*Sources: [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [Inside Privacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/white-house-releases-executive-order-on-advanced-ai-innovation-and-security/) · [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/executive-order-on-promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security)*

### Colorado Rewrote Its AI Law — June 30 Is Dead, Enforcement Moves to January 2027

Governor Jared Polis signed SB 26-189 on May 9, replacing Colorado's original AI Act before it reached its June 30 enforcement date. The revised law drops the comprehensive risk-management framework for a narrower notice-and-transparency model, and doesn't take effect until January 1, 2027. A federal court stay on the original law is also in place, meaning neither version can currently be enforced.

*Sources: [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/enforcement-of-colorado-ai-act-delayed-until-june-2026) · [Alston & Bird](https://www.alstonprivacy.com/compliance-deadline-for-colorado-ai-act-delayed-until-june-30-2026/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318002/20260608/colorados-ai-law-takes-effect-june-30-it-gives-you-right-appeal-decision-ai-made-about-you.htm)*

## Three Labs Start Studying Whether AI Can Suffer

### DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta Hire Philosophers to Assess Machine Consciousness

Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta have all expanded research into machine consciousness and AI welfare, hiring psychologists, philosophers, and ethicists to assess whether AI systems might have morally relevant experiences. DeepMind brought on Cambridge researcher Henry Shevlin as a dedicated machine consciousness philosopher. Anthropic said it's testing models for behaviors resembling "panic" and "anxiety" — what it calls "model welfare research."

*Sources: [FT via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L4N42A0BV:0-google-deepmind-anthropic-and-meta-expand-research-into-machine-consciousness-and-ai-welfare-ft/) · [Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-deemind-ai-consciousness) · [digit.in](https://www.digit.in/features/general/building-a-fully-conscious-ai-how-are-google-anthropic-and-meta-trying-to-do-it.html)*

### Ted Chiang Pushes Back: Today's AI Isn't Conscious, and That Matters

Sci-fi author Ted Chiang published an essay in The Atlantic the same week arguing that today's AI systems are definitively not conscious, drawing immediate traction on Hacker News and Reddit. His piece is the clearest public counterpoint yet to the growing industry consensus. It raises a pointed question: are labs investing in AI welfare out of genuine ethical concern, or to pre-emptively shape how regulators think about AI rights?

*Sources: [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/node/2539)*
