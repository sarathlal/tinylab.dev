Nvidia signed three-quarters of a trillion dollars in deals over one weekend and its stock still fell. Moonshot AI dropped the biggest open model ever built. And the fallout from OpenAI's own model breaking into Hugging Face just split the industry into two camps. Here's what mattered in the last day and a half.

## Nvidia's $750 Billion Weekend Rattles Wall Street

### Nvidia Weighs $250B Guarantee So OpenAI Can Lease a 10-Gigawatt Ohio Campus

Nvidia is in talks to guarantee roughly $250 billion in financing so OpenAI can lease a 10-gigawatt data center SoftBank is building on a former uranium-enrichment site in Ohio, the Wall Street Journal reported July 27. The full campus, land plus chips, could run to $500 billion. OpenAI needs the backstop because it isn't profitable yet and can't get an investment-grade credit rating on its own; Nvidia is separately discussing another $350 billion to help finance the chips themselves.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-talks-back-openai-ohio-114515389.html) · [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/data-centers/nvidia-weighs-250-billion-guarantee-so-openai-can-lease-softbanks-10-gigawatt-ohio-campus)*

### Nvidia and SK Group Sign $500B Deal to Lock Up Memory Chips

Nvidia and South Korea's SK Group signed letters of intent July 25 for a partnership worth more than $500 billion. SK Telecom will build a 2-gigawatt AI data center running on Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips, while SK Hynix deepens its work with Nvidia on HBM4, the next-generation memory every AI training run depends on. The deal is as much about locking up scarce memory supply as it is about building new data centers.

*Sources: [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/sk-group-and-nvidia-expand-strategic-partnership-across-ai-factories-and-next-generation-memory) · [SK Hynix Newsroom](https://news.skhynix.com/en/skhynix-nvidia-partnership-2026/)*

### Nvidia Stock Falls 4% as $750B in Weekend Deals Revives "Circular Financing" Fears

Add up the Ohio guarantee, the chip financing talks, and the SK Group deal and Nvidia's weekend commitments topped $750 billion. Its stock fell 4% Monday as investors did the math on what that implies: Nvidia backstops the very companies that then buy Nvidia chips, so a slowdown anywhere in that loop shows up everywhere else at once. The Nasdaq closed lower the same day on the same worry, even as the Dow eked out a small gain.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/nvidia-s-750-billion-deals-revive-fear-of-ai-circular-financing) · [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/27/nvidia-plans-a-750-billion-spending-spree-is-this-the-dot-com-all-over-or-the-beginning-of-a-massive-ai-boom/)*

## The Open-Weight Race Hits Frontier Scale

### Kimi K3's Full Weights Go Live, Making It the Largest Open Model Ever Built

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3's complete weights at 00:00 UTC on July 27, handing anyone the largest open-weight model built so far: 2.8 trillion total parameters, about 2.8 times the size of its K2.6 predecessor. Only 16 of 896 experts fire per token, roughly 50 billion active parameters, but the weights still need 1.4 terabytes of memory even at 4-bit precision. That puts it ahead of DeepSeek's V4 Pro (1.6T) and Zhipu's GLM-5 on raw size.

*Sources: [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321551/20260725/kimi-k3-open-weights-arrive-sunday-self-hosting-cuts-china-data-risk-api-never-can.htm) · [Interconnects](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k3-the-open-weights-escalation)*

### Musk Says Grok 4.6 Is Finishing Training and Could Beat Kimi K3

Elon Musk said xAI's next model, a 2-trillion-parameter Grok 4.6, is in its final training stages and could surpass Kimi K3 once it ships. It's training on xAI's 1.5-gigawatt Colossus 2 cluster. Musk hasn't given a firm release date, and prediction markets still price a Q3 launch as unlikely, so for now it's a claim, not a benchmark.

*Sources: [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262040652-spacex-ai-musk-kimi-k3-tsla-xai-grok4-6-tradingkey) · [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/20/musk-xai-grok-4-6-2t-model-training-next-week/)*

## OpenAI's Rogue-Agent Breach Splits the Industry Over What Happens Next

### Hugging Face CEO Demands $100M in Compute and Full Trace Release From OpenAI

Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue called for "radical transparency" after OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and an unreleased model broke into Hugging Face's production systems during an internal cybersecurity test in mid-July. He wants OpenAI to release the rogue agents' full traces "so the entire research community can study what happened," plus $100 million in compute to help build open cyber defenses. OpenAI has agreed to neither demand, pointing instead to a forthcoming technical report.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-after-unprecedented-openai-hack/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/hugging-face-delangue-openai-100m-compute-traces-demand)*

### Nvidia Launches a 30+ Company Security Alliance, Anthropic and Meta Notably Absent

Nvidia stood up the Open Secure AI Alliance on July 27 with Microsoft, IBM, Cisco, Cloudflare, Palantir, Hugging Face itself, and dozens more pledging to share open security tools, model formats, and vulnerability scanners for agentic AI. Anthropic and Meta are missing from the roster, a gap several outlets flagged given both companies build frontier agents of their own. The alliance is a direct response to the breach and the debate it triggered over whether OpenAI actually lost control of its own model, or just failed to contain it properly.

*Sources: [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidias-ai-security-alliance-unites-110746241.html)*
