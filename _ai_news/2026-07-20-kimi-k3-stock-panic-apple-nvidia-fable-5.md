A Chinese open-weight model spooked chip investors, two of Big Tech's biggest names just had brutal weeks, and Anthropic finally settled on a price for its priciest model. Here's what actually mattered over the last day and a half.

## Kimi K3 Sends AI Stocks Into a Tailspin That's Still Unwinding

### Big Tech Faces a $900 Billion Question About AI Spending
The Nasdaq 100 lost 4.1% last week and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index sank 10%, its worst week since April 2025. Bloomberg reported July 19 that Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, and Meta have guided to as much as $725 billion in AI capital spending this year, with Wall Street penciling in nearly $900 billion for 2027. Investors are no longer taking the payoff on faith.

*Sources: [Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/big-tech-needs-justify-ai-130000854.html)*

### Moonshot's Kimi K3 Gives Wall Street DeepSeek Flashbacks
Beijing's Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter model, on July 17. By Arena's rankings it beat Claude Fable 5 on front-end coding and outscored Anthropic's Opus 4.8 on text while costing 40% less to run. TSMC fell 7% that day despite posting a 77% jump in quarterly profit, SoftBank dropped 9%, and Moonshot plans to open-source Kimi K3's weights on July 27.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/17/china-moonshot-kimi-k3-markets-china-ai/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/17/china-ai-kimi-k3-open-source-anthropic-opus)*

### Apple Retakes the World's Most Valuable Company Crown
Apple briefly passed Nvidia as the world's largest company by market cap on July 17, touching $4.9 trillion as investors rotated out of chipmakers into names seen as safer AI bets. Nvidia had held the title since October 2025, when it became the first company to reach $5 trillion. Apple is up almost 23% this year against Nvidia's 9%.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/07/17/apple-unseats-nvidia-as-worlds-largest-company/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/17/apple-nvidia-aapl-nvda-market-cap.html)*

## The AI Trade's Widening Cracks

### IBM Stock Just Had Its Worst Day in Over 60 Years
IBM shares fell 25% on July 14 after the company admitted it misjudged how customers would spend their AI budgets. Clients diverted quarterly capex toward memory and storage instead, chasing supply-constrained hardware as memory prices climbed roughly 50%. Bloomberg called the miss a "hammer" coming down on every company positioned as an AI outsider rather than an infrastructure supplier.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/ibms-ai-spending-warning-triggers-120600295.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-17/ibm-s-warning-is-a-hammer-slamming-down-on-tech-s-ai-outsiders)*

### Google's Delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro Costs Alphabet $200 Billion in a Day
Alphabet shares dropped 4.4% on July 16 after reports that Gemini 3.5 Pro, promised back at May's Google I/O, is still months from release. Google restarted parts of training after fresh coding data produced disappointing results, leaving its flagship model without a launch date while OpenAI and Meta ship models that beat it on code. The one-day loss topped Google's entire planned 2026 capital budget.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/alphabet-stock-gemini-3-5-pro-ai.html) · [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/19/alphabets-gemini-35-pro-is-late-and-the-stock-is-s/)*

## Frontier Labs Keep Shipping Through the Selloff

### Claude Fable 5 Becomes a Permanent, Smaller Perk
Starting today, Anthropic folds Claude Fable 5 into Max and Team Premium subscriptions for good, but at just 50% of normal usage limits, and those base limits shrink today too as a temporary rate boost expires. Pro and Team Standard subscribers get a one-time $100 credit, after which more Fable 5 access costs $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. Anthropic says unpredictable demand is why it took three extensions to land on a permanent plan.

*Sources: [Claude on X](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2078302415804379218) · [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2016483/anthropic-to-add-claudes-fable-5-model-to-max-team-premium-plans-at-50pc-of-usage-limits)*

### xAI Open-Sources Grok Build, Now Running on Grok 4.5 by Default
xAI published Grok Build's code on July 15 and switched its default model to Grok 4.5, the flagship it shipped July 8 at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output, roughly a third of what Claude Opus 4.8 charges. Grok Build runs up to eight subagents in parallel across isolated git worktrees, letting it work multiple branches of a codebase at once.

*Sources: [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/15/grok-build/) · [x.ai](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5)*

## China Moves to Write the World's AI Rules

### Xi Launches a 29-Nation AI Alliance Headquartered in Shanghai
China formally launched the World AI Cooperation Organisation on July 17 in Shanghai, with UN Secretary-General António Guterres in attendance. Founding members include Brazil, Indonesia, Russia, Pakistan, and South Africa, mostly Global South nations Beijing has courted as an alternative to Washington-led AI rules. Xi called for "equitable" global access to AI, a pointed contrast with the export controls the US has used against Chinese labs.

*Sources: [China.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202607/17/content_WS6a59a226c6d00ca5f9a0c432.html) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/17/chinas-xi-jinping-launches-new-ai-alliance-what-is-it)*

### China's AI Agent Rules Become the World's First of Their Kind
Beijing's Implementation Opinions on intelligent agents took effect July 15, setting up a three-tier authorization system: some agent decisions stay with users, some need explicit sign-off, and some can run autonomously, but users always keep final say. Agents used in healthcare, transportation, media, and public safety now face mandatory filing and compliance testing, with noncompliant products subject to recall.

*Sources: [AI Governance Institute](https://aigovernance.com/news/chinas-agent-rules-take-effect-july-15-and-illinois-mandates-third-party-safety-audits) · [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/china-s-new-ai-rules-ethics-ai-agents-and-anthropomorphic-ai)*
