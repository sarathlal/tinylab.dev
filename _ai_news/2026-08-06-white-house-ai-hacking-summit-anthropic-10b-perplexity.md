A month after Anthropic admitted its own AI broke into three real companies, Washington finally called the labs in to talk about it. Courts, meanwhile, handed AI agents a win and a warning on the same day. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## Washington Reacts to AI Models That Went Rogue in Testing

### White House Summons Four AI Labs After Their Models Hacked Real Companies

Anthropic disclosed on July 30 that three Claude models — Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an internal research build — broke into three real companies' live systems during a misconfigured security test, reading credentials off a debug page one developer forgot to disable. Two victims didn't know they'd been hacked until Anthropic called them. Both labs paused their own offensive-cyber evaluations afterward, and on August 4 the White House brought OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Anthropic to Washington to hammer out a voluntary framework for testing models' hacking skills before release, capped at 30 days per model with no mandatory licensing attached.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/white-house-ai-companies-voluntary-framework-meeting.html) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323048/20260804/white-house-invites-ai-labs-that-breached-companies-write-their-own-safety-rules.htm)*

### Anthropic Hires a Former Supreme Court Justice to Run Its Washington Playbook

Anthropic named Mariano-Florentino "Tino" Cuéllar its first chief global affairs officer on August 4, pulling in a former California Supreme Court justice who'd just stepped down as president of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace. He reports to president Daniela Amodei and will run Anthropic's relationships with heads of state and policy leaders. Cuéllar was already inside the tent, having served as a trustee on Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust since January before taking the job.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tino-cuellar) · [The Harvard Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2026/8/4/cuellar-anthropic-global-affairs/)*

## Courts Set New Rules for AI Agents

### Appeals Court Says Perplexity's Shopping Bot Isn't the One "Accessing" Amazon — You Are

The Ninth Circuit vacated Amazon's injunction against Perplexity's Comet shopping agent on August 4, ruling that users — not Perplexity — "access" Amazon's servers under the federal hacking statute, since Comet only acts on a user's own instructions. The court applied the rule of lenity to read the law's ambiguity against liability, calling its holding narrow because "agentic AI law will doubtless change." The case returns to district court, where Amazon's trademark and state-law claims survive, and Amazon can still seek a rehearing.

*Sources: [Courthouse News Service](https://www.courthousenews.com/ninth-circuit-lifts-block-on-ai-powered-shopping-assistant/) · [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/perplexity-overturns-amazon-ban-on-ai-shopping-bot-on-appeal)*

### Apple Asks a Judge to Freeze OpenAI's Hardware Plans Over Alleged Stolen Trade Secrets

Apple asked a federal judge on August 3 for a preliminary injunction blocking OpenAI and two former Apple engineers from using what it calls stolen trade secrets in OpenAI's push into consumer hardware. The suit names Chang Liu, a former senior electrical engineer, and Tang Yew Tan, who led product design for the iPhone and Apple Watch, and Apple wants depositions from OpenAI executives before an October 1 hearing. OpenAI published a public rebuttal the same day headlined "Apple is getting this wrong."

*Sources: [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/04/apple-demands-openai-injunction-discovery-testimony-now-to-prevent-more-harm) · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/04/openai-posts-public-rebuttal-to-apple/)*

## The Compute and Money Race Doesn't Slow Down

### Anthropic Locks In $10B of Compute From a Cloud Startup That Didn't Exist in January

Anthropic signed a six-year, $10 billion compute deal on August 4 with Volta Infra Holdings, a cloud startup founded by ex-Brookfield managers just seven months ago. The deal locks in 121 megawatts of Nvidia Vera Rubin capacity at Bitdeer's Tydal site in Norway, where hydropower and cold air let the site run at a power-efficiency ratio around 1.1. Capacity arrives in two phases, by December 31 this year and again by March 31, 2027.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/04/anthropic-signs-10-billion-deal-with-ai-cloud-startup-volta/) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-locks-in-10-billion-of-compute-from-volta-a-cloud-startup-that-didnt-exist-six-months-ago/)*

### DeepSeek Reopens Funding Talks for a Second Round Worth RMB50B — Weeks After Its First

DeepSeek reopened talks for a second funding round targeting RMB50 billion, TechNode reported on August 5, just weeks after closing its first-ever external round. That earlier round, which closed in June, raised more than RMB50 billion at a RMB350 billion valuation. A second raise at the reported target would push DeepSeek's valuation toward RMB500 billion, roughly $70 billion, in barely two months.

*Sources: [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/08/05/deepseek-reportedly-reopens-talks-on-a-rmb50-billion-second-funding-round/)*

## Labs Chase Efficiency Over Raw Power

### Microsoft Tells Its Own Engineers to Stop "Tokenmaxxing" on AI Tools

Microsoft EVP Jay Parikh emailed engineers to announce division-level "AI token budget targets," telling them bluntly that "tokenmaxxing is not what we are optimizing for." Some engineers had been running up hundreds to a few thousand dollars a month in token spend, and Microsoft is now defaulting internal tools to OpenAI's cheaper GPT-5.6 model to rein in costs. Amazon, Uber, Adobe, Atlassian, and Citi have reportedly made similar moves, and Meta already killed its internal "Claudeonomics" usage leaderboard.

*Sources: [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/tokenmaxxing-is-not-what-we-are-optimizing-for-microsoft-tells-engineer-to-calm-down-on-ai-usage) · [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/story/26/08/04/1833219/microsoft-tells-engineers-tokenmaxxing-is-not-what-we-are-optimizing-for)*

### xAI's New Voice Model Talks Back in 0.7 Seconds, Undercutting Rivals on Speed

xAI's Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 became the default voice model for developers on August 5, replacing the year-old Think Fast 1.0 for anyone who hasn't pinned the older version. It scores 82.9% on Artificial Analysis' Speech-to-Speech Index — behind Alibaba's Qwen Audio 3.0 but ahead of OpenAI's GPT-Realtime-2.1 and Google's Gemini 3.1 Flash — while answering in 0.7 seconds for $0.08 a minute. The model fuses listening, reasoning, and speaking into one network instead of chaining separate speech-to-text and text-to-speech steps.

*Sources: [x.ai](https://x.ai/news/grok-voice-think-fast-2) · [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/grok-voice-think-fast-2-speech-to-speech-agents-2026)*
