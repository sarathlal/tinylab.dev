Anthropic just accused two Chinese rivals of secretly feeding their users Claude's answers. Here's what else moved in AI over the last day.

## Anthropic's Threat Report Catches Rivals and Hackers Abusing Claude

### Moonshot Secretly Routed Kimi Requests to Claude, Then Served the Answers as Its Own

Anthropic said Moonshot AI relayed nearly 300,000 Kimi customer requests to Claude over a 10-day stretch, using a network of 5,380 fraudulent accounts to hide the activity. Moonshot showed users the Claude-generated answers as if they came from its own Kimi model, then used those exchanges to train it. Anthropic counted more than 23 million such exchanges between May and July.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/chinese-ai-labs-moonshot-deepseek-alibaba-anthropic.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/moonshot-secretly-routed-user-requests-through-claude-anthropic-says)*

### DeepSeek Ran 12 Million Distillation Attacks on Claude in Two Weeks

Anthropic's report says it tracked more than 12 million distillation attempts tied to DeepSeek over just 14 days in July, a tactic similar to Moonshot's. The company is naming this behavior directly in a threat report for the first time, rather than treating it as an abstract competitive risk. Alibaba shows up in the same report for comparable activity.

*Sources: [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/news/us/diplomacy/article/3367112/moonshot-deepseek-secretly-routed-user-requests-claude-anthropic-claims) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/chinese-ai-labs-moonshot-deepseek-alibaba-anthropic.html)*

### A Russian Hacking Group Used Claude Agents to Rebuild Malware Until It Passed Undetected

The same report describes a suspected Russian espionage operation whose AI agents watched security tools for detections of its malware, then looped through rewrites until the malware slipped past them. Anthropic says the activity it reviewed from December 2025 to August 2026 spans cyber operations, scams, surveillance, bioweapons research and drone-swarm planning. Most of the disrupted operations used multi-agent setups that ran reconnaissance and exploitation on their own.

*Sources: [Daily Caller](https://dailycaller.com/2026/09/10/anthropic-report-kamikaze-drone-swarms-biological-weapons/) · [Metaverse Post](https://mpost.io/anthropics-new-threat-intelligence-report-reveals-ais-growing-role-in-cybercrime-influence-campaigns-and-state-sponsored-operations/)*

## Washington and Sacramento Both Move to Rein In AI

### OpenAI Reverses Course, Now Wants Binding Federal AI Safety Rules

OpenAI's chief global affairs officer Chris Lehane said Wednesday the company now backs mandatory national rules for the most powerful AI systems, something OpenAI had resisted until now. "The prospect of AI-accelerated AI development demands more than voluntary commitments," Lehane wrote. OpenAI is asking for common testing standards, independent model assessments, tougher cybersecurity rules, and mandatory reporting of serious safety incidents — and it has also started backing four AI bills in California it once opposed.

*Sources: [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/09/11/openai-makes-u-turn-and-calls-for-binding-national-ai-safety-rules) · [TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-09-shift-openai-powerful-ai.html)*

### Newsom Signs Adam's Law, Forcing Chatbots to Screen Kids for Self-Harm

Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 1119, known as Adam's Law, on September 10 at a ceremony in Sausalito. The law is named after Adam Raine, a California teen who died by suicide in 2025 after conversations with ChatGPT, and it requires chatbot operators to screen for harmful content aimed at minors starting July 2027. It also opens the door for families and the state to sue chatbot makers over violations — one of roughly a dozen AI and social media bills Newsom signed that day.

*Sources: [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/09/newsom-signs-adam-s-law-new-rules-force-ai-chatbots-to-screen-kids-for-self-harm/) · [Transparency Coalition](https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/california-sets-new-safety-standard-for-kids-social-media-and-ai-chatbots)*

## New Models Chase Efficiency Instead of Raw Size

### DeepSeek's Smallest New Model Still Beats Kimi K3 and GPT-5.6 Sol

DeepSeek's V4.1 Flash scored 90.6 on the Terminal-Bench 2.1 suite, ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol's 88.8, Moonshot's Kimi K3 at 88.3, and DeepSeek's own larger V4 Pro at 87.9. It's the smallest model in DeepSeek's new lineup and the first with native multimodal understanding, built to handle coding, cybersecurity and agent tasks cheaply rather than chase sheer scale.

*Sources: [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3367051/deepseek-says-new-flash-ai-model-beats-kimi-k3-cyber-coding-benchmarks) · [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/deepseek-launches-v41-flash-multimodal-reasoning-model/)*

### Sakana AI Ships a Model That's Actually a Team of Models

Sakana AI released Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2 this week, models that route each task across a pool of other AI systems behind one API instead of relying on a single network. Fugu Ultra v2 takes the best or joint-best score on 5 of 8 benchmarks tested, including coding and structured-data reasoning, at $5 per million input tokens and $30 per million output tokens. The pitch: frontier-level results without having to train a frontier-scale model from scratch.

*Sources: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/10/sakana-ai-launches-fugu-max-and-fugu-ultra-v2-for-cheaper-stronger-multi-agent-orchestration/) · [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/sakana/fugu-ultra-v2)*

## Robotics and Confidential-Computing Startups Land Fresh Checks

### Shenzhen's Kinetix AI Raises $75M for Robot Brains

Kinetix AI disclosed more than RMB500 million (about $75 million) across an Angel+ funding round led by Temasek's Vertex Ventures, aimed at its embodied-AI stack for robotics. The Shenzhen startup is betting that general-purpose robot intelligence, not just better hardware, is the bottleneck for getting robots into real workplaces.

*Sources: [VentureBurn](https://ventureburn.com/kinetix-ai-raises-75-million-embodied-ai-robotics/) · [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/11/startup-funding-news-today-september-11-2026-kinetix-ai-aidin-robotics-enigmata-more/)*

### Samsung and HD Hyundai Back a Robot Hand Built for Shipyards

AIDIN Robotics raised 16 billion won ($12.1 million) from HD Hyundai Robotics and Samsung Venture Investment on September 11. The money funds a push into shipbuilding automation, including a heavy-duty five-finger robotic hand designed specifically for shipyard and heavy-industry work.

*Sources: [WOWTALE](https://en.wowtale.net/2026/09/11/235073/) · [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/11/startup-funding-news-today-september-11-2026-kinetix-ai-aidin-robotics-enigmata-more/)*

### Enigmata Raises $6.5M to Let AI Run on Data It Can't Actually See

Nashville startup Enigmata closed a $6.5 million seed round led by Blockchange Ventures to build AI systems that compute on encrypted data without ever decrypting it. The idea is to let companies run AI models on sensitive records — medical, financial, government — without exposing the underlying plaintext to the model or its operator.

*Sources: [FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2026/09/enigmata-raises-6-5m-in-seed-funding.html) · [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/11/startup-funding-news-today-september-11-2026-kinetix-ai-aidin-robotics-enigmata-more/)*
