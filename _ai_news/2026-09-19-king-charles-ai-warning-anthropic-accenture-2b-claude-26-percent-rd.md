AI's power players spent the last two days getting lectured by a king, writing checks to watch themselves, and quietly automating their own labs. Here's what mattered.

## A King and a Chipmaker Argue About Who's in Charge

### King Charles Tells AI Leaders to Rein It In "Before It's Too Late"

King Charles III hosted about 30 AI executives, policymakers, and ethics researchers at Dumfries House in Scotland on September 17, warning that the technology's pace was "both intriguing and deeply concerning." He gave the opening speech, stayed roughly 20 minutes, then left so guests could talk freely. Attendees included Nvidia's Jensen Huang, Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis, OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar, and the UK's new AI minister, Kanishka Narayan.

*Sources: [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-18/king-charles-warns-ai-leaders-of-existential-dangers/107166548) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/even-the-king-of-england-has-his-hesitations-about-ai/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/17/king-charles-ai-existential-dangers-control/)*

### Jensen Huang's Answer: No Pause, and Nvidia's Doubling Chip Sales Anyway

Responding to the King's warning, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang argued that safe deployment is each company's job, not something a coordinated industry pause can fix. He made the same point days later at Nvidia's UK AI ecosystem event, where he also told reporters Nvidia expects to sell twice as many chips next year as this year. The contrast was hard to miss: a monarch asking for restraint, and the industry's biggest supplier promising to ship faster.

*Sources: [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-18/king-charles-warns-ai-leaders-of-existential-dangers/107166548) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/who-made-the-guest-list-for-king-charles-iii-s-ai-summit)*

### Anthropic and Accenture Put $2B Behind Watching AI Labs From the Inside

Anthropic and Accenture announced a partnership on September 18 to build a team of "embedded evaluators" with employee-like access inside Anthropic — able to watch models take shape in training, sit in on deployment decisions, and talk directly to staff. Both companies plan to invest at least $1 billion each over five years. The work is led by Faculty, Accenture's AI safety unit, and is the first concrete step in CEO Dario Amodei's "pacing the frontier" plan; Anthropic says more evaluator partners are coming.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/anthropics-first-embedded-evaluator-is-accenture/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/accenture-anthropic-partner-build-team-201500758.html)*

## Data Centers Meet the Power Grid, and the Bill Comes Due

### The US House Just Voted 417-3 to Make Data Centers Pay Their Own Power Bill

The House passed the bipartisan Ratepayer Protection Act on September 16 in a near-unanimous 417-3 vote. It sets a federal standard requiring large-load customers — data centers pulling at least 100 megawatts — to cover the full cost of the grid upgrades built to serve them, instead of spreading that cost across everyone's utility bill. Only three progressive Democrats voted no. The bill now heads to the Senate.

*Sources: [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/house-bill-data-center-energy-costs/) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327645/20260917/house-voted-417-3-make-data-centers-pay-electricity-costs-states-can-still-say-no.htm)*

### India Commits $13.5B to a Second Chip Push

Prime Minister Modi launched Phase 2 of India's Semiconductor Mission at SEMICON India 2026 on September 17, with a $13.5 billion outlay running 12 years — up from $8 billion over 5 years in phase one. Twelve chip-making projects have already been approved, with more expected. The move lands as global chip demand tightens further on the back of Nvidia's own sales forecasts.

*Sources: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/semicon-india-2026-pm-modi-highlights-13-5-billion-semiconductor-mission-12-chip-projects-approved-556095-2026-09-17) · [The Print](https://theprint.in/tech/5-pillars-12-yrs-13-5-bn-outlay-modi-launches-2nd-phase-of-chip-mission-at-semicon-india/3045265/)*

## AI Goes to Work, Including on Itself

### ChatGPT Now Lives Inside Microsoft Word, Free Plan Included

OpenAI launched a ChatGPT sidebar add-in for Microsoft Word on September 17, letting users draft from notes, summarize documents, revise text, and fix formatting without leaving the page. It's available on every ChatGPT plan, including Free, and joins the same add-in already running in Excel and PowerPoint. Install is through the Microsoft Marketplace, no separate app needed.

*Sources: [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/a937bced-d2c2-403c-aaf3-13b592ed2ec4) · [WinCentral](https://thewincentral.com/chatgpt-microsoft-word-sidebar-add-in/)*

### Claude Now Leads 26% of Anthropic's Own AI Research

Anthropic's new R&D Automation Index found Claude "leads" 26% of the company's AI research and development work as of August, up from under 1% in February. The index rates tasks on a 0-to-5 automation scale built with Epoch AI, and Claude itself did most of the classifying: it read staff Slack messages and docs, sorted roughly 15,000 tasks into 542 categories, and scored them. Claude now touches more than 90% of Anthropic's R&D in some capacity.

*Sources: [Implicator.ai](https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-claude-leads-26-percent-ai-research/) · [Officechai](https://officechai.com/ai/claude-is-leading-26-of-ai-research-at-anthropic-collaborating-on-over-90/)*
