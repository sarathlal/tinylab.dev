Cisco and Alibaba both posted AI-driven earnings beats yesterday, while Palo Alto issued a concrete timeline for when AI-powered hacking goes mainstream. The five stories below make one thing clear: AI is generating revenue and risk at the same time.

## AI Revenue Shows Up in Two Big Earnings Reports

### Cisco AI Orders Hit $5.3B — Stock Up 17%, 4,000 Jobs Cut Starting Today

Cisco received $5.3 billion in AI infrastructure and hyperscaler orders in the first nine months of fiscal 2026, then raised its full-year order target from $5 billion to $9 billion. Revenue hit $15.84 billion, beating the $15.56 billion Wall Street expected, and the stock surged 17% in after-hours trading. The company is cutting about 4,000 jobs, roughly 5% of its workforce, with layoffs starting May 14. CEO Chuck Robbins described the cuts as a "strategic shift toward AI and related growth areas."

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-13/cisco-gives-better-than-anticipated-forecast-plans-to-cut-jobs) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/cisco-csco-q3-earnings-report-2026.html)*

### Alibaba AI Revenue Grows for 11th Straight Quarter; $56B Investment Pledged

Alibaba's Cloud Intelligence Group grew 38% in the January-March quarter to $6.1 billion. AI-related products generated $1.32 billion — now 30% of cloud revenue — marking the 11th consecutive quarter of triple-digit AI revenue growth. CEO Eddie Wu said the company is prioritizing AI over short-term profit, with annual recurring revenue from AI models targeting 10 billion yuan ($1.5B) by June and 30 billion yuan by year-end. Alibaba also pledged 380 billion yuan ($55.96B) in AI and cloud infrastructure over three years.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/05/13/china-alibaba-earnings-artificial-intelligence/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/alibaba-earnings-march-quarter-ai-cloud-growth.html)*

### Gartner: Half of Enterprises Will Lose Their Best AI Talent by 2027

Gartner predicted May 13 that 50% of enterprises without a people-centric AI strategy will lose top AI employees to better-prepared competitors by 2027. Its survey of 12,004 workers across 40 countries found 88% of enterprise AI users also run personal AI tools for work — a shadow-AI pattern Gartner says signals both data risk and attrition. Only 27% of executives surveyed have a comprehensive AI strategy, and just 20% believe their workforce is AI-ready. Gartner calls the gap an "enablement illusion": confusing tool access with actual workforce transformation.

*Sources: [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-13-gartner-predicts-by-2027-50-percent-of-enterprises-without-a-people-centric-ai-strategy-will-lose-their-top-ai-talent)*

## AI-Powered Hacking: Working Exploits 70% of the Time

### Palo Alto Found 75 Security Bugs in Weeks Using Claude Mythos and GPT-5.5

Palo Alto Networks ran Anthropic's Claude Mythos and OpenAI's GPT-5.5 on its own codebase and found 75 vulnerabilities — more than seven times what its security team typically finds in a month. The models generated working exploits over 70% of the time. They also chained multiple flaws into complete attack paths, something earlier AI systems couldn't do reliably. Palo Alto estimates organizations have a three-to-five-month window before AI-driven exploits become the "new norm" for attackers.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/13/palo-alto-networks-mythos-gpt-cybersecurity) · [Palo Alto Networks](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/blog/2026/05/defenders-guide-frontier-ai-impact-cybersecurity-may-2026-update/)*

### Claude Mythos Exposed Zero-Days in Every Major OS — And It's Not Publicly Available

Claude Mythos Preview, the model Palo Alto used, identified zero-day vulnerabilities in every major operating system and every major browser during Anthropic's pre-release safety evaluation. It solved 73% of expert-level Capture-the-Flag tasks and became the first AI to complete a 32-step simulated corporate network attack from start to finish. Anthropic isn't making Mythos public; access is gated under "Project Glasswing," a research preview for vetted partners. The UK AI Safety Institute independently confirmed the findings in its own evaluation.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://red.anthropic.com/2026/mythos-preview/) · [AISI](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/our-evaluation-of-claude-mythos-previews-cyber-capabilities)*
