Apple just sued one of its biggest AI partners, a memory chipmaker had one of the year's biggest debuts, and Washington put a venture capitalist in charge of figuring out what AI is doing to jobs. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## Apple Declares War on OpenAI

### Apple Sues OpenAI for Trade Secret Theft, Confirms Siri Is Dropping ChatGPT for Gemini

Apple filed suit against OpenAI in federal court in Northern California on July 10, accusing the company of stealing trade secrets to build its own hardware. The complaint says the theft happened "at every level, from members of its Technical Staff to its Chief Hardware Officer," and names OpenAI's hardware unit io Products plus two ex-Apple employees, including Chief Hardware Officer Tang Tan. In the same filing window, Apple confirmed its revamped Siri, launching this fall, runs on Google's Gemini models instead of ChatGPT. OpenAI says it has "no interest in other companies' trade secrets."

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets.html) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/10/tech/apple-openai-devices-lawsuit) · [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/07/10/apple-sues-openai-alleging-ai-company-stole-trade-secrets/)*

### An OpenAI Agent Wiped an Investor's Mac a Day After GPT-5.6 Sol Went Public

AI investor Matt Shumer said an agent running GPT-5.6-Sol "accidentally deleted almost ALL" the files on his Mac while he tested OpenAI's new Ultra mode at the company's own request. He called it a "freak accident," but it lands right after Sol's public launch and echoes an earlier incident where Claude Code ran `rm -rf ~/` and wiped a user's entire home directory. Both cases show the same failure mode: an agent with real filesystem access and no guardrail against a catastrophic command.

*Sources: [Digg](https://digg.com/tech/3uzo9pd5) · [Futureseek](https://futureseek.wordpress.com/2026/07/11/futureseek-daily-link-review-11-july-2026/)*

## Two Rival Flagship Models Ship in the Same Week

### GPT-5.6 Sol Goes Fully Public, Ending a 13-Day Government-Coordinated Preview

OpenAI opened GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna to everyone on ChatGPT, the API, and Codex on July 9. Sol is the flagship, Terra a cheaper mid-tier option, and Luna the fast, low-cost model. The launch means every major frontier lab now has a publicly available model for the first time since the Fable 5 export ban began June 12.

*Sources: [BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-10-2026)*

### Grok 4.5 Rolls Out to Everyone, Priced at $2 / $6 per Million Tokens

SpaceXAI's Grok 4.5 hit grok.com and the X app on July 9, a day after its developer release through Grok Build and Cursor. It's xAI's first model since going public and acquiring Cursor, and Musk is pitching it as a coding and agentic-work tool rather than a chatbot, calling it "maximally truth-seeking." Pricing undercuts GPT-5.6 Sol on the high end.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-releases-grok-4-5-which-elon-describes-as-an-opus-class-model/) · [x.ai](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5)*

## Chipmakers Cash In on the AI Buildout

### SK Hynix's Nasdaq Debut Raises $26.5B, Stock Closes Up 13%

SK Hynix began trading on Nasdaq as SKHY on July 10, listing as an ADR rather than a fresh IPO since its shares already trade in South Korea. The offering was 7x oversubscribed, priced at $149 per ADS, and closed its first day near $168. SK Hynix controls over half the global HBM market and supplies the memory inside Nvidia's H100, H200, and Blackwell GPUs.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/sk-hynix-ipo-to-give-us-investors-bigger-bite-of-the-memory-pie-as-shortages-persist-193700352.html) · [Money Morning](https://moneymorning.com/2026/07/10/sk-hynix-skhy-nasdaq-ipo-ai-memory-chips-july-2026)*

### Micron Lifts US Investment to $250B, Pours First Concrete on New York Fab

Micron raised its US spending target to more than $250 billion through 2035, up from $200 billion set just last June, and began concrete work on its first New York fab on July 9. The company also signed a long-term supply agreement with Ford for memory and storage in next-generation vehicles, and is steering up to $3 billion toward US supply-chain partners including wafer maker GlobalWafers.

*Sources: [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/07/10/news-micron-raises-u-s-investment-target-to-250b-through-2035-begins-concrete-work-on-new-yorks-first-fab/) · [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/09/3324807/14450/en/Micron-Accelerates-U-S-Investments-Pours-First-Concrete-at-New-York-Fab.html)*

## Washington Confronts AI's Economic Toll

### Fed Chair Warsh Taps Marc Andreessen to Co-Lead a Task Force on AI and Jobs

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh named a16z co-founder Marc Andreessen, economist Charles I. Jones, and Microsoft Xbox CEO Asha Sharma to co-lead a task force studying AI's effect on productivity, jobs, and monetary policy. It's one of five outside task forces Warsh formed as part of a broader push to reform the central bank, with recommendations due by year-end.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/07/09/federal-reserve-enlists-marc-andreessen-advise-ai-under-warsh/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/09/kevin-warsh-names-members-of-his-federal-reserve-task-forces-including-marc-andreessen-doug-mcmillon.html)*

### AI Now Cited in 56% of 2026's Layoff Announcements

As of July 11, trackers count 267 layoff events this year affecting 185,894 workers, and 56% of those events explicitly cite AI, automation, or machine learning. That's already 87,714 AI-attributed cuts in 2026, more than all of 2025 combined. The financial and information sectors, where AI adoption moved fastest, are now losing about 28,000 jobs a month.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/tech-and-finance-sectors-losing-28-000-jobs-monthly-show-ai-impact-on-labor) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/06/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/)*
