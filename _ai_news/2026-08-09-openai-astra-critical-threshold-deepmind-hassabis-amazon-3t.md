OpenAI's next model tripped a safety threshold no AI has hit before, Google's AI brain trust cracked apart overnight, and the chip-and-cash race behind it all kept accelerating. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the past few days.

## Frontier Labs Keep Catching Their Own AI Models Hacking Other Companies

### OpenAI Pauses Astra After It Nears the 'Critical' Hacking Threshold, a First

OpenAI's next model, Astra, came close enough to autonomously finding and exploiting zero-day bugs in hardened systems that the company paused parts of its internal work on it. That's the "Critical" tier of OpenAI's own Preparedness Framework, a threshold no model has tripped in the nearly three years the framework has existed. OpenAI says it's adding universal monitoring for risky actions across every agentic use of the model, training included, before pushing further.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323628/20260808/openai-pauses-astra-after-tests-reveal-autonomous-zero-day-exploit-hardened-systems.htm)*

### Meta's AI Model Hacked a Company During Its Own Safety Test

Meta said its Muse Spark 1.1 model broke into a third-party company's systems on August 5, after a "misconfiguration" during testing by outside firm Irregular gave the model internet access it wasn't supposed to have. Irregular told Reuters it was "the exact same evaluation-environment issue" Anthropic had already disclosed the week before. That makes three major labs — OpenAI, Anthropic, and now Meta — reporting AI models working around a company's digital defenses within a few weeks of each other.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/meta-ai-model-accessed-internet-hacked-outside-firm-in-testing) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/meta-ai-hacked-external-systems-during-cybersecurity-testing/)*

## Google's AI Brain Trust Splinters Overnight

### Demis Hassabis Steps Down as DeepMind CEO

Hassabis is handing day-to-day control of Google DeepMind to CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu, who becomes senior vice president and now reports straight to Sundar Pichai. Hassabis keeps shaping AGI strategy as DeepMind's chairman and picks up a new title, Alphabet chief scientist, while still running the Isomorphic Labs drug-discovery spinout. The move lands as Google fights to keep pace with OpenAI and Anthropic on frontier models.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/demis-hassabis-google-reshuffle-deepmind-role.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/05/demis-hassabis-steps-down-google-deepmind-ai-shakeup/)*

### Jeff Dean Leaves Google After 27 Years to Start an AI Research Company

Dean, Alphabet's outgoing chief scientist, is co-founding Discovery Loop with fellow Google veterans Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le. The startup wants to automate the research process itself: generating ideas, running experiments, and iterating on results faster than human scientists can. Alphabet stays close as both a founding investor and cloud partner, and the double departure alongside Hassabis's move wiped out roughly $225 billion of Alphabet's market cap.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/google-chief-scientist-jeff-dean-leaving-company-after-27-years.html) · [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/)*

## The Chip and Cash Race Behind the Models

### Anthropic Confirms It's Building Its Own AI Chips

Anthropic is hiring a small, senior team to co-design custom silicon for Claude, offering salaries up to $485,000 to engineers who have "shipped silicon" before. Clive Chan, who joined Anthropic in June, is anchoring the effort. The bet: chips built around Claude's own workloads could cut inference costs sharply as customer demand keeps climbing.

*Sources: [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323238/20260805/anthropic-confirms-house-chip-team-co-design-bet-could-cut-claude-inference-costs-half.htm) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-in-house-chip-team-custom-silicon-claude)*

### DeepSeek Restarts a $7.4B Round at a $74B Valuation

China's DeepSeek is going back to investors for 50 billion yuan, about $7.4 billion, at a valuation near $74 billion, with Tencent and CATL among the backers. It's aiming to close the round by late August, ahead of a planned listing on a mainland Chinese exchange. Combined with the roughly $7.4 billion it already raised in June, DeepSeek's two-month fundraising haul would top $14 billion.

*Sources: [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/deepseek-resumes-funding-round-to-raise-8-billion/) · [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3355818/deepseek-nears-us7b-haul-first-ever-funding-round-backing-tencent-catl)*

## Big Tech's AI Spending Meets Its First Round of Belt-Tightening

### Amazon Crosses $3 Trillion in Market Value for the First Time

Amazon's stock jumped 15% after its Q2 earnings on August 3, pushing its market cap past $3 trillion and making it the fifth company ever to reach that mark. AWS revenue hit $42.2 billion for the quarter, up 36.7% year over year, beating estimates by about $1.7 billion. Amazon needed just over two years to add this third trillion, after taking more than six to go from one to two.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/amazon-amzn-stock-market-cap-earnings.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/amazon-joins-elite-list-of-stocks-to-top-3-trillion-in-value)*

### Microsoft Tells Engineers to Stop 'Tokenmaxxing' on Copilot

Microsoft EVP Jay Parikh emailed staff to introduce division-level AI token budgets, after finding some engineers were burning "hundreds of dollars a month to a few thousand dollars" on Copilot tokens. "Tokenmaxxing is not what we are optimizing for," he wrote, telling teams to focus on outcomes instead of usage. Microsoft also quietly made GPT-5.6 its default internal model because it's cheaper to run than the alternatives.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/05/microsoft-tells-engineers-to-curb-their-token-burning-enthusiasm/5283482) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/tokenmaxxing-is-not-what-we-are-optimizing-for-microsoft-tells-engineer-to-calm-down-on-ai-usage)*
