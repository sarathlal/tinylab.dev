Shanghai became the center of gravity for AI politics this week, Wall Street decided AI valuations got ahead of themselves, and the open-weight model race keeps getting less American. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## China Builds Its Own AI Governance Track

### 29 Nations Sign Charter for China-Led AI Governance Body

Foreign ministers from 29 countries signed a founding charter on July 16 establishing the World AI Cooperation Organization, headquartered in Shanghai. Members include Russia, Brazil, Indonesia, Pakistan, Kazakhstan, and South Africa — but no G7 country, and not the UK, Germany, France, or Japan either. Xi Jinping called it "a milestone in the history of world AI development" a day before hosting the broader World AI Conference, and countries in the bloc won't need their AI rules to match the EU AI Act or the G7's Hiroshima Process, splitting global AI governance into two separate tracks.

*Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/17/chinas-xi-jinping-launches-new-ai-alliance-what-is-it) · [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260716/b0449aa2133542868e310fdc45ef2969/c.html)*

### World AI Conference Opens in Shanghai With 1,100 Exhibitors

The 2026 World AI Conference opened July 18 with an exhibition floor topping 100,000 square meters for the first time, hosting more than 1,100 companies. Organizers counted over 3,000 products on display, 300 of them making their global debut. The conference runs alongside the new WAICO governance body's launch, making this Shanghai's biggest AI week yet.

*Sources: [CCTV](https://english.cctv.com/2026/07/18/ARTIq1oydZdePMMNa0lmABHJ260718.shtml) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320812/20260717/china-launches-rival-ai-governance-bloc-waic-2026-opens-300-product-debuts.htm)*

### Apple Intelligence Clears China After 22 Months, Running on Alibaba's Qwen

China's Cyberspace Administration approved Apple Intelligence for the Chinese market on July 15, nearly two years after Apple first hit the regulatory wall. Alibaba's Qwen model powers the feature across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS in mainland China, compressed from 54GB down to under 4GB so it can run on-device on phones as old as the iPhone 15. Alibaba beat out DeepSeek and ByteDance for the deal, partly because Qwen had already been optimized for Apple's on-device inference framework.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320811/20260717/apple-intelligence-wins-china-approval-after-22-months-qwen-handles-language-baidu-handles-search.htm) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/apple-intelligence-approved-china-alibaba-131501096.html)*

## AI Stock Rally Hits a Wall

### AI Selloff Deepens, Apple Retakes Most-Valuable-Company Crown From Nvidia

The S&P 500 fell 1% on July 18, closing its first losing week in three, while the Dow dropped 406 points and the Nasdaq sank 1.4%. Chip stocks led the slide, with Nvidia, Micron, and AMD all down on worries that AI spending has outrun the profits to justify it. Apple briefly overtook Nvidia as the world's most valuable company in the middle of the rotation out of AI infrastructure names and into everything else.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/18/the-ai-rotation-stole-the-spotlight-from-a-strong-start-to-earnings-season-.html) · [Arkansas Democrat-Gazette](https://www.arkansasonline.com/news/2026/jul/18/ai-sell-off-yanks-stock-markets-down/)*

### TSMC Posts Record 77% Profit Jump — and Its Stock Falls Anyway

TSMC beat estimates on both revenue and profit, with profit up 77% and capex guidance raised to $60-64 billion for 2026 on what its CEO called "very strong" cloud demand. None of that stopped the stock from sliding alongside the rest of the chip sector. The disconnect shows this week's selloff was about stretched valuations, not weakening AI demand.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/tsmc-raises-capex-and-revenue-forecast-highlighting-growing-ai-chip-demand-113101950.html) · [Blockonomi](https://blockonomi.com/market-turbulence-ai-chip-stocks-plunge-despite-tsmcs-record-earnings-netflix-guidance-disappoints/)*

## Open Weights Keep Closing the Gap, and the Price War Bites

### Moonshot's Kimi K3 Becomes the Largest Open-Weight Model Ever at 2.8 Trillion Params

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on July 16, a 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 1-million-token context window built for long coding and agent tasks. On the GDPval-AA v2 benchmark it scored 1,687, landing third behind only Claude Fable 5 Max (1,815) and GPT-5.6 Sol Max (1,747.8) — and ahead of Claude Opus 4.8. Moonshot plans to fully open-source the weights by July 27, letting anyone download and run it for free.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/17/moonshot-ai-kimi-k3-model-openai-anthropic-china.html) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems)*

### Claude Fable 5's Free Ride Ends Tonight After Three Extensions

Anthropic's free access to Claude Fable 5 for Pro, Max, and Team subscribers expires tonight at 11:59:59 PM PT — the third deadline extension since the promotion started July 7. Once it lapses, using Fable 5 requires prepaid credits at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. Anthropic has kept pushing the cutoff back each time a rival ships something new, most recently OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and xAI's Grok 4.5, which undercuts Opus 4.8 on price by roughly 3x on input tokens.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/07/13/ai-model-wars-anthropic-extends-fable-access-again-after-openais-sol-release/) · [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/af0d1286-8e98-49b1-9689-fdf159955ceb)*

## Money Keeps Flowing to AI Infrastructure Despite the Jitters

### Fireworks AI Raises $1.5B Series D at $17.5B Valuation as Revenue Hits $1B Run Rate

Fireworks AI closed a $1.505 billion Series D on July 16 at a $17.5 billion valuation, led by Atreides Management, Index Ventures, and TCV with Nvidia also participating. Annualized revenue crossed $1 billion, up 5x from its last round, while daily token volume on its platform nearly tripled to more than 40 trillion. The company turns general-purpose models into specialized ones trained on customer data — exactly the kind of "AI implementation" bet investors kept making even as public AI stocks wobbled this week.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/fireworks-nvidia-cloud-ai-startup-value.html) · [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260716264405/en/Fireworks-Raises-a-$1.5-Billion-Series-D-to-Lead-the-Specialized-Intelligence-Revolution)*

### Microsoft Preps Cut-Rate AI Bug Hunter to Undercut Anthropic's Mythos

Microsoft is readying Project Perception, an AI vulnerability-scanning tool that routes tasks across models from Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic to keep costs down. Its pitch is price: Anthropic's Mythos reportedly costs roughly twice as much per query as Opus and 82% more than GPT for the same kind of security work. Project Perception plugs directly into Microsoft's existing security products, giving it distribution Anthropic can't match on its own.

*Sources: [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-microsoft-project-perception-ai-security-tool/) · [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/exclusive-microsoft-preps-mythos-like-ai-bug-finder)*
