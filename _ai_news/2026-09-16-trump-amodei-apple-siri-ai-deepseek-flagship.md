Dario Amodei asked the AI industry to slow down last week, and the president just told him no. Here's everything else that moved in AI over the last day.

## Washington and the Open Web Pull AI Guardrails in Opposite Directions

### Trump Attacks Amodei by Name, Calls AI Danger Fears a "Hoax"

Trump posted on Truth Social that "the only control or 'guardrails' that AI needs is a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT, and the U.S.A. has that, in spades!" He named Amodei directly, saying the Anthropic CEO is "pretending to be a 'perfect little angel'" days after publishing an essay calling for AI labs to deliberately slow capability gains. In a follow-up post, Trump called fears that AI could harm humanity a "HOAX" and said his administration already holds "tremendous CRIMINAL and REGULATORY power over these companies."

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/14/trump-ai-safety-anthropic-dario-amodei) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/trump-ai-data-centers-anthropic-dario-amodei.html)*

### Cloudflare Starts Blocking "Mixed-Use" AI Crawlers by Default

Cloudflare's new default kicked in this week: any crawler that blends search indexing with AI training or agent retrieval now gets blocked on pages that carry ads. The company splits bot traffic into three buckets — Search, Agent, and Training — and if a site blocks Training bots, combined crawlers like Googlebot and Applebot get blocked too, even where Search is still allowed. That forces any AI vendor running a multi-purpose bot to split it into separate, single-purpose crawlers or lose access to ad-supported publisher sites.

*Sources: [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/07/02/cloudflare-ai-crawler-controls/)*

## Open-Weight Models Keep Redrawing the Frontier

### Shanghai AI Lab Quietly Ships a 744B-Parameter Agent Model

A GitHub repo called Atria-Dawn-Preview appeared on September 11 with no launch blog post, credited to Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. The model is a 744B-parameter mixture-of-experts built on the GLM-5.2 base, released under an MIT license so any team with enough GPUs can run it without a licensing deal. Three days later, a 143-author preprint followed, testing the model across 16 benchmarks for tool use and multi-step task completion.

*Sources: [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/shanghai-ai-lab-quietly-releases-atria-a-free-744-billion-parameter-ai-agent/) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/internlm/Atria-Dawn-Preview)*

### DeepSeek Quietly Retires Its Own Flagship Model

Since 04:00 UTC on September 14, every request sent to deepseek-v4-pro has actually been served by the smaller V4.1 Flash, at Flash's lower price. DeepSeek says the month-old Flash model "comprehensively surpassed" its own bigger sibling on performance, cost, and speed, so it's routing traffic there until a V4.1 Pro ships. V4.1 Flash also adds native multimodal image understanding, something the old V4 Pro lacked.

*Sources: [DeepSeek API Docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/) · [Emergent](https://emergent.sh/news/deepseek-v4-1-flash-launches-multimodal)*

## Consumer AI Ships While the Money Keeps Flowing

### Apple Finally Ships Siri AI, With Usage Caps and a Future Price Tag

Siri AI went out to everyone Monday as part of the iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate rollout, but it's still labeled beta, still English-only, and still capped on daily use. Apple plans a paid tier down the line and says French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish support won't arrive until October. The rollout also skips the EU and China entirely for now, leaving two of the world's biggest phone markets without the feature at launch.

*Sources: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/apple-releases-macos-golden-gate/) · [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/09/09/siri-ai-will-launch-in-beta-complicated-by-daily-usage-caps-future-paid-access)*

### AI-Search Startup Profound Hits $1.8B Valuation, 7 Months After Its Last Round

Profound raised $180 million in a Series D co-led by Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins, taking its valuation to $1.8 billion. The New York startup helps brands track and shape how often they get mentioned in answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI chatbots, a category some call "answer engine optimization." It's raised over $335 million total since founding in 2024, and this round landed less than seven months after its $96 million Series C.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/aeo-startup-profound-hits-unicorn-valuation-raises-180m-series-d-7-months-after-last-round/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/profound-hits-1-8-billion-value-to-boost-brands-in-ai-search)*
