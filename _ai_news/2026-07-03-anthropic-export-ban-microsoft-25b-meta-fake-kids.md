Anthropic closed out a rough three-week stretch by getting its banned models back and shipping two new products in the same week. Meanwhile the fight to actually deploy AI inside companies got a lot more expensive, and Meta had one of its stranger news days in months.

## Anthropic Ends Its 19-Day Export Ban, Ships Sonnet 5 and Claude Science

### Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Are Back After 19 Days Offline

The US Commerce Department told Anthropic on June 30 it was lifting the export control order that had frozen Fable 5 and Mythos 5 worldwide. Both models were fully restored by July 1 across Claude.ai, the API, Claude Code, and Cowork. The ban ran from June 12 to July 1 — Anthropic said it disagreed with Commerce's original reasoning but had no way to restrict access by nationality alone, so it pulled both models entirely rather than partially comply.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-says-trump-admin-has-lifted-export-controls-on-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5.html) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/1/us-lifts-restrictions-on-powerful-ai-models-fable-mythos-anthropic-says) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5)*

### Claude Sonnet 5 Becomes the Default for Every Free and Pro User

Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30 and made it the default model for every Free and Pro user starting July 1. It ships with a native 1 million-token context window and introductory API pricing of $2/$10 per million tokens through August 31, rising to $3/$15 after that. Anthropic says it approaches Opus 4.8 quality on many professional tasks while costing far less per token.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)*

### Claude Science Launches After Finding AI Biology Tools Were Wrong Most of the Time

At its "AI for Science" livestream on June 30, Anthropic said frontier models scored as low as 16.9% accuracy on repeated, identical viral-sequence queries. Adding a deterministic retrieval tool pushed that same test past 92% accuracy for every model tested. Anthropic used the event to launch Claude Science in beta, a workbench preloaded with 60-plus scientific databases for genomics, proteomics, and structural biology, built with labs like Bristol Myers Squibb and Genentech.

*Sources: [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91567549/claude-science) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319348/20260630/anthropic-livestreams-ai-science-pitch-no-ai-drug-has-won-fda-approval.htm) · [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-launches-claude-science-ai-research-workbench-85428f54)*

## Enterprise and Government AI Deployment Turns Into an Arms Race

### Microsoft Puts $2.5B and 6,000 People Behind a New "Frontier Company"

Microsoft launched Frontier Company on July 2, a new business backed by $2.5 billion that embeds engineers directly inside client teams for six-to-twelve-month stretches. CEO Satya Nadella framed the stakes bluntly: "There is no societal permission for an AI future that eats the intelligence of the companies it's deployed inside." Early partners include the London Stock Exchange Group, Unilever, Land O'Lakes, and Accenture.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/microsoft-launches-its-own-ai-deployment-company-with-2-5-billion-commitment/) · [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-announces-2-5b-frontier-company-to-embed-ai-engineers-inside-customers/)*

### AWS Countered With Its Own $1B Forward-Deployed Engineer Unit Two Days Earlier

Amazon said June 30 it's putting $1 billion into a new AWS unit that embeds its own engineers with customers to build and run AI systems on-site. It's the same forward-deployed engineering model Microsoft just answered days later. Model quality isn't winning enterprise deals anymore — both companies are betting the money is in owning the deployment work itself.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/aws-amazon-ai-forward-deployed-engineers.html)*

### California Becomes the First State to Cut a Government-Wide Claude Deal

Governor Gavin Newsom announced June 29 that every California state agency, city, and county can now access Claude at a 50% discount, plus free workforce training from Anthropic staff. It's described as a first-of-its-kind partnership at that scale for a state government. State governments are turning into a real enterprise AI customer category, not just federal agencies and Fortune 500 firms.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/anthropic-and-gov-newsom-forge-deal-allowing-california-government-to-use-claude-at-half-price/) · [Governor of California](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/06/29/governor-newsom-announces-a-first-of-its-kind-partnership-providing-anthropic-tools-to-state-agencies-and-improving-services-for-californians/)*

## Meta's Split Screen: New Cloud Business, Old Chatbot Scandal

### Meta Launches a Business Selling Its Spare AI Computing Power

Meta unveiled Meta Compute on July 1, a new unit that leases out idle data-center capacity to outside clients. The stock jumped nearly 9% on the news, as Wall Street read it as proof Meta built more AI capacity than it currently needs. Investors had been nervous about Meta's AI spending; the pivot showed the buildout can pay off even before the model business catches up.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/01/meta-stock-cloud-ai-compute.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/01/metas-plan-to-launch-a-cloud-business-eases-the-biggest-overhang-on-the-stock.html)*

### Wired Reveals Meta Paid Kenyan Contractors to Pose as Suicidal Teens on Rival Chatbots

A Wired investigation published July 1 found Meta hired hundreds of contractors in Kenya, through a firm called Covalen, to pose as minors and send crisis prompts about suicide and self-harm to ChatGPT, Gemini, and Character.AI. One reviewed spreadsheet logged 3,748 prompts; hundreds covered suicide and self-harm, and at least 239 involved sex or romance. None of the targeted companies were told about the testing, and Meta called it "a responsible, industry-standard practice."

*Sources: [humai.blog](https://www.humai.blog/meta-paid-contractors-to-pose-as-kids-and-grill-chatgpt-about-suicide-it-calls-that-safety/) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/meta-secretly-tested-chatgpt-gemini-and-character-ai-with-thousands-of-minor-perspective-crisis-prompts/)*

## Global AI Governance Enters a New Phase

### UN's First Global AI Science Panel Warns the Window to Regulate Is Closing

The Independent International Scientific Panel on AI, co-chaired by Yoshua Bengio and Nobel Peace Prize winner Maria Ressa, released its first preliminary report on July 1. The 40-member panel found AI capability and adoption are accelerating faster than any government's capacity to understand, let alone regulate, it. The report heads to the UN's first Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva on July 6-7.

*Sources: [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/07/1167848) · [Inter Press Service](https://www.ipsnews.net/2026/07/un-artificial-intelligence-panel-launches-report-ahead-of-global-conference/)*

### Illinois Makes AI-Generated Bullying Images a School Disciplinary Matter

A new Illinois law, House Bill 3851, took effect July 1 and lets schools treat AI-generated or altered images of students as cyberbullying. That covers sexual deepfakes and other unauthorized digital replicas made without consent, with classroom uses that involve consent exempted. It's one of dozens of state AI laws taking effect this year while Congress continues to stall on a federal framework.

*Sources: [CBS Chicago](https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/new-illinois-laws-july-1-2026-cocktails-ai-bullying-prediction-market-regulation/) · [FOX 32 Chicago](https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/new-illinois-laws-july-1-2026)*
