Two of tech's loudest billionaires spent the weekend calling each other scammers, Nvidia's stock hit its cheapest valuation in seven years, and Beijing's AI companion ban kicks in tomorrow. Here's what mattered in the last day and a half.

## Musk and Altman's Feud Boils Over — Again

### Apple Sues OpenAI, Alleging a Coordinated Scheme to Steal Hardware Secrets
Apple filed suit in federal court in Northern California on July 10, accusing OpenAI's hardware chief Tang Tan of directing Apple staff to leak secrets during job interviews — even asking candidates to bring "actual parts" to interviews for "show and tell." Apple also says former engineer Chang Liu emailed himself supplier information and walked out with a company laptop. OpenAI says it has "no interest in other companies' trade secrets." The suit is the ugliest turn yet in a partnership that soured after OpenAI bought Jony Ive's hardware startup, io Products, for $6.4 billion.
*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/10/apple-sues-openai-over-alleged-trade-secret-theft/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets-theft-allegations/)*

### Musk and Altman Trade "Scam" Accusations on X
The lawsuit reignited the two founders' running feud over the weekend. "Scam Altman strikes again," Musk posted, later adding Altman "might literally love scamming more than any human alive." Altman shot back, mocking Musk's plan to put data centers in orbit: "homeboy you're the one selling public market investors on short-term space datacenters." Musk invited him to watch a launch in person, "pending approval from his parole officer." Altman closed by joking that the real proof GPT-5.6 Sol is the best model around is that "elon is obsessed with me again."
*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/12/elon-musk-and-sam-altman-spar-.html) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5965640-apple-openai-trade-secret-lawsuit/) · [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/13/apple-sued-openai-for-stealing-secrets-elon-musk-couldnt-resist-piling-on/)*

## Chip Wars: Nvidia Slides as Rivals Chase Independence

### Nvidia Stock Falls to Its Cheapest Valuation Since 2019
Nvidia dropped 3.39% on July 13, part of a two-day tech selloff, and now trades at roughly 18 times projected earnings — cheaper than both the S&P 500 (20x) and the Nasdaq 100 (23x). Investors are citing rising Treasury yields, a more hawkish Fed under new chair Kevin Warsh, reports that SK Hynix is slowing its memory-chip expansion, and hyperscalers building more of their own silicon in-house.
*Sources: [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262027012-market-movers-nvda-20260713) · [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8948989/nvidia-nvda-stock-declines-amid-market-turmoil)*

### DeepSeek Is Quietly Building Its Own AI Chip to Ditch Nvidia and Huawei
DeepSeek has spent roughly a year developing an in-house inference chip, according to Reuters and Semafor reporting, aiming to cut its dependence on both companies. Its R1 model ran on Nvidia's China-only H800 chip before Washington tightened export rules further, and the new chip is designed only to run trained models, not train them. The move puts DeepSeek alongside OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Amazon in building custom silicon.
*Sources: [CyberNews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/deepseek-dependence-nvidia-chips/) · [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/07/08/deepseek-begins-in-house-ai-chip-development-to-cut-reliance-on-nvidia-sources-say/)*

## The Next Model Wave

### Gemini 3.5 Pro Targets July 17 After Google Scrapped Its Original Build
Google reportedly threw out its original Gemini 2.5 Pro-based architecture and rebuilt the model from scratch, pushing the launch to July 17. Leaks point to a 2-million-token context window, a "Deep Think" reasoning layer, and autonomous coding workflows, with pricing rumored around $15/$60 per million input/output tokens and Deep Think gated behind a $250-a-month Ultra tier. None of it — the date, the specs, or the price — has been confirmed by Google.
*Sources: [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm) · [AIToolsRecap](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/gemini-3-5-pro-july-17-launch-specs-pricing-2026)*

### Grok 4.5 Claims "Opus-Class" Performance at a Fraction of the Price
xAI's first release since going public and buying Cursor, Grok 4.5, is priced at $2/$6 per million tokens. Musk says it beats Anthropic's Opus 4.8 on several benchmarks while using fewer tokens and costing less to run. It was trained across tens of thousands of Nvidia GB300 GPUs and is available now in Grok, Cursor, and the xAI console — but not yet in the EU.
*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-releases-grok-4-5-which-elon-describes-as-an-opus-class-model/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-grok-new-model)*

## Regulators and Power Brokers Tighten the Reins

### China's AI Companion Crackdown Takes Effect Tomorrow
Beijing's "Interim Measures for the Administration of AI Anthropomorphic Interactive Services" kicks in July 15, the first national rulebook targeting AI that simulates human relationships. ByteDance's Doubao is killing its custom-persona agent features, and Alibaba's Qwen and Tencent's Yuanbao are doing the same. Doubao users keep read-only access to old chats until October 15, after which the data is gone for good; ByteDance is steering users toward its other app, Maoxiang, instead.
*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-06/bytedance-alibaba-pull-ai-companions-as-beijing-tightens-rules) · [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3359482/bytedance-and-alibaba-disable-humanlike-ai-custom-agents-new-rules-loom) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319703/20260704/china-ai-companion-law-arrives-july-15-doubao-qwen-agent-data-will-deleted.htm)*

### White House Preps Voluntary Pledge to Shield Households From AI Power Costs
The White House plans to bring utilities and data-center developers together on a pledge to keep AI's electricity demand from raising bills for ordinary ratepayers, with an announcement event expected in the coming weeks. The Department of Energy already projects data centers could account for 12% of U.S. electricity demand by 2028, and hyperscaler AI capital spending is set to hit $750 billion this year and top $1 trillion in 2027.
*Sources: [The Daily Signal](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/07/13/white-house-data-centers-ai-pledge/)*
