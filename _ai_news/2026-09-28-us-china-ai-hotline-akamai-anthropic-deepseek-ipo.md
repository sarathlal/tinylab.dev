Trump and Xi just set up a hotline for AI incidents, three of the biggest AI labs want to build their own regulator, and money keeps flowing into everything from cloud contracts to training data. Here's what mattered in AI over the last couple of days.

## Washington and the Labs Both Move to Write AI's Rules

### US and China Agree to an AI "Red Phone" After the Trump-Xi Summit
The US and China just agreed to set up a hotline for AI incidents, the result of a three-day summit between Presidents Trump and Xi in Washington. The White House calls it a "bilateral communication channel for SI incidents," using "SI," for super intelligence, Trump's preferred term for the technology. The two sides also created a "US-China Super Intelligence Dialogue," with its first meeting set for November.

*Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/26/china-us-to-open-ai-communication-channel-after-summit-white-house-says) · [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/china-and-u-s-agree-to-establish-ai-safety-channel-and-continue-trade-and-military-talks) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/26/us-china-ai-si-deal)*

### OpenAI, Google and Anthropic Plan to Build Their Own AI Regulator
OpenAI, Google and Anthropic have approached Sriram Krishnan, the former White House AI policy adviser, to run a self-regulatory body they're calling the Frontier AI Standards Agency. It's modeled on Wall Street's FINRA and could launch by year-end or early 2027, with the labs also courting Condoleezza Rice, Arati Prabhakar and venture capitalist David Friedberg for leadership roles. Critics point out the obvious problem: a safety regulator built and funded by the labs it's meant to police isn't really independent oversight.

*Sources: [BankInfoSecurity](https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/google-openai-anthropic-plan-frontier-ai-standards-body-a-32926) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/google-openai-anthropic-court-sriram-krishnan-for-ai-safety-body)*

## Big Tech's Billion-Dollar Bets, Made and Unmade

### Akamai Signs $11.6B Cloud Deal With Anthropic, Its Biggest Contract Ever
Akamai just landed the largest contract in its history: $11.6 billion over seven years to run Anthropic's CPU workloads, with an option to add $9 billion more. Unlike most AI cloud deals, which chase GPU capacity, this one centers on CPUs, the general-purpose chips Akamai's network already runs on. Akamai also gave Anthropic a warrant for roughly 5% of its shares at $111.33 each, and its stock jumped as much as 20% after-hours on the news.

*Sources: [Akamai](https://www.akamai.com/newsroom/press-release/akamai-announces-11-6-billion-multi-year-agreement-with-anthropic-to-support-growing-demand) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropic-to-pay-akamai-11-6-billion-over-seven-years-in-cloud-deal/)*

### Crusoe Walks Away From Its $1.25B Turbine Deal With Boom Supersonic
Crusoe just killed a $1.25 billion agreement to buy 29 of Boom Supersonic's 42-megawatt turbines for its Abilene, Texas data center campus. Boom CEO Blake Scholl said plainly that "turbines are no longer part of Crusoe's near term primary power mix at Abilene." The cancellation lands weeks after Crusoe closed a $3.9 billion Series F and stepped back from a planned Wyoming AI campus, a pivot toward flexible, modular power over big fixed commitments.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/crusoe-abandons-1-25b-plan-to-use-boom-turbines-at-ai-data-centers/) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/crusoe-kills-125b-boom-supersonic-turbine-deal-weeks-after-closing-39b-series-f)*

### Meta Unveils a Palm-Sized Pendant So You Never Have to Open an App to Talk to Its AI
Meta's new Muse Charm is a keychain-sized pendant with a roughly 2-inch screen and a fingerprint sensor that opens a live voice channel to its Muse AI agent. Zuckerberg said "if you're not wearing glasses, this is going to be by far the fastest way to talk to your Muse and to show it what's going on around you." It ships in time for the holidays, price still unannounced, alongside the $1,299 Meta VR Glasses unveiled at the same event.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/mark-zuckerberg-1299-meta-vr-glasses-ai-agent.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/meta-debuts-a-dedicated-palm-sized-muse-charm-device-to-use-ai-on-the-go)*

## The AI Money Machine Keeps Humming

### DeepSeek's Revenue Run Rate Hits $1B as It Preps a $7.5B Shanghai Listing
DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate has more than doubled to $1 billion in just a few months, after CEO Liang Wenfeng raised API prices 2.3x to 4.5x last month. The company kept its customers despite the hikes and now runs at 82.9% gross margins on its API business. It's now lining up a fresh raise of 50 billion yuan, about $7.5 billion, at a 500 billion yuan valuation, aiming to file for a Shanghai STAR Market listing by year-end and go public in 2027.

*Sources: [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/deepseek-doubles-annual-revenue-run-rate-to-1-billion-ahead-of-ipo/) · [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/info-1jq5etc-deepseeks-annualized-revenue-hits-1-billion-as-startup-finalizes-7-5-bil/)*

### OpenEvidence Raises $250M at $15B, Up 25% Since January
OpenEvidence, the clinical search chatbot known as "ChatGPT for doctors," raised $250 million at a $15 billion valuation, up from $12 billion in January. Backers this round include Andreessen Horowitz, Byers Capital and undisclosed hospital systems. The company says 40% of US physicians now use it and it topped $100 million in annual revenue last year, up from a $1 billion valuation just twelve months ago.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/health-tech-deals/2026/09/25/openevidence-250m-raise-15b-valuation-a16z) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/21/openevidence-chatgpt-for-doctors-doubles-valuation-to-12-billion.html)*

### Snorkel AI Triples Its Valuation to $3.5B as Labs Pay Up for Training Data
Snorkel AI raised $350 million at a $3.5 billion valuation, nearly triple the $1.3 billion mark it hit in its last round in May 2025. Insight Partners and S32 led the round, joined by Addition, Lightspeed, Greylock and GV. Annualized revenue hit $375 million, an 18-fold jump in a year, as the company shifts from selling data-labeling software to selling finished, ready-to-train datasets outright.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/snorkel-ai-triples-valuation-to-3-5b-as-demand-for-ai-training-data-booms/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/snorkel-ai-raises-350m-series-e-at-3-5b-valuation-to-build-the-frontier-lab-for-ai-data/)*
