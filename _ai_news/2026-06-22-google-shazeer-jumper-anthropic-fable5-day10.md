Google lost two of its most celebrated AI researchers to rival labs in a 48-hour stretch — while Anthropic's top models stayed offline for a tenth straight day under an unprecedented U.S. export ban.

## Google Drops Two Stars to Rivals in 48 Hours

### Noam Shazeer, Transformer Co-Author and Gemini Co-Lead, Joins OpenAI

Noam Shazeer announced on June 18 that he's leaving Google DeepMind for OpenAI — a move Sam Altman called "only 10 years in the making." Shazeer co-wrote the 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper that invented the transformer, served as Gemini co-lead and VP of Engineering, and had only returned to Google roughly two years ago when it acquired his startup Character.ai. His role at OpenAI hasn't been disclosed.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/06/53269428/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-joins-openai-sam-altman-says-its-10-years-in-the-making) · [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/star-google-ai-researcher-shazeer-joins-openai)*

### Nobel Laureate John Jumper Leaves DeepMind for Anthropic

One day later, John Jumper announced he's leaving DeepMind after nearly nine years to join Anthropic. Jumper shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for co-creating AlphaFold2, which has been used by more than two million scientists in 190 countries to predict protein structures. Anthropic hasn't disclosed his role, but the hire fits its push into life sciences and computational biology — and comes as it prepares for an IPO at a $965 billion valuation.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/john-jumper-nobel-deepmind-leaves-anthropic-alphafold) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318754/20260620/alphafold-nobel-laureate-john-jumper-joins-anthropic-after-nine-years-deepmind.htm)*

## Fable 5 Still Offline — Anthropic Hints at What's Next

### Day 10: No Restoration Date, Anthropic Disputes the Jailbreak Claim

Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 remain suspended for all users as of June 22 — ten days after the Commerce Department issued an export control directive over a possible cybersecurity jailbreak. The concern: the model could be prompted to read a codebase and identify software vulnerabilities. Anthropic argues GPT-5.5 and other unrestricted models share that capability. Senior engineers met Commerce officials face-to-face on June 16, Trump says talks are "going fine," and Anthropic's Chris Ciauri told reporters at the Seoul office opening that restoration was expected "in the coming days." No official date exists.

*Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/13/us-orders-anthropic-to-disable-ai-models-for-all-foreign-nationals) · [Anthropic on X](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2065597531644743999) · [explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/is-fable-5-back-2026)*

### Privacy Policy Update and Closing Free Trial Signal a Path Forward

Anthropic quietly updated its privacy policy — effective July 8 — to allow collection of government-issued ID, biometrics, and facial geometry. The change points toward a likely resolution: restore Fable 5 access for U.S. citizens who verify their identity, without requiring the directive to be fully lifted for everyone. Separately, today marks the close of the Fable 5 free-trial window promised to paid Claude subscribers. The trial ran June 9–22 on paper; neither model was ever actually available to use.

## AI's Growing Footprint — Grids, News, and Hardware

### FERC Orders Six Grid Operators to Reform Rules for AI Data Centers

The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission issued show cause orders to six U.S. regional grid operators, directing them to defend current interconnection frameworks or propose reforms to speed grid access for large-load customers. FERC Chair Laura Swett called it a "national priority." AI data centers now drive a large share of new interconnection requests, and queues currently stretch years.

*Sources: [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-21-2026)*

### ChatGPT's Global Market Share Falls Below 50% for the First Time

ChatGPT held 46.4% of the global AI assistant market by late May 2026 — the first time it's held less than half since launch. Claude, Gemini, and a growing field of alternatives are each taking share as overall usage expands. It's a symbolic threshold, but it signals the winner-take-all dynamic that defined 2023–24 is over.

*Sources: [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-21-2026)*

### 1 in 10 People Now Get News from AI Chatbots Weekly

Ten percent of global internet users now rely on AI chatbots for news at least once a week, up from 7% a year ago. Only 4% of those users regularly click through to original sources — meaning AI is increasingly the last stop in news consumption, not a gateway to publishers. The trend is accelerating as AI summary quality improves and search traffic to news sites declines.

*Sources: [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-21-2026)*

### Google's First Smart Speaker in Six Years Ships Thursday at $99

The new Google Home Speaker goes on sale June 25 for $99.99 — the first audio device Google has shipped in six years. Gemini replaces Google Assistant as the voice interface, with 10 new voices and 360-degree audio. An optional $10/month subscription adds Gemini Live, camera history search, and daily home briefs. The launch lands while Gemini's own co-lead just walked out the door.

*Sources: [Google Blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/google-home-speaker-gemini-features/) · [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/accessories/smart-home/google-home-speaker-launched) · [Eastern Herald](https://easternherald.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-home-speaker-launch-2026/)*
