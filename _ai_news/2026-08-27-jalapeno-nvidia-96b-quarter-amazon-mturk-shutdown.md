Nvidia posted a record quarter and got a real challenge to its inference crown on the very same day. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## Nvidia's Record Quarter Collides With a Homegrown Rival

### Nvidia Posts $96.2B Quarter, Guides Above $100B for the First Time

Nvidia's revenue hit $96.2 billion in the quarter ended July 26, up 106% from a year earlier. Data center sales alone came in at $89 billion, up 117%. Nvidia guided next quarter's revenue to $108 billion, plus or minus 2%, topping the roughly $105 billion Wall Street expected and the first time it has pointed past $100 billion. CEO Jensen Huang told analysts he expects the Vera Rubin chip, shipping in Q3, to outpace Blackwell and open up a new $200 billion CPU market.

*Sources: [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027) · [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/nvidia-nvda-q2-fy27-earnings-results/)*

### OpenAI's Jalapeño Chip Beats Nvidia's Blackwell on Power Efficiency

OpenAI's first custom chip, built with Broadcom and code-named Jalapeño, delivers 1.5 to 1.9 times more AI work per watt than Nvidia's Blackwell in independent benchmarks. The tests ran on SemiAnalysis's InferenceX suite, with some runs verified on-site. Nvidia's newer Vera Rubin chip closes most of the gap and still edges out Jalapeño on total cost per token, but it's the clearest sign yet that Nvidia's inference lead has real competition. Jalapeño is set to start shipping in OpenAI's own data centers by the end of 2026.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/openai-jalapeno-ai-chip-nvidia.html) · [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia)*

## AI Labs Go Vertical, Into Legal Contracts and Cyber Defense

### Google Launches Gemini Enterprise for Legal With Four Elite Firms

Google Cloud rolled out a legal-specific version of Gemini Enterprise built to review contracts, redline documents, and scan for regulatory changes. Cleary Gottlieb, Freshfields, Weil, and Williams & Connolly signed on as launch partners, and the product plugs into tools firms already run, like iManage, NetDocuments, and RelativityOne. Cleary managing partner Jeff Karpf said the tool "can slot in seamlessly with other daily work tools" to unlock efficiency for legal teams.

*Sources: [Google Cloud Blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/introducing-gemini-enterprise-for-legal) · [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/google-cloud-rolls-gemini-enterprise-090220861.html)*

### Anthropic Adds Claude Mythos 5 to Its Cybersecurity Product

Anthropic expanded Claude Security for enterprise customers by adding its Claude Mythos 5 model, which now runs codebase scans, flags vulnerabilities, and suggests patches. The company also launched a Defender Advantage Fund and is widening its Cyber Verification Program. Claude's connectors directory has quietly grown past 950 MCP servers, used daily by millions of people.

*Sources: [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic) · [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)*

## AI Money Goes Global, From Riyadh to Seoul

### Mistral Partners With Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN on Arabic AI

Mistral AI struck a deal with HUMAIN, the Saudi state-backed AI company, to build Arabic-language models and expand computing capacity across the region. The partnership covers infrastructure, model development, and deployment of AI tools in Saudi Arabia and nearby markets, Mistral's clearest bet yet on regional AI sovereignty over exporting one global model.

*Sources: [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/ai-now-summit-2026/)*

### Korea's Wrtn Becomes First Local AI Startup Past a $722M Valuation

Wrtn Technologies raised roughly $72 million in a Series C round that pushed its valuation past 1 trillion won, or about $722 million, a first for a Korean AI service startup. New investors Coreline Ventures and Eugene Asset Management joined existing backers including Goodwater Capital and Korea Development Bank. Wrtn's U.S.-focused AI entertainment app OOC crossed $7.2 million in monthly revenue within three months of its May launch.

*Sources: [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260826/wrtn-raises-72-mil-in-series-c-funding-round) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/korean-ai-startup-wrtn-raises-funds-at-870-million-value-to-fund-global-growth)*

## AI's Reality Check: Old Work Ends, New Hype Cools

### Amazon Shuts Down Mechanical Turk After 21 Years

Amazon will close Mechanical Turk on September 30, ending the crowdsourced task platform Jeff Bezos once called "artificial artificial intelligence." The service launched in 2005, once had more than 500,000 workers, and paid people cents at a time for small tasks that helped train early machine-learning systems. Amazon quietly stopped taking new customers on July 30 — the labeling work MTurk pioneered has since moved to specialized shops like Scale AI and Mercor, or been automated outright.

*Sources: [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91596625/amazon-is-shutting-down-mechanical-turk-after-21-years-quietly-ending-the-human-powered-platform) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/amazon/2026/amazon-sunsets-crowd-sourced-work-platform-mturk/)*

### Unitree's Stock Keeps Sinking After Its 460% IPO Pop

Unitree shares have fallen about 45% from their post-IPO peak, wiping out more than 200 billion yuan ($28 billion) in market value in under a week. The stock rocketed 460% on its Shanghai debut on August 19, but the reversal followed Unitree's own admission that humanoid robots aren't ready for broad factory use, plus a 53% drop in adjusted first-quarter profit. It's the clearest gut-check yet on how far humanoid robotics still is from real commercial deployment.

*Sources: [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/08/25/unitree-stock-crashes-45-after-460-debut-as-chinas-robot-frenzy-meets-reality/) · [Colombia One](https://colombiaone.com/2026/08/25/unitrees-stock-plunge-ipo-raises-fears-robotics/)*
