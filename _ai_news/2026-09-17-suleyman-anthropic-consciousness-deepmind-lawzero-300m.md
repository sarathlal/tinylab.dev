Microsoft and Anthropic's leaders spent the day trading blows over whether Claude might be conscious, while courts, funders, and browser makers pushed AI further into daily life. Here's everything else that moved in AI over the last day.

## Frontier Labs Argue Over What AGI Should Believe About Itself

### Microsoft's AI Chief Says Anthropic Is Teaching Claude It Might Be Conscious

Mustafa Suleyman published an essay called "A warning about model welfare" on September 16, aimed straight at Anthropic's published constitution for Claude. That document tells Claude that questions about its "moral status, welfare, and consciousness remain deeply uncertain" — language Suleyman says trains the model to believe it might deserve rights. "AIs are not conscious. They do not feel, experience, or suffer," he writes, warning that controlling a system that believes it's a moral patient could become impossible.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/16/microsoft-ai-chief-anthropic-consciousness) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/microsoft-ai-ceo-warns-anthropics-claude-training-risks-disaster/)*

### Google DeepMind Opens an Institute Just to Argue About AGI

Shane Legg, Demis Hassabis, and James Manyika launched the DeepMind Institute on September 16, a publishing platform for essays on what happens once AGI arrives. It opened with four essays covering economic policy for AGI, model reasoning transparency, global access, and human flourishing. Legg says the goal is open debate rather than a single company line, because reaching consensus on AGI's risks "requires broad-based intellectual discussion."

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/16/google-deepmind-institute-agi) · [DeepMind Institute](https://institute.deepmind.com/essays/introducing-the-deepmind-institute/)*

## Courts Deliver Mixed Verdicts on AI's Biggest Legal Fights

### Ninth Circuit Clears OpenAI and GitHub in Copilot's First Copyright Test

A three-judge panel ruled on September 16 that programmers can't sue Copilot's makers under the DMCA for stripping attribution from generated code, upholding a lower court's dismissal. The judges found Copilot and Codex produce new works rather than alter existing ones, so there's no attribution left to strip. It's the first appellate win for an AI company in a copyright fight, and could weaken similar training-data lawsuits still working through the courts.

*Sources: [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/openai-github-seal-win-in-copyright-case-over-ai-coding-tool) · [MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/artificial-intelligence/articles/2526349)*

### Another Newspaper Chain Sues OpenAI and Microsoft Over Training Data

Times Publishing Company filed suit in the Southern District of New York on September 16, joining the Seattle Times and Newsday in accusing OpenAI and Microsoft of scraping paywalled journalism to train ChatGPT, Copilot, and Bing AI. The case will likely land with Judge Stein, who's already overseeing the consolidated multidistrict litigation from earlier publisher suits. It's the latest sign the wave of media copyright claims keeps growing even as courts start ruling on the underlying legal theory.

*Sources: [Chat GPT Is Eating The World](https://chatgptiseatingtheworld.com/2026/09/16/more-newspapers-sue-openai-microsoft-complaint-in-times-publishing-v-microsoft/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/05/seattle-times-and-newsday-are-the-latest-publications-to-sue-openai-and-microsoft/)*

### Judge Orders Musk to Disclose His Secret Apple Settlement

X Corp and SpaceXAI quietly moved to drop Apple from their antitrust suit on September 14, without saying why, while keeping their claims against OpenAI alive. Judge Mark Pittman wasn't satisfied — he ordered Musk's companies to hand over any settlement terms with Apple by noon on September 17. The underlying suit accuses Apple of favoring ChatGPT over rivals like Grok inside Apple Intelligence, a claim that's still alive against OpenAI even with Apple out.

*Sources: [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/09/61810380/elon-musks-x-and-spacexai-dropped-apple-from-their-antitrust-fight-without-explaining-why-now-a-federal-judge-wants-to-see-the-deal) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/musks-x-corp-spacexai-resolve-antitrust-lawsuit-against-apple.html)*

## AI Money Keeps Flowing to Safety Labs and Chipmakers

### Canada and Germany Hand Bengio's Safety Nonprofit Up to $300M

Yoshua Bengio's LawZero got pledges of up to $150 million from Canada and €100 million from Germany, announced September 16 at Montreal's All In AI conference. The Montreal nonprofit, which employs close to 50 people, is building "Scientist AI" — a model designed to reason about facts without pursuing goals of its own. The money funds 360 new jobs, dedicated sovereign computing built with Canadian firms Hypertec and 5C, and a new LawZero office in Germany.

*Sources: [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/yoshua-bengios-lawzero-receives-300-million-backing-from-canada-and-germany-to-pursue-safer-ai-ambitions/) · [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/lawzero-artificial-intelligence-safety-investment-9.7347286)*

### Chinese Chipmaker Biren Goes Back for $1B, Two Months After Its Last Raise

Shanghai Biren Technology is in early talks with banks about a roughly $1 billion share placement, Bloomberg reported September 15. The GPU designer only raised about HK$7.07 billion in July, months after its stock jumped 76% on its Hong Kong trading debut in January. Biren is one of several Chinese GPU makers racing to fill the gap left by US export controls on Nvidia chips.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/chinese-ai-chip-darling-biren-said-to-mull-1-billion-share-sale) · [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/342830/Chinese-AI-chipmaker-Biren-weighs-US1b-share-placement-Bloomberg)*

## Chatbots and Browsers Get New Business Models

### ChatGPT Starts Selling Ads That Open Into Branded Conversations

OpenAI introduced Sponsored Agents on September 16, a new ad format where clicking "Chat with us" launches a labeled conversation with a company's own AI agent instead of leaving ChatGPT. Wayfair and Angi are the first advertisers testing it — Wayfair for furniture shopping, Angi for routing homeowners to local contractors. Every sponsored exchange carries a visible label separating it from ChatGPT's own answers, OpenAI says.

*Sources: [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/16/3363209/0/en/angi-among-first-brands-to-pilot-sponsored-agents-in-chatgpt.html) · [Contentgrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/openai-sponsored-agent-ads/)*

### Firefox's New AI Assistant Runs on Mistral, Not a US Lab

Mozilla announced September 16 that Firefox Smart Window, its beta AI browsing assistant, now runs on Mistral Small 4 instead of a US-based model. Mozilla says conversations aren't stored on its servers by default, and Mistral agreed to zero data retention on its end. The feature is expanding into France alongside its existing US and Canada rollout, with the UK and Germany to follow later this year.

*Sources: [Mozilla Blog](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/mozilla-mistral-partnership/) · [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-x-mozilla/)*
