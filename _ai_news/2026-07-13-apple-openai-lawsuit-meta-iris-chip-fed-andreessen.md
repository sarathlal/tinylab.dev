Apple and OpenAI went from partners to courtroom rivals this week, Meta put real money behind ditching Nvidia, and the Fed just handed AI policy input to a venture capitalist. Here's what mattered in the last day and a half.

## OpenAI's Legal Troubles Pile Up

### Apple Sues OpenAI, Says Scheme to Steal Hardware Secrets Ran "At Every Level"

Apple filed suit against OpenAI in Northern California federal court on July 10, accusing it of stealing trade secrets to build its own hardware. The complaint centers on Chang Liu, an eight-year Apple engineer who left for OpenAI in 2026, kept his company laptop, and messaged a former colleague "LOL, I found out I can access the [network storage], so funny" before downloading hardware designs and manufacturing details. Apple says OpenAI hardware chief Tang Tan, a former Apple VP, used candidate interviews to extract product information, and calls OpenAI's hardware unit "rotten to its core." OpenAI says it has "no interest in other companies' trade secrets."

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/10/apple-sues-openai-over-alleged-trade-secret-theft/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets.html)*

### NYT-Led Coalition Asks Judge to Sanction OpenAI Over Hidden Evidence

The New York Times, Daily News, Ziff Davis and other publishers asked a federal judge on July 9 to sanction OpenAI in their copyright case. They say OpenAI told the court it couldn't search its models for copyrighted text while it had already done so, and that it deleted or hid billions of ChatGPT conversations relevant to the case. The publishers want OpenAI to pay the attorney fees spent chasing the withheld evidence. OpenAI says it's protecting user privacy and that the Times is making "blatantly false allegations" as its claims weaken.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/07/09/openai-new-york-times-ai-copyright-lawsuit/1f749fa0-7ba4-11f1-b194-f872dd4ec5aa_story.html) · [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/new-york-times-news-outlets-accuse-openai-of-lying-lawsuit-1236805648/)*

## The Infrastructure Arms Race Escalates

### Meta's In-House "Iris" AI Chip Enters Mass Production in September

Meta will start mass-producing its in-house AI chip, codenamed Iris, in September, according to an internal memo Reuters obtained. Broadcom helped design it and TSMC will manufacture it. The chip passed testing in six weeks with no major issues, a fast turnaround for an effort that's struggled for five years. Meta's plan calls for 7 gigawatts of compute capacity online in 2026, doubling to 14 gigawatts by 2027 — not to replace Nvidia GPUs outright, but to cut costs and reduce dependence on them. The stock jumped nearly 6% on the news.

*Sources: [US News (Reuters)](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-07-09/exclusive-meta-to-put-ai-chip-into-production-in-september-as-it-looks-to-double-computing-capacity-memo-shows) · [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/10/mark-zuckerberg-is-turning-meta-into-a-bigger-chip/)*

### Meta Puts $10B Into Its First Canadian Data Center, Paired With a New Gas Plant

Meta is spending roughly $9-10 billion on a 1-gigawatt data center in Sturgeon County, Alberta — its 33rd data center and first in Canada. The project comes with a new 932-megawatt natural gas power plant that can expand past 1.8 gigawatts later. Construction needs 3,000 workers and will take two to three years; the finished site will employ 300 people full-time.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/meta-is-building-its-first-big-data-center-in-canada-amid-ai-push.html) · [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-meta-ai-data-centre-sturgeon-county-alberta/)*

### SambaNova Raises $1B at $11B Valuation, Five Months After Its Last Round

SambaNova closed the first tranche of a $1 billion Series F led by General Atlantic, valuing the AI chip startup at $11 billion — up from a $350 million raise just five months ago. BlackRock, Intel Capital, T. Rowe Price, and Qatar's sovereign fund joined in. JPMorganChase picked SambaNova as an inference infrastructure partner, deploying its SN40 and SN50 systems for on-prem AI. A second close is expected in the coming weeks.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/ai-chip-startup-sambanova-raises-funds-at-11-billion-valuation) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/sambanova-draws-1b-at-11b-valuation-in-series-f-first-close/)*

## Frontier Model Race Hits Its Busiest Week Yet

### Google Scraps Gemini 3.5 Pro's Base Model, Pushes Launch to July 17

Google DeepMind delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro to July 17 after scrapping its planned architecture for a full rebuild. Enterprise testers flagged coding gaps, token-inefficiency, and weak long-task reasoning that fell short of Google's flagship bar. Ditching a completed pre-training run cost hundreds of millions of dollars and months of work. The rebuilt model targets a 2-million-token context window and a new "Deep Think" reasoning layer, landing five days after GPT-5.6 and nine days after Grok 4.5.

*Sources: [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a) · [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model)*

### OpenAI Ships GPT-5.6 in Three Tiers, Launches ChatGPT Work

OpenAI released GPT-5.6 on July 9 as three models: Sol for frontier reasoning, Terra for everyday use at half the cost of GPT-5.5, and Luna, its cheapest and fastest tier. OpenAI calls it "the strongest cybersecurity model yet." Alongside it, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work, which pulls in a team's tools and drafts to finish tasks rather than just answer questions. The government had delayed public release from late June over a Trump administration cybersecurity review.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/openai-launches-its-new-family-of-models-with-gpt-5-6/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/09/ai-openai-gpt-release)*

## Washington and Central Banks Zero In on AI's Economic Risks

### Fed Taps a16z's Andreessen to Co-Lead Its New AI and Jobs Task Force

The Federal Reserve named Marc Andreessen on July 9 to co-lead a new Productivity and Jobs task force under incoming Chair Kevin Warsh, one of five external panels set up to review monetary policy. His co-leads are Stanford economist Charles Jones, currently on leave at Anthropic, and Microsoft's Asha Sharma. The group's job: assess how AI and other general-purpose technologies should shape Fed policy, with recommendations due by year-end. Warsh and Andreessen have been friends for 30 years.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/07/09/federal-reserve-enlists-marc-andreessen-advise-ai-under-warsh/) · [TFTC](https://www.tftc.io/andreessen-fed-ai-productivity-task-force-warsh)*

### Taiwan's Central Bank Chief Warns Over-Leveraged AI Spending Could Snap Back

Taiwan's central bank governor warned this week that debt-financed AI capital spending needs close monitoring, saying "AI is driven by real growth potential, but it's the possibility of over-expansion via over-leveraging that concerns us." The warning echoes the Bank for International Settlements, which flagged that the five largest hyperscalers are on pace to spend over $1 trillion combined on AI capex in 2025-2026, a sum already outpacing their earnings and free cash flow.

*Sources: [TaiwanPlus](https://www.taiwanplus.com/news/newscasts/heres-what-happened/260711011/central-bank-chief-warns-of-ai-bubble-risks-july-12-2026) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/taiwan-central-bank-chief-warns-075123349.html)*
