Anthropic's flagship models came back online after an 18-day government freeze, and two more labs pushed pricier compute problems onto customers and partners. Here's what mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## Anthropic's Whiplash Week

### Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Return After an 18-Day Export Standoff

The Commerce Department froze foreign access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 on June 12, citing a possible jailbreak vulnerability. Anthropic took both models offline worldwide rather than risk violating the order — it said there was no way to filter users by nationality in real time. Eighteen days later, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick said the agency had "worked closely with Anthropic to analyze and approve Fable 5," and access returns globally this week.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-says-trump-admin-has-lifted-export-controls-on-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5.html) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/1/us-lifts-restrictions-on-powerful-ai-models-fable-mythos-anthropic-says)*

### Sonnet 5 Becomes Anthropic's Default Model

Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30 and made it the default for Free and Pro users the very next day. The company calls it "the most agentic Sonnet ever built," running close to flagship Opus 4.8 on many tasks. It costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through August 31, then jumps to $3 and $15.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-as-a-cheaper-way-to-run-agents/)*

## Frontier Labs Ship New Models Under Tighter Wraps

### GPT-5.6 Lands for Just 20 Partners, Not the Public

OpenAI is letting roughly 20 trusted organizations try GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna, after coordinating the rollout with the US government. Sol runs $5 per million input tokens and $30 per million output tokens, aimed at hard problems like security research, while Terra and Luna cost less for everyday business work. Sol ships with what OpenAI calls its most robust safety stack yet, including classifiers that watch for cyber and bio misuse in real time during inference.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov)*

### Google's Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image Cuts Costs to $0.034 per 1,000 Images

Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image, nicknamed Nano Banana 2 Lite, on June 30. It generates images in about four seconds at a flat $0.034 per 1,000 images, undercutting the standard Gemini 3.1 Flash Image tier, which runs $0.045 to $0.151 per image depending on resolution. It's live now in Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/google-unveils-nano-banana-2-lite-aka-gemini-3-1-flash-lite-for-low-cost-4-second-fast-enterprise-image-generations) · [Google Cloud Docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/gemini/3-1-flash-image)*

## The Enterprise AI Deployment Arms Race

### AWS Puts $1B Behind an Army of Embedded AI Engineers

AWS is investing $1 billion in a new Forward Deployed Engineering unit, embedding pods of five or six engineers directly inside customer companies. VP Francesca Vasquez says the goal is to compress deployment timelines from months to days, then leave customers self-sufficient. The Allen Institute, Cox Automotive, the NBA, the NFL, Ricoh, and Southwest Airlines are already working with AWS teams, joining a race Anthropic and OpenAI started with their own forward-deployment joint ventures, valued at $1.5B and $4B.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/aws-amazon-ai-forward-deployed-engineers.html) · [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/aws-1-billion-forward-deployed-ai-engineers)*

### California Gets Claude at Half Price in New Anthropic Deal

Governor Newsom announced June 29 that every California state agency and local government can now use Claude at a 50% discount. State workers also get free training and workflow help from Anthropic — the DMV and the Department of Health Care Services are already piloting it internally. The deal comes months after Anthropic walked away from a Pentagon contract over surveillance and autonomous-weapons language Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth wouldn't budge on; the Pentagon signed with OpenAI instead.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/anthropic-and-gov-newsom-forge-deal-allowing-california-government-to-use-claude-at-half-price/) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/newsoms-office-touts-anthropic-partnership-50-discount-claude-ai-california-agencies-localities)*

## New State AI Laws Take Effect

### Illinois Redefines Cyberbullying to Cover AI-Generated Images

Illinois' HB 3851 took effect July 1, expanding school cyberbullying rules to cover unauthorized AI-generated images of students. State Sen. Meg Loughran Cappel, who sponsored the bill, said "many people are using AI to produce harmful materials, which has led to a whole new level of cyberbullying." Schools now have clearer authority to investigate AI-deepfake incidents, though classroom use with consent is exempt.

*Sources: [CBS Chicago](https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/new-illinois-laws-july-1-2026-cocktails-ai-bullying-prediction-market-regulation/) · [WIFR](https://www.wifr.com/2026/06/25/new-illinois-law-expands-cyberbullying-definition-include-ai-generated-images/)*

### Tennessee Bans AI From Posing as a Therapist

Tennessee's SB 1580 also took effect July 1, barring any AI system from advertising itself as a licensed mental health professional. Violations run up to $5,000 each under the state's consumer protection law, and the bill includes a private right of action. It's part of a wider wave of state chatbot rules this year, following California's SB 243, which already requires companion chatbots to flag self-harm signals from minors.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lanceeliot/2026/04/20/tennessee-becomes-another-state-to-enact-new-law-that-restricts-ai-acting-as-a-mental-health-advisor/) · [Troutman Pepper](https://www.troutmanprivacy.com/2026/04/tennessee-enacts-health-care-ai-bill-with-private-right-of-action/)*
