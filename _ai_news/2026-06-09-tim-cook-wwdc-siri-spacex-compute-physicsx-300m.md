Yesterday's Apple keynote was Tim Cook's last as CEO — and he used it to announce the biggest Siri overhaul in the assistant's 15-year history, backed by Google's cloud. Meanwhile, AI compute is becoming a strategic commodity, a physics-AI startup doubled its valuation in a year, and models keep moving quietly into new roles.

## Apple's AI Moment at WWDC26

### Siri Gets a Full Rebuild, Powered by Google's Infrastructure

Apple shipped a redesigned Siri at WWDC26 on June 8 — the biggest update since the assistant launched in 2011. The new version keeps conversation history, understands what's on your screen, and pulls personal context from your calendar and messages. The heavy compute runs on Apple Foundation Models on Cloud (AFM Cloud Pro), built in partnership with Google using NVIDIA GPUs in Google's data centers. It's a striking pivot for a company that built its AI pitch on on-device privacy.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/08/apple-wwdc-2026-live-updates.html) · [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/08/tech/apple-wwdc-tim-cook) · [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/08/nx-s1-5847937/apple-wwdc-2026-siri-ai-tim-cook)*

### Tim Cook Steps Down as CEO After 15 Years — WWDC Was His Final Keynote

Cook confirmed this is his last WWDC as Apple's chief executive. He moves to Executive Chairman in September, with hardware chief John Ternus taking the top job. Cook spent 15 years building Apple into the world's first $3 trillion company. Ternus inherits an AI race that Apple joined late, with Siri still catching up to rivals that shipped capable assistants years earlier.

*Sources: [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/live-news/apple-tim-cook-wwdc26-keynote-iphone-ai-tech-june-8) · [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/wwdc26-keynote-tim-cook-opens-final-wwdc-as-apple-ceo-macos-golden-gate-revealed/)*

## Compute Is the New Currency

### Google Is Paying SpaceX $920M a Month for Access to xAI's Data Centers

Google signed a $30 billion deal with SpaceX for roughly 110,000 NVIDIA GPUs at xAI's Colossus facilities, running from October 2026 through June 2029 at $920 million a month. Google calls it a bridge for unexpected demand surging through Gemini Enterprise. The deal went public one week before SpaceX's Nasdaq IPO, which targets a $1.75 trillion valuation. Anthropic already locked in a larger deal — $1.25 billion a month for about twice the compute — turning xAI's infrastructure into an unexpected landlord for its two biggest rivals.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-05/google-buying-computing-from-spacex-in-920-million-a-month-deal) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/google-to-pay-spacex-920-million-a-month-for-xai-compute-capacity.html)*

### PhysicsX Raises $300M at $2.4B to Simulate Physics for Industrial Manufacturers

London-based PhysicsX closed a $300 million Series C on June 8, led by Temasek, with NVIDIA and Siemens among returning investors. Founded in 2019 by former Formula 1 engineers Jacomo Corbo and Robin Tuluie, the company builds AI models that simulate physical systems — aerodynamic testing for drones, engine components, and industrial equipment without expensive physical prototypes. Its valuation more than doubled from under $1 billion at its Series B last year. Headcount jumped from 150 to 350 in twelve months.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-08/startup-physicsx-hits-2-4-billion-valuation-to-provide-ai-for-manufacturing) · [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/physicsx-funding-round-temasek-valuation)*

## Models Moving Quietly and Loudly

### GPT-5.5 Instant Is Now ChatGPT's Default Model

OpenAI updated its default ChatGPT model to GPT-5.5 Instant on June 8, replacing GPT-5.3 Instant with no announcement — just a release note. The update brings sharper answers, better STEM and image handling, and tighter web search integration. Plus and Pro users get expanded context handling. A frontier-capable model becoming the baseline with a changelog entry is a sign of how fast the floor is rising.

*Sources: [OpenAI Release Notes via Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai)*

### Gemini 3.5 Pro Is Close — 2 Million Tokens, Deep Think Reasoning

Gemini 3.5 Pro is in limited enterprise preview on Vertex and expected to reach general availability this month. A TechTimes report from June 6 confirmed the model ships with a 2 million token context window and a new Deep Think reasoning mode. Sundar Pichai told the Google I/O audience in May to "give us until next month" — that month is now more than half gone. A mid-to-late June release looks likely.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-june-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm)*

### OpenAI Opens Its Cybersecurity Model to EU Security Agencies

OpenAI extended limited access to GPT-5.5-Cyber across Europe under a new EU Cyber Action Plan, giving vetted defenders — governments, businesses, and EU institutions including the EU AI Office — reduced classifier-based refusals for authorized work like vulnerability research and malware analysis. Hard blocks on credential theft and malware deployment stay in place. Anthropic moved first: it gave ENISA access to Claude Mythos on June 1. Both labs are now competing on which governments trust them with their most sensitive AI workflows.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317891/20260605/openai-gpt-55-cyber-reaches-eu-anthropic-mythos-opens-enisa-days-later.htm) · [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/openai-gpt-5-5-cyber-eu-rollout/)*
