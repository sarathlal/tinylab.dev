Microsoft's CEO just sided with Anthropic over the White House on how fast AI should move. Here's what else moved in AI over the last day.

## The AI Slowdown Debate Splits Washington, Wall Street, and Now Microsoft

### Nadella Breaks With Trump, Backs "Deliberate Pacing" on AI

Satya Nadella posted on X Sunday that Microsoft welcomes "deliberate pacing" in AI development. He said Microsoft will publish a Code of Conduct for its own MAI models today for public consultation, and floated "embedded evaluators" to check frontier systems before release. His post responded directly to Dario Amodei's essay "We Must Pace the Frontier," making Microsoft the first Big Tech giant to publicly back Amodei's slowdown push.

*Sources: [ANI](https://aninews.in/news/business/microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-backs-need-for-evaluators-for-ai-systems-amid-slowdown-debate20260914051616/) · [Anadolu Agency](https://www.anews.com.tr/tech/2026/09/14/microsoft-chief-backs-pacing-ai-independent-evaluators-for-superintelligence)*

### Trump Calls AI Slowdown Warnings "Very Negative Forces"

Trump told reporters Sunday that AI critics raise scenarios that "will not happen," dismissing the same warnings Nadella had just endorsed. "We're leading China in AI... whoever wins AI wins," he said, rejecting calls to slow development while leaving the door open to guardrails. The comments put the White House at odds with Amodei, Altman, Musk, and now Nadella over how fast AI should move.

*Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/13/nx-s1-5968078/trump-mike-johnson-ai-slowdown) · [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/sci-tech/article/trump-says-very-negative-forces-raising-exaggerated-concerns-over-ai/)*

### Wall Street Wobbles as AI Safety Fears Spread

Nasdaq 100 futures fell more than 1% and S&P 500 contracts dropped 0.6% Monday after the weekend's slowdown warnings rattled investors. Asia's MSCI Pacific index slid 0.5%, led down by Japan and South Korea. The sector that powered most of 2026's stock rally is now the one investors are second-guessing.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-13/us-stock-futures-fall-on-ai-warning-oil-gains-markets-wrap) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/13/stock-futures-today-live-updates.html)*

## Anthropic Races Toward a $2 Trillion IPO

### Anthropic Tells Investors It Will Post a Second Straight Profitable Quarter

Anthropic projects $559 million in adjusted operating profit on $10.9 billion in Q2 revenue, up 130% from Q1, the Financial Times reports. Gross margins are running above 80% before Amazon's cut and training costs. Back-to-back profitable quarters would make Anthropic the first frontier AI lab to pull that off.

*Sources: [Investing.com (FT)](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-tells-investors-it-will-post-second-straight-quarterly-profit--ft-4898778) · [R&D World](https://www.rdworldonline.com/anthropic-backers-eye-2-trillion-valuation-its-projected-q2-revenue-was-10-9b/)*

### Nvidia in Talks for Up to $10 Billion Anchor Stake in Anthropic's IPO

Anthropic picked Nasdaq for a listing that could value it near $2 trillion, and Nvidia is discussing anchoring the deal with as much as $10 billion. The IPO is reportedly targeted to price before November's midterm elections. Nvidia would end up both Anthropic's chip supplier and one of its biggest shareholders.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-may-put-10-billion-103618397.html) · [Techi](https://www.techi.com/nvidia-anthropic-ipo-anchor-investor/)*

## Apple Ships Gemini-Powered Siri, Anthropic Resets Claude Code Limits

### Apple Ships iOS 27 With Gemini-Powered Siri, Skips Europe

iOS 27 rolled out worldwide today as a free update, headlined by a rebuilt Siri that leans on Google's Gemini 2.5 Pro for complex requests. The Apple-Google deal is reportedly worth about $1 billion a year. Apple left the new Siri features off iPhones and iPads in Europe, citing its ongoing dispute with regulators over the Digital Markets Act.

*Sources: [Eastern Herald](https://easternherald.com/2026/09/13/ios-27-siri-google-gemini-apple-launch/) · [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/apple-event-2026-ios-27-announced-rollout-date-compatible-devices-whats-new)*

### Claude Code's Temporary Usage Boost Ends, Replaced by a Smaller Permanent One

Anthropic's 50% temporary weekly-limit increase for Claude Code expired today, replaced by a permanent 25% bump over the original allowance. Net effect: Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise users get about 17% less usage than they had during the promotion. Anthropic frames it as a permanent increase; users comparing it to last month's boost are calling it a cut.

*Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-is-cutting-claude-codes-current-weekly-limits-by-17-percent/) · [MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/claude-code-weekly-rate-limit-changes)*
