NVIDIA's Jensen Huang opened Computex in Taipei this morning with the company's biggest open-weights move yet. Alongside that, two major AI billing models flipped this week, and a historic IPO is days from launch.

## NVIDIA at Computex: Open-Weights Flagship and a Full-Stack Pivot

### NVIDIA Launches Nemotron 3 Ultra — 550B Parameters, Ships June 4

Jensen Huang unveiled Nemotron 3 Ultra at his Computex keynote in Taipei on June 1, putting a 550B-parameter model (55B active via mixture-of-experts) at the top of US open-weights rankings with an Intelligence Index of 48. It runs 5x faster and costs 30% less to operate than today's leading open models. Decrypt noted it still trails China's best open-weights models — but for US developers it's the clearest alternative to locked-down API models yet.

*Sources: [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/nvidia-nemotron-3-ultra-computex-2026/) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/369689/nvidia-open-ai-model-nemotron-3-ultra) · [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-gtc-taipei-computex-2026-news/)*

### Vera Rubin Platform, Vera CPU, RTX Spark: NVIDIA Bets on the Full Stack

Alongside Nemotron, Huang announced the Vera Rubin AI computing platform and a matching Vera CPU built for hyperscale data centers, plus the RTX Spark chip targeting AI inference on consumer PCs. The message is that NVIDIA is no longer just a GPU company — it's selling the full stack from silicon to model to deployment. No pricing was disclosed; the Vera Rubin platform is positioned directly against AWS and Azure custom silicon buildouts.

*Sources: [ExplainX.ai](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/nvidia-computex-2026-nemotron-3-ultra-complete-recap) · [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261938407-nvda-verarubin-ai-jensenhuang-nvidia-tradingkey)*

## Developer Billing Gets More Expensive

### GitHub Copilot Switches to Token Billing Today

Starting June 1, every GitHub Copilot plan charges by token consumption instead of counting premium requests. Plan prices hold — Business stays $19/user/month — but heavy agentic users are reporting 10x to 50x cost jumps depending on workflow intensity. One safeguard: set your additional-spend budget to $0 and Copilot simply stops when credits run out, so there's no surprise overage.

*Sources: [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/) · [How2Shout](https://www.how2shout.com/ai/github-copilot-token-billing-june-2026-ai-credits-developer-backlash.html)*

### Anthropic Agent SDK Gets a Separate Credit Pool on June 15

Starting June 15, Claude's programmatic usage — Agent SDK, `claude -p`, GitHub Actions, and third-party Agent SDK apps — draws from a new monthly credit pool billed at full API rates, separate from chat subscriptions. Pro users get $20 in credits, Max 5x gets $100, Max 20x gets $200. One published analysis puts the effective price increase at 12x to 175x depending on how much agent usage you'd been running under the old flat subscription.

*Sources: [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-agent-sdk-credits/) · [GitHub Gist](https://gist.github.com/MagnaCapax/d9177e35b355853f03c730dfcaa693ef)*

## Big Money Week: Record Raises and a Historic IPO

### Anthropic Closes $65B Series H, Valuation Hits $965B

Anthropic raised $65 billion in a Series H announced May 28, pushing its post-money valuation to $965 billion. Run-rate revenue had already crossed $47 billion earlier in May. At that pace the company could clear a $1T valuation before its widely expected 2027 IPO — no date is set, but this round shortens that window considerably.

*Sources: [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/ai/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-anthropic-65b-dominates/)*

### SpaceX-xAI IPO Roadshow Kicks Off June 4

SpaceX — which absorbed xAI in an all-stock deal in February 2026 — filed its S-1 on May 20 and targets a $1.75T to $2T valuation. The roadshow starts June 4, pricing is expected June 11, and trading on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX begins June 12. The $75 billion target raise would make it the largest IPO in history, surpassing Saudi Aramco's $25.6 billion record.

*Sources: [TechStackIPO](https://www.techstackipo.com/ipo/spacex) · [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/04/27/spacex-ipo-timeline-every-important-date-need-know/)*

## Safety, Governance, and AI Missions

### Colorado Delays Its AI Law to January 2027

Colorado Governor Polis signed SB 189 on May 14, replacing the original June 30 enforcement date with January 1, 2027. A federal court also stayed the original law on April 27 pending xAI's preliminary injunction motion. The replacement law targets high-risk AI in employment, housing, and credit decisions — violations carry fines up to $20,000 each, enforced only by the state attorney general after a 60-day cure notice.

*Sources: [Hunton Privacy Blog](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/colorado-ai-act-amended-and-effective-date-delayed) · [Byte Back Law](https://www.bytebacklaw.com/2026/05/colorado-repeals-replaces-its-ai-act-what-the-new-law-means/)*

### Italy Closes Hallucination Probes Against DeepSeek, Mistral, Nova AI

Italy's AGCM competition authority wrapped up three investigations on April 30, securing binding commitments from all three AI chatbot providers on how they warn Italian users about hallucinations. DeepSeek committed to real-time external source citations and reinforcement learning to cut hallucination outputs; Mistral agreed to in-chat disclaimers plus a full Italian-language website and terms of service; Nova AI addressed both hallucination warnings and confusing service descriptions. Each firm has 90-120 days to comply.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/agcm-deepseek-mistral-nova-ai-hallucination-probes) · [Concurrences](https://www.concurrences.com/en/bulletin/news-issues/preview/consumers-should-be-informed-that-ai-chatbots-hallucinate-the-italian)*

### OpenAI Rosalind Biodefense: Frontier AI for Pandemic Preparedness

OpenAI launched its Rosalind Biodefense Program on May 29, sponsoring free access to GPT-Rosalind — a frontier reasoning model optimized for biology, drug discovery, and genomics — for public health teams doing epidemiological modeling, early outbreak detection, and preparedness work. Paying customers already using the model include Amgen, Moderna, and the Allen Institute. Named after Rosalind Franklin, the program also extends access to select U.S. government and allied partners supporting public health missions.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/29/openai-biodefense-program) · [R&D World](https://www.rdworldonline.com/openai-launches-rosalind-biodefense-offers-federal-agencies-early-access-to-its-life-sciences-model/)*
