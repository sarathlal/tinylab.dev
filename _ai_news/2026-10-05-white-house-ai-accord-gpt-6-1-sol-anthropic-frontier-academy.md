Washington got a voluntary AI safety pact, OpenAI cut the price of its top coding model, and Anthropic opened its wallet for talent. Here's what mattered over the last day or two.

## Washington and the rules of AI

### Six AI leaders sign a two-page, voluntary safety pact at the White House
The "White House Accord on Super Intelligence" was signed by Dario Amodei, Greg Brockman, Sundar Pichai, Mark Zuckerberg, Elon Musk and Jensen Huang. It promises internal controls, compliance teams, external audits and board oversight for cyber, bio and chemical risks. It's explicitly voluntary, sets no penalties and doesn't name the auditors.

*Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/29/trump-top-tech-firms-sign-accord-to-self-police-ai-development) · [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/white-house-major-ai-developers-reach-morally-binding-safety-commitments) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/after-trump-meeting-with-tech-leaders-ai-safety-in-more-chaotic-state.html)*

### Trump names four officials to a new "Super Intelligence Force"
The group will coordinate federal contact with consumers, public interest groups, religious organizations, infrastructure providers and AI companies. It arrives alongside the accord, and fits the administration's push to show the industry can police itself.

*Sources: [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)*

### Connecticut's AI act kicks in as Japan and China start enforcing
Connecticut's CART Act has its first effective date on October 1. Japan's AI Act took full effect September 1. China's new cyberspace security inspection rules landed October 1, which gives AI operators there an immediate compliance deadline. In the US, tech groups keep pushing Congress for one preemptive federal law.

*Sources: [Cubbbix](https://cubbbix.com/blog/ai-regulation-october-2026-global-update) · [MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2533503/new-ai-regulation-may-be-needed-to-impede-cyber-risks-imf-official-says)*

## New models and agent tools

### OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol costs $2/$10 per million tokens, a fifth of Astra
OpenAI launched it at DevDay with near-Astra coding and computer use. Astra runs $10/$50. The Agents API now supports computer use, so apps can drive software through its screen.

*Sources: [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/devday-2026-announcements-and-developer-resources/1402006) · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/openai-devday-2026/)*

### Codex goes to the cloud, and ChatGPT gets always-on "Dots"
Codex can now run remote tasks in cloud environments you start from another device. The CLI adds voice input and an /agents view for managing several tasks. OpenAI also announced a Decisions API and Dots, agents that stay on inside ChatGPT.

*Sources: [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/devday-2026-announcements-and-developer-resources/1402006) · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/openai-devday-2026/)*

## Money and talent: the enterprise AI race

### Anthropic commits $100M to train 10,000 engineers by end of 2027
Claude Frontier Academy trains "Frontier Deployed Engineers" with in-person training, then a 12-week real deployment inside their own company. First cohorts include Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, Commonwealth Bank and Novo Nordisk. Anthropic's pitch is that the shortage is people, not models.

*Sources: [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/anthropic-launches-100-million-claude-frontier-academy-training-program-for-enterprises/) · [Mixed](https://mixed-news.com/en/anthropic-claude-frontier-academy-100-million-10000-engineers/)*

### Anthropic reportedly targets a mid-November IPO
Marketing could start the week of November 9. Selected institutional investors are invited to an October 14 meeting at Anthropic's San Francisco headquarters.

*Sources: [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/anthropic-eyes-october-2026-ipo-that-could-raise-60b) · [Forge](https://forgeglobal.com/insights/anthropic-upcoming-ipo-news/)*

### Instinct raises $1B at a $10B valuation, a month after a $2.5B round
Noah Shinn's personal AI agent startup is still in early access. Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue backed the Series C. The valuation is four times what it was in August's $250M Series B.

*Sources: [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/157240-ai-startup-instinct-raises-1b-series-c-at-10b-valuation/) · [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/instinct-raises-1-billion-series-c-at-10-billion-valuation/)*

### EliseAI hits $4B and Arcee tops $1B in a week of AI-heavy rounds
EliseAI raised $350M led by Andreessen Horowitz and Bessemer. Open-weight model maker Arcee AI closed $150M at a valuation above $1B. Crunchbase says the week's biggest US rounds were almost all AI.

*Sources: [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-space-fintech-temporal/) · [Value Add VC](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/weekly/2026-09-28)*
