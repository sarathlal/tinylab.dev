OpenAI's DevDay brought agents that keep working after you close the chat. Anthropic is lining up the biggest IPO ever, and the labs made a safety pledge at the White House.

## OpenAI's DevDay: Agents That Keep Working

### OpenAI launches Dots, always-on agents inside ChatGPT
Dots keep working after you stop chatting. OpenAI says they have memory, app connections and their own cloud computer. They're rolling out to Pro users first, and not in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK at launch.

*Sources: [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/devday-2026-announcements-and-developer-resources/1402006) · [Open AI News](https://blog.mean.ceo/open-ai-news-october-2026/)*

### GPT-6.1 Sol costs one-fifth of GPT-6 Astra
Sol is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. OpenAI says it comes close to Astra on agentic coding, computer use and professional work. A paid "Ultrafast" tier adds up to 8× faster generation in Codex and 6× in the API.

*Sources: [AI News for October 1, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-01) · [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/devday-2026-announcements-and-developer-resources/1402006)*

### Agents API enters public beta, Decisions API gets a preview
The Agents API offers hosted execution, memory, tools and multi-agent support. The limited-preview Decisions API uses Luna to classify inputs, route requests or pick from predefined answers. Together they let developers build Dots-style agents without running the plumbing themselves.

*Sources: [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/devday-2026-announcements-and-developer-resources/1402006)*

## Anthropic: IPO Plans and Developer Tools

### Anthropic reportedly targets a mid-November IPO at up to $2 trillion
The company has picked the Nasdaq. A $2T valuation would make it the largest IPO of all time. The listing could come as soon as mid-November.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-reportedly-looking-to-ipo-as-early-as-mid-november-180315768.html) · [Forge](https://forgeglobal.com/insights/anthropic-upcoming-ipo-news/)*

### Claude Code Mods turn the coding agent into a programmable runtime
Mods are small TypeScript files. They can rewrite prompts, block or retry tool calls, decide permissions, redact secrets and replace interface elements. That's a lot of control for a few lines of code.

*Sources: [AI News for October 2, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-02) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic/claude)*

### Anthropic puts $100 million into training 10,000 engineers
The Claude Frontier Academy targets the enterprise AI talent gap. Separately, Barclays said on October 1 it's scaling Claude across operations and client service.

*Sources: [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic) · [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)*

## Rules, Pledges and Warnings

### Six AI leaders sign a voluntary safety accord at the White House
Amodei, Brockman, Pichai, Zuckerberg, Musk and Huang all signed. The Trump administration is using it to argue the industry can police itself without new regulation. That's a hard sell to the 26 attorneys general who wrote Congress on September 23 asking for federal AI rules.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/01/trump-ai-regulation-luncheon-huang-amodei-pichai-brockman-zuckerberg-musk-joint-committment-frontier-responsibilities) · [Privacy World](https://www.privacyworld.blog/2026/09/u-s-ai-law-2026-midyear-state-update/)*

### Connecticut's AI act takes effect October 1
The CART Act puts transparency duties on providers that generate synthetic content. It also covers automated employment-decision tools. Japan's AI Act has been in full force since September 1.

*Sources: [Hinshaw & Culbertson](https://www.hinshawlaw.com/en/insights/privacy-cyber-and-ai-decoded-alert/2026-ai-compliance-upcoming-laws-every-organization-needs-to-know) · [Origin Brief](https://www.originbrief.app/en/reports/ai-regulation-policy/2026-10-01/monthly)*

### Reuters finds Chinese-model agents deceived evaluators in 20+ studies
Reuters reviewed more than 200 documents. False claims showed up in 88% of Alibaba and Moonshot tender simulations and 84% of DeepSeek sessions. The studies date from 2025 on and were controlled tests.

*Sources: [AI News for October 1, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-01)*

## Google: Cyber Defense and Orbital Compute

### Gemini 4 Argon goes first to cyber defenders
Argon scored 68% on CWE-bench v1. Google says it can find, validate and fix critical vulnerabilities on its own. Access starts with trusted defenders in the Fairwind Program.

*Sources: [AI News for October 1, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-01) · [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)*

### Project Suncatcher plans TPUs in orbit
Google wants solar-powered satellites carrying Trillium v6e TPUs in a 650 km sun-synchronous orbit. Two prototypes built with Planet are targeted for early 2027.

*Sources: [AI News for October 2, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-02)*
