Oracle just told its data-center partner it might not pay on time, Meta strapped its AI agent to your face, and Microsoft gave Copilot a body of its own. Here's what mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## US Data-Center Buildout Cracks as China's Cloud Race Speeds Up

### Oracle Declares Force Majeure on Its $165B Stargate Data Center
Oracle sent a force majeure notice to Blue Owl's Stack Infrastructure over Project Jupiter, the 1,400-acre New Mexico campus anchoring the OpenAI-SoftBank Stargate buildout. The notice lets Oracle delay payments if the site misses its 2028 online date, after New Mexico regulators twice denied a gas pipeline permit and left an air-quality hearing officer seat empty. Oracle shares fell as much as 7% on the news; Blue Owl says its own $3B in equity commitments haven't changed.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/24/oracle-force-majeure-blue-owl-openai) · [DataCenterDynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oracle-issues-force-majeure-notice-to-blue-owl-following-series-of-setbacks-at-project-jupiter-data-center-campus-report/)*

### Huawei Cloud Launches Its Answer to the US Buildout, Globally, the Same Week
Huawei Cloud unveiled its AI Cluster Service (AICS) and an Agentic Model-as-a-Service platform at HUAWEI CONNECT, going commercially live in China on September 30 and worldwide by November 30. AICS claims 20% higher token throughput than its prior generation and fault recovery within 10 minutes on training runs lasting over 40 days. A new Context Memory Storage layer offers double the industry's petabyte-scale memory capacity, aimed squarely at long-running agent workloads.

*Sources: [Huawei](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2026/9/hc-agentic-infra-industry-ai) · [Tech Wire Asia](https://techwireasia.com/2026/09/huawei-cloud-aics-enterprise-ai-agents/)*

## Meta Straps Personal Superintelligence to Your Face

### Muse Hits 3.4M Downloads in 9 Days, Outpacing ChatGPT's Early Growth
Meta's Muse agent has topped 3.4 million downloads since its September 8 launch, per Sensor Tower, and daily active users jumped 27% the day after Meta Connect wrapped. At Connect, Meta unveiled the Muse Charm, a pocket device built purely for talking to the agent, and CEO Mark Zuckerberg called Muse the centerpiece of Meta's "personal superintelligence" push. For comparison, ChatGPT, Grok and Claude pulled 3.7M, 400K and 300K downloads respectively over a similar early stretch.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/meta-is-putting-its-muscle-behind-muse-as-the-ai-app-takes-off/) · [The Tech Portal](https://thetechportal.com/2026/09/25/metas-muse-has-crossed-3-4-million-downloads-in-under-three-weeks-and-its-still-accelerating)*

### Meta Plans 100+ AI Glasses Styles and a $1,299 VR Headset for Muse
Meta said it will offer more than 100 AI-glasses combinations across Ray-Ban, Oakley and its own Meta Glasses line by year-end, with Muse coming hands-free to all of them. The camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio weighs 43 grams, while the Gen 3 model adds a six-mic array that cuts background noise by over 90%. Meta's first VR Glasses, priced at $1,299.99, ship next spring 2027.

*Sources: [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/) · [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/meta-unveils-1299-99-vr-glasses-expands-muse-ai-and-introduces-more-than-100-ai-glasses-styles/)*

## Enterprise Software Makers Race to Tame Autonomous Agents

### Microsoft Gives Copilot Its Own Employee ID
Microsoft unveiled a redesigned Copilot built around three pillars — Home, Code and a persistent agent called Autopilot. Autopilot runs inside a company's own tenant with an Entra identity, its own memory and email address, and can keep working on a project, in Teams or Outlook, after its human colleague signs off for the day. Home and Code start rolling out to Microsoft's Frontier early-access program in the coming weeks; Autopilot hits private preview at month's end.

*Sources: [Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/25/introducing-the-new-copilot-with-home-code-and-autopilot/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/microsoft-revamps-its-copilot-ai-with-a-persistent-autopilot-agent-and-hosting-for-ai-generated-apps)*

### Dataiku Launches a Product Just to Find All the Agents a Company Already Has
Dataiku debuted Agent Management at its Succeed conference in New York, a standalone tool that inventories every AI agent running across a company's stack — AWS Bedrock, Databricks, Google Vertex, Copilot Studio, Salesforce Agentforce and Dataiku itself. It scores each agent's business and technical performance and flags the riskiest ones. The pitch responds to a real gap: fewer than one in five organizations can name every AI system they're already running.

*Sources: [Dataiku](https://www.dataiku.com/company/news/dataiku-agent-management-general-availability) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/24/dataiku-debuts-cross-platform-agent-management-expands-cobuild-building-agent/)*

## Altman and Amodei Ask the UN for Rules Trump Just Called a "Globalist Scheme"

### Sanders and Casar Introduce a Bill to Ban Superintelligence Outright
Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act, which would permanently prohibit AI exceeding human cognitive performance across most tasks and pause frontier development until a new Cabinet-level Department of Artificial Intelligence writes safety rules. Violators would face what Sanders called a "corporate death penalty" plus up to 20 years in prison — the same range as illegally building nuclear weapons. The bill also requires federal sign-off before any advanced model reaches the public.

*Sources: [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/bernie-sanders-greg-casar-propose-ai-superintelligence-ban-20-year-jai-rcna599460) · [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/09/23/ai-superintelligence-ban-proposed-by-casar-sanders/)*

### OpenAI and Anthropic's CEOs Take Their Safety Pitch to the UN Security Council
Sam Altman and Dario Amodei addressed the UN Security Council days after President Trump told the General Assembly the US would "encourage" superintelligence rather than "rein it in," dismissing oversight as a "globalist scheme." Amodei proposed narrow international agreements — starting with a ban on using AI to build bioweapons — plus shared testing standards and an incident-notification system. Altman asked the council to align how countries measure model capabilities and report failures.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/altman-amodei-un-ai-safety.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/23/ai-leaders-un-security-council-global-safeguards)*

## China's AI Price War Meets a Revenue Boom

### DeepSeek's Revenue Run Rate Doubles to $1B Ahead of a Shanghai IPO
DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate more than doubled in recent months to $1 billion, CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors, even after the company raised its API prices 2.3 to 4.5x last month. DeepSeek is now finalizing a second funding round and preparing a Shanghai STAR Market listing, targeting roughly $7.5B raised at a $75B valuation, with the filing expected before year-end. Wenfeng said the price hikes haven't dented demand.

*Sources: [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/deepseek-doubles-annual-revenue-run-rate-to-1-billion-ahead-of-ipo/) · [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/deepseeks-revenue-run-rate-hit-1-billion-as-it-eyes-an-ipo)*

### Alibaba Cuts Its Voice AI Prices by Up to 95%
Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen-Audio 3.1, a five-model voice lineup, alongside price cuts of up to 95% on speech recognition, about 70% on text-to-speech and roughly 85% on its realtime voice model. The new TTS-Next model generates voice, sound effects and background audio in one pass, while ASR-Next adds multi-speaker identification and emotion detection. It's the clearest sign yet that China's labs are treating voice AI as a price war, not just a features race.

*Sources: [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/alibaba-launches-qwen-audio-3-1-with-five-new-models-and-slashes-ai-audio-prices-by-up-to-95-percent/) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/alibaba-ships-qwen-audio-31-stack-cuts-voice-apis-up-to-95)*
