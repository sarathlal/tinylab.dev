The Pentagon picked ChatGPT and Grok over Claude this week, days after a judge called its Anthropic ban illegal. Here's what else moved in AI over the last two days.

## Pentagon Locks Out Claude Days After a Judge Called Its Ban Illegal

### The Pentagon's New AI Portal Skips Claude for ChatGPT and Grok

The Defense Department added custom versions of ChatGPT and Grok to its GenAI.mil platform on August 31, reaching more than 3 million military and civilian personnel. Over 1.7 million users had already signed up for the portal, which launched last year with Google Gemini. Anthropic's Claude is nowhere on the list — talks to add it broke down after Anthropic refused to drop guardrails against mass surveillance and autonomous weapons use.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/the-pentagon-now-has-its-own-version-of-chatgpt-and-grok/) · [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/08/31/grok-chatgpt-added-to-genai-mil/)*

### DoD Presses Ahead With Its Claude Purge Even After Losing in Court

A federal judge ruled last Friday that the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic was "illegal and baseless," finding Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth retaliated against the company for refusing his terms. Judge Rita Lin said the move looked like punishment for what she called Anthropic's "arrogance" in standing its ground. Government lawyers say removing Claude from military systems is still on track to finish by the end of September anyway.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/28/anthropic-pentagon-ruling-rita-lin-arrogance/) · [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91597954/pentagons-measures-against-anthropic-illegal-baseless-says-federal-judge)*

## Anthropic Spends $80B on Compute While Chasing a Record IPO

### Anthropic Signs a Second Multibillion-Dollar Cloud Deal in a Week

Anthropic locked in a $35 billion cloud agreement with Nvidia-backed Lambda on August 31, tied to a 350-megawatt data center in Nueces County, Texas. Nvidia itself holds the lease on the site, run by former bitcoin miner Hut 8. That's on top of the $45 billion Nscale deal from days earlier — $80 billion in compute commitments in about a week, driven largely by demand for Claude Code.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/anthropic-seals-35-billion-cloud-deal-with-nvidia-backed-lambda) · [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/anthropic-35-billion-lambda-nvidia-texas-deal/)*

### Anthropic Preps a Public Filing That Could Outraise SpaceX

Anthropic is moving toward filing publicly for an IPO that bankers expect could raise as much as $86 billion, topping SpaceX's record $86.2 billion debut from June. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley are leading the offering, targeted for October. Some investors are now pricing in a $2 trillion valuation, more than double the $965 billion Anthropic fetched in its May funding round.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/anthropic-s-mega-ipo-plan-looms-over-packed-us-listing-calendar) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/13/anthropic-eyes-2-trillion-in-october-ipo-a-record-breaking-debut/)*

## Big Tech's AI Money and Leadership Shakeups

### OpenAI's Ad Business Hits $1 Billion in Under 200 Days

OpenAI said Monday that ChatGPT's ad business crossed a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate, just over six months after launch. The company opened self-serve ad buying to marketers in India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa. OpenAI is targeting $2.5 billion in ad revenue this year, on a path toward $40 billion in total annualized revenue — roughly double where it ended 2025.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/open-ai-chatgpt-ads-revenue.html) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-says-ad-business-reaches-1-billion-run-rate/)*

### Apple Hands the CEO Job to a Hardware Chief Facing an AI Reckoning

John Ternus became Apple's CEO on September 1, ending Tim Cook's 15-year run; Cook moves to executive chairman. Ternus spent 24 years running Apple's hardware engineering, including the iPad and AirPods, but now has to prove Apple can compete on AI, not just devices. His first big test: a foldable iPhone and a wave of AI-infused hardware, from camera-equipped AirPods to a robotic-arm home display.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/apple-enters-ternus-era-as-ai-challenges-and-memory-crunch-intensify.html) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/1/john-ternus-succeeds-tim-cook-as-apple-ceo-after-15-years)*

## Fresh Billions for AI Infrastructure and Security

### Europe Bets €388 Million on AMD Chips for Its Next AI Supercomputer

The EuroHPC Joint Undertaking signed a €387.8 million contract with France's Bull to build LUMI-AI, a supercomputer running AMD's Instinct MI430X GPUs, in Kajaani, Finland. It's the sixth AI Factory contract EuroHPC has signed and promises ten times the AI capacity of the current LUMI machine. The deal is a rare win for AMD in a market Nvidia dominates almost completely.

*Sources: [EuroHPC JU](https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/eurohpc-ju-signs-contract-deploy-lumi-ai-supercomputer-2026-08-31_en) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326138/20260901/europe-signs-451m-amd-deal-lumi-ai-defying-nvidia-ai-factory-dominance.htm)*

### Ex-Israeli Intelligence Officers Raise $50M to Build a Firewall for AI Agents

AIR Security launched publicly on September 1 with $50 million in seed funding, split between a $10 million round led by Sequoia and a $40 million round led by Greenoaks. Founders Yair Saban and Niv Hoffman, both veterans of Israel's Unit 8200, built a platform that vets the tools and skills AI agents use and blocks the ones that fail security checks. It's a bet that AI agents, not chatbots, are the next big attack surface for enterprises.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/air-raises-50m-to-help-companies-vet-the-skills-and-add-ons-ai-agents-use/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/01/air-security-launches-with-50m-to-build-a-firewall-for-ai-agents/)*
