Nine days after a U.S. export order pulled Claude Fable 5 from global markets, Anthropic is still waiting for Washington's clearance — and its IPO bankers are watching $10.9 billion in projected quarterly revenue sit on hold.

## Fable 5 Shutdown Drags Into Second Week

### Day 9: Anthropic's "Coming Days" Restoration Promise Has Not Been Kept

On June 12 at 5:21 PM ET, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick invoked the Export Control Reform Act to block foreign nationals from accessing Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5. Anthropic suspended all access globally to ensure compliance. On June 18, Anthropic's Managing Director of International Chris Ciauri said at the Seoul office launch that access would return "in the coming days." Three days on, it hasn't. The June 20 refund deadline passed for subscribers who paid for Fable 5 access between launch day (June 9) and June 14. The government's stated concern is a jailbreak posted to X on June 10 by "Pliny the Liberator" — a technique Anthropic calls "narrow, non-universal" and says is already replicated in GPT-5.5.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-trump-mythos-fable-national-security) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318668/20260618/fable-5-export-ban-day-six-anthropic-opens-seoul-office-vows-models-back-days.htm) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-disables-fable-mythos-export-controls-national-security-threat/)*

### Anthropic's IPO Prospectus Projects $559M Q2 Profit on $10.9B Revenue

Anthropic disclosed to prospective investors that it expects Q2 2026 operating profit of $559 million on $10.9 billion in revenue — a 130% jump over Q1's $4.8 billion and the company's first projected profitable quarter. The October Nasdaq listing, led by Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley, targets a valuation around $965 billion. The company warned planned data center spending could push later quarters back into the red. OpenAI filed its own confidential S-1 eight days after Anthropic, on June 8.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-confidentially-files-ipo-965-billion-valuation/) · [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/featured-articles/761/anthropic-ipo) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/08/following-anthropic-openai-files-confidentially-for-ipo/)*

## Frontier Models — Gemini Stalls, Open Weights Race On

### Gemini 3.5 Pro Passes One Month Without a Public Launch

Sundar Pichai promised Gemini 3.5 Pro at Google I/O on May 19. Five weeks later, it's in limited enterprise preview on Vertex AI only. Prediction markets put the odds at 50-55% for a public launch before June 30. The model targets a 2M-token context window, a "Deep Think" reasoning mode, and pricing roughly 10x above Gemini 3.5 Flash — around $15 per million input tokens. Google shipped Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite earlier in June to keep developers active while Pro waits.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-june-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm) · [WaveSpeed Blog](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/gemini-3-5-pro-coming-next-month/)*

### MiniMax M3 Cuts Inference Cost 20x With Open-Weight Sparse Attention

MiniMax launched M3 on June 1 as the first open-weights model combining frontier coding benchmarks, a 1M-token context window, and native multimodality. The key innovation is MSA (MiniMax Sparse Attention): per-token compute drops to one-twentieth of its predecessor at 1M context, with 9x faster prefilling and 15x faster decoding. M3 scores 59% on SWE-Bench Pro, beating GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding. It launched at roughly $0.30 per million input tokens, temporarily half-price.

*Sources: [MiniMax](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m3) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/01/minimax-releases-minimax-m3-with-msa-architecture-supporting-1m-token-context-native-multimodality-and-agentic-coding/)*

## AI Funding — Agents Capture Half the Quarter

### Q2 2026 Lands at $42.6B Total, Agentic Startups Take $20B

Q2 2026 AI funding totaled $42.6 billion across 312 rounds. Startups building autonomous agents captured roughly $20 billion — nearly half. That marks a clear shift from Q1, when four mega-rounds (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Waymo) ate 63% of global VC. The distribution is broadening: enterprise agentic tools for software development, legal research, and customer operations drew the largest checks after the lab-funding wave wound down.

*Sources: [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/top-50-ai-startups/) · [Qubit Capital](https://qubit.capital/blog/ai-startup-fundraising-trends)*

### Suno Closes $400M at $5.4B While Fighting Two Record Labels in Court

AI music startup Suno raised a $400M Series D led by Bond Capital, valuing it at $5.4B — more than double its November valuation. The company has 2 million paying subscribers and generates 7 million songs per day. Universal Music and Sony Music are still suing over 61,000 allegedly infringing songs; Warner Music settled and signed a licensing deal last November. Suno used the new capital to announce its first fully licensed model, built in partnership with the music industry.

*Sources: [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/ai-music-suno-funding-round-400-million-5-4-billion-valuation-1236765727/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317917/20260606/suno-raises-400-million-54-billion-valuation-while-record-labels-sue-over-61000-songs.htm)*

## Regulation — Deadlines Shift in Both Directions

### EU AI Act Transparency Rules Go Live August 2, But High-Risk Deadline Pushed to December 2027

The EU AI Act reaches full general applicability on August 2, activating transparency requirements and GPAI rules. But the Digital Omnibus agreement signed May 7 gives companies 16 extra months for the toughest obligations: Annex III rules covering biometrics, employment, education, and critical infrastructure now kick in December 2, 2027, not August. The prohibited-practices ban has been live since February 2025 and GPAI rules since August 2025. August 2 is still a real deadline — it's just narrower than most companies planned for.

*Sources: [McKenna Consultants](https://www.mckennaconsultants.com/eu-ai-act-high-risk-compliance-a-technical-readiness-guide-for-august-2026/) · [Travers Smith](https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/eu-agrees-to-delay-key-ai-act-compliance-deadlines/)*

### Colorado Replaced Its AI Act With a Lighter Law, Effective January 2027

On May 14, Governor Jared Polis signed SB 189, replacing the original Colorado AI Act with a narrower law effective January 1, 2027. The original required impact assessments, risk management programs, and a duty of care to prevent algorithmic discrimination — all dropped. The new law requires only disclosures and record-keeping. The Attorney General enforces with a 60-day cure period that sunsets in 2030.

*Sources: [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/colorado-ai-act-amended-and-effective-date-delayed) · [Seyfarth](https://www.seyfarth.com/news-insights/colorado-enacts-artificial-intelligence-replacement-law.html)*
