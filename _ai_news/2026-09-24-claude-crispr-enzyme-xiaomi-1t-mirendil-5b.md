Anthropic's new biology lab just made gene-editing investors nervous, Xiaomi gave away a trillion-parameter model for free, and an AI startup barely nine months old is chasing a $5B price tag. Here's what mattered in the last day.

## Claude's New Biolab Finds Something That Looks Like CRISPR

### Claude Discovers a Novel Enzyme System With CRISPR-Like Repeats

Anthropic's Bay Area wet lab set about 950 Claude agents loose on a public DNA database, and 21 hours and 210 million tokens later, they'd found something nobody had documented before. The agents combed through 200,000 reverse transcriptases, narrowed 3,500 candidates down to 20, and one flagged a repeating DNA pattern sitting next to an unusual gene. Anthropic named the system ART, for array-associated reverse transcriptases, and says early lab tests show it produces short RNAs, but admits "we don't yet know its function."

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-claude-enzyme-system-crispr-like-repeats)*

### Gene-Editing Stocks Slide on News of the Claude-Found Enzyme

Wall Street didn't wait for peer review. CRISPR Therapeutics fell 6%, Prime Medicine dropped 12%, and Intellia Therapeutics slid on Wednesday after Anthropic's announcement, with Beam Therapeutics and Q32 Bio also in the red. Nobody has shown ART can actually edit genes yet, but a big AI lab casually finding a CRISPR-shaped system with 950 agents was enough to spook a sector that spent two decades building its edge the old way.

*Sources: [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4646229-gene-editing-stocks-fall-anthropic-crispr-like-discovery) · [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9094094/crispr-therapeutics-crsp-shares-dip-amid-anthropic-ai-enzyme-discovery-highlighting-valuation-challenges)*

## Anthropic Grows Its Storefront While Its Alumni Chase a 5x Valuation

### Claude Marketplace Expands to Over 2,000 Partners

Anthropic widened Claude Marketplace on Wednesday, folding software integrations, purchasable AI products, and consulting services into one catalog of more than 2,000 connectors, plugins, agents, and service partners. Enterprise customers can spend their existing Anthropic commitment on tools from partners like GitLab, Harvey, Snowflake, and CrowdStrike, and Anthropic still isn't taking a cut. It's the same enterprise-procurement playbook AWS and Azure have run for years, just with Claude at the center.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-launches-claude-marketplace-giving-enterprises-access-to-claude) · [Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/anthropic-opens-claude-marketplace-for-partner-software-and-services)*

### Ex-Anthropic Startup Mirendil in Talks to 5x Its Valuation to $5B

Mirendil raised $200M at a $1B valuation three months ago. Now Kleiner Perkins is negotiating to lead a round of up to $1B that would value the nine-month-old startup at $5B, with Andreessen Horowitz also in talks to join. Founders Behnam Neyshabur and Harsh Mehta, both ex-Anthropic researchers, have built a 20-person team pulled from Anthropic, xAI, DeepMind, and OpenAI, all working on AI systems that do the job of AI researchers themselves.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/ex-anthropic-staffers-ai-startup-in-talks-to-raise-at-5-billion-value) · [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/report-ex-anthropic-duos-mirendil-in-talks-for-5b-valuation-just-3-months-after-1b-seed/)*

## Open Models and Robot Software Both Get a Serious Upgrade

### Xiaomi Open-Sources a 1.02 Trillion-Parameter Model Under MIT License

Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro, at 1.02 trillion parameters, now tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index among open-weight models with a score of 46. The six-day reinforcement-learning training run reportedly cost about $2.62M, and Xiaomi released the weights, a 9B distilled version, a technical report, and more than 7,000 RL training environments, all under MIT license. A smaller Flash variant, 309B parameters, handles high-volume use at an estimated $850K training cost.

*Sources: [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/xiaomi-releases-mimo-v2-6-with-trillion-parameter-models-and-7-000-rl-environments) · [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/09/22/xiaomi-open-sources-mimo-v2-6-models-after-scaling-reinforcement-learning/)*

### Nvidia Ships Isaac ROS 5.0 With AI Agents Built for Robot Developers

Nvidia released Isaac ROS 5.0 at ROSCon in Toronto, adding AI-agent skills that let coding assistants configure environments and migrate software for robotics developers directly. The update adds support for ROS 2 Lyrical and wider deployment across Nvidia's Jetson edge hardware. It's a small but telling detail: AI models are now helping build the robots that will eventually run AI models themselves.

*Sources: [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-accelerates-robotics-research-and-development-with-new-open-models-and-simulation-libraries) · [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/23/top-tech-news-today-september-23-2026-anthropic-att-google-microsoft-nvidia-openai-xiaomi-more/)*

## Microsoft Takes Down an AI Phishing Ring as Washington Frets Over Chips

### Microsoft and Coinbase Dismantle an AI Phishing Service That Hit 12,000 Inboxes

EvilTokens sold cybercriminals a subscription — $1,500 to join, $500 a month — for an AI chatbot that read stolen inboxes and picked which contacts to defraud. Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit, working with Coinbase, Cloudflare, and OpenAI, seized 50 websites and disabled 150 more domains under a federal court order. UK police arrested two men, Felix Utomi and Waidi Segun Adams, on September 11, and Coinbase traced roughly $1.1M in crypto proceeds across more than 700 wallets.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/microsoft-takes-down-eviltokens-device.html) · [The Record](https://therecord.media/two-arrested-in-uk-after-microsoft-takedown-eviltokens)*

### Senate Democrats Press Trump to Tighten AI Chip Curbs Before Xi Summit

Seven Senate Democrats, including Chuck Schumer, Elizabeth Warren, and Jeanne Shaheen, issued a joint statement Tuesday urging Trump to close loopholes letting China buy advanced AI chips and chipmaking tools, ahead of his summit with President Xi. Their statement argues "China is accelerating its drive for AI dominance by exploiting weaknesses in the administration's export controls." It's a direct shot at the administration's shift from a blanket ban to case-by-case chip export reviews earlier this year.

*Sources: [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6104468-senate-democrats-ai-china/) · [Warner Senate](https://www.warner.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/top-senate-democrats-warn-trump-ai-chip-deal-with-china-raises-legal-and-national-security-risks/)*
