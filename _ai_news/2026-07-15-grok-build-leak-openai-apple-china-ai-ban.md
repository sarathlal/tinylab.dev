xAI had its worst 48 hours of the year, OpenAI is now fighting two lawsuits at once, and China just shut off two of its biggest AI companion apps overnight. Here's what mattered in the last day and a half.

## OpenAI and xAI's Legal and Security Mess

### Grok Build Shipped Developers' Entire Codebases to xAI's Cloud

A security researcher's wire-level analysis, published July 12, proved that xAI's Grok Build coding tool was uploading developers' full Git history — commits, secrets, and all — to a Google Cloud Storage bucket. The volume was roughly 27,800 times more data than the actual coding task needed, and the tool's privacy toggle did nothing to stop it. The story hit Hacker News's front page July 14, and Elon Musk promised to delete all previously uploaded user data.

*Sources: [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320420/20260714/grok-build-shipped-entire-codebases-xai-cloud-privacy-toggle-did-nothing.htm)*

### OpenAI Tells Judge Apple's Trade-Secret Suit "Has No Merit"

OpenAI broke its silence July 14 on Apple's lawsuit accusing it of stealing hardware designs for a rival device, four days after Apple filed. "We're not aware of any evidence that this complaint has merit," the company said, adding it believes in "fair competition and allowing people the freedom to work wherever they choose." Apple's suit names former Apple engineer Chang Liu and ex-hardware chief Tang Tan, now at OpenAI, plus Jony Ive's io Products.

*Sources: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/14/openai-apple-lawsuit-response/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/openai-pushes-back-on-apple-trade-secret-lawsuit/)*

### OpenAI Asks Judge to Toss xAI's Poaching Suit, Wants $1M+ Back

The same day it answered Apple, OpenAI filed to have xAI's employee-poaching lawsuit thrown out entirely, arguing it "should never have been filed," and is seeking more than $1 million in legal costs from Musk's company. The filing came hours after xAI said it would appeal its already-dismissed claims that OpenAI encouraged staff to steal confidential information.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/openai-s-feud-with-xai-carries-on-as-apple-secrets-fight-revs-up)*

## Anthropic Pushes Deeper Into Classrooms and Teams

### Claude for Teachers Gives US K-12 Educators Free Premium Access

Anthropic launched Claude for Teachers on July 14, giving verified US K-12 educators a year of free access to premium Claude tools, teaching skills, and curricula aligned to standards in all 50 states. The product plugs into Learning Commons for standards-aligned lesson plans and pulls in resources like OpenSciEd and Illustrative Mathematics. Detroit Public Schools will pilot it as part of a study on educator well-being, and educators can sign up through June 30, 2027.

*Sources: [Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/07/14/anthropic-launches-claude-for-teachers-as-ai-companies-battle-for-classrooms/) · [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5968601-claude-for-teachers-launch/)*

### Claude Artifacts Now Support Public Sharing and Team Editing

Anthropic also shipped public sharing and multiplayer editing for Artifacts on July 14, letting users publish interactive dashboards or mini-apps beyond their organization and co-edit the same Claude Code artifact as a team. The features can also be triggered through Claude Tag, Anthropic's Slack integration. Internally, Anthropic says Claude now writes 65% of the code its own product teams ship.

*Sources: [Progressive Robot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/07/14/anthropic-enables-public-artifact-sharing-and-team-editing-in-claude/)*

## China's New AI Rules Take Effect

### Doubao and Qwen Kill Their AI Companion Agents Overnight

China's Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services took effect July 15, and ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen chose to shut down their personalized AI agent features rather than rebuild for compliance. Doubao users get read-only access to old chats and can export data until October 15; Qwen announced no migration path at all, so its agent configurations and histories are simply gone.

*Sources: [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3359482/bytedance-and-alibaba-disable-humanlike-ai-custom-agents-new-rules-loom) · [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/07/06/bytedances-doubao-and-alibabas-qwen-to-shut-down-ai-agent-features-on-july-15/)*

### Beijing Issues Its First National Rulebook for AI Agents

Alongside the companion crackdown, China's CAC, NDRC, and MIIT jointly enforced Implementation Opinions on AI agents starting July 15 — the country's first dedicated policy framework treating agents as distinct from generative AI. Developers must classify agent decisions into three tiers, from user-made to fully autonomous, and agents in healthcare, transport, media, or public safety now face mandatory filing and compliance testing. Beijing's target: 70% agent adoption in smart terminals by 2027.

*Sources: [Rimon Law](https://www.rimonlaw.com/china-ai-law-brief/)*

## This Week's Big AI Funding Rounds

### Chai Discovery Triples Its Valuation to $3.8B for AI Drug Design

Chai Discovery announced a $400 million Series C on July 14, led by Index Ventures with Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia, and Dimension joining, valuing the company at $3.8 billion — roughly triple its mark from seven months ago. Its Chai-3 model designs antibodies that bind more tightly to drug targets, and customers already include Eli Lilly and Pfizer.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/14/chai-discovery-nabs-400m-series-c-as-ai-designed-antibodies-reach-big-pharma/) · [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260713849009/en/Chai-Discovery-Announces-$400M-Series-C-to-Advance-AI-Driven-Molecular-Design)*

### Joulent Raises $1.75B to Build Power Plants for AI Data Centers

Houston-based Joulent secured a $1.75 billion strategic investment backed by National Grid Ventures on July 13, underscoring how much of this year's AI capital is now flowing into electricity, not just models. The energy-infrastructure startup builds power generation specifically sized for AI data center demand, a bottleneck that's increasingly the real constraint on how fast labs can scale.

*Sources: [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-energy-biotech-joulent/)*
