Two financial disclosures landed inside 48 hours and reshaped the week's AI narrative: Anthropic is about to post its first-ever operating profit, and SpaceX's IPO prospectus revealed just how much that profit costs to generate.

## Anthropic's Record Quarter

### Anthropic Is Heading for Its First-Ever Quarterly Operating Profit

Anthropic told investors its Q2 2026 revenue will reach at least $10.9 billion — more than double the $4.8 billion it made in Q1. Operating profit is projected at $559 million, the first positive quarter in the company's five-year history, driven by enterprises using Claude for code, automation, and cybersecurity. An October 2026 IPO is on the calendar at a $900 billion valuation.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-20/anthropic-on-pace-for-first-profitable-quarter-as-revenue-surges) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-says-its-about-to-have-its-first-profitable-quarter/)*

### SpaceX Files for a $1.75T IPO — and Buries Anthropic's $1.25B/Month Compute Secret Inside

SpaceX filed its public S-1 on May 20 targeting a raise of up to $75 billion at a $1.75 trillion valuation — roughly three times the largest US IPO on record. Buried in the prospectus: Anthropic is paying xAI $1.25 billion per month through May 2029 for access to both Colossus data centers in Memphis, Tennessee, which run more than 220,000 NVIDIA GPUs. That's $15 billion a year — up to $45 billion before the deal expires — though either side can walk with 90 days' notice.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-will-pay-xai-1-25-billion-per-month-for-compute/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-spacex-compute) · [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/spacex-ipo-filing-reveals-anthropic-set-to-pay-musks-firm-125bn-a-month-to-rent-xai-data-center-space/)*

### Pope Leo XIV Is Releasing His AI Encyclical Alongside Anthropic Co-Founder Christopher Olah

Pope Leo XIV will present "Magnifica Humanitas," his first papal teaching document, on May 25 alongside Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah. The encyclical centers on protecting human dignity in the AI age and was signed May 15 — exactly 135 years after Leo XIII's Rerum Novarum addressed workers' rights during the industrial revolution. Olah pioneered AI interpretability research, which tries to explain what neural networks are actually computing inside.

*Sources: [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/pope-leo-xiv-to-launch-his-first-encylical-a-document-on-artificial-intelligence-with-anthropics-co-founder) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/anthropic-s-co-founder-to-launch-encyclical-on-ai-with-pope-leo) · [National Catholic Reporter](https://www.ncronline.org/vatican/vatican-news/pope-leo-present-his-encyclical-ai-alongside-anthropic-co-founder)*

## OpenAI Prepares for Wall Street

### OpenAI Files Confidential S-1 Today, Eyes September Listing at Up to $1 Trillion

OpenAI is filing its confidential draft IPO prospectus with the SEC today, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading the deal. The target is a September 2026 listing at a valuation between $852 billion and $1 trillion. Anthropic is targeting an October IPO at $900 billion — meaning two of the largest AI labs could go public within weeks of each other.

*Sources: [WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/05/21/openai-eyes-friday-ipo-filing-september-debut-in-view-xcxwbn/) · [OpenTools](https://opentools.ai/news/openai-ipo-filing-september-2026)*

### OpenAI's ChatGPT Ads Manager Opens to All Businesses, Drops $50K Minimum

OpenAI launched self-serve advertising inside ChatGPT on May 21, removing the $50,000 minimum spend that had kept most businesses out. The platform supports CPC and CPM bidding, with launch agency partners including Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis, and WPP. OpenAI is targeting $2.5 billion in ad revenue for 2026 and $100 billion annually by 2030 — numbers that make the IPO timing click.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/new-ways-to-buy-chatgpt-ads/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/05/openai-self-serve-ad-platform) · [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/brands-can-now-customize-how-their-ads-appear-in-chatgpt/)*
