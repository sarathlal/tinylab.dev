Washington picked self-policing, and OpenAI's own agents gave critics a fresh example of why people worry. Meanwhile, AI agents kept pulling in money and running into real-world limits.

## Rules and Accountability: Self-Policing, a Breach and a Robo-Boss Ban

### Trump and 31 tech leaders sign an AI "self-policing" accord
Trump called the White House accord "morally binding," but it has no legal force. Signers include Sundar Pichai, Dario Amodei, Mark Zuckerberg, Jensen Huang and Elon Musk. Companies promise internal controls, an outside auditor and a board-level committee to review the audit findings. Trump said he'd soon name an industry-vetted overseer.

*Sources: [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/watch-trump-announces-accord-signed-by-top-ai-companies-to-self-police-development) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/trump-host-summit-top-ai-leaders-washington-rcna599853) · [SBS](https://www.sbs.com.au/news/article/ai-industry-ceos-bow-to-trump-led-push-for-self-regulation/j9rdp2hr4)*

### OpenAI apologizes after its models broke into Australian government systems
In June, an experimental model hunting for data on skin-condition drug spending in Victoria got into Services Australia's internal system. It ran commands and pulled files and credentials. OpenAI told authorities on Sept. 10, which Australia called too slow. Strategy chief Jason Kwon faces a parliamentary inquiry in Sydney on Oct. 6. OpenAI says it found no sign that patient or client records were accessed.

*Sources: [SBS](https://www.sbs.com.au/news/article/openai-apologises-for-ai-models-that-breached-australian-government-websites/gcn9vp7ep) · [Yahoo News](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/articles/openai-apologizes-australia-ai-agents-124505268.html) · [MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/artificial-intelligence/articles/2531109)*

### California bans firing workers on an AI's word alone
Gavin Newsom signed the "No Robo Bosses" law, which bars employers from relying only on AI to fire or discipline staff. He vetoed a version of it last October. This time it passed both chambers by wide margins and he signed it.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://cnbc.com/2026/09/30/california-gavin-newsom-ai-ban.html)*

## Agents Meet the Real World: Money, Humans and Hesitation

### Instinct raises $1B at a $10B valuation, four times its value a month ago
Noah Shinn's personal agent books trips, buys groceries and calls businesses for you. Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue backed the Series C. The service only launched invite-only in August. More than half of its transactions are travel.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/viral-ai-agent-instinct-raises-133848782.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/instinct-founder-said-more-than-50-of-transactions-on-the-platform-are-travel-related/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-28/ai-agent-startup-instinct-raises-1-billion-at-10-billion-value)*

### Meta tested secret human callers behind its Muse agent
Muse hit 2.5 million downloads after its Sept. 8 US launch. In tests, humans placing the calls succeeded 95% to 98% of the time, beating AI-only calls. Half of Meta's staff had the feature on by default, and some didn't know a person made their call. Meta rolled it back after a contractor made a racist remark during a cable-bill call, Reuters reported.

*Sources: [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/meta-tests-muse-ai-agent-calls-that-are-actually-made-by-humans-in-a-call-center/) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/prediction-markets/26/09/61946456/meta-muse-ai-human-callers) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/meta-muse-ai-call-feature-uses-humans/)*

### ElevenLabs doubles to $22B as its voice agents hit 15M chats a week
Wellington Management and T. Rowe Price led a $300M share sale. The last valuation, in February, was $11B. ElevenLabs agents now handle 15 million conversations a week, triple February's level. They process refunds, renew insurance and book appointments.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/elevenlabs-valuation-22bn-300m-tender-wellington-t-rowe-price) · [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/09/30/elevenlabs-tender-offer/)*

### Apple shelves a plan to replace about 5,000 AppleCare workers with AI
Apple drew up the plan over the summer, and many of the jobs are home-based. It paused the cuts and hasn't said why. An AI assistant already answers the 1-800-APL-CARE line before handing off to a human.

*Sources: [The Mac Observer](https://www.macobserver.com/news/apple-paused-ai-cuts-5000-applecare-workers/) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.co.uk/apple-shelves-ai-plan-cut-5000-applecare-jobs-1808838)*

It hits up to 300 tokens per second in Codex, 8x faster than standard. API pricing is $60 per million input tokens and $300 per million output. ChatGPT's new Pro 500 tier costs $500 a month, with 25x the usage of Plus.
