Nvidia moved deeper into AI investing with a $40B equity spree while OpenAI laid the foundations of an ad business that could rival its API revenue. Across the Atlantic, Google's London researchers voted to push back — hard — against their employer's latest government contract.

## Nvidia Bets $40B on the AI Stack

### Nvidia Tops $40B in Equity Deals — Its Largest Single Check Was $30B to OpenAI

Nvidia has committed more than $40 billion to equity investments in 2026, including a $30 billion stake in OpenAI, rights to invest up to $3.2 billion in Corning, and up to $2.1 billion in data center operator IREN. The company generated $97 billion in free cash flow last fiscal year and is now backing many of the same companies that buy its chips. Critics are comparing the arrangement to vendor financing that helped inflate the dot-com bubble.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/09/nvidia-has-already-committed-40b-to-equity-ai-deals-this-year/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/09/nvidia-embraces-ai-investor-topping-40-billion-in-equity-bets-2026.html)*

### Kevin O'Leary's 9-Gigawatt Utah Data Center Gets County Approval

Box Elder County commissioners approved the Stratos Project on May 4 — a 40,000-acre AI data center campus in northwest Utah backed by Kevin O'Leary's O'Leary Digital, with a price tag above $100 billion. When complete, the facility would consume more electricity than the entire state of Utah currently uses. Hundreds of residents filed petitions to block it over water and environmental concerns, and a local group is seeking a ballot measure in November to reverse the decision.

*Sources: [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/09/tech/ai-data-center-utah-kevin-oleary-opposition) · [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/kevin-olearys-massive-data-center-project-in-utah-gets-the-greenlight-locals-are-furious-2000755168)*

## OpenAI Builds an Ad Business Inside ChatGPT

### OpenAI Launches Self-Serve Ads Manager with CPC Bidding

On May 5, OpenAI opened its ad platform to self-serve buyers in the US — letting companies set budgets, upload creatives, and manage campaigns in a beta Ads Manager without needing an agency. The launch added cost-per-click bidding alongside expanded measurement tools. Major agencies including Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis, and WPP are available as buying partners for brands that want managed service.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/05/openai-self-serve-ad-platform) · [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/openai-launches-self-serve-ads-manager-for-chatgpt/573971/)*

### ChatGPT Ads Expand to Five New Countries; OpenAI Targets $2.5B in Ad Revenue This Year

OpenAI confirmed on May 7 that sponsored listings in ChatGPT will roll out to the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea in the coming weeks, after earlier pilots in the US, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. Ads appear only to logged-in adults on Free and Go tiers — paid subscribers see none. OpenAI is targeting $2.5 billion in ad revenue this year and $100 billion by 2030.

*Sources: [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/expand-thoughtfully-openai-offers-chatgpt-ads-to-new-markets-including-the-u-k-brazil-and-japan/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/new-ways-to-buy-chatgpt-ads/)*

## Google's Pentagon Deal Tears Its Researchers in Two

### Google Cleared Gemini for Use Inside US Classified Military Networks

Google agreed to let the US Department of Defense run Gemini models inside classified military networks "for any lawful purpose" — language critics say is broad enough to cover autonomous weapons and mass surveillance. The deal walked back an unofficial commitment Google had maintained since employee backlash over Project Maven in 2018, when the company had pledged not to build AI for weapons systems that violate international norms.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/google-deepmind-unionize-vote-military-ai-contracts-internal-backlash-pentagon-deal-israeli-defense-forces/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2164765/google-us-military-deepmind-union/)*

### 98% of DeepMind's London Workers Vote to Unionize — a First for Any Major AI Lab

In a CWU membership vote on May 5, 98% of DeepMind's London staff backed a formal unionization bid covering roughly 1,000 researchers and engineers. Their letter gives Google management 10 working days to recognize the Communication Workers Union and Unite, or face a compelled legal recognition process. It's the first time employees at a major AI research lab have organized collectively against a military contract.

*Sources: [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/deepmind-workers-vote-to-unionize-following-googles-pentagon-ai-deal-2000754603) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/google-deepmind-unionize-vote-military-ai-contracts-internal-backlash-pentagon-deal-israeli-defense-forces/)*

## AI's Growing Footprint

### Microsoft: AI Now Reaches 17.8% of the World's Working-Age Population

Microsoft's Q1 2026 Global AI Diffusion Report, published May 7, shows AI usage rose 1.5 percentage points in the quarter — from 16.3% to 17.8% of the world's working-age population. Adoption is accelerating faster in emerging markets than in the US or Europe. Microsoft frames the figure as a proxy for productivity gains that don't yet show up in GDP statistics.

*Sources: [Microsoft On the Issues](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/07/the-state-of-global-ai-diffusion-in-2026/)*

### OpenAI Acquires Hiro — Its Seventh Deal of 2026

OpenAI closed its acquisition of Hiro, a personal-finance agent startup, making it the company's seventh acquisition of 2026. The deal brings in Hiro's engineering and product team. OpenAI has not disclosed terms or said when Hiro's technology will appear in its products.

*Sources: [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai)*
