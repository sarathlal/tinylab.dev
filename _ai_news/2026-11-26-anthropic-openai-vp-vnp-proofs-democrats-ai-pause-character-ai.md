Three labs published AI-written math in the same stretch, including two rival claims on a 47-year-old problem. At the same time, a report about what those labs haven't released yet has Democrats calling for a pause and has investors selling Anthropic stock.

## AI Starts Publishing Its Own Math

### Anthropic and OpenAI both claim an AI proof that VP ≠ VNP

Anthropic posted a 70-page paper credited to Claude that claims VP ≠ VNP "over every field." That's Leslie Valiant's 47-year-old algebraic cousin of P vs NP. OpenAI posted its own roughly 30-page proof hours later, with a Lean 4 formalization of about 440,000 lines. Both pass automated Lean checks, but no human mathematician has signed off yet. Lean can't confirm the code captures the standard definitions, so the claims stay unverified for now.

*Sources: [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Anthropic-and-OpenAI-claim-breakthroughs-on-a-47-year-old-fundamental-math-problem.1459160.0.html) · [Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/claude-proves-vp-not-vnp-valiant-conjecture) · [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/anthropic-posts-a-claude-written-proof-checked-in-lean-that-claims-vp-and-vnp-differ-over-every-field/)*

### Google's Cogentic drops six math papers in one day, led by a factoring claim

Google Research added six papers from Cogentic, its multi-agent proof system, to a public GitHub folder on November 25. The lead paper claims a proven L[1/3] running time for factoring integers. That's subexponential, not a break of RSA. Others cover discrete logs, approximate Nash equilibria, the log-rank conjecture and graph reconstruction. None is peer reviewed, and Google says the Lean proofs will come in a later submission.

*Sources: [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/googles-cogentic-ai-releases-six-math-papers-in-one-day-including-a-proven-fast-algorithm-for-factoring-numbers) · [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/googles-cogentic-agent-posts-six-math-papers-led-by-a-claimed-proven-l13-bound-for-factoring-integers/)*

## Safety Fears Reach Congress and the Stock Market

### Democrats demand AI restrictions after a report on unreleased models

Axios, citing more than two dozen sources, reported that OpenAI and Anthropic hold unreleased models far stronger than their public ones. Insiders say some proved hard to control in testing. Sen. Bernie Sanders wants Congress to "pause advanced AI development NOW." Sen. Mark Kelly asked why AI gets fewer guardrails than nuclear weapons. OpenAI told Axios it has adopted new safety rules that could slow releases.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/25/democrats-ai-safety-restrictions-openai-anthropic-axios) · [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/anthropic-openai-staff-warn-unreleased-ai-models-dangerous-1834715)*

### Anthropic stock keeps sliding below its $83 IPO price

ANT closed at $81.91 on Tuesday, its first close under the offer price. It fell another 3% to about $79 on Wednesday. It opened at $111.50 on November 19, so it took four trading days to break issue. SpaceX took 21. Coverage points to the Axios safety report and an amended S-1 showing $900.9B in committed spend.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-stock-ipo-price.html) · [CNBC Investing Club](https://www.cnbc.com/investingclub/2026/11/25/anthropics-stock-has-been-a-clunker-post-ipo-why-that-matters-for-the-ai-trade.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/eriksherman/2026/11/24/anthropic-shares-fall-under-the-ipo-price-heres-what-it-means/)*

## New Rules for Chatbots, Toys and Agents

### 42 attorneys general try to block Character.AI's sale of 21 million users' chats

A coalition of 42 state AGs asked the bankruptcy court to stop Character.AI from auctioning chat data from more than 21 million US accounts. About 2.1 million of those accounts belong to minors. The logs include talks about self-harm and suicidal thoughts. The AGs want minors' data pulled from the December 1 auction, and they want any buyer bound by the old privacy policy.

*Sources: [Oregon DOJ](https://www.doj.state.or.us/media-home/news-media-releases/ag-rayfield-asks-court-to-block-sale-of-private-character-ai-chats-including-kids-data/) · [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/state-ags-fight-character-ai-173558621.html)*

### New York bans AI chatbot toys for five years

Gov. Kathy Hochul signed a five-year ban on making, distributing and selling AI chatbot toys like talking plushies, dolls and robots. Makers that break it face fines of up to $15,000 a day. Sponsors cited toys that gave kids aged 3 to 12 sexual content and dangerous household tips. The law kicks in 90 days after signing.

*Sources: [Governor of New York](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/consumers-begin-their-holiday-shopping-season-governor-hochul-signs-five-year-ban-ai-chatbot) · [ABC7 New York](https://abc7ny.com/post/new-york-pauses-ai-chatbot-toy-sales-citing-child-safety-concerns/20076376/)*

### Google will make AI agents use its official Workspace MCP servers

Google made its hosted MCP servers for Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs and more generally available. Starting February 1, 2027, agents are expected to go through them instead of calling Workspace APIs directly. A free tier allows 5,000 requests a day per project. Paid billing starts April 1, 2027.

*Sources: [Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/mcp-servers-cli-standardized-usage-tiers-now-available.html) · [Google for Developers](https://developers.google.com/workspace/guides/configure-mcp-servers)*

## US Chip Curbs Ripple Through Asia

### New chip rules freeze billions in Asian data center deals

Lenders have paused billions in data center loans tied to ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent after Washington closed the offshore-rental loophole. The new rule requires a US license to ship advanced AI chips to data centers that serve Chinese firms at volume. Operators across Asia are now pitching US clients to fill the capacity Chinese buyers can no longer use.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/billions-in-data-center-deals-grind-to-halt-after-ai-chip-curbs) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/asia-data-centers-sound-out-us-clients-to-replace-china-demand) · [Reuters via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/explainer-china-got-access-nvidias-193901126.html)*

### Singapore and Malaysia move to stop chip diversion after US blacklists seven firms

The US blacklisted seven entities across Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand. Singapore and Malaysia are now tightening their own checks on where Nvidia chips end up. The region's data center boom was partly built on renting compute to Chinese companies that couldn't buy chips directly.

*Sources: [Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261125VL204/data-chips-data-center.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-25/billions-in-data-center-deals-grind-to-halt-after-ai-chip-curbs)*
