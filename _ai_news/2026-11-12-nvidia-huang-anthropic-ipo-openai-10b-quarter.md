The world's most valuable chipmaker may be about to fire its founder. Meanwhile, Anthropic's IPO is drawing more orders than it can fill, and OpenAI posted its first $10B quarter.

## Nvidia's leadership crisis

### Nvidia's board is reportedly preparing to remove Jensen Huang
The Wall Street Journal reported Wednesday that Nvidia's board is getting ready to oust Huang over two affairs. One was with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset. The other, per The New York Times, was with an Nvidia vice president. Shares fell more than 9% after hours, and Nvidia has lost about $600B in market value this week. The company hasn't confirmed the report or named a successor.

*Sources: [Detroit News (Reuters)](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/tech/2026/11/11/nvidia-is-preparing-to-oust-ceo-jensen-huang-wsj-reports/92733506007/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-jensen-huang-affair-employee.html) · [SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2026/11/11/jensen-huang-reportedly-fired-nvidia-board-affair-direct-report-alleged/)*

### Congress turns up the heat, and Nvidia's 2026 gains are gone
House Foreign Affairs Chair Brian Mast threatened a subpoena unless Huang answered by November 11. Lawmakers want him at a closed-door briefing on November 18. Some are now calling for his resignation and a federal probe. Nvidia stock was down more than 18% for November by Tuesday, wiping out the whole year's gains. Earnings land in less than a week.

*Sources: [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382974/nvidia-erases-2026-gains-republicans-threaten-ceo-subpoena) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/maryroeloffs/2026/11/11/lawmakers-urge-federal-probe-into-jensen-huang-over-affair-with-chinese-spy-and-call-for-his-resignation/) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/11/investing/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang)*

## The AI money machine: IPOs, revenue and compute

### Anthropic's IPO order book filled on the first day
Institutional investors covered all 825 million shares on day one of Anthropic's roadshow. The marketed range is $70 to $78, about $1.4T fully diluted. The FT reports bankers are now weighing a price above that range, pushing the valuation to $1.5T or more. Backers say Anthropic cares more about raising cash for compute than the headline number. It prices November 18 and trades November 19 on Nasdaq as "ANT".

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/anthropic-targets-64-4-billion-ipo-nearing-spacex-s-record) · [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/11/2026/anthropics-bankers-mull-raising-ipo-valuation-to-15t) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/11/anthropic-sets-a-1-4-trillion-ipo-target-but-bankers-may-already-be-aiming-higher-cfo/)*

### OpenAI crosses $10B in quarterly revenue for the first time
OpenAI made about $10B in the July-September quarter, up nearly 50% from $6.7B the quarter before. CFO Sarah Friar told investors the enterprise business doubled in three months. Losses still grew: operating loss hit $10.9B, up from $10.1B. Anthropic reportedly booked around $17B in the same quarter, though the two count revenue differently.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/openais-quarterly-revenue-reportedly-crosses-10b-for-the-first-time/) · [Investing.com (Bloomberg)](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/openai-quarterly-sales-surge-nearly-50-to-10-billion-bloomberg-reports-5017246)*

### CoreWeave revenue jumps 168% as its backlog hits $132.5B
CoreWeave posted $3.66B in Q3 revenue, up from $1.37B a year ago. CEO Michael Intrator called it "a breakout quarter." It raised full-year guidance to $13B-$13.3B and capex to $37B-$39B. The net loss also widened to $709M, so the bill for all those GPUs is still landing.

*Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/coreweave-q3-2026-slides-revenue-surges-168-margins-expand-93CH-5017372) · [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/10/coreweave-crwv-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/11/63136415/coreweave-posts-solid-q3-you-could-almost-hear-sigh-relief-analyst)*

## Google DeepMind ships power and proofs

### Gemini 4 Carbon opens to every paying developer
Google opened Gemini 4 Carbon to paid API users and AI Ultra subscribers on November 10. That ends a two-week window limited to cyber defenders. Launch pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output, doubling to $4/$20 after November 30. Google still says Argon is the better pick for everyday coding. Carbon is for the hardest, longest tasks.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-gemini-4-carbon-available-developers-ultra) · [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/11/11/gemini-4-carbon-available-developers-ai-ultra-pricing/) · [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/google-gemini-4-carbon-now-available-who-can-use-the-new-ai-model-and-how-much-does-it-cost-566790-2026-11-11)*

### DeepMind's AI formally verified Signal's encryption in four weeks
DeepMind used a system called AlphaProof Code to prove core security guarantees of libsignal 0.105.4, down to the compiled Arm machine code. Human experts reviewed each spec the AI wrote. It found no new flaws, though it did flag known bugs in older versions. Work like this used to take years, which is why it matters.

*Sources: [CyberInsider](https://cyberinsider.com/google-deepmind-signal-encryption-machine-code-verification/) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/10/google-deepmind-analysis-proves-signal-and-whatsapp-encryption-is-secure/) · [SC World](https://www.scworld.com/brief/google-deepmind-uses-ai-to-verify-signal-protocol-security)*

## Governments start writing the rulebook for frontier labs

### New York opens its frontier AI registry under the RAISE Act
New York launched a registration portal Tuesday for large frontier developers like OpenAI and Anthropic. They must sign up by January 1, 2027. A new office called DIGIT will enforce the law, with fines up to $1M for a first violation and $3M after that. From 2027, labs must report critical safety incidents within 72 hours.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/) · [Mezha](https://mezha.net/eng/news/mezha-636bec9b1738d1053c35ef6f_new_york-s_raise)*

### Washington plans an inventory of rules for AI labs, not new ones
The White House plans to publish a list of existing federal laws that already apply to domestic AI labs, per Reuters. It's explicitly not a regulation or executive order. Separately, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Tuesday the US and China should cooperate on AI risks while competing. Trump and Xi meet at APEC in Shenzhen on November 18-19.

*Sources: [FinChannel (Reuters)](https://finchannel.com/u-s-plans-to-publish-rulebook-inventory-for-ai-labs-not-new-regulations/140649/america/2026/11/) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/bessent-us-china-ai-superintelligence-dialogue/)*
