Meta fired 8,000 employees this week on the same day a leaked recording showed Zuckerberg explaining the company had been mining workers' keystrokes to train AI. Anthropic, meanwhile, is days away from closing a $30 billion round that would make it more valuable than OpenAI.

## Meta's AI Pivot Costs 8,000 Jobs and Its Workers' Trust

### Leaked Audio: Zuckerberg Says Meta Tracked Employee Gmail and VS Code to Build Training Data

A recording from a Meta all-hands meeting, leaked May 20, captures Mark Zuckerberg explaining that Meta has been monitoring employees' Gmail, Google Chat, the internal tool Metamate, and VS Code to train AI models. His reasoning: "the AI learns from watching really smart people do things." More than 1,000 employees had already signed a petition against the program — called the Model Capability Initiative — citing privacy concerns. Meta hasn't confirmed the audio's authenticity or officially responded.

*Sources: [Techlusive](https://www.techlusive.in/news/mark-zuckerberg-viral-audio-clip-reveals-meta-allegedly-tracked-employees-to-train-ai-before-layoff-1663209/) · [Common Dreams](https://www.commondreams.org/news/meta-ai-layoff) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneurondaily.com/p/meta-used-staff-as-ai-training-data-then-cut-them)*

### Meta Fires 8,000 on the Same Day the Audio Surfaced

Roughly 8,000 layoff notices went out starting at 4 AM Singapore time. Meta says the cuts fund its AI buildout — the company raised its projected 2026 capital spending to $125–$145 billion, almost entirely for AI data centers, custom chips, and model training. The timing was bleak: employees who had been used as AI training material learned of their termination as the story of that training went viral.

*Sources: [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/meta-employee-tracking-ai-layoffs-neuron/) · [The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/amp/tech/meta-fires-8000-employees-to-fund-massive-artificial-intelligence-push-1077405)*

## Anthropic's Expanding Reach

### Anthropic Set to Close $30B+ Round at $900B — Passing OpenAI as AI's Most Valuable Private Company

Bloomberg reported May 22 that Anthropic will close a new funding round topping $30 billion at a valuation above $900 billion, possibly this week. Co-leads Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks are each putting in roughly $2 billion, with Peter Thiel's Founders Fund and General Catalyst also participating. At $900 billion+, Anthropic would leapfrog OpenAI's $852 billion as the world's most valuable AI startup. The company told investors its annualized revenue will exceed $50 billion by the end of June.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317066/20260523/anthropic-funding-round-top-30b-900b-valuation-would-surpass-openai-most-valuable-ai-startup.htm)*

### Gates Foundation Puts $200M Behind Anthropic for Global Health, Education, and Agriculture

Anthropic and the Gates Foundation announced a four-year, $200 million partnership to deploy AI in underserved regions. The health focus starts with polio, HPV, and eclampsia — diseases where earlier detection could save lives at scale. In sub-Saharan Africa and India, the two organizations are building Claude-powered foundational literacy apps under the Global AI for Learning Alliance (GAILA). The deal gives Anthropic a significant footprint outside the enterprise market that currently drives nearly all its revenue.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/gates-foundation-partnership) · [Gates Foundation](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/ideas/media-center/press-releases/2026/05/ai-anthropic-partnership) · [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/anthropic-gates-foundation-launch-200m-initiative-to-tackle-disease-and-education-gaps-with-ai/)*

## AI Regulation Takes Opposite Paths

### Trump Kills AI Executive Order After Direct Calls from Musk, Zuckerberg, and Sacks

The White House canceled its planned AI oversight executive order hours before the signing ceremony. The order would have required AI companies to share advanced models with the government for a 90-day security review before public release. Trump said he "didn't like certain aspects of it" and didn't want to "get in the way of" America's AI lead. The final calls came directly from Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, and AI adviser David Sacks, all of whom opposed any new regulatory framework.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/21/trump-ai-executive-order-postponed-why) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/21/trump-ai-executive-order-postponed.html) · [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/05/21/white-house-tore-down-ai-rules-now-its-building-new-defenses/)*

### EU AI Act Delays High-Risk Compliance 16 Months in Broader Simplification Deal

The EU Council and Parliament agreed to slim down the AI Act and push its most demanding rules. High-risk AI systems under Annex III — covering employment tools, education, and law enforcement AI — now have until December 2027 to comply, 16 months past the original August 2026 deadline. The deal also adds a new prohibition on AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and extends SME exemptions to companies with up to 500 employees. The extended timeline reflects implementation challenges the EU hadn't initially anticipated.

*Sources: [Consilium](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) · [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/05/21/the-eu-simplified-its-toughest-ai-law-what-changed-and-why-it-matters) · [Global Policy Watch](https://www.globalpolicywatch.com/2026/05/eu-ai-act-update-timeline-relief-targeted-simplification-and-new-prohibitions/)*
