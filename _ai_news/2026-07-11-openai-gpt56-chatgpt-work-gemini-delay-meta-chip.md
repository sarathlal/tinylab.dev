OpenAI finally shipped its delayed flagship model this week, Google blinked on its own release date, and Meta made clear it wants out from under Nvidia. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## The Frontier Model Race Heats Up

### OpenAI Ships Three-Tier GPT-5.6 Family and a New "ChatGPT Work" Agent

OpenAI released GPT-5.6 in three flavors on July 9: Sol for frontier reasoning and long-horizon coding, Terra as a balanced everyday model at roughly half the cost of GPT-5.5, and Luna built for speed. Alongside it, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work, an agent that pulls context from connected apps and files to draft documents, spreadsheets, and presentations on its own. Investor Matt Shumer, who tested both, said Anthropic's Fable was still better "for almost every task," while T3 Chat's Theo Browne called Sol "world-leading in computer use."

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/09/ai-openai-gpt-release) · [InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4195478/openai-launches-chatgpt-work-as-it-broadens-gpt-5-6-rollout.html)*

### SpaceXAI Launches Grok 4.5, Calls It "Opus-Class"

Two days before OpenAI's launch, the company formerly known as xAI released Grok 4.5, its first model built specifically for coding and agentic work, trained in partnership with Cursor. Elon Musk described it as "an Opus-class model, but faster, more token-efficient and lower cost," pricing it at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. The launch came two days after xAI rebranded itself SpaceXAI, nesting its old logo inside SpaceX's identity.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/spacexai-releases-grok-4-5-which-elon-describes-as-an-opus-class-model/) · [x.ai](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-5)*

### Google Scraps Gemini 3.5 Pro's Base Model, Pushes Launch to July 17

Google DeepMind abandoned its planned Gemini 2.5 Pro-based architecture for Gemini 3.5 Pro and is rebuilding it from scratch, pushing the launch from June to July 17. Enterprise testers had flagged coding gaps and weak long-task reasoning against GPT-5.6 and Fable 5. The rebuilt version reportedly targets a 2-million-token context window and a dedicated "Deep Think" reasoning layer, but Google hasn't confirmed the date with a model card or pricing yet.

*Sources: [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/6f0c6bb2-795f-4c57-9d09-6db691d7638a) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319877/20260708/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-deepseeks-july-24-deadline-hits-developers-now.htm)*

## The Chip Wars Escalate

### Meta Building Its Own AI Chip, "Iris," to Cut Nvidia Reliance

Meta plans to start manufacturing its custom AI processor, code-named Iris, in September after wrapping initial testing. The chip is co-designed with Broadcom and built by TSMC, meant to complement rather than replace the GPUs Meta still buys from Nvidia and AMD. Nvidia shares still rose about 2% the day the news broke, as investors judged the threat as years away.

*Sources: [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/07/10/nvidia-stock-falls-as-memory-chipmakers-like-micron-surge-amid-easing-gpu-shortage-in-ai-infrastructure-market/)*

### Nvidia Down 15% From Its May Peak as Memory Becomes the New Bottleneck

Nvidia's stock has slid 15% since May even as its revenue forecasts keep climbing, while memory maker Micron has nearly tripled in value over the same stretch. High-bandwidth memory, not GPU supply, is now the tightest constraint in AI data centers — the spot price for an hour of H100 time has fallen steadily since peaking near $3.20 in May. Google, Amazon, Microsoft, and OpenAI are all now running custom chips of their own, chipping away at Nvidia's pricing power.

*Sources: [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/07/10/nvidia-stock-falls-as-memory-chipmakers-like-micron-surge-amid-easing-gpu-shortage-in-ai-infrastructure-market/)*

### DeepSeek Is Designing Its Own Inference Chip

DeepSeek is quietly building an AI chip focused on inference rather than training, aiming to cut its dependence on Nvidia and Huawei hardware, Reuters reports. The push lines up with the company's first-ever outside funding round, said to value it between $52 billion and $59 billion on a $7 billion raise. DeepSeek has already started talks with manufacturing partners and is hiring engineers for the effort.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/chinese-ai-startup-deepseek-developing-own-ai-chip-reuters-says) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2209378/deepseek-reportedly-developing-ai-chips/)*

### Perplexity Switches Some Agent Workloads to Nvidia's New CPU

Perplexity confirmed it will use Nvidia's new CPU for parts of its Comet agent workload, saying it runs AI coding tasks about 1.5 times faster than standard CPUs. It's a small vote of confidence for Nvidia's push beyond GPUs, at a moment when rivals are racing to build around it instead.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/perplexity-says-plans-nvidias-cpu-190347648.html)*

## Big Money Keeps Flowing Into Compute and Agents

### SambaNova Raises $1B at $11B Valuation, Lands JPMorgan Chase

SambaNova closed the first tranche of a $1 billion Series F on July 8, led by General Atlantic, pushing its valuation to $11 billion just five months after its last mega-round. JPMorgan Chase picked SambaNova as an inference-infrastructure partner, deploying its SN40L and SN50 systems for on-premises AI at the bank. CEO Rodrigo Liang said a second close is coming within weeks as more investors join.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/ai-chip-startup-sambanova-raises-funds-at-11-billion-valuation) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/sambanova-draws-1b-at-11b-valuation-in-series-f-first-close/)*

### An AI Agent Startup Let Its Own Agent Run the $100M Fundraise

Lyzr, which builds AI agents for enterprises, had its own agent, nicknamed SivaClaw, field questions from more than 130 investors and draft investment memos for its own Series B. The round is on track to close at $100 million on a roughly $500 million valuation, with $400 million in total investor interest from Silicon Valley, Middle Eastern, and financial-sector funds. It's a neat proof point for a company whose entire pitch is that agents can do real work unsupervised.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/a-startup-that-builds-ai-agents-used-one-to-raise-100-million) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/an-ai-agent-startup-just-let-its-agent-run-its-100-million-fundraise/)*

## Regulators Turn Their Attention to AI Accuracy and Security

### FTC Opens Comments on Policy Treating "Steered" AI Outputs as Deception

The FTC voted 2-0 on July 1 to propose a policy statement arguing that AI companies which secretly steer their models' outputs away from what users expect could be deceiving consumers under Section 5 of the FTC Act. The notice, published in the Federal Register on July 7, stems from a December 2025 executive order and takes public comments through July 31. It's the first time a federal regulator has floated treating quiet output-steering as consumer-protection violation, not just a safety question.

*Sources: [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-seeks-public-comment-policy-statement-addressing-ai-accuracy)*

### EU Unveils Action Plan on AI-Driven Cybersecurity Threats

The European Commission presented an Action Plan on Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence on July 7, aiming to build EU testing capacity for frontier models by 2027. The plan sets up a secure platform, run with the EU Agency for Cybersecurity and the Joint Research Centre, to simulate and test how AI models hold up against cyberattacks before they hit the market. It also launches an EU-wide "Grand Challenge" to fund cybersecurity-focused AI development at home.

*Sources: [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-presents-eu-action-plan-cybersecurity-and-artificial-intelligence)*
