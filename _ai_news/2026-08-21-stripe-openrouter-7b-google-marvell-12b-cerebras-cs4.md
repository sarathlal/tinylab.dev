Money moved fast in AI infrastructure this week: a payments giant bought a model router, a chipmaker handed Google a $12 billion stake path, and a wafer-scale challenger claimed a 30x speed record. Meanwhile the assistants racing onto your desktop picked up both new features and a scary bug.

## Custom Silicon Deals Reshape the AI Chip Race

### Google Wins Right to Buy $12.2B of Marvell Stock in Expanded Chip Deal

Marvell Technology announced August 19 that Google can buy up to $12.2 billion of its shares at $206.58 apiece, on top of an expanded deal to build custom AI silicon together. The custom chips cover inference accelerators, storage controllers, and memory interface hardware for Google's TPU ecosystem. Marvell's stock jumped nearly 10% on the news, and the warrant shares vest in tranches tied to every $500 million in chips Google actually buys.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/marvell-google-ai-chips.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/marvell-gives-google-right-to-buy-up-to-12-2-billion-in-shares)*

### Nvidia Denies It's Building a China-Specific AI Chip

The Information reported Nvidia plans to ship a language processing unit tailored for Chinese buyers by year-end. Nvidia shot the report down the same day, telling reporters flatly: "We have no LPU sales in the China market today, and no China-specific LPU product in our roadmap." The denial lands as Washington keeps tightening export licensing on any chip shipment touching Chinese-controlled buyers, even through offshore subsidiaries.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-ship-ai-chip-china-170938345.html) · [93.3 The Drive](https://www.933thedrive.com/2026/08/20/nvidia-to-ship-ai-chip-for-china-by-year-end-the-information-reports/)*

### Cerebras Claims Its New CS-4 Is 30x Faster Than GPU Systems

Cerebras unveiled the CS-4 on August 18, the first system built on its new Nexus architecture, packing three WSE-3 Turbo wafers per rack. The company says it delivers over 1,000 tokens per second on 10-trillion-parameter models and up to 4,400 tokens per second per user on GPT-OSS-120B, cutting wafer-to-wafer latency to 2 microseconds. That's double the speed of last year's CS-3 and 10x better throughput per watt, with first shipments due this quarter.

*Sources: [Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/introducing-cerebras-cs-4) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324925/20260819/cerebras-cs-4-extracts-record-decode-speed-without-new-silicon-generation.htm)*

## Stripe's $7B OpenRouter Bet Gets a Reality Check

### Stripe Finalizes $7B+ Purchase of AI Model Router OpenRouter

Stripe closed its deal to buy OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, with $1.5 billion of that going to the startup's founders. OpenRouter routes requests across 400+ AI models for 8 million users, including customers like Nvidia, Zoom, and Lovable, and the price is a 5.4x markup on the $1.3 billion valuation it raised at just months ago. CEO Patrick Collison said Stripe is "building the economic infrastructure for AI" so businesses can route tokens where they're cheapest and fastest.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/) · [Stripe](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-agrees-to-acquire-openrouter)*

### Collison Says "The Singularity" Has Begun, and the Internet Pushed Back

Days after the OpenRouter deal, Collison told Axios the singularity is already underway, pointing to Stripe's own token-routing bet as evidence economic activity is shifting to AI systems making purchasing decisions. TechCrunch and other outlets called the framing overheated, arguing the deal is really a straightforward payments-infrastructure land grab dressed up in sci-fi language.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/19/stripe-payments-openrouter-singularity) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/stripe-didnt-really-buy-openrouter-because-of-the-singularity/)*

## Desktop AI Assistants Expand — and Show Cracks

### Meta AI Gets a Native Mac App With Screen Sharing and Dictation

Meta shipped a dedicated Mac app for Meta AI on August 19, its first native desktop home. Users can point the assistant at any open window for context, or hold a shortcut to dictate text into any app system-wide. The 16MB beta app targets creators and small businesses, plugging into Facebook and Instagram analytics plus Google Workspace documents and calendars.

*Sources: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/19/meta-ai-mac-app/) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/19/meta-ai-is-now-available-as-a-more-capable-desktop-app-for-mac/)*

### OpenAI Makes GPT-5.6 Luna the Default for Free ChatGPT Users

OpenAI is rolling out GPT-5.6 Luna as the default model for Free and Go tier ChatGPT users this week, with unlimited text chats and a new "Think" button for harder questions following soon after, subject to abuse guardrails. It's OpenAI's clearest move yet to keep free users inside its own app rather than trading up to a paid competitor.

*Sources: [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/chatgpt-gpt-5-6-luna-free-default-unlimited-chats) · [ExplainX](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/gpt-5-6-sol-luna-chatgpt-unlimited-free-august-2026)*

### Microsoft Patches "CoSnitch," a One-Click Copilot Data-Theft Bug

Varonis Threat Labs found three chainable flaws in Microsoft Copilot Personal that let a single malicious link auto-execute a hidden prompt and exfiltrate data from a victim's connected Gmail, Drive, and Calendar accounts. An undocumented "?autorun=1" URL parameter was the trigger. Microsoft patched the bug, tracked as CVE-2026-24301, on August 18, and Varonis found no evidence it was exploited before the fix.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/microsoft-copilot-personal-flaws-could.html) · [Redmondmag](https://redmondmag.com/articles/2026/08/19/cosnitch-flaws-turned-microsoft-copilot-into-a-one-click-data-theft-tool.aspx)*
