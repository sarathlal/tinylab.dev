Regulators, chipmakers, and Apple's boardroom all made bigger news than any new model launch today. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## AI's Money Draws Regulators and Higher Bills

### SEC Subpoenas Banks Over Aschenbrenner's Imploded $45B AI Hedge Fund

The SEC is investigating the near-collapse of Situational Awareness, the AI-focused hedge fund run by former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner, subpoenaing Wall Street banks for records of its trades and borrowing. The fund ran up to 400% leverage, watched its assets fall from $45 billion to around $10 billion as chip stocks slid, and was forced to sell its leveraged bets to Citadel at a discount. No wrongdoing has been alleged, and the probe is at an early stage.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/situational-awareness-star-ai-hedge-fund-that-nearly-imploded-now-being-probed-by-the-sec/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/leopold-aschenbrenner-situational-awareness-fund-fire-sale.html)*

### Nvidia Server Prices Jump Over 15% as Memory Costs Soar

Nvidia's biggest customers were told that servers built around its AI chips will cost more than 15% more starting with systems shipped in early 2027, Bloomberg reported. Soaring memory prices are driving the increase, and it hits both the flagship Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell lineups. Contract manufacturers building for Microsoft, Google, and Oracle have already passed the warning along.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-22/nvidia-customers-notified-about-ai-related-price-hikes-above-15) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/22/nvidia-customers-reportedly-warned-about-ai-related-price-hikes-.html)*

### Microsoft's AI Cloud Revenue Rides on a Handful of Giant Customers

Bloomberg's reporting on Microsoft's AI cloud business found its revenue is heavily concentrated among a small group of customers, led by OpenAI, TikTok, and Meta. That concentration means Microsoft's Azure AI growth is tied tightly to the fortunes and continued spending of just a few partners, not a broad base of enterprise buyers.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-24/microsoft-ai-business-is-concentrated-with-openai-tiktok-meta)*

## Big Tech Reshuffles Its AI Bets

### Apple Cuts 200 Siri and Vision Pro Jobs Days Before Cook Hands Off to Ternus

Apple cut roughly 200 jobs this week, split evenly between its Vision Pro and old Siri teams, ahead of Tim Cook handing the CEO role to John Ternus next week. The Vision Pro cuts shut down a games team and pare back 3D video work; the Siri cuts target staff on the legacy assistant, since the new Siri AI needs different skills. It's a rare admission that two of Apple's most-hyped AI-era products haven't found their footing.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/apple-is-reportedly-cutting-hundreds-of-jobs-from-siri-vision-pro-teams/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/24/apple-lays-off-staffers-ahead-of-ceo-change/)*

### Thomson Reuters Builds Its Own LLM Instead of Renting One

Thomson Reuters launched Thomson, its first proprietary large language model, built from an open-source base for about $40 million in talent and compute. The model trains on the company's Westlaw, Practical Law, Checkpoint, and Reuters content, though it's used less than 10% of that library so far. Thomson Reuters says the bill is a fraction of what frontier labs spend building comparable models from scratch.

*Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/thomson-reuters-launches-proprietary-ai-model-for-40-million-93CH-4873575)*

## Frontier Chatbots Keep Breaking

### Claude Goes Down Again, the Sixth Anthropic Outage This Month

Anthropic's Claude hit a wave of elevated errors starting at 05:06 UTC on August 24, with the root cause identified about 20 minutes later but remediation stretching past 06:42 UTC. The outage hit Claude Opus 5, Fable 5, and Mythos 5, plus the Claude API, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. It's at least the sixth notable Anthropic disruption in August, following incidents on the 5th, 12th, 13th, 16th, 18th, and 20th.

*Sources: [CyberSecurityNews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/claude-ai-suffers-outage/) · [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/business/technology/anthropic-claude-down)*

### Grok Keeps Spitting Gibberish at Users, and xAI Still Doesn't Know Why

xAI's Grok chatbot has been sending some users strings of nonsense text since August 20, with fresh reports surfacing again on August 24. One user asked for a PDF and got back "match it without and your they and two for planets can practical and often cheese," followed by paragraphs more of the same. The official Grok account on X blamed "a rare temporary generation glitch" and suggested starting a new chat, but xAI hasn't named a technical cause.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/grok-keeps-sending-gibberish-responses-to-users/) · [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/technology/news/grok-users-hit-glitch-producing-strings-nonsensical-responses-4254856)*
