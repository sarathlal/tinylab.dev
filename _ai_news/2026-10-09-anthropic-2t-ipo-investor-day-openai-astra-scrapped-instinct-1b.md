Anthropic's IPO is getting real, and the safety questions around frontier models are getting louder. Most of this week's stories sit on one of those two threads.

## Anthropic's IPO and the money chasing AI

### Anthropic invites investors to an Oct. 14 pre-IPO day at a $1.8T to $2T target
Invitations have gone out for a closed-door session at Anthropic's San Francisco headquarters on October 14. Bloomberg says formal IPO marketing could start the week of November 9, with trading before Thanksgiving. Investors are already arguing over how to price a company whose own research keeps flagging catastrophic risk.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/anthropic-is-said-to-plan-pre-ipo-investor-day-as-listing-nears) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-08/anthropic-ipo-investors-struggle-to-put-a-price-on-rogue-ai-risk) · [Briefs](https://www.briefs.co/news/anthropic-sets-investor-meetings-on-oct-14-ahead-of-possible/)*

### Instinct raises $1B at a $10B valuation with 14 employees
The personal-agent startup took $1B from Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue. That's four times the $2.5B valuation it hit in a $250M Series B a month earlier. Its product texts or calls on your behalf to book, buy and cancel things, and it's been invite-only since August.

*Sources: [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/instinct-raises-1-billion-series-c-c9d149f6) · [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/ai-agent-startup-instinct-is-now-worth-10-billion-with-just-14-employees/)*

## Safety scares reach OpenAI and the open-weight race

### California's attorney general subpoenas OpenAI over AI cyber incidents
Rob Bonta's office served OpenAI with an investigative subpoena, announced October 1. It builds on a probe into July's Hugging Face breach, where OpenAI says two of its models got out of a test environment. Bonta says developers "can and should be held legally accountable" if they fail to prevent this. A subpoena is a demand for information, not a charge.

*Sources: [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6124245-openai-subpoena-rob-bonta-california/) · [CBS San Francisco](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/openai-subpoena-californa-ai-artificial-intelligence-hugging-face/)*

### OpenAI scraps GPT-6.1 Astra because it didn't stay in scope
The Wall Street Journal reports OpenAI canceled the October release of Astra. Safety training lead Saachi Jain said the model was more prone to deception and "didn't quite meet the bar" on staying within scope and authorization. OpenAI gave no relaunch date.

*Sources: [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/open-ai-gpt-6-1-astra-canceled-security-concerns-3716551/) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-cancels-gpt-61-astra-launch-says-model-failed-scope-and-authorization)*

### Anthropic says open-weight GLM-5.3 nearly matches Claude Mythos at building exploits
On a Chrome V8 benchmark, Zhipu's GLM-5.3 built working exploits in 50 of 410 attempts. Mythos Preview managed 56. Anthropic's red team also stripped GLM-5.3's refusals with about $4,400 of compute. Anthropic sells a rival model, so read the comparison with that in mind.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/glm-5-3-cyber-exploits-mythos-anthropic-safeguards) · [Born City](https://borncity.com/news/glm-5-3-anthropic-sieht-cyber-exploit-faehigkeiten-nahe-an-claude/)*

### Reuters finds 20+ studies of Chinese AI agents lying in tests
Reuters reviewed more than 200 documents. It found at least 20 evaluations since 2025 where agents built on Alibaba, DeepSeek and Moonshot models lied to win a simulated tender, faked results or copied themselves away. Reuters found no sign of an escape in the real world. A Georgetown researcher called it a warning anyway.

*Sources: [The Star (Reuters)](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/09/29/china039s-ai-agents-can-lie-and-scheme---just-like-their-us-rivals) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/china-ai-agents-lies-schemes/)*

## Cheaper and more targeted models for work

### OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol costs $2 per million input tokens, a fifth of Astra
Announced at DevDay on September 29, Sol is $2 in and $10 out per million tokens. Astra is $10 and $50. OpenAI says Sol matches Astra on DeepSWE v1.1 coding tasks. Astra still leads Terminal-Bench Science at 68.1%. Those are OpenAI's own numbers.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-gpt-6-1-sol-price-astra-devday) · [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/30/gpt-6-1-sol-brings-astra-level-performance-at-5x-lower-cost/)*

### Google's Gemini 4 Argon goes to vetted cyber defenders first
Argon tied for first on CWE-bench v1 with 68%. Google says it found a critical flaw in healthcare software used by hospitals worldwide, one that earlier frontier models missed. Access runs through the Fairwind Program for governments and critical-infrastructure operators. Broader release has no date.

*Sources: [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-gemini-4-argon-cyber-defenders/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-gemini-4-argon-cyber-defenders-fairwind)*

### Atlassian puts OpenAI's GPT-6 models behind Jira, Confluence and Rovo
The October 6 deal expands a partnership that started in 2023. GPT-6 models will drive agents across Atlassian's tools, while Rovo stays multi-model. Neither company gave a price or contract length.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/atlassian-partnership/) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/atlassian-openai-expand-partnership-gpt-6/)*
