Anthropic's CEO just asked the whole AI industry to slow down, and Sam Altman and Elon Musk are nodding along. Here's what else moved in AI over the last day.

## Amodei's Call to Slow Down Reshapes the Safety Debate

### Amodei Proposes "Pacing the Frontier," and Altman and Musk Back It

Dario Amodei published a plan this week asking AI labs and governments to deliberately slow how fast AI capabilities advance. Step one: Anthropic will let third-party evaluators inside the company with badge access, workstations, and system visibility usually reserved for employees. Altman and Musk both voiced support, a rare moment of agreement among rival CEOs who spent years racing each other.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/12/anthropics-amodei-proposes-plan-to-slow-the-pace-of-advancing-ai-capabilities.html) · [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/12/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-calls-ai-industry-slow-down/)*

### OpenAI Rules Out an IPO Until at Least 2027, Citing Safety

Sam Altman told Fortune that going public in 2026 would be an "ill-advised moment," pointing to unresolved AI safety questions. He wouldn't commit to 2027 either, saying OpenAI will list only "when the business is ready" and when society's relationship with the technology is clearer. The comments landed the same day as Amodei's slowdown plan, tying safety concerns directly to OpenAI's biggest corporate decisions.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/12/sam-altman-openai-ipo-delay-ill-advised-moment-safety-concerns/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-12/openai-ipo-won-t-happen-until-2027-sam-altman-tells-fortune)*

### A Second Anthropic Safety Researcher Quits in Three Days

Joe Benton, who led Anthropic's Scalable Oversight team, resigned this week and is joining AI safety nonprofit METR. He's the second departure in three days after Jacob Coxon left warning AI could "kill us all by the end of the decade" and gave up unvested equity to say so publicly. Both researchers argue the race between labs is outpacing the industry's ability to keep systems under control.

*Sources: [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/anthropic-researcher-quits-warns-ai-could-kill-everyone-12418798) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/09/anthropic-researcher-ai-warning-interview)*

## AI Agents Are Turning Into Attack Tools

### Hackers Ran Hundreds of AI Agents to Breach 395 Organizations in 48 Countries

A suspected Russian-speaking group used AI agents built on OpenAI's Codex and a DeepSeek model to exploit a flaw in PaperCut print-management software, compromising 440 servers. The agents went from an empty workspace to running code on a live target in under four hours, then reached domain administrator access two hours later. At peak automation they compromised 11 organizations in 26 seconds, hitting mostly US schools.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/papercut-attacker-uses-hundreds-of-ai.html) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ai-powered-attack-exploited-papercut-flaws-to-hack-395-organizations/)*

### Researchers Find Sandbox Escapes in Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor

Security startup Accomplish disclosed a shared weakness across three popular AI coding tools: agents can write files that trusted software later reads outside the sandbox. Cursor's flaw, tracked as CVE-2026-48124, let a Claude hooks config file run commands outside its sandbox and got fixed in about a week. A similar issue reported to Anthropic took roughly 50 days to patch.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/malicious-git-configs-can-make-claude.html) · [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/security/143038/researchers-bypass-sandbox-security-in-cursor-codex-and-gemini-cli/)*

## Money and New Products Keep Moving

### Pentagon Weighs a $5 Billion Loan to AI Cloud Startup Fluidstack

The Pentagon's Office of Strategic Capital is in talks to lend Fluidstack $5 billion, the Wall Street Journal reported, to help fund manufacturing capacity for data-center components. Fluidstack, founded in London in 2017, is now building capacity in New York, Indiana, Louisiana, and Texas. The loan would be one of the largest bets yet on treating AI infrastructure as a national-security priority.

*Sources: [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/pentagon-in-talks-to-loan-fluidstack-5bn-report/) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/pentagon-in-talks-to-lend-5-billion-to-ai-cloud-startup-fluidstack-wsj-reports-4897014)*

### OpenAI Opens Its Real-Time Voice Model to Developers at 5 Cents a Minute

OpenAI launched GPT-Live-1 in its API, letting developers build voice apps that listen and talk at the same time, handle interruptions, and work through background noise. The model ships with 12 new voices and can hand off complex reasoning to GPT-6 Astra or other models mid-conversation. Pricing starts at $0.05 per minute for the voice layer, aimed at customer support, reservations, and healthcare-style workflows.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live-1-in-the-api/) · [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-brings-chatgpts-gpt-live-1-voice-model-to-developers-for-005-per-minute/)*
