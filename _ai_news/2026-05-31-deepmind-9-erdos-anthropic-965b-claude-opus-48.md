Two AI math breakthroughs landed this week from different labs on different problems, while Anthropic crossed $965B in valuation and shipped its fastest model upgrade yet.

## AI Breaks Open Mathematics

### DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus Proves Nine Erdős Problems, Some Unsolved for 56 Years

AlphaProof Nexus published Lean-verified proofs for nine open Erdős research problems on May 21, including problems posed in the 1960s that no human had solved. The system pairs Gemini 3.1 Pro with Lean — a formal proof checker that validates every logical step against mathematical axioms — running agentic loops until each proof passes or fails. All proofs and natural-language writeups are open on GitHub.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317447/20260530/ai-math-proof-milestone-deepmind-cracks-9-erds-problems-magnetar-confirmed.htm) · [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/deepmind-alphaproof-nexus-erdos-problems/)*

### OpenAI's Reasoning Model Disproves an 80-Year Erdős Geometry Conjecture

An OpenAI internal model independently disproved the unit distance conjecture — a problem Paul Erdős posed in 1946 — by finding an infinite family of point constructions that beat square grids by a polynomial factor. Princeton's Will Sawin refined the improvement to δ = 0.014. External reviewers said the result clears the bar for a top-tier math journal.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/) · [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-just-solved-an-80-year-old-erdos-problem-and-mathematicians-are-amazed/)*

## New Models and Developer Capabilities

### Anthropic Ships Claude Opus 4.8 With Dynamic Workflows and Effort Control, 41 Days After 4.7

Anthropic released Opus 4.8 on May 28 — just 41 days after 4.7, its fastest upgrade cycle yet. Agentic coding benchmarks jumped from 64.3% to 69.2%, and knowledge work scores rose from 1,753 to 1,890. Claude Code picks up a new "dynamic workflows" feature for large-scale, multi-step problems. Fast mode runs at 2.5× speed and now costs three times less than for previous models.

*Sources: [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-opus-4-8-model-heres-whats-new/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-releases-opus-4-8-with-new-dynamic-workflow-tool/)*

### OpenAI Extends Codex with Computer Use on Windows

OpenAI added Computer Use on Windows to Codex on May 29, letting eligible users see, click, and type inside live Windows apps while iterating on builds. The update also adds remote continuation — resume any Codex session from a phone or Mac — and faster browsing for real-time debugging.

*Sources: [Releasebot / OpenAI](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/chatgpt)*

## The Valuation Race and Enterprise Spending

### Anthropic Raises $65B at $965B Valuation, Overtaking OpenAI for the First Time

Anthropic closed a $65B funding round at a $965B pre-money valuation on May 28 — the first time it has eclipsed OpenAI in market value since ChatGPT launched. Annual revenue run rate reached $47B, up from $30B earlier this year and $10B for all of 2024. About 80% of that revenue comes from enterprise API and cloud-provider deals.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/anthropic-raises-at-965-billion-valuation-eclipsing-openai) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html)*

### Dell's AI Server Revenue Hits $16.1B, Up 757% Year-Over-Year

Dell reported AI server revenue of $16.1B for its most recent quarter — a 757% jump from the same period last year and the company's fastest overall revenue growth since returning to public markets in 2018. The number shows directly how much physical infrastructure the current AI cycle is consuming.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/29/dell-stock-earnings-ai-servers.html)*

## Big Warnings and the Platform Shift to Agents

### DeepMind CEO Hassabis Calls AI a "Species-Level Transition" at Stanford

At Stanford's Graduate School of Business on May 29, Demis Hassabis said AI is advancing about 10 times faster than the Industrial Revolution and described the moment as a "species-level transition" that leaves humanity with "little margin for error." He put AGI 3-4 years out and called for global coordination on AI governance within the next five to ten years. Yann LeCun, also present, argued the opposite: current AI systems aren't intelligent at all.

*Sources: [Stanford Daily](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/05/29/deepmind-ceo-warns-about-ai/) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/deepminds-hassabis-sees-humanity-in-the-foothills-of-the-singularity-while-lecun-says-current-ai-isnt-intelligent/)*

### Microsoft Build Opens June 2 with Windows Repositioned as an AI Agent Operating System

Microsoft Build 2026 kicks off in San Francisco on June 2 with Satya Nadella's keynote pitching Windows as the platform for AI agents. Expected launches include new Agent Framework APIs, a Windows Agent Store with 85% revenue share for developers, WSL 3, and the formal addition of Anthropic Claude to Azure AI Foundry alongside OpenAI models.

*Sources: [Windows News](https://windowsnews.ai/article/microsoft-build-2026-windows-becomes-the-platform-for-ai-agents.420503) · [FAQ.com.tw](https://faq.com.tw/en/developer-tools/2026-05-27-microsoft-build-2026-sf-agents-azure-ai-foundry-en/)*
