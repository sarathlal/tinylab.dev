Google's Android Show on Tuesday shipped a platform-wide AI overhaul, while Anthropic launched its first major product for law firms. Add a $2.1B drug discovery round and three AI laws closing in on final adoption, and it's a dense 48 hours.

## Google Turns Android Into an AI Task-Runner

### Gemini Intelligence Can Book Rides, Fill Forms, and Add Groceries to Your Cart — Across Every App

Android's Gemini can copy a grocery list from Notes and add every item to your shopping cart without you switching apps. Google unveiled this and other agentic capabilities as Gemini Intelligence at Tuesday's Android Show: I/O Edition, a suite that completes multi-step tasks across all Android apps. The suite also adds Rambler to Gboard, which transcribes spoken messages and strips filler words, and a tool to create custom widgets in plain language. Rollout starts on Pixel and Samsung Galaxy devices this summer.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/12/google-brings-agentic-ai-and-vibe-coded-widgets-to-android/) · [Google Blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/android-show-io-edition-2026/)*

### Google Announces Googlebook — Android-Powered Laptops With Gemini Built In

Google unveiled a new laptop category called Googlebook at the same event, with Acer, ASUS, and Lenovo as launch hardware partners. The machines run Android apps natively, stream phone apps to the bigger screen, and ship with a Magic Pointer optimized for laptop workflows. First Googlebooks arrive fall 2026, taking direct aim at Windows Copilot+ PCs.

*Sources: [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/live/the-android-show-google-i-o-edition-live-all-the-latest-android-gemini-ai-and-android-xr-news-as-it-happens) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2171038/everything-announced-at-android-show-google-io-2026/)*

## AI Goes Deep in Legal Work and Drug Discovery

### Anthropic Launches Claude For Legal With 12 Plugins and a Thomson Reuters Integration

Anthropic released Claude For Legal on May 12, shipping 12 practice-area plugins covering contract law, employment law, and litigation, plus more than 20 MCP connectors to software that law firms run on daily: DocuSign, iManage, LexisNexis, Ironclad, Everlaw, and others. Thomson Reuters rebuilt CoCounsel Legal on the Claude Agent SDK, so lawyers can move between general AI and citation-grounded legal research in one interface. The launch marks Anthropic's first major vertical push beyond coding tools.

*Sources: [LawSites](https://www.lawnext.com/2026/05/anthropic-goes-all-in-on-legal-releasing-more-than-20-connectors-and-12-practice-area-plugins-for-claude.html) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/thomson-reuters-and-anthropic-expand-partnership-to-connect-claude-with-cocounsel-legal-302769890.html)*

### Isomorphic Labs Closes $2.1B to Bring AI-Designed Drugs to Clinical Trials

Alphabet's drug discovery spinout Isomorphic Labs closed a $2.1B Series B on May 12, led by Thrive Capital with backing from Temasek, MGX, CapitalG, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company, co-founded by DeepMind's Demis Hassabis, uses AI to design drug molecules from scratch and already has partnerships with Novartis, Lilly, and J&J. Isomorphic now expects its first clinical trials by end of 2026, a slight slip from Hassabis's original 2025 target.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/deepmind-spinout-isomorphic-labs-raises-2-1-billion-to-design-drugs-with-ai) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/isomorphic-labs-secures-2-1-billion-funding-to-scale-its-ai-drug-design-engine-302769674.html)*

## US States and the EU Rewrite AI Rules

### Connecticut's Omnibus AI Law Passes 131-17, Governor Lamont Expected to Sign

Connecticut's SB5 cleared the House 131-17 and Senate 32-4 on May 1, and Governor Lamont has said he'll sign it. The law requires chatbot operators to include safety features specifically for minors, mandates embedded provenance data in all AI-generated images, audio, and video from platforms with more than one million monthly users, and sets transparency rules for automated employment decisions. Most provisions take effect October 1, 2026.

*Sources: [CT Mirror](https://ctmirror.org/2026/05/01/artificial-intelligence-house-regulation-passage-ct/) · [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/05/unpacking-connecticuts-new-ai-law)*

### Colorado Rewrites Its 2024 AI Law Before It Ever Took Effect

Colorado passed SB 26-189 on May 9 and sent it to Governor Polis, replacing the original 2024 law after three sessions of industry resistance. The original targeted broad "algorithmic discrimination"; the rewrite narrows the scope to automated decisions in seven domains: employment, education, financial services, insurance, healthcare, residential real estate, and essential government services. It takes effect January 1, 2027.

*Sources: [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-labor-report/overhaul-of-colorado-ai-bias-law-headed-to-polis-for-signature) · [Colorado Newsline](https://coloradonewsline.com/2026/05/04/narrow-scope-colorado-ai-law/)*

### EU Delays High-Risk AI Deadlines to 2027-2028 and Bans Deepfake Nude Imagery

High-risk AI compliance deadlines in the EU shift to December 2027 under a provisional deal the Council and Parliament struck on May 7 — a significant reprieve from the August 2026 schedule. The agreement extends SME exemptions to companies with up to 500 employees and adds an outright ban on AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery. The deal still needs formal adoption by both institutions before it takes force.

*Sources: [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) · [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/eu-agrees-to-amend-ai-act-clarifies-overlap-with-machinery-rules)*
