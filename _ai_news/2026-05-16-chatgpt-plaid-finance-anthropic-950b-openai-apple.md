OpenAI dominated the past 36 hours: a new personal finance product went live, and a legal dispute with Apple started taking shape. Anthropic meanwhile is in talks for a funding round that would value it above nearly every public company on earth.

## OpenAI's Week — Finance Tools and Apple Trouble

### ChatGPT Can Now Read Your Bank Balance

ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US can link bank accounts, brokerages, and credit cards directly to the chatbot through Plaid — covering 12,000+ institutions including Chase, Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, and American Express. Access is read-only: ChatGPT sees balances, transactions, investments, and liabilities but can't move money or touch full account numbers. The feature is gated to Pro users ($100/month); Plus subscribers ($20/month) are excluded until OpenAI widens the rollout.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-15/openai-taps-plaid-to-bring-tailored-financial-advice-to-masses)*

### OpenAI Hired Outside Lawyers to Rework or Exit Its Apple Deal

OpenAI brought in an outside legal firm to examine its Apple partnership after the Siri/ChatGPT integration failed to deliver expected results, Bloomberg reported May 14. OpenAI wanted ChatGPT integrated deeper into Siri and placed prominently across Apple apps; instead it got limited placement and weaker-than-forecast subscription growth. The legal review may produce a breach-of-contract notice short of a full lawsuit. Neither company has commented publicly.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-14/openai-apple-partnership-frays-setting-up-possible-legal-fight) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-is-reportedly-preparing-legal-action-against-apple-it-wouldnt-be-the-first-partner-to-feel-burned/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/15/openai-legal-action-apple-siri-chatgpt-integration/)*

## Anthropic's Valuation Climb and Enterprise Lead

### $30B ARR, and Now a $950B Fundraising Target

Anthropic is in talks to raise $30B–$50B at a valuation of up to $950B — ahead of OpenAI's latest reported $825B. The company crossed $30B in annualized revenue in April, three times its figure from just five months earlier, driven almost entirely by enterprise API use and cloud-provider commitments from Google ($40B) and Amazon ($25B). A successful close would put Anthropic's paper value alongside the handful of public companies — Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia — that trade above $900B.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/anthropic-in-talks-to-raise-30-billion-at-900-billion-valuation) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/29/anthropic-weighs-raising-funds-at-900b-valuation-topping-openai.html) · [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/tech/anthropic-in-talks-for-funding-at-a-valuation-as-high-as-950-billion-which-would-make-it-bigger-than-openai/)*

### Anthropic Passed OpenAI in Business Customers

Data from corporate spend tracker Ramp shows 34.4% of businesses now pay for Anthropic versus 32.3% for OpenAI — a flip from a year ago when OpenAI led comfortably. Anthropic's business adoption quadrupled over the past year while OpenAI's rose just 0.3 percentage points. The shift tracks with Claude's dominance in coding and agentic workflows where API reliability matters more than consumer brand recognition.

*Sources: [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/tech/anthropic-in-talks-for-funding-at-a-valuation-as-high-as-950-billion-which-would-make-it-bigger-than-openai/) · [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/global-startup-funding-april-2026-anthropic-jeff-bezos-project-prometheus-biggest-deals/)*

## Video AI Race Heats Up Before Google I/O

### Gemini Omni Surfaces Inside Google's Own Interface

A UI string reading "Start with an idea or try a template. Powered by Omni" appeared inside Gemini's video tab on May 2, next to "Toucan" — the internal codename for Veo 3.1 — making clear Omni is a separate, newer system. Gemini Omni is expected to unify text, image, and video generation in one pipeline, a first for any frontier model. Google I/O runs May 19–20, with Omni likely as the keynote centerpiece and a public rollout expected within 2–4 weeks, tiered to Gemini subscription plans.

*Sources: [testingcatalog.com](https://www.testingcatalog.com/googles-gemini-omni-video-model-surfaces-ahead-of-i-o-debut/) · [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/) · [iMini AI](https://imini.com/blogs/gemini-omni-google-io-2026)*

### Runway Hits $5.3B and Openly Targets Google at Video

Runway added $40M in ARR during Q2 2026, pushing its valuation to $5.3B. The company built its name on video-generation models for filmmakers — most recently Gen-4.5 — but is now positioning itself as a world-model company, arguing that video generation is the path to general-purpose AI. That puts it in direct competition with Google (Veo 3.1), OpenAI (Sora), and soon Gemini Omni.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/runway-started-by-helping-filmmakers-now-it-wants-to-beat-google-at-ai/)*

## Open-Weight Models Keep Shipping

### DeepSeek V4 Pro: 1.6 Trillion Parameters, 75% Off

DeepSeek released V4 Pro on April 24 — a 1.6T-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 49B active parameters and a 1M-token context window. It requires just 27% of the inference compute of DeepSeek-V3.2 at the same context length and hits top-tier scores on coding and reasoning benchmarks. API pricing starts at $1.74 per million input tokens, with a 75% promotional discount running through May 31. Weights are open on Hugging Face; the Flash variant (284B params) runs at $0.14 input.

*Sources: [DeepSeek API Docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260424) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Pro) · [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/deepseek-v4-flash)*

### Mistral Medium 3.5: 128B Dense, Open Weights, Frontier Coding

Mistral released Medium 3.5 in late April — its first "merged" flagship that handles instruction-following, reasoning, vision, and code in a single 128B dense model rather than separate specialized ones. It scores 77.6% on SWE-Bench Verified and 91.4% on the Tau-3 Telecom agentic benchmark. Open weights ship under a modified MIT license via Hugging Face; API pricing is $1.50 input / $7.50 output per million tokens.

*Sources: [Mistral AI Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/model-cards/mistral-medium-3-5-26-04) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/mistrals-new-flagship-medium-3-5-folds-chat-reasoning-and-code-into-one-model/) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/02/mistral-ai-launches-remote-agents-in-vibe-and-mistral-medium-3-5-with-77-6-swe-bench-verified-score/)*
