Google lost the co-author of the transformer paper and a Nobel Prize winner to rival labs in the same three-day stretch. Meanwhile, DeepMind announced a $75M bet on Hollywood and published a security framework that treats its own AI agents as potential adversaries.

## Google Loses Two Foundational Researchers in Three Days

### Transformer Co-Author Noam Shazeer Leaves Google for OpenAI

Google's Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer announced June 18 he's joining OpenAI as Lead for Architecture Research. Shazeer co-authored "Attention Is All You Need," the 2017 paper that introduced transformers — the architecture every major LLM now runs on. Google spent $2.7 billion in September 2024 acquiring Character.AI assets partly to bring him back. He left less than two years later. Sam Altman called it a hire he'd "wanted since the very beginning of OpenAI."

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html) · [MLQ News](https://mlq.ai/news/openai-hires-transformer-co-inventor-noam-shazeer-away-from-google-deepmind/) · [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/noam-shazeer-leaves-google-for-openai/)*

### AlphaFold Nobel Laureate John Jumper Leaves DeepMind for Anthropic

John Jumper, a DeepMind VP and 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry winner for co-creating AlphaFold, announced June 19 he's joining Anthropic. His departure came one day after Shazeer's. AlphaFold has predicted over 200 million protein structures and cut years off biological research. Anthropic has been building AI-for-science capabilities all year — wet labs, partnerships with the Allen Institute and the Howard Hughes Medical Institute — a track that maps directly to Jumper's expertise.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/nobel-winner-john-jumper-to-leave-google-deepmind-for-anthropic) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/)*

## AI Moves Into Creative Production

### DeepMind Puts $75M Into A24 to Build AI Tools for Filmmakers

Google DeepMind announced a $75 million equity investment in indie studio A24 on June 22, paired with a research partnership to build AI production tools. DeepMind researchers will work directly with A24's directors and crews on new workflows, while A24 retains full creative control. Google receives no access to A24's content library or its data. First projects include AI-generated storyboards. A24 — known for *Everything Everywhere All at Once* and *Hereditary* — marks a departure from the stock-footage and VFX automation deals that have defined Hollywood's earlier AI relationships.

*Sources: [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/google-a24-ai-filmmaking-tools-1236787297/) · [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/06/google-a24-partnership-ai-filmmaking-tools-1236963944/) · [Google DeepMind Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/deepmind-a24-research-partnership/)*

### Suno Doubles Its Valuation to $5.4B on $400M Series D, Despite Active Lawsuits

AI music generator Suno closed a $400 million Series D led by Bond Capital in early June, reaching a $5.4 billion valuation — more than double its $2.45 billion mark from seven months earlier. The platform generates 7 million tracks daily and hit $300 million ARR after crossing 2 million subscribers in February. Warner Music Group settled and signed a licensing deal. Sony and UMG are still in active litigation over 61,000 songs, making Suno the clearest test yet of whether AI music companies can grow fast enough to negotiate from strength.

*Sources: [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/ai-music-suno-funding-round-400-million-5-4-billion-valuation-1236765727/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/still-facing-copyright-lawsuits-ai-music-generator-suno-raises-another-400m/)*

## US and EU Turn Up Regulatory Heat on Frontier AI

### Fable 5 Enters Day 11 Offline — Anthropic Says the Jailbreak Finding Was Too Narrow

Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 have been offline since June 12, when the US Commerce Department issued an emergency export control directive barring any foreign national from access. Rather than segment by nationality, Anthropic shut both models down globally. June 22 was the last day of complimentary Fable 5 access for existing Claude subscribers — meaning users are now losing both the model and its free trial window simultaneously. Anthropic publicly pushed back, calling the triggering jailbreak "narrow" and arguing it didn't justify a global recall of a model used by hundreds of millions of people.

*Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/13/us-orders-anthropic-to-disable-ai-models-for-all-foreign-nationals) · [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/ai-company-anthropic-suspends-access-claude-fable-5-claude-mythos-5-following-us)*

### EU High-Risk AI Compliance Deadline Is 40 Days Out — and Some Requirements Are Already Shifting

August 2 is when EU AI Act obligations hit high-risk AI systems: conformity assessments, registration, risk management, data governance, and human oversight all apply. The scope is broad — any company whose AI outputs touch the EU in a meaningful way may be in scope, including US operators. EU lawmakers reached political agreement in May on revisions that push some requirements to December 2027, giving enterprise teams more runway, though the core August 2 date for part of the framework holds.

*Sources: [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/us-companies-face-eu-ai-acts-possible-august-2026-compliance-deadline) · [Travers Smith](https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/eu-agrees-to-delay-key-ai-act-compliance-deadlines/)*

## Agent Safety Gets Formal

### DeepMind Released a Framework That Treats Its Own AI Agents as Potential Insider Threats

Google DeepMind's AI Control Roadmap, published June 18, starts from the premise that model alignment alone isn't enough. The framework adds 15 system-level defenses on top of alignment: a Supervisor Agent for real-time monitoring, cryptographic signing of agent actions, and an emergency kill switch, all drawn from MITRE ATT&CK threat taxonomy. The logic is defense-in-depth — if an agent ever acts against intent, structural stops exist. It's the most explicit acknowledgment yet from a major lab that deployed AI agents are, in part, an insider-threat problem.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/securing-the-future-of-ai-agents/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318758/20260620/google-deepmind-ai-control-roadmap-when-alignment-fails-defense-depth-takes-over.htm)*

### DeepMind Leads a $10M Fund to Study What Happens When AI Agents Interact at Scale

Google DeepMind, Schmidt Sciences, the Cooperative AI Foundation, and ARIA launched a $10 million research call on June 11 targeting multi-agent AI risks. The core concern: millions of agents from different organizations will soon operate across shared digital environments, and there are no good tools yet to evaluate or control emergent behavior when they collide. Applications close August 8.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/investing-in-multi-agent-ai-safety-research/) · [Blockchain.news](https://blockchain.news/news/google-deepmind-multi-agent-ai-safety-funding)*
