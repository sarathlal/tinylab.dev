The US just gave SpaceX permission to put a million AI computers in orbit. Back on the ground, Nvidia is fighting export rules and a credit agency, and the Bank of England wants to talk about who's paying for all this.

## Compute Goes to Orbit and Gets Leaner on the Ground

### The FCC lets SpaceX launch up to 1 million AI satellites

The FCC approved SpaceX's Starmind plan on November 25. It covers up to 1,000,000 solar-powered satellites that run AI workloads in orbit and beam results down over laser links. The license comes with hard deadlines: 100,000 satellites by November 2035 and all of them by November 2040, or the license is void. Astronomers, Viasat and Amazon's Kuiper team all objected over debris and collision risk. Musk says the first launches could come in Q3 2027 on Starship.

*Sources: [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/science/space/spacex-fcc-authorizes-1-million-satellites-data-centers-space-rcna611341) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/us-grants-spacex-approval-for-1-million-space-data-centres) · [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/green-light-for-starmind-spacex-gets-approval-to-deploy-1-million-ai-satellites)*

### Anthropic rewrote Claude's API in Rust and cut CPU per request by about 70%

Anthropic moved its Messages API from Python to Rust without taking it offline. The new service uses about 70% less CPU per request and over 80% less memory. Median time to first token dropped about 20%. Claude wrote the first version of the core request path in about a week. Proving it matched the old Python behavior took months, with output parity climbing from 24% in April to 99.6% by August.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/migrating-api-to-rust) · [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-anthropic-claude-messages-api-rust-migration/)*

## Nvidia's Bad Month Keeps Going

### Nvidia halts AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia

Partners like Dell and Supermicro told buyers in those four countries that Nvidia won't let their orders ship, the FT reported. The move follows new US rules, in force since last week, that require export licenses for advanced chips sent there. Washington says Chinese AI developers have used cloud servers in the region to get around existing curbs. Nvidia hasn't confirmed the halt, and it's unclear whether it's temporary.

*Sources: [US News / Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-11-26/nvidia-halts-server-shipments-to-four-southeast-asian-countries-ft-reports) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/11/26/nvidia-reportedly-halts-ai-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia-as-us-tightens-china-curbs/)*

### S&P puts Nvidia's AA credit rating on downgrade watch

S&P placed Nvidia on CreditWatch negative on November 25, after Jensen Huang's exit and return. "The long tenure of certain board members may have compromised their independence," the agency wrote. It also flagged possible export-rule issues tied to China. Any cut would likely be one notch, to AA-, with a decision due within 90 days. Nvidia shares are down about 38% since October 6.

*Sources: [RTÉ](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1126/1600723-s-p-on-nvidia-credit-rating/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-credit-rating-sp-creditwatch-governance)*

## Central Bankers and Banks Get Nervous About AI Debt

### Bank of England puts hyperscalers' AI exposure at just over $4 trillion

The Bank of England's November Financial Stability Report says AI infrastructure borrowing is almost double its 2025 level. Hyperscalers are on track to issue more than $305B of bonds this year. That's more than most governments borrow, Britain included. The Financial Policy Committee called the financing "increasingly complex, circular and opaque." It also warned that many smaller AI firms depend on money or contracts from a handful of giants.

*Sources: [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability-report/2026/november-2026) · [US News / Reuters](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-11-26/bank-of-england-sees-growing-financial-risks-as-ai-borrowing-surges) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ai-financing-bank-of-england-financial-stability-hyperscaler-bonds)*

### Citi and JPMorgan are shopping portfolios of AI loans

Semafor reports that both banks are marketing bundles of AI-related loans to outside investors. Some deals use synthetic risk transfers. The loans stay on the bank's books, but investors take the losses if borrowers default. The banks say it's about managing risk or freeing up room to lend more. Semafor's read is blunter: the risk isn't going away, it's just getting harder to see.

*Sources: [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/26/2026/banks-look-to-dump-ai-debt) · [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/citi-and-jpmorgan-shop-portfolios-of-ai-loans-to-shift-risk/)*

## OpenAI Retools ChatGPT for Businesses and Heavy Users

### OpenAI tests Private Inference so it can't read business prompts

OpenAI started an alpha of Private Inference with a small group of business customers. It runs prompts and replies inside confidential-computing hardware, so data stays sealed while the model processes it. Customers can connect AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or Oracle, and must keep safety records in their own storage for at least 30 days. Pricing and a general release date haven't been announced.

*Sources: [Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/enterprise/news-openai-private-inference-alpha-msp/) · [Computing.net](https://computing.net/openai-tests-private-inference-as-customers-take-custody-of-safety-records/)*

### ChatGPT merges Chat and Work under one usage limit

A small group of Pro users now sees Chat and Work as one product. Starting in December, Plus, Pro and Business plans will draw ChatGPT and Codex usage from a single shared pool. Instant chats won't count against it. OpenAI says most people won't notice, but "a small fraction of the heaviest users" may hit limits sooner. Exact token budgets per tier are still unpublished.

*Sources: [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/383963/openai-changes-chatgpt-drastically-cut-usage-limits) · [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/11/openai-merges-chatgpt-chat-work-shared-limits.html)*
