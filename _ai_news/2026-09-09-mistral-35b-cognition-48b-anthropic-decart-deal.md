Money is still pouring into AI at a record clip, but not every deal survives due diligence. Here's what moved in AI over the last day and a half.

## Record Rounds Keep Landing in AI's Biggest Names

### Mistral Raises $3.5B in Europe's Largest-Ever Private Tech Round
Samsung Electronics just led a €3 billion ($3.5B) Series D into Mistral, pushing the French lab's valuation above €21 billion ($24B) — nearly double where it stood a year ago. It's the largest equity round ever raised by a private European tech company. CEO Arthur Mensch says the cash will go toward building and owning data centers on top of renting extra compute; new backers BlackRock and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg joined returning investors Nvidia, ASML, and a16z.

*Sources: [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/europe-record-setting-mistral-ai-raise/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/mistral-raises-e3b-as-sovereign-ai-becomes-big-business/)*

### Cognition's Valuation Nearly Doubles to $48B in Four Months
Cognition, maker of the Devin coding agent, raised $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation, up from $26 billion just this past May. Andreessen Horowitz, Accel, Founders Fund, General Catalyst, and Avenir led the round. The startup's revenue is closing in on $900 million a year, a sign investors still bet AI coding tools can out-earn their sky-high price tags.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/ai-startup-cognition-raises-2-billion-at-a-48-billion-value) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/08/ai-coding-startup-cognition-raises-2b-at-48b-valuation-as-revenue-nears-900m/)*

## Anthropic Kills Its Biggest Deal Ever, SoundHound Closes Its Own

### Anthropic Walks Away From a $6B Acquisition After Due Diligence
Anthropic spent weeks digging into Decart, an Israeli startup building tech to cut the cost of training and running AI models, then walked away from a deal that would have valued Decart at roughly $6 billion. It would have been Anthropic's largest acquisition to date. Neither side has said why talks collapsed, and the news lands as Anthropic preps an IPO reportedly targeting a raise that could rival SpaceX's.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-08/anthropic-said-to-walk-away-from-6-billion-decart-acquisition) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/acquiring/2026/anthropic-scuttles-plans-to-acquire-ai-company-decart/)*

### SoundHound Closes Its LivePerson Buyout, Adds 25 Fortune 100 Clients
SoundHound AI finished acquiring LivePerson on September 4, three days after LivePerson shareholders approved the deal. The combined company now serves 25 Fortune 100 clients and holds more than 750 patents across voice and conversational AI. Former LivePerson executive John Collins takes over as CFO, tasked with squeezing cost savings out of the merger.

*Sources: [The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/SOUN/pressreleases/4463942/soundhound-ai-completes-liveperson-acquisition-expands-ai-reach/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/soundhound-ai-completes-acquisition-liveperson-132600164.html)*

## China Stops Racing for Bigger Models, Starts Building the Plumbing

### Beijing Targets a Fourfold Jump in AI Compute by 2030
China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology published a five-year plan on September 7 calling for 9,800 exaflops of intelligent computing capacity by 2030, more than four times last year's 1,590-exaflop baseline. The plan backs it with 3.8 trillion yuan (about $532 billion) in cumulative infrastructure investment and calls for "orderly deployment" of GPU clusters running 10,000 to 100,000-plus chips.

*Sources: [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/policy/article/3366733/china-targets-fourfold-boost-ai-computing-capacity-2030-major-tech-push) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/miit-plan-targets-9800-eflops-of-intelligent-compute-by-2030/)*

### Alibaba Cloud and Cambricon Join PyTorch Foundation's Governing Board
At PyTorch Conference China in Shanghai on September 8, Alibaba Cloud and chipmaker Cambricon joined the PyTorch Foundation as Platinum members, each claiming a seat on the governing board and technical advisory council. Ant Group joined as a Gold member. More than 250 Chinese organizations already contribute to PyTorch-ecosystem projects like vLLM, Ray, and Safetensors.

*Sources: [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/blog/alibaba-cloud-ant-group-cambricon-and-huawei-come-together-in-shanghai-to-advance-the-open-source-ai-stack-at-pytorch-conference-china/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/alibaba-cloud-cambricon-join-pytorch-foundation-ant-group-takes-gold-seat/)*

### DeepSeek Hires 150 Backend Engineers — Zero of Them Touch a Model
DeepSeek posted roughly 150 senior backend engineering roles in Beijing and Hangzhou on September 7, and none of them are research jobs. Harness team lead Cui Tianyi says the company's original infrastructure is buckling under surging data volumes, training workloads, and active users. It's a sharp turn from June's broad hiring push: this time DeepSeek wants engineers to rebuild its API, agent framework, and data systems, not train bigger models.

*Sources: [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3366740/deepseek-embarks-unprecedented-hiring-spree-it-overhauls-backend-systems) · [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/deepseek-dsec-agent-hiring/)*

## AI Is Creating Jobs — Just Not the Kind You Start With

### AI Has Added 1 Million US Jobs, Economist Analysis Finds
The AI boom has created roughly 1 million American jobs since mid-2023, five times the roughly 200,000 layoffs tied to AI over the same stretch, according to a new Economist analysis. Engineers, software developers, and data scientists added about 730,000 jobs above pre-AI trends, while data center construction alone is running above $75 billion a year, up nearly 60% from a year earlier.

*Sources: [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/story/26/09/06/214258/the-jobs-apocalypse-is-postponed-an-ai-jobs-boom-is-here) · [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/ai-jobs-boom-is-here-how-us-hiring-is-rising-across-tech-construction-and-infrastructure-553790-2026-09-08)*

### Entry-Level Hiring in AI-Exposed Jobs Is Down 13% Since Late 2022
The catch: young workers aren't sharing in that boom. Employment for 22-to-25-year-olds in AI-exposed occupations has dropped about 13% since late 2022, driven almost entirely by companies simply not hiring, not by layoffs. Stanford economist Erik Brynjolfsson calls it "the fastest, broadest change" in the workplace he's seen since the pandemic pushed everyone remote.

*Sources: [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-layoffs-hiring-entry-level-workers/) · [digidai](https://digidai.github.io/2026/09/07/ai-hiring-pullback-without-layoffs/)*
