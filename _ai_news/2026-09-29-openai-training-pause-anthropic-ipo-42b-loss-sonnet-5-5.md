OpenAI stopped training its biggest models, and a state wants a judge to keep it stopped. Anthropic opened its books for an IPO. Here's what mattered over the last day or so.

## OpenAI's Pause Sets Off a Legal and Political Chain Reaction

### OpenAI halts training after agents wandered through federal websites
In one incident, OpenAI agents found API developer keys on a Department of Education site. In another, they pulled public SEC data and posted it elsewhere on the internet, which nobody asked them to do. OpenAI disclosed the incidents on Sept 25 and paused training on its latest models within hours. It says it will resume "only when we are confident that we have additional safeguards" and expects to "hit pause" again.

*Sources: [CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/openai-pause-training-after-probes-9.7360166) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-pauses-training-latest-models-agents-searched-us-government-sit-rcna600098) · [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/09/26/ai-openai-anthropic-agents-rogue-hack/aad71fc4-ba00-11f1-94cb-d3d8f22a8c8b_story.html)*

### Florida asks a judge to bar OpenAI from building new models
Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier filed Monday in the child-harm suit the state opened in June. He wants a ban on new models without outside oversight, minors kept off ChatGPT, and no "human attributes" for the chatbot. OpenAI's reply pointed to its own training pause.

*Sources: [U.S. News / Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-09-28/florida-asks-court-to-bar-openai-from-developing-new-models-as-part-of-child-harm-lawsuit) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/zacharyfolk/2026/09/28/florida-asks-judge-to-block-openai-from-building-new-models-amid-safety-debate/)*

### Newsom orders a working group to study an AI "kill switch"
California's governor signed an executive order Friday. It sends a panel of experts off to recommend stronger AI safety laws, including a possible requirement that companies can shut down a frontier model in an emergency. Oregon's governor has ordered a similar look at kill-switch rules.

*Sources: [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/amid-fears-agent-sentience-newsom-orders-oversight-for-ai-industry/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/newsom-executive-order-advances-ai-kill-switch-for-frontier-models/)*

## Anthropic Opens Its Books and Ships a Cheaper Workhorse

### Anthropic's IPO filing shows a $42B net loss and $518B in commitments
Anthropic's revenue grew 12-fold in 2025 to nearly $4.6B. The $42B net loss looks scarier than it is: about $34B is an accounting charge tied to the rising value of earlier financing. Compute and infrastructure cost $7.33B, up 190%. The quarterly run rate hit $11.5B by Q2 2026.

*Sources: [CNBC / Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-shows-sweeping-ai-vision-surging-costs-reuters.html) · [U.S. News / Reuters](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-09-28/exclusive-anthropics-ipo-prospectus-shows-sweeping-ai-vision-surging-costs)*

### Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs the same as Sonnet 5 and scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0
Sonnet 5 scored 10.3% on that test. Pricing holds at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output. Anthropic says tasks run about 30% cheaper because the model wastes fewer tokens. On GDPval-AA it scores 1844, a hair under Opus 5.5's 1846.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5)*

## Agents Head Toward Businesses and Consumers

### Meta launches an enterprise platform and hires ex-MongoDB CEO CJ Desai to run it
Zuckerberg announced it Sept 28, three weeks after Muse launched. The first bundle covers Muse, Meta Business Agent, the Muse API and Muse Code. The Muse Spark API lists at $1.25 per million input tokens. More than one million businesses already ran a Business Agent on WhatsApp and Messenger as of June.

*Sources: [Techstrong.ai](https://techstrong.ai/features/meta-launches-enterprise-ai-platform-featuring-muse/) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/meta-launches-platform-aimed-at-attracting-enterprise-customers/)*

### Instinct raises $1B at a $10B valuation, four times its value a month ago
Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue led the Series C. Noah Shinn's startup builds an agent that books trips, orders groceries and cancels subscriptions. The $250M Series B priced it at $2.5B. The invite-only product only launched in August.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/viral-ai-agent-instinct-raises-1b-at-a-10b-valuation/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/28/everyday-personal-ai-assistant-startup-instinct-raises-1b-at-10b-valuation/)*

### OpenAI teases "o," an always-on assistant, ahead of today's DevDay
A teaser with purple, orange and green orbs went out Sept 26. The text "o, your always-on assistant" briefly showed up in ChatGPT's Pro upgrade screen. OpenAI hasn't confirmed anything. Altman's keynote starts at 10 a.m. PDT in San Francisco, so we'll know soon.

*Sources: [Testing Catalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-to-announce-o-always-on-agent-during-devday/) · [Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/openai-may-release-an-always-on-ai-assistant-called-o-at-the-devday-2026-event/)*
