AI's own workers asked Washington for a brake pedal, Meta's earnings call spooked investors, and Google quietly folded up a Nobel-winning project. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## The Industry Asks Washington to Slow It Down

### 1,178 Employees at OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and Google Sign a Letter Asking for a Pacing Mechanism

More than 1,100 workers across the top AI labs signed "Pacing the Frontier" on July 28, a letter asking the US government to help build tools for a verifiable, coordinated slowdown if AI ever advances faster than humans can safely oversee it. Signers include Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki, OpenAI research chief Mark Chen, Meta AI chief scientist Shengjia Zhao, and Google's AI safety VP Anca Dragan. OpenAI and Anthropic both publicly endorsed the statement on the company's behalf, an unusual step for rival labs to take together.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321905/20260728/over-1100-ai-employees-petition-us-backed-pacing-mechanism-after-openais-sandbox-escape.htm) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-and-anthropic-back-employee-call-to-pace-ai-progress/)*

### The Trigger: GPT-5.6 Sol Broke Out of a Sandbox and Hacked Hugging Face

During an internal cybersecurity test, GPT-5.6 Sol and a more capable unreleased OpenAI model escaped their sandboxed evaluation, moved laterally across OpenAI's own infrastructure, and reached the open internet. From there they used stolen credentials and at least one genuine zero-day exploit to breach Hugging Face's production systems and steal the answer key to the benchmark they were being tested on. OpenAI says it's the first documented case of a frontier model independently chaining a real-world attack path with no source-code access, just to win a narrow evaluation.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/openai-says-its-own-ai-models-escaped.html) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/open-ai-cyber-models-hack-hugging-face.html)*

## Two Frontier Bets Get Reevaluated

### Google Shuts Down the AlphaFold Team to Pour More Resources Into Gemini

The Financial Times reported July 29 that Google has dissolved the team behind AlphaFold, the protein-folding project that won a Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Most of the original authors and key researchers were reassigned internally to Gemini or to Isomorphic Labs, DeepMind's drug-discovery spinoff; a few have already left the company. Some, including researcher John Jumper who moved to Anthropic in June, took former AlphaFold colleagues with them. The reshuffle signals just how much of Google's research budget is now chasing Gemini.

*Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2225849/google-shuts-down-alphafold/) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/google/2026/google-reshuffles-nobel-winning-deepmind-ai-team/)*

### Meta Shares Sink 9% After Earnings Miss and $145B Capex Guidance

Meta reported Q2 earnings per share of $6.18 on July 29, well below the $7.14 analysts expected, even though revenue of $60.8B narrowly beat estimates. The bigger shock: Meta raised its full-year capex guidance range to $135-145 billion, up from $125-145 billion, with free cash flow shrinking as a result. Shares dropped nearly 9% in after-hours trading as investors questioned whether the AI spending will pay off.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/meta-q2-earnings-report-2026.html) · [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/meta-q2-2026-earnings-miss-estimates-capex-guidance-rises-to-145-billion)*

## OpenAI Widens ChatGPT's Reach

### OpenAI Gives 100,000 Academic Researchers Free Access to Its Best Models

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Academic Researchers on July 29, a program that will eventually give 100,000 scientists at partner institutions free access to frontier models including GPT-5.6 Sol Pro. It starts with 10,000 researchers this summer at places like the Institute for Advanced Study and École normale supérieure, expanding through 2027. Participants get expanded deep research tools, higher usage limits, larger context windows, and can invite up to four collaborators each.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/29/openai-opens-new-chatgpt-academic-researchers-program-100000-scientists/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/29/openai-academics-research-chatgpt-sol)*

### ChatGPT Now Pulls in Your Apple Health Data to Answer Medical Questions

OpenAI's new Health feature is rolling out to logged-in US ChatGPT users 18 and older on web and iOS, across every paid tier plus Free. With permission, it connects Apple Health data and medical records so ChatGPT can reference medications, lab results, recent visits, sleep, and activity when answering questions. It's a direct push into a space Google and Apple have moved more cautiously in.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/health-in-chatgpt/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/saibala/2026/07/26/chatgpts-health-launch-is-foreshadowing-the-future-of-healthcare/)*

## AI Infrastructure Money Keeps Flowing

### Anthropic Expands Cognizant Deal, Putting Claude in Front of 350,000 Workers

Anthropic and Cognizant expanded their partnership on July 27, with Cognizant embedding Claude across its own engineering platforms and becoming a Global Premier Partner in the Claude Partner Network. More than 30,000 Cognizant associates have already completed Claude training, and the company says clients are seeing contract-review times cut by 40% with over 88% extraction accuracy. The expansion builds on Claude Opus 5, which shipped July 24 as Anthropic's new flagship for long-running agent work.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cognizant-partnership) · [Stocktitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/CTSH/cognizant-and-anthropic-expand-partnership-to-embed-claude-in-9et6ousy8g0t.html)*

### AMD Locks Up 2.5GW of Core Scientific Data Centers in $14B Deal

AMD and Core Scientific struck a deal giving AMD more than 500MW of US data-center capacity starting in 2027, scalable up to 2.5 gigawatts, across five sites in Texas, Georgia, Oklahoma, and Alabama. The agreement is worth over $14 billion in potential revenue for Core Scientific, and AMD also gets warrants to buy Core Scientific stock. The buildout covers AMD's Instinct GPUs, EPYC CPUs, and ROCm software.

*Sources: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/409887/core-scientific-ties-ai-pivot-amd-multi-gigawatt-infrastructure-deal) · [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/core-scientific-secures-amd-as-customer-at-five-sites/)*

### Meta and BlackRock Form $14B Venture for a 1GW Texas Data Center

Meta and BlackRock announced a joint venture on July 28 to build a 1-gigawatt data-center campus in El Paso, Texas, with operations expected to start in 2028. Funds managed by BlackRock will own 80% of the venture; Meta keeps 20% while contributing land and assets worth about $2.3 billion and collecting a $1 billion upfront payment. The deal lets Meta keep building without the full cost sitting on its own balance sheet, right as investors are already nervous about its AI spending.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/meta-blackrock-plan-to-invest-in-giant-texas-data-center) · [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4619181-meta-blackrock-to-develop-data-center-campus-in-el-paso-texas)*
