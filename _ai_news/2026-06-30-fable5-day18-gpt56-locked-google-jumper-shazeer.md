Three weeks into the first US AI export ban, Anthropic's most-used model remains offline — while OpenAI ships a new model family no one outside twenty companies can touch. Google, meanwhile, lost two of its most important AI researchers in one week.

## The Government's New Checkpoint for Frontier AI

### Anthropic's Fable 5 Hits Day 18 Offline as US Clears Mythos 5 for Critical Infrastructure

The US Commerce Department issued an export control directive on June 12 ordering Anthropic to shut down all access to Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 for foreign nationals. Both models had launched on June 9. Anthropic complied within 90 minutes. On June 26, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick sent a letter to Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown granting a partial reprieve: Mythos 5 — the unrestricted variant — may now be accessed without an export license by US organizations defending critical infrastructure. Fable 5, the version most developers rely on, is still down. Talks between Anthropic and Commerce are continuing.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319213/20260628/claude-fable-5-still-offline-us-clears-mythos-5-critical-infrastructure.htm) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kateoflahertyuk/2026/06/29/us-government-partially-lifts-anthropic-ai-export-ban-what-it-means/) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access)*

### OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna Land — But Only for ~20 Trusted Partners

OpenAI previewed three new models on June 26: Sol (flagship, strongest on reasoning and cybersecurity), Terra (balanced, lower cost), and Luna (fastest, cheapest). Only roughly 20 pre-screened organizations can access them via the API and Codex so far. The restricted rollout follows Trump's June 2 executive order, which asks AI labs to give the government up to 30 days to evaluate new models before public release. OpenAI says a wider rollout is coming in "the coming weeks."

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/openai-limits-new-ai-models-to-trusted-partners-request-us-government.html) · [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001325-a-preview-of-gpt-56-sol-terra-and-luna)*

### Trump's June 2 Executive Order Is the Framework Behind These Restricted Releases

President Trump signed "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" on June 2 — a voluntary framework asking AI labs to submit their most capable models for government testing up to 30 days before public release. Federal agencies have 60 days to develop a classified benchmark for AI cybersecurity capabilities, run by the NSA. The thresholds won't be public. The original draft proposed a 90-day review window; the signed version cut it to 30.

*Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/trump-executive-order-ai.html)*

## Google Loses Two of Its Best Researchers in One Week

### Noam Shazeer — Transformer Co-Author and Gemini Co-Lead — Moves to OpenAI

Shazeer announced on June 18 that he was leaving Google DeepMind for OpenAI. He's one of the eight co-authors of the 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper and was co-leading Gemini when he resigned. Alphabet had paid an estimated $2.7 billion in a 2024 acqui-hire to bring him back — a deal designed specifically to install him as Gemini's co-lead. He lasted under two years before moving to Google's biggest competitor. Alphabet shares fell more than 5% after the news.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/23/google-deepmind-ai-researcher-departures-raise-doubts-about-ability-to-win-the-ai-race-shazeer-jumper-eye-on-ai/)*

### Nobel Laureate John Jumper Leaves DeepMind for Anthropic

Two days after Shazeer's exit, John Jumper — who shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for AlphaFold — announced he was also leaving Google DeepMind for Anthropic, after nearly nine years at the company. Jumper co-led development of AlphaFold, which predicted 3D protein structures from amino acid sequences and solved a 50-year biology challenge. Anthropic paid $400 million in stock in April for Coefficient Bio, a computational biology startup mostly staffed by former Genentech researchers. Today, Anthropic is running an "AI for Science" livestream with pharma, biotech, and research institutions — the Jumper hire makes the timing deliberate.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/nobel-winner-john-jumper-to-leave-google-deepmind-for-anthropic)*

### Gemini 3.5 Pro Misses Sundar Pichai's Own June Deadline

At Google I/O on May 19, Sundar Pichai asked developers to "give us until next month" to ship Gemini 3.5 Pro. June is over and it's still not publicly available. Google says feedback from early enterprise testers is being used to refine coding performance and token efficiency. A public release is now expected in July. Gemini 3.5 Flash shipped at I/O; Pro has stayed in limited Vertex AI preview since.

*Sources: [Swisher Post](https://www.swisherpost.com/technology/gemini-3-5-pro-june-launch-delay/) · [Bind AI](https://blog.getbind.co/gemini-3-5-pro-slips-to-july-and-four-senior-google-researchers-just-left-for-anthropic/)*

## State AI Laws Shift Gear This Summer

### Colorado's Landmark AI Act — Due Today — Gets Replaced with a Lighter Law

June 30 was the date Colorado's 2024 AI Act was set to take effect — the first comprehensive state AI law in the US. It didn't. Governor Polis signed SB 26-189 on May 14, replacing it with a narrower statute targeting automated decision-making in consequential decisions like employment, housing, and credit. The original law required broad risk management programs and impact assessments. The replacement asks only for pre-use consumer notices and 30-day explanations after adverse outcomes. It takes effect January 1, 2027, with attorney general-only enforcement.

*Sources: [Seyfarth](https://www.seyfarth.com/news-insights/colorado-enacts-artificial-intelligence-replacement-law.html) · [Baker Botts](https://ourtake.bakerbotts.com/post/102msga/colorado-repeals-and-replaces-ai-act) · [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-us/knowledge/publications/18733d31/colorado-enacts-revised-ai-law)*

### California's AI Watermark Law Takes Effect August 2

California's SB 942 — the AI Transparency Act — takes effect on August 2, 2026. It requires generative AI providers to offer watermarks, latent disclosures, and detection tools for synthetic content. The law targets providers, not just deployers, and covers any system capable of producing synthetic media at scale. It's the first US law to mandate SynthID-style watermarking across AI-generated content.

*Sources: [verifywise.ai](https://verifywise.ai/blog/state-of-ai-governance-regulations-united-states-2026)*
