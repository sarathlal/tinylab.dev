The money today went into making Nvidia's chips less necessary, from Amazon's biggest AI buy of the year to a six-month-old startup with a billion-dollar seed valuation. Meanwhile, AI agents got permission to approve code, and OpenAI says its models can now design working proteins.

## Amazon and Startups Bet Against Nvidia's Grip

### Amazon buys Decart's optimization tech and team for a reported $7.5B
Amazon is buying the software Decart built to make AI models run faster on different chips, plus the people who built it. Bloomberg puts the price at about $7.5B, and Globes reports the founders will share $4.8B. Amazon wants the software to speed up its own Trainium and Inferentia chips, so they look like a real alternative to Nvidia. Anthropic reportedly talked about buying Decart for around $6B before those talks fell apart in September. The deal still needs regulatory approval.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/amazon-snaps-up-decarts-ai-optimization-tech-for-7-5b-as-it-prepares-to-sell-trainium-chips/) · [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/amazon-to-buy-israeli-startup-decart-for-a-reported-7-5-billion-tapping-ai-talent/) · [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-amazon-confirms-decart-acquisition-1001564520)*

### Marlowe raises a $277M seed to run AI on AMD and Etched chips
The startup is six months old and already valued at $1.15B. Accel led the round, with Bessemer, Altimeter and Atreides joining. Marlowe doesn't design chips. It buys them from AMD, Etched and others, then tunes the hardware, networking and software for each customer. It claims 2-3x more output per watt and per dollar than the usual setup. Its first site, running Etched chips, is due in December.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/marlowe-277m-seed-multi-silicon-ai-inference-factories) · [Investing.com (Bloomberg)](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/marlowe-raises-277-million-for-nvidia-chip-alternatives--bloomberg-93CH-5045596)*

## AI Agents Approve Code, and Models Design Proteins

### Anthropic's Claude Ship lets AI agents fix and approve pull requests
Claude Ship bundles three agents for the work that happens after code is written. Code Review is already out. PR Steward fixes failing CI checks, merge conflicts and review comments. Claude Approvals can sign off on changes using rules a team writes in plain English. Both new agents are in beta. Anthropic suggests running Approvals in observe-only mode first. It's for Team and Enterprise customers on GitHub, billed by tokens. Anthropic says its own engineers more than doubled merged changes between June and September.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-launches-claude-ship-letting-ai-agents-review-fix-and-approve-code-changes/) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/383929/anthropic-claude-ship-ai-write-review-approve-own-code) · [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/11/24/anthropic-claude-ship-code-review-pr-steward-claude-approvals/)*

### OpenAI says GPT-6 can design proteins without biology tools
In wet-lab tests with partner Onava, 35 of 322 minibinders designed by GPT-6 bound their targets. That's a 10.9% hit rate. The model worked with only reasoning and code, no specialist biology models. On MotifBench it hit 43.7% versus 27.8% for RFdiffusion. OpenAI admits it still trails specialist models at predicting full protein structures. It also says this isn't yet an end-to-end workflow without human experts.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/) · [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-research/2026/gpt-6-designs-proteins-without-bio-models)*

### Z.ai ships GLM-5.4, its first loop transformer
GLM-5.4 runs 28 layers of its roughly 750B-parameter base model twice on each pass. That's a different way to get more thinking out of the same weights. It adds vision and a 1M-token context window. For now it's only in Z.ai's coding plans, which start at $18 a month. The API and open weights wait on safety checks, and there are no benchmark scores yet. Z.ai admits the model sometimes gets stuck in long reasoning loops.

*Sources: [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/z-ai-launches-glm-5-4-its-first-loop-transformer-model/) · [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/glm-5-4)*

## Governments and Courts Set the Terms

### Trump backed Huang's return partly to protect open-weight AI
Reuters reports that Trump and JD Vance personally weighed in on Nvidia's decision to reinstate Jensen Huang. Sources say they saw him as a defender of US-made open-weight models. Huang wrote to Washington earlier this year warning against "premature restrictions" on open models. A White House official called support for open weights a "clear and long-standing policy." The administration is also weighing rules that would make US companies disclose their use of Chinese AI models.

*Sources: [Investing.com (Reuters)](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/trump-saw-nvidias-huang-as-protector-of-openweight-ai-models-sources-say-5046265) · [Yahoo Finance (Reuters)](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/huangs-return-nvidia-brings-open-222900636.html)*

### Australia's duty of care law will cover AI chatbots
Communications Minister Anika Wells struck a deal with the Greens on Tuesday and said the bill will pass this week. It sets safety rules for social media, games and AI chatbots, with a duty to protect under-18s from harmful content. The Greens won two big changes. Fines can reach 5% of global revenue, and Australians will be able to sue platforms directly. Companies get 12 months to comply. The Trump administration had raised "serious concerns" about the draft.

*Sources: [Local10 (AP)](https://www.local10.com/tech/2026/11/24/australia-set-to-pass-a-digital-duty-of-care-law-with-fines-up-to-5-of-tech-giants-global-revenue/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/australia-duty-of-care-right-to-sue-fines) · [Prime Minister of Australia](https://www.pm.gov.au/media/digital-duty-care)*

### Bids close for Character.AI, with users' chats up for sale
Bids for the bankrupt chatbot company were due Tuesday at noon, and the auction is set for December 1. The lead bid for the app, its models and its user data is $10M from Smirks and Pangs Acquisition. Disney holds a separate $20M bid for a license to some of its code and patents. Forty state attorneys general have filed conditions any buyer must accept. If no one offers to keep the service running, Character.AI says it will shut down.

*Sources: [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/biz/news/disney-bids-on-character-ai-assets-chapter-11-bankruptcy-1236909984/) · [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/character-ai-users-chats-face-a-10m-sale-to-smirks-and-pangs/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/10/13/characterai-files-for-bankruptcy-disney-bids-20-million-after-copyright-dispute/)*
