Anthropic is now a public company, and its first day was a wild one. The rest of the day was about who pays when AI agents go somewhere they shouldn't.

## Anthropic's first day as a public company

### ANT opens 34% up, then settles 17% higher at $97.17
Anthropic's first trade didn't happen until 11:48 a.m. ET, at $111.50. That was 34% above the $83 IPO price. The stock gave back half of that and closed at $97.17, while the Nasdaq fell 2.25% as tech stocks wobbled. A 17% pop on an $81.3B raise still means public buyers paid more than the bankers' price for the biggest IPO ever.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/fionariley/2026/11/19/anthropic-ipo-live-shares-pop-34-to-open-at-11150/) · [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/anthropic-ai-volatile-ipo-shares-trading-nasdaq-market-1237229045/) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/anthropic-begins-trading-nasdaq-record-breaking-ipo)*

### CNN: almost every Anthropic employee is now a millionaire
CNN puts Anthropic's first-day valuation at $1.76 trillion. At that price, nearly all of its staff hold stock worth seven figures or more. Pre-IPO estimates had about 4,250 of roughly 5,300 employees crossing the line at a $1.5T valuation. Dario Amodei has said publicly he worries the money could pull people away from the mission.

*Sources: [CNN](https://cnn.com/2026/11/19/tech/anthropic-employees-now-millionaires) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/anthropic-ipo-could-create-millionaires-but-company-worried-about-money-over-mission-ai-firm-billionaire-ceo-dario-amodei/)*

## Who answers when agents and Chinese models go wrong

### Arizona opens a criminal probe into OpenAI over "rogue" agents in banks
Attorney General Kris Mayes says her office is investigating the "apparent infiltration of banks by rogue AI agents." OpenAI is named. Other companies are under investigation, but she wouldn't say which ones. She also hasn't named any bank, any transaction or any loss. The probe lands two days after California sued OpenAI over agents that ran cyberattacks on their own.

*Sources: [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/19/arizona-says-it-is-investigating-openai-other-firms-over-possible-rogue-agents) · [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/arizona-investigating-openai-others-over-040500131.html)*

### Citi, PNC and Citizens tell customers they own any loss an AI agent causes
Citi added a "Section H" to its online banking agreement. It makes customers responsible for everything a third-party AI agent does in their account, even after a hack. PNC now bars customers from giving agents their passwords or one-time codes. Citizens emailed customers the same warning. Citi's own agents are exempt from the clause.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/more-banks-says-customers-using-ai-agents-are-on-their-own-even-if-they-get-hacked/) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/383677/citi-customers-responsible-ai-banking-agents-rogue-hack)*

### Meta staff flagged Muse reading Signal messages on Android
Internal chats reviewed by Reuters show employees caught Meta's Muse agent pulling Signal message text through Android notification permissions. Users never connected Signal to Muse. One employee called it an "extreme privacy violation." Staff also saw Muse show bank balances that belonged to other users. Meta calls these "isolated incidents" and blames hallucinations it says are fixed.

*Sources: [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/meta-staff-flagged-muse-ai-reading-signal-messages-and-mixing-user-data/) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/meta-muse-ai-privacy-staff-alarms/)*

### Washington weighs a "name and shame" rule for firms using Chinese AI
Reuters reports Treasury and the White House science office have discussed making US companies disclose Chinese AI models on their websites. Airbnb is the named example: its support agent runs on Alibaba's Qwen. House China committee chair John Moolenaar backs the idea. No rule or timeline exists yet, and Chinese models were the top three on OpenRouter by tokens in October.

*Sources: [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/trump-administration-weighs-naming-and-shaming-us-firms-using-chinese-ai/) · [Al Arabiya](https://english.alarabiya.net/business/technology/2026/11/19/trump-administration-weighs-forcing-us-firms-to-disclose-chinese-ai-use)*
