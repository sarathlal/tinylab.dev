Anthropic passed OpenAI in paying business customers last week. The driver was almost entirely Claude Code. A $30B funding round may close this month that would push its valuation past $900B. Meanwhile, Google spent the last 24 hours teasing what's coming at I/O tomorrow.

## Anthropic Overtakes OpenAI on Every Front

### Claude Passes OpenAI in Business Adoption for the First Time

34.4% of U.S. businesses now pay for Anthropic's Claude vs. 32.3% for OpenAI's ChatGPT, according to Ramp's May AI Index. Anthropic quadrupled its business adoption in a year; OpenAI grew by just 0.3%. The crossover tracks Claude Code directly — developers brought Claude into their stacks, and the finance data followed.

*Sources: [Ramp AI Index](https://ramp.com/leading-indicators/ai-index-may-2026) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/anthropic-now-has-more-business-customers-than-openai-according-to-ramp-data/)*

### Claude Code Now Writes Nearly 1-in-10 Public GitHub Commits

Claude Code's share of global public GitHub commits has grown from 4% in February to roughly 10% now, with SemiAnalysis projecting 20% by year-end. At peak it's producing over 326,000 daily commits. Anthropic's annualized revenue has doubled in just two months off the back of it.

*Sources: [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/claude-code-is-the-inflection-point) · [CoreMention](https://coremention.com/blog/claude-code-tracker/)*

### Anthropic in Talks to Raise $30B at a $900B Valuation

Bloomberg reported Anthropic is negotiating a new round at a post-money valuation above $900B — which would put it past OpenAI's private market price for the first time. The deal could close by end of May. If it lands, Anthropic's total capital raised will exceed $60B.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/anthropic-in-talks-to-raise-30-billion-at-900-billion-valuation) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/anthropic-targets-900-billion-valuation-220005674.html)*

## Google Reveals I/O Highlights a Day Early

### Gemini Omni Video Model Surfaces Before Tomorrow's Keynote

A "Gemini Omni" mode appeared inside the Gemini UI before Google announced it. The model is expected to unify text, image, and video generation into a single pipeline — a first for any mainstream AI assistant. Google's I/O keynote starts May 19 at 10 AM PT; a full Gemini model reveal is expected alongside it.

*Sources: [Testing Catalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/googles-gemini-omni-video-model-surfaces-ahead-of-i-o-debut/) · [Nokia Power User](https://nokiapoweruser.com/google-io-2026-gemini-spark-omni-gemini-3-5-rumors/)*

### Google Previews Googlebook and Android XR Glasses at Pre-Show

At the Android Show: I/O Edition on May 17, Google revealed Googlebook — a new category of Android-powered laptops built specifically for Gemini — and Android XR glasses developed with Samsung, Warby Parker, and Gentle Monster. Android 17 features also got airtime. The full Gemini model and capability details land tomorrow.

*Sources: [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/live/the-android-show-google-i-o-edition-live-all-the-latest-android-gemini-ai-and-android-xr-news-as-it-happens) · [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/google-pixel/google-io-2026-how-to-watch-what-you-need-to-know)*

## Enterprise AI: From Pilot to Production

### SAP Sapphire: 224 AI Agents Now Run Core Business Operations

At Sapphire 2026, SAP introduced the "Autonomous Enterprise" — a unified platform of 224 AI agents built on a new SAP Knowledge Graph. Partners include Anthropic, Nvidia, AWS, Microsoft, and Google Cloud. Users interact through Joule, a natural-language interface that replaces traditional ERP navigation screens entirely.

*Sources: [SAP News Center](https://news.sap.com/2026/05/sap-sapphire-keynote-business-ai-platform-power-autonomous-enterprise/) · [Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366643054/Sapphire-2026-SAP-heralds-dawn-of-autonomous-enterprise)*

### OpenAI Launches $4B+ Deployment Arm to Get Enterprises Running on AI

OpenAI's new Deployment Company — backed by 19 firms including TPG, Bain Capital, and Brookfield with more than $4B in initial capital — matches businesses with engineers to build and run AI systems. OpenAI is acquiring Tomoro, an applied AI firm, for its 150 forward-deployed engineers. The move mirrors Anthropic's own enterprise consulting push from earlier this month.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-launches-the-deployment-company/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/openai-launches-4-billion-ai-134916653.html)*

### OpenAI Opens ChatGPT Ad Platform to Any Advertiser

OpenAI dropped the $50,000 minimum spend from its ChatGPT ad platform and opened a self-serve Ads Manager to all brands. Agency partners include Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis, and WPP. OpenAI is targeting $2.5B in ad revenue this year and $100B by 2030.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/05/openai-self-serve-ad-platform) · [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/414857/openai-opens-ad-platform-to-cpc-bidding-self-serv.html)*
