OpenAI's newest model went live for everyone today, Google tore up its own blueprint rather than ship late, and Chinese AI is eating into US labs' market share faster than anyone expected. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## OpenAI Ships GPT-5.6 While Google Tears Up Gemini's Blueprint

### GPT-5.6 Launches Publicly in Three Tiers, Fresh Off a Government Review
OpenAI released GPT-5.6 to the public on July 9 in three variants: Sol, Terra, and Luna. Sol is the strongest of the three, tuned for biology, chemistry, and cybersecurity work. Terra matches GPT-5.5's performance at half the price, and Luna costs just $1 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. The wider rollout came after the Trump administration's Center for AI Standards and Innovation ran extra tests and held additional meetings with OpenAI before signing off.

*Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2210308/openai-rolls-out-gpt5-6-july-9/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)*

### Google Scraps Gemini 2.5 Pro's Architecture, Pushes 3.5 Pro to July 17
Google DeepMind abandoned the Gemini 2.5 Pro base architecture entirely and is running a fresh pretraining cycle from scratch, according to reporting July 8. The rebuild targets three weak spots the old architecture couldn't close: math reasoning, SVG scene generation, and image quality. The rebuilt model is expected to ship with a 2-million-token context window and a "Deep Think" reasoning layer, but staff turnover, including senior researchers leaving for Anthropic, has added friction to the timeline.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319877/20260708/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-deepseeks-july-24-deadline-hits-developers-now.htm) · [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model)*

## China's AI Models Are Winning on Price — and Beijing Is Getting Nervous

### Chinese Models Now Handle Up to 46% of US Enterprise AI Traffic
The share of tokens US companies route to Chinese AI models on OpenRouter has topped 30% every week since February 8, spiking as high as 46%, CNBC reported July 7. That's up from just 11% over the prior 12 months and 4.5% in the first half of 2025. Zhipu AI's GLM-5.2, which launched June 13, is the biggest driver: its daily token volume grew 27x and its customer count grew 80x in its first week on Vercel's AI Gateway, priced at roughly 15% of what OpenAI charges for a comparable model.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/07/chinese-ai-models-costs-us-openai-anthropic.html) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/zhipu-ai-explores-custom-asic-chip-as-glm52-usage-surges-27x--the-information-4779575)*

### Beijing Weighs Cutting Off Overseas Access to Its Own AI Models
Reuters reported July 7 that China's Ministry of Commerce is in talks with Alibaba, ByteDance, and Z.ai about restricting foreign access to advanced Chinese models, including unreleased systems and some open-weight releases. The stated goals are protecting IP, guarding national security, and cutting reliance on outside resources. Chinese open-weight models have gone from under 2% of OpenRouter's top-model traffic in late 2024 to roughly 61% today, which means any new restrictions would hit the same US teams now building cost and redundancy plans around them.

*Sources: [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/china-considers-restricting-overseas-access-to-advanced-ai-m-812db030) · [ExplainX](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/china-overseas-ai-model-restrictions-reuters-july-2026)*

## Anthropic Tightens Access, Then Backs Off a Fee Hike

### Claude's ID Verification Policy Takes Effect
Anthropic's updated privacy policy took effect July 8, letting Claude request a government-issued ID, a selfie, and a facial geometry scan from Free, Pro, and Max users "in certain circumstances." Business, Enterprise, and API customers are exempt. The verification data is collected and stored by identity vendor Persona, not Anthropic itself, and the company says the policy is meant to curb abuse and enforce its usage rules.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/anthropic-says-claude-may-want-to-see-your-id/) · [SC Media](https://www.scworld.com/brief/anthropic-updates-privacy-policy-to-require-government-id-for-some-users)*

### Anthropic Delays the Fable 5 Paywall to July 12 After Backlash
Anthropic had planned to cut off Fable 5 access for Pro, Max, and Team subscribers at midnight Pacific on July 8, pushing anyone who wanted the model onto pay-per-token credits instead. Users pushed back hard enough that Anthropic reversed course at 1:15 a.m. ET on July 8, extending free access on paid plans through July 12 at 11:59:59 p.m. PT. When the switch does happen, credits will run $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens.

*Sources: [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/claude-fable-5-free-extension-3685103/) · [Fable5.app](https://fable5.app/fable-5-usage-limits/)*

## AI Money Keeps Flowing While Meta's Bet Stalls

### Global AI Startup Funding Hits $510B for the First Half of 2026
AI is driving a record startup funding boom: global venture investment hit $510 billion in the first six months of 2026. AI alone pulled in $242 billion in Q1, 80% of all global venture funding that quarter. OpenAI closed a $110 billion round at a $730 billion valuation, one of four AI mega-rounds — alongside Anthropic, xAI, and Waymo — that together made up 65% of Q1's global venture total.

*Sources: [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/na-startup-funding-ma-shattered-records-ai-q2-2026/)*

### Meta Cut 8,000 Jobs. Zuckerberg Just Admitted the AI Bet Isn't Paying Off Yet.
At a July 2 town hall, Mark Zuckerberg told Meta staff that AI agent development "hasn't really accelerated in the way that we expected" over the prior four months. The admission came two months after Meta cut about 8,000 jobs, roughly 10% of its workforce, and reassigned 7,000 more employees to AI teams. Zuckerberg said returns on Meta's up to $145 billion 2026 AI infrastructure spend should show within three to six months; CTO Andrew Bosworth had already called the company's Applied AI rollout "atrocious" in an internal memo.

*Sources: [247 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/07/after-laying-off-8000-employees-zuckerberg-admits-metas-ai-hasnt-really-accelerated-as-expected/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/laying-off-8-000-employees-121545621.html)*
