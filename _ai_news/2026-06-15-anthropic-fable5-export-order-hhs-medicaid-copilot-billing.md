The most consequential AI event of the week didn't come from a lab — it came from the Commerce Department. Here's where things stand heading into the week of June 15.

## Government Shuts Down Anthropic's Two Newest Models

### Commerce Department Disables Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Globally

Anthropic's two most capable models went dark on June 12 at 5:21 p.m. ET after the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security issued an export control directive under Secretary Howard Lutnick. The order barred access by "any foreign national — whether inside or outside the United States, including Anthropic's own foreign-national employees." Since Anthropic couldn't filter users in real time, it disabled both models for everyone worldwide. The stated trigger was a jailbreak posted to X in which a user asked Fable 5 to read a codebase and fix software flaws — Anthropic called this a routine cybersecurity workflow, not a safety breach. Fable 5 had launched just three days earlier. Claude Opus 4.8 and all other models stayed online; Anthropic is issuing refunds for the affected period.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-disables-fable-mythos-export-controls-national-security-threat/) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-pulls-claude-mythos-5-and-claude-fable-5-following-us-government-directive/) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/13/anthropic-disables-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-after-us-government-order/)*

### Anthropic Had Already Lost the Government's Trust Over Military AI

The export order didn't arrive without context. In February 2026, Trump directed all federal agencies to stop using Anthropic's AI after the company refused to allow Claude for lethal autonomous weapons and mass civilian surveillance. Three weeks later, Anthropic received a "supply chain risk" designation from the administration. The X jailbreak gave the Commerce Department a legal mechanism to act, but the friction traces back months. What's notable is that the directive hit a commercial product used by paying subscribers worldwide, not a government contract or restricted deployment.

## Washington Puts AI to Work

### Trump's AI Executive Order Sets a 30-Day Government Review Window for Frontier Models

Signed June 2, the White House executive order on AI asks frontier model developers to voluntarily give the US government 30 days to review new models before wider release to "trusted partners." Federal agencies have until July 2 to deploy AI-powered cyber defenses on their own systems and until August 1 to finalize the broader review framework. The order also establishes a voluntary AI cybersecurity clearinghouse — a channel for the government and critical infrastructure operators to share vulnerability data and coordinate patches at scale.

*Sources: [White House Fact Sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-promotes-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [Inside Privacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/white-house-releases-executive-order-on-advanced-ai-innovation-and-security/) · [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/trumps-new-ai-executive-order-drastically-shifts-the-administrations-stance-on-the-tech/)*

### HHS Is Using AI to Screen $600 Billion in Medicaid Claims Before They're Paid

The Department of Health and Human Services is replacing its decades-old "pay and chase" fraud approach with real-time AI screening — flagging suspicious claims before payment goes out. Every state must now submit plans for revalidating providers, and the HHS Inspector General has begun auditing every Medicaid Fraud Control Unit across all 50 states. Units that underperform face funding cuts or decertification. Medicaid processes over $600 billion in annual claims; improper payments have historically run $50–100 billion per year.

*Sources: [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/hhs-ai-fraud-state-audit-aero/820989/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/hhs-ai-fraud-detection-federal-health-programs) · [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/hhs-ai-crackdown-medicaid-fraud/)*

## AI Costs Catch Up With Developers and Enterprises

### GitHub Copilot's Token Billing Is Triggering Meter Shock

GitHub moved Copilot to usage-based billing on June 1, replacing premium request allocations with GitHub AI Credits — 1 credit equals $0.01, priced per token. Copilot Pro+ costs $39/month and includes $39 in monthly credits. The problem: chat sessions, agentic coding workflows, and multi-file code review burn through credits faster than most developers expected. Developer forums filled with billing complaints within days of the switch. Base plan prices didn't change on paper, but heavy users are paying substantially more in practice.

*Sources: [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/) · [Visual Studio Magazine](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/articles/2026/06/04/copilot-billing-shock-hits-developers.aspx) · [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/github-copilot-ai-credits-billing-2026-cost-audit-playbook)*

### 94% of CIOs Increased AI Spending Last Year — and Half Think It's Too Fast

A global survey of more than 1,000 CIOs found that 94% of organizations increased AI budgets over the past year. But 51% simultaneously believe adoption is already moving too fast. The split captures where enterprise AI stands right now: spending keeps rising, governance hasn't caught up, and the people responsible for IT systems are divided on whether the pace is sustainable.

*Sources: [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-12-2026)*
