DeepSeek's valuation went up sevenfold in three months, Apple accused OpenAI of running a corporate espionage playbook, and Mira Murati's lab put its first model out in the open. Here's what actually mattered in the last day and a half.

## DeepSeek's Valuation Run Keeps Going

### DeepSeek in Talks to Raise $1.5B at a $71B Valuation, Prepping an IPO

DeepSeek is negotiating a new funding round that would value it at roughly $71 billion, Bloomberg reported July 14, a 37% jump from the $52 billion the Hangzhou lab was worth just weeks earlier. That's about seven times its ~$10 billion valuation in April. Founder Liang Wenfeng, who still holds roughly 78% of the company, now has a net worth near $36 billion, making him the wealthiest AI founder anywhere. Advisors are simultaneously prepping financials for an IPO, aiming for a mainland China or Hong Kong listing as soon as 2027.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/deepseek-reportedly-in-talks-to-raise-1-5b-then-ipo/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/deepseek-mulls-new-funding-weeks-after-7-billion-round-ft-says)*

### South Korea Commits $880B to Chips, Data Centers, and Robots

President Lee Jae-myung unveiled a 10-year, 1,350 trillion won ($880 billion) plan covering semiconductors, AI data centers, and humanoid robotics. Samsung alone is committing 1,000 trillion won (about $646 billion) of that over the decade, with SK Hynix and SK Group adding hundreds of trillions more. The plan also targets growing Korea's humanoid robotics market share from 1% to 20% by 2028.

*Sources: [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/112926-south-korea-betting-880-billion-next-ai-race.html) · [MLQ News](https://mlq.ai/news/south-korea-unveils-880-billion-plan-to-double-memory-chip-output-and-mass-produce-humanoid-robots-1/)*

## Trade Wars and Trade Secrets

### Apple Sues OpenAI, Alleging a Scheme to Steal iPhone Hardware Secrets

Apple filed suit against OpenAI on July 10, alleging the company asked former Apple employees, and even prospective recruits, to bring information about unreleased products when they came aboard. Apple claims OpenAI used a checklist built by Apple's former iPhone design chief to help new hires evade the company's security procedures. Apple wants damages plus a court order forcing OpenAI to stop and destroy any proprietary material; OpenAI still expects to unveil its first hardware device this year, though the case could squeeze both recruiting and the timeline.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/apple-openai-lawsuit-trade-secrets.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/how-apple-s-lawsuit-threatens-to-disrupt-openai-s-bid-to-rival-the-iphone)*

### Nvidia's H200 Sales to China Are Real But "Trivial," Commerce Official Says

Jeffrey Kessler, the Commerce Department's under secretary for industry and security, told the House Foreign Affairs Committee on July 14 that H200 shipments to China have begun under approved licenses, but the volume is tiny. "Very small quantity of chips, so it's trivial," he said, despite roughly $10 billion worth of licenses already approved. It's the first on-record confirmation that any H200s are reaching China since Washington reopened limited sales.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/nvidia-h200-ai-chips-china.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-14/small-amount-of-nvidia-ai-chips-shipped-to-china-with-us-license)*

## Global AI Governance Tightens

### WHO's 37-Country Lisbon Summit Finds Most Nations Are Flying Blind on AI in Health

WHO/Europe and Portugal co-hosted a global conference on AI in health on July 15-16, drawing ministers and officials from 37 countries across all six WHO regions, plus leaders from the World Bank and the Gates Foundation. WHO's own assessment found nearly two-thirds of countries are already using AI for diagnostics, but only 8% have a health-specific AI strategy and only 8% have liability rules for when an AI system gets it wrong.

*Sources: [WHO](https://www.who.int/europe/news-room/events/item/2026/07/15/default-calendar/global-who-conference--shaping-ai-in-health) · [Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/239619/WHO+Brings+37+Countries+Together+in+Lisbon+to+Get+AI+Governance+Right+and+Make+It+Work+for+Every+Patient)*

### EU's Cloud and AI Development Act Hits the Official Journal

Brussels published the Cloud and AI Development Act in the EU's Official Journal on July 15, formally starting the clock on a four-tier cloud sovereignty framework meant to keep sensitive public-sector data under European control. The law takes effect August 4, 2026, though the toughest tier of requirements doesn't become mandatory until August 2029. Cloud providers and AI vendors selling into the EU public sector now have a fixed date to start proving where their infrastructure actually sits.

*Sources: [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cloud-and-ai-development-act) · [Regulations.ai](https://regulations.ai/news/eu-cloud-ai-act-prepare-july-2026-publication)*

## New Open Models Challenge the Frontier

### Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Releases Its First Model, Inkling

Thinking Machines put out Inkling on July 15, a 975-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model that only activates about 41 billion parameters per task. It was trained on 45 trillion tokens of text, image, audio, and video, and reasons natively across all four under an Apache 2.0 license, with weights on Hugging Face and fine-tuning live on the company's Tinker platform. Murati's team is upfront that Inkling isn't the strongest model out there; the bet is that enterprises want a model they can reshape rather than just rent.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/thinking-machines-amps-up-its-bet-against-one-size-fits-all-ai-with-its-first-open-model-inkling/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/15/mira-murati-thinking-machines-open-weight-model-inkling)*

### DeepSeek V4 Graduates From Preview With Peak-Hour Pricing

DeepSeek is moving V4 from its April preview to an official release this month, giving both V4-Pro (1.6 trillion total, 49 billion active parameters) and V4-Flash (284 billion total, 13 billion active) a full 1-million-token context window across the lineup. The official version also introduces peak-hour API pricing, charging double the base rate from 9 a.m. to noon and 2 to 6 p.m. Beijing time, alongside gains in agentic tasks, math, and coding.

*Sources: [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/06/30/deepseek-to-launch-v4-in-mid-july-with-new-peak-time-api-pricing/) · [DeepSeek](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260424/)*

## The Classroom and Agent Product Race

### Anthropic Launches Free Claude for Teachers for US K-12 Educators

Anthropic opened Claude for Teachers on July 14, giving verified US K-12 educators free access to premium Claude tools and a teaching-skills library tied to Learning Commons, which maps down to the academic standards of all 50 states. Claude for Teachers data isn't used for model training, and student data is covered by a FERPA-compliant K-12 agreement. Anthropic is piloting the program with Detroit Public Schools, and teachers who sign up by June 30, 2027 get a full year free.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers) · [Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/07/14/anthropic-launches-claude-for-teachers-as-ai-companies-battle-for-classrooms/)*

### Perplexity Gives Its Computer Agent a Memory, Then a Faster Sandbox

Perplexity rolled out "Brain" for its Computer agent on July 13, a private context graph that refreshes overnight so each new task starts already knowing what worked before; in testing it lifted answer correctness 25% and cut cost 13% on tasks with prior context. Two days later, Perplexity switched every Computer session onto SPACE, a new sandbox built on AWS's Firecracker microVMs that cut sandbox creation time from 185ms to 60ms.

*Sources: [Perplexity](https://perplexity.ai/changelog/brain-faster-computer-models-website-publishing) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/15/perplexity-launches-secure-sandbox-make-ai-agents-secure-powerful/)*
