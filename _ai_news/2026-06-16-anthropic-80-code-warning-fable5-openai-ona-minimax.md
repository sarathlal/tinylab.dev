Anthropic's two most capable models remain offline four days after the US government shut them down. The full picture is coming into focus — including a safety paper Anthropic published three weeks before the ban that may explain why it happened.

## The Fable 5 Fallout — What Subscribers Face Next

### Fable 5 Drops From Claude Subscription Plans on June 23

Anthropic set June 22 as the last day Fable 5 is included in Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans at no extra charge. Starting June 23, users need usage credits to access it. There's still no date for restoring Fable 5 as a standard subscription feature. For now, the company is routing users to Opus 4.8 and issuing refunds for the period both models were unavailable. Anthropic called the shutdown a "misunderstanding" but hasn't said how long resolving it will take.

*Sources: [Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/claude-fable-5-june-22-deadline) · [ExplainX.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/when-will-fable-5-be-available-again-2026) · [ITECS](https://itecsonline.com/post/anthropic-fable-5-mythos-5-suspension-commerce-pentagon-business-impact-2026)*

### The Paper That Preceded the Shutdown: Anthropic Said AI Now Writes 80% of Its Own Code

Three weeks before the export order, Anthropic published "When AI Builds Itself" — a June 4 paper disclosing that Claude now authors over 80% of the code merged into Anthropic's production codebase. That's up from low single digits in early 2025, when Claude Code launched. The paper also showed that Anthropic engineers now merge 8× more code per day than in 2024, and that Claude can complete 12-hour engineering tasks — up from 4-minute tasks in March 2024. Anthropic called for "verifiable international coordination" to slow frontier AI if recursive self-improvement becomes uncontrollable. TechCrunch noted that Anthropic's safety transparency "may have just backfired" — giving the government a detailed picture of exactly what the model could do.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement) · [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-warns-ai-may-soon-begin-recursive-self-improvement/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-released-claude-fable-5-its-most-powerful-model-publicly-days-after-warning-ai-is-getting-too-dangerous/)*

## OpenAI Builds Out Its Enterprise Infrastructure Stack

### OpenAI Acquires Ona to Keep AI Agents Running in Enterprise Clouds

Bloomberg reported on June 11 that OpenAI has agreed to buy Ona, a startup that provides secure, persistent cloud environments where AI agents keep working after a developer closes their laptop. Most agentic deployments lose context and progress the moment a session ends — Ona solves that. The deal focuses on Codex, which over 5 million people now use each week. Financial terms weren't disclosed, and the acquisition is still subject to regulatory approval.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/openai-to-acquire-cloud-platform-ona-to-support-ai-agents) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/open-ai-ona-acquisition-codex.html) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-acquires-ona-codex)*

### OpenAI's $150M Partner Network Targets 300,000 Certified Consultants by Year-End

OpenAI announced on June 14 a $150M commitment to a new global Partner Network — a three-tier program (Select, Advanced, Elite) for consultants, systems integrators, and technology providers. The goal is 300,000 certified practitioners by end of 2026. The program goes live in July. A Forward Deployed Experts track pairs qualified partner engineers with OpenAI's own deployment teams for complex enterprise rollouts. Early example partnerships: BCG with Agilent, Accenture with T-Mobile, and Bain with Paychex.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-partner-network/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318436/20260615/openai-launches-partner-network-150m-bet-that-implementation-beats-model-power.htm) · [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/06/15/openai-launches-150-million-partner-network/)*

## Challengers Gain Ground

### MiniMax M3 Claims to Beat GPT-5.5 at Coding — and It's Open Weights

Chinese AI lab MiniMax released M3 on June 1 — the first open-weights model to combine a 1M-token context window, native multimodal input across text, image, and video, and frontier coding performance. On SWE-Bench Pro, M3 scored 59.0%, which MiniMax says beats GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro and falls within range of Claude Opus 4.7. These are vendor benchmarks; independent verification is still pending. Pricing at launch: $0.60 per million input tokens and $2.40 per million output, with a 50% promotional discount bringing it to $0.30/$1.20 per million.

*Sources: [FelloAI](https://felloai.com/minimax-m3/) · [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/3934/minimax-m3-chinese-open-weights-coding-model) · [MyClaw.ai](https://myclaw.ai/blog/minimax-m3)*

### Perplexity Brings Its Computer Agent to Windows, Inside Microsoft Office

Perplexity's Personal Computer agent came to Windows on June 3, expanding from Mac. The app connects to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and local files, and orchestrates across 19 AI models — including Claude Opus 4.8 for Max subscribers. A hybrid inference engine automatically routes simpler tasks to the device and complex ones to the cloud. CEO Aravind Srinivas demonstrated it at Computex 2026 alongside Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan. The move plants Perplexity inside the same Microsoft ecosystem it's competing against.

*Sources: [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/personal-computer-is-coming-to-windows) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/05/perplexity-ai-introduces-hybrid-local-server-inference-orchestrator-for-personal-computer-automatic-on-device-and-cloud-task-routing/) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/perplexity-takes-its-computer-ai-agent-into-the-enterprise-taking-aim-at)*
