China used its home-turf AI summit to push a rival to Western governance today, Moonshot shipped the biggest open-weight model anyone has released, and half a billion dollars a day kept landing in AI health and coding startups. Here's what actually mattered in the last 24 hours.

## China Stages Its Biggest AI Power Play Yet

### Xi Jinping Makes His First-Ever WAIC Appearance as 29 Countries Sign a Rival AI Body

Xi delivered the opening keynote at Shanghai's World AI Conference today, his first personal appearance at the summit since it launched in 2018. A day earlier, 29 countries — including Russia, Brazil, Pakistan, Indonesia, and a mix of African and Asian states — signed a treaty founding the World AI Cooperation Organization, an intergovernmental body headquartered in Shanghai. Foreign Minister Wang Yi signed for Beijing, and UN Secretary-General António Guterres attended the ceremony, giving China's alternative to the EU's AI Act and the G7 process a UN-adjacent stamp of legitimacy.

*Sources: [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260716/b0449aa2133542868e310fdc45ef2969/c.html) · [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3360404/xi-jinping-attend-world-ai-conference-first-time-china-elevates-tech-push)*

### WAIC Opens With 300+ Product Debuts, Including the World's First AI Agent Phone

More than 1,100 companies are showing over 3,000 exhibits at WAIC's four-day run through July 20, spread across 100,000 square meters of Shanghai show floor — a record for the event. Huawei unveiled its Atlas 950 SuperPoD, a cluster linking 8,192 Ascend chips into computing power equivalent to more than 500,000 cards, while MiniMax debuted its M3 multimodal model with a 1-million-token context window. Organizers say the floor also includes the first AI agent smartphone and a lineup of humanoid robots.

*Sources: [Yicai Global](https://www.yicaiglobal.com/news/shanghai-to-host-record-size-2026-waic-with-300-global-product-debuts) · [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3359733/huaweis-new-computing-cluster-worlds-first-ai-agent-phone-debut-china-ai-summit)*

## The Open-Weight Race Gets a New Heavyweight

### Moonshot's Kimi K3 Ships as the Largest Open-Weight Model Ever, at 2.8 Trillion Parameters

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on July 16, a mixture-of-experts model with roughly 2.8 trillion total parameters and a 1-million-token context window — about 75% bigger than DeepSeek's V4 Pro. Two variants shipped immediately: K3 Max for chat and agent work, and K3 Swarm Max for large-scale parallel processing, priced at $0.30 per million cached input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Full weights follow on July 27 under Moonshot's modified MIT license, continuing the open pattern it set with Kimi K2.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/16/moonshot-kimi-ai-china-model-openai-anthropic)*

### Google Targets Today for Gemini 3.5 Pro After Scrapping Its Original Model

Google is aiming to ship Gemini 3.5 Pro today, more than a month after missing its original June target. The company reportedly scrapped its first attempt at the model after engineers found it kept failing at recursive tool-calling and SVG rendering, forcing a rebuild from scratch. Leaks point to a 2-million-token context window and a "Deep Think" reasoning mode gated to the $250-a-month Ultra tier, but as of this morning Google still hadn't published a model card or pricing.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320308/20260713/gemini-35-pro-targets-july-17-after-full-rebuild-every-spec-remains-unconfirmed.htm) · [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model)*

## AI Money Keeps Flowing Into Health and Coding Startups

### Daniel Ek's Neko Health Raises $700M to Scan Bodies for Disease Before Symptoms Show

Neko Health closed a $700 million Series C on July 15, led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and O.G. Venture Partners, valuing the Spotify founder's body-scanning startup at nearly $7 billion — up from $1.7 billion just last year. The company's 60-minute AI-powered scan captures millions of health data points without radiation, and the new cash funds its US expansion into New York and other cities.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/daniel-eks-body-scanning-startup-neko-health-raises-another-700m/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/health-tech-deals/2026/07/15/neko-health-raises-700m-series-c)*

### Chai Discovery Triples Valuation to $3.8B as Big Pharma Buys Its AI-Designed Antibodies

Chai Discovery raised a $400 million Series C on July 14, led by Index Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia Capital, and Dimension. The valuation triples what it was seven months ago, driven by deals with Lilly, Novartis, and Pfizer that use Chai's models to design antibody molecules instead of screening them in a lab first.

*Sources: [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/chai-brews-400m-series-c-fuel-ai-used-lilly-novartis-and-pfizer) · [Endpoints News](https://endpoints.news/chai-discovery-gets-400m-tripling-valuation-from-seven-months-ago/)*

### Anthropic's $1.5B Ode Launches to Sell AI Implementation, Not Just Models

Anthropic, Blackstone, and Hellman & Friedman officially launched Ode with Anthropic on July 15 — a $1.5 billion firm built to help midsize companies actually deploy AI instead of just piloting it. Ode grew out of Fractional AI, which the consortium acquired in May, and now runs about 100 engineers under CEO Chris Taylor. Goldman Sachs, Sequoia Capital, and GIC are among the backers.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/anthropic-blackstone-bet-the-next-trillion-dollar-ai-business-is-implementation-not-models/) · [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260715205134/en/Anthropic-Blackstone-and-Hellman-Friedman-Introduce-Ode-with-Anthropic-an-Enterprise-AI-Services-Firm)*

### India's Emergent Hits $1.5B Valuation Selling AI as "an Engineering Team in a Box"

Emergent, an Indian AI coding startup barely a year old, raised $130 million in a Series C led by Creaegis on July 15, a five-fold jump from its valuation six months earlier. CEO Mukund Jha's pitch: let trucking companies, factories, and property managers build their own software without hiring developers. The company says it now has 200,000 paying customers and $120 million in annual run-rate revenue, up 70% in four months.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/indian-ai-coding-startup-emergent-becomes-a-unicorn-just-over-a-year-after-launch/)*
