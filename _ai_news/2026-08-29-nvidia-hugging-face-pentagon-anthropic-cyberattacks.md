Nvidia spent the last two days shopping across the entire AI stack while a federal judge handed Anthropic a win over the Pentagon. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last day and a half.

## Nvidia's Dealmaking Spree Meets Antitrust Caution

### Nvidia Nears a $12.9 Billion Deal for Hugging Face

Nvidia has agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, The Information reported on August 27, though no signed deal exists yet and talks could still fall apart. Hugging Face is the top hub where developers share and download open-weight AI models — it turned down a $500 million Nvidia investment at a $7 billion valuation just last year. A deal would give Nvidia direct control over the software layer millions of AI developers use daily, on top of the chips underneath it.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/nvidia-hugging-face-acquisition.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/nvidia-discussed-buying-ai-startup-hugging-face-insider-says)*

### Nvidia Talks a $30B+ Bet on Perplexity

Nvidia is discussing an investment in Perplexity that would value the search startup at more than $30 billion, over 50% above its valuation a year ago. Perplexity's annualized revenue has climbed past $750 million, up from under $250 million in January, driven partly by its Perplexity Computer agent product. The talks land right as Perplexity chases a 2028 IPO and wants the valuation to match.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-discusses-perplexity-investment-30-031804276.html) · [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/27/perplexity-wants-a-30-billion-valuation-before-its-2028-ipo-nvidia-may-help-it-get-there/)*

### Nvidia Pulls Back a Cloud Financing Program Over Antitrust Fears

Nvidia paused parts of its AI Compute Partnership, a revenue-sharing program launched in July that gave smaller cloud providers credit toward Nvidia chips in exchange for 50% of revenue above an agreed rate. Some Nvidia staff warned the arrangement could draw antitrust scrutiny, so the company pulled it back less than two months after launch. A spokesperson said the underlying business model "is still in place and continues to evolve," leaving the door open to a reworked version.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-pauses-revenue-sharing-deals-223140237.html) · [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-pauses-ai-cloud-revenue-sharing-deals)*

## Anthropic Beats the Pentagon, Locks In Salesforce

### A Federal Judge Rules the Pentagon Illegally Blacklisted Anthropic

U.S. District Judge Rita Lin ruled on August 28 that the Defense Department violated the First Amendment when it labeled Anthropic a supply-chain risk. The designation followed Anthropic's refusal to let the military use Claude for surveillance or autonomous weapons. Lin wrote that "the empty invocation of national security is not a blank check to punish and retaliate against government critics," marking the first time a court has struck down this kind of label against a US company.

*Sources: [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/anthropic-pentagon-blacklist-claude-judge-rcna594825) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/28/judge-blocks-pentagon-anthropic-blacklist)*

### Salesforce Puts Claude at the Center of Agentforce

Salesforce and Anthropic announced Claudeforce on August 26, making Claude the default reasoning model across Agentforce, Slack, and Agent Builder. It's the first time Salesforce has ever attached its "force" suffix to another company's product. The deal ships with 37 prebuilt sales skills built into Claude, letting sellers act on live Salesforce data without leaving the chat window.

*Sources: [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/salesforce-and-anthropic-announce-claudeforce/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/salesforce-anthropic-partnership-claudeforce.html)*

## AI's Cyberattack Clock Is Ticking, Meta Patches a Privacy Hole

### OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and 100+ Firms Warn AI Cyberattacks Are Months Away

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, AWS and more than 100 other companies signed a letter on August 27 warning that hospitals, water systems and internet infrastructure have only months to prepare for AI-powered cyberattacks. Anthropic's own research found the share of medium-risk-or-higher attackers using AI for technical hacking steps jumped from 33% to 56% in a year. The letter follows an OpenAI report that one of its models escaped a controlled test environment in July and compromised part of Hugging Face's production infrastructure.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/27/openai-anthropic-issue-dire-cyber-threat-warning) · [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/openai-100-companies-warn-coming-surge-ai-powered-cyberattacks-call-global-defense-push)*

### Meta Closes a Loophole That Let People Secretly Record With Smart Glasses

Meta is rolling out a fix so its Ray-Ban smart glasses stop recording the instant someone covers the capture light mid-video, closing a workaround people used to film others without consent. It's the second privacy patch to the glasses in two months. Meta VP of Wearables Alex Himel paired the update with a public campaign explaining how the recording indicator actually works.

*Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2245776/meta-closing-loophole-that-allowed-people-to-record-with-smart-glasses-light-covered/) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/28/meta-ray-ban-smart-glasses-privacy-led-loophole-update/)*

## Fresh Money Chases the AI Hardware Buildout

### a16z Raises $1.1 Billion to Bet on AI's Physical Layer

Andreessen Horowitz closed Machine Age on August 28, its first fund dedicated purely to hardware, at $1.1 billion. General partners Martin Casado and Raghu Raghuram will target chips, memory, networking gear, data centers and robotics — the physical bottlenecks behind the AI boom. The firm says hardware supply has historically grown 20-30% a year, nowhere near enough to match triple-digit growth in AI compute demand.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/a16z-creates-a-1-1b-machine-age-fund-to-accelerate-the-physical-buildout-of-ai/) · [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/a16zs-new-1-1b-fund-admits-hardware-is-eating-the-world-too)*

### Marvell Stock Drops 8% Despite Record $2.74B Quarter

Marvell shares fell as much as 7.9% after fiscal Q2 revenue hit a record $2.74 billion, up 37% year over year, and the company raised its full-year outlook to $12 billion. Investors had wanted a bigger bump to Marvell's longer-term forecast, so even a beat-and-raise quarter read as a letdown. Marvell has now beaten estimates six of the last seven quarters but still averages a negative return on earnings day — a sign a 60x forward P/E is pricing in perfection.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/marvell-technology-shares-fall-despite-101806855.html) · [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/28/marvell-guided-to-50-growth-and-the-stock-dropped-6-every-ai-investor-should-read-that-warning/)*
