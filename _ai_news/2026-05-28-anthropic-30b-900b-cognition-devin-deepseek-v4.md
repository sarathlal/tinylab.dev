Two landmark funding rounds closed this week: Anthropic became the world's most valuable private AI startup, and Cognition's AI software engineer Devin — which writes 90% of the company's own code — raised $1 billion. DeepSeek shipped two new open-weights models and became the subject of IP theft allegations from Anthropic in the same week.

## Two Funding Records in One Day

### Anthropic Closes $30B Round at $900B+ — Passing OpenAI for the First Time

Anthropic's new funding round closed this week at a pre-money valuation above $900 billion, the first time any non-Microsoft-backed AI lab has topped OpenAI's $852 billion March 2026 valuation. Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks each put in roughly $2 billion and co-led the round; Founders Fund and General Catalyst also joined. Anthropic expects Q2 revenue of $10.9 billion, putting its annualized run rate on track to clear $50 billion by the end of June. It's the company's second $30 billion raise in 2026 — its February Series G was at a $380 billion valuation — and a board decision on a potential IPO is expected as early as October.

### Cognition Raises $1B at $26B as Devin Writes 90% of Its Own Codebase

Cognition, maker of the autonomous AI software engineer Devin, raised $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation on May 27 — more than double its $10.2 billion valuation from eight months ago. Lux Capital and General Catalyst led the round, with Founders Fund, 8VC, Ribbit Capital, Atreides, and Layer Global participating. Run-rate revenue has grown more than 10x since January to $492 million. Customers include Citi, Goldman Sachs, Mercedes-Benz, and both the U.S. Army and Navy. CEO Scott Wu confirmed that more than 90% of Cognition's internal code is now written by Devin — making it one of the first companies operating at this scale where AI generates the majority of its own product.

## DeepSeek Doubles Down — and Gets Accused

### DeepSeek Releases V4-Pro and V4-Flash with 1 Million Token Context

DeepSeek dropped V4-Pro and V4-Flash, two new Mixture-of-Experts models with 1 million token context windows and a hybrid attention mechanism aimed at cutting inference costs. Both are open-weights and available for immediate self-hosting. The models target long-running agentic tasks — the same category where Cognition's Devin and OpenAI's Codex compete. DeepSeek has consistently used open-weights releases to put pricing pressure on proprietary labs; V4-Pro and V4-Flash continue that pattern with a direct shot at the agentic workflow market.

*Sources: [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)*

### Anthropic Says DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax Ran Coordinated Distillation Campaigns Against Claude

Anthropic accused three Chinese AI labs — DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax — of using thousands of fraudulent accounts to systematically extract knowledge from Claude models at industrial scale. The company called it a national security concern. If the claim holds up, it would be the largest documented case of organized model distillation used as a knowledge-extraction operation rather than ordinary research. For frontier labs spending billions on training runs, the accusation frames a real structural problem: if a model's outputs can be systematically harvested through API queries, IP protection for AI looks more like an unsolved problem than a policy gap.
