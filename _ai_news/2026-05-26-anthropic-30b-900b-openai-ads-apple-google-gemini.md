The two biggest US AI labs are raising money in very different ways this week: Anthropic is closing a $30 billion private round, while OpenAI just opened its ad platform to any advertiser with a credit card.

## AI Monetization at Record Pace

### Anthropic Closes $30 Billion at a $900 Billion Valuation This Week

Bloomberg reported on May 22 that the round would close "as soon as next week" — meaning this week. Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks are each putting in roughly $2 billion, with Founders Fund and General Catalyst also participating. The deal values Anthropic above $900 billion — past OpenAI's $852 billion valuation from March — and the company told investors its annualized run rate will exceed $50 billion by the end of June.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/anthropic-set-close-30-billion-203545596.html)*

### OpenAI Drops the $50,000 Ad Minimum and Opens ChatGPT to All Advertisers

OpenAI opened its ChatGPT Ads Manager to all US advertisers this month, removing the $50,000 spend floor that had blocked smaller buyers since the pilot launched. The company targets $2.5 billion in ad revenue this year and projects $100 billion annually by 2030. Ads appear in tinted boxes below AI responses and, per OpenAI's policy, don't alter the content of the answers above them.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/26/openai-ads-pilot-tops-100-million-in-arr-in-under-2-months.html) · [The Tech Portal](https://thetechportal.com/2026/04/09/openai-projects-100bn-in-ad-revenue-by-2030-around-2-5bn-in-2026-report)*

## Platforms, Hardware, and Policy

### Apple Bets on Google Gemini to Power the Next Siri

Apple signed a multi-year deal worth roughly $1 billion to build the next generation of Apple Foundation Models on Google's Gemini technology. Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian confirmed the partnership at Google I/O on May 19, saying Gemini will power "a new, more personalized version of Siri" later this year. The new Siri will still run on Apple devices and Private Cloud Compute — Gemini sits underneath the Apple models, not in place of them.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/12/apple-google-ai-siri-gemini.html) · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/22/google-gemini-powered-siri-2026/)*

### Brett Adcock's Hark Raises $700 Million at $6 Billion for a Secretive AI Device

Hark closed a $700 million Series A on May 21, led by Parkway Venture Capital with NVIDIA, AMD Ventures, Intel Capital, and Qualcomm Ventures in the round. The company says it's building a "universal interface between humans and machines" — AI software models first, hardware devices later this summer. Adcock put in $100 million of his own money to start the company in late 2025 after founding Figure AI and Archer; Hark now has 70 employees running a data center with NVIDIA B200 GPUs.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-21/ai-hardware-startup-hark-valued-at-6-billion-in-new-funding-round) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/21/hark-raises-700m-series-a-for-its-secretive-universal-ai-interface/)*

### Colorado's AI Discrimination Law Collapses Under Federal Pressure

xAI sued Colorado on April 9 to block the state's anti-discrimination AI law, which required companies to disclose how their models affect hiring, loans, and housing decisions. The DOJ intervened on April 24 — the first time federal prosecutors have joined a lawsuit against a state AI statute — and a federal judge suspended enforcement three days later. Colorado's legislature rewrote the law on May 12, swapping disclosure requirements for a basic consumer notification rule and pushing enforcement to January 2027.

*Sources: [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-us/knowledge/publications/de3ad9de/xai-sues-doj-intervenes-enforcement-of-colorados-ai-act-suspended) · [The Colorado Sun](https://coloradosun.com/2026/05/12/colorado-ai-law-rewrite-passes/) · [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-intervenes-xai-lawsuit-challenging-colorados-algorithmic-discrimination)*
