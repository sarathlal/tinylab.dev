Anthropic grew 80-fold in a quarter and is now renting Elon Musk's data center to cope. That growth is also pushing Washington — an administration that ran on deregulation — toward new AI oversight rules.

## Anthropic's Growth Is Outrunning Its Infrastructure

### Dario Amodei's 10x Plan Became 80x Reality

Anthropic's annualized revenue hit $30B at the end of March — up from roughly $375M a year earlier, an 80-fold jump CEO Dario Amodei said the company hadn't planned for. Enterprise customers now account for about 80% of that revenue, with more than 1,000 businesses spending over $1M annually on Claude. The stated plan had been to prepare for 10x growth; the company got eight times that instead.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/08/anthropic-80fold-growth-quarter-renting-elon-musk-data-center/)*

### Anthropic Signs $1.8B Compute Deal with Akamai, Rents SpaceX Data Center

To handle demand it didn't plan for, Anthropic signed a $1.8B agreement with Akamai Technologies for cloud compute capacity on May 8. The company also secured all available capacity at SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center in Memphis — over 300 megawatts — the same facility Elon Musk built for xAI. Anthropic is now pulling compute from cloud providers, a CDN company, and a competitor's data center simultaneously.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-08/anthropic-inks-1-8-billion-computing-deal-with-akamai) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/06/anthropic-spacex-data-center-capacity.html)*

### Anthropic Eyes $900B Valuation in $50B Fundraise

Investors have pitched preemptive offers valuing Anthropic at $850B to $900B — above OpenAI's $852B post-money valuation from its $122B round earlier this year. Anthropic's last round, in February, valued it at $380B, less than half the figure now being discussed. The new round has no firm timeline, but sources tell Bloomberg it could close within two months.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/anthropic-considering-funding-offers-at-over-900-billion-value) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/29/sources-anthropic-could-raise-a-new-50b-round-at-a-valuation-of-900b/)*

## Washington Pivots on AI Risk

### Trump White House Considers Mandatory Pre-Release AI Vetting

The Trump administration is drafting a plan to require government review of AI models before public release — a sharp reversal from the deregulatory stance it took on day one. The catalyst is Anthropic's Mythos model, which Anthropic described as capable of finding thousands of critical software vulnerabilities and too dangerous for public release. White House staff have already briefed leaders from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI on the proposed framework.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/trump-administration-embraces-ai-oversight-policies-it-once-rejected-anthropic-mythos-caisi/) · [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/05/08/trump-white-house-ai-regulation-mythos/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/05/trump-anthropic-ai-regulation-mythos-cyber)*

### Commerce Department to Security-Test Google, Microsoft, and xAI Models

CAISI — the Center for AI Standards and Innovation, housed inside NIST — will run security tests on models from Google DeepMind, Microsoft, and xAI before those models get deployed in government settings. The program will also develop best practices for commercial AI systems. It's the first time a US agency has directly evaluated frontier models from multiple competing labs.

*Sources: [Nextgov/FCW](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/05/commerce-ai-center-will-evaluate-google-deepmind-microsoft-and-xai-models/413349/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/05/ai-oversight-trump-google-microsoft-xai.html)*

### Five Eyes Publish First Joint Guide on Agentic AI Security

Six cybersecurity agencies across the Five Eyes alliance published a 30-page guide on May 1 covering how to deploy AI agents safely. The central message: apply zero trust, least-privilege access, and defense-in-depth to agentic systems — don't invent new frameworks. For high-impact actions, the agencies recommend human sign-off, with each agent carrying a cryptographically verified identity and short-lived credentials.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/05/04/five_eyes_agentic_ai_recommendations/) · [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/cisa-nsa-five-eyes-guidance-secure-deployment-ai-agents/)*

### FDA Scales AI Use from 1% to 80% of Staff in 15 Months

The FDA launched Elsa 4.0, an upgrade to its internal AI tool, alongside HALO — a platform that consolidates more than 40 separate data portals across all FDA centers into one. Staff use of generative AI jumped from 1% in early 2025 to over 80% today. Elsa now sits on top of HALO so reviewers and investigators can query data and draft documents without manually uploading files to each chat.

*Sources: [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-expands-ai-capabilities-and-completes-data-platform-consolidation) · [Nextgov/FCW](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/05/fda-launches-updated-ai-and-consolidated-data-platform/413370/)*

## OpenAI Ships Faster, Shorter Answers

### GPT-5.5 Instant Replaces GPT-5.3 as ChatGPT's Default Model

GPT-5.5 Instant became ChatGPT's default model on May 5, producing 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims on high-stakes prompts in medicine, law, and finance. Answers run about 30.2% shorter, and it scored 81.2 on AIME 2025 — up from 65.4 for its predecessor. GPT-5.3 Instant stays available for paid users for three months before it's retired.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-5-instant/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/05/openai-releases-gpt-5-5-instant-a-new-default-model-for-chatgpt/)*

### OpenAI Adds Voice Models and Account Security to the API

On May 7, OpenAI updated its API with new voice intelligence models and introduced Trusted Contact to ChatGPT — a backup identity verification route for users who get locked out. The update also added phishing-resistant sign-in, shorter session lengths, and login alerts across all account tiers.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/news/) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai)*
