Google I/O 2026 dominated today's news with a wave of agentic AI features, new models, and hardware reveals. A pair of major enterprise deals and a landmark funding story round out a packed Tuesday in AI.

## Google Goes Agentic at I/O 2026

### Gemini Spark: Google's 24/7 Personal AI Agent
Google launched Gemini Spark at I/O 2026, a personal agent that acts on your behalf around the clock, even with your devices off. It runs on dedicated Google Cloud VMs, uses Gemini 3.5, and handles browsing, scheduling, and task execution through a new orchestration layer called Antigravity. AI Ultra subscribers in the US get access next week as part of Google's new $100/month tier.

*Sources: [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/news/live/google-io-2026-live-news-updates) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-news/)*

### Gemini 3.5 Flash Runs 4x Faster Than Competing Frontier Models
Google's new Gemini 3.5 Flash surpasses Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding, agentic, and multimodal benchmarks while running 4x faster than competing frontier models. It rolls out today across the Gemini app, Search, and Gemini API. That speed advantage is what makes persistent agent loops like Spark viable.

*Sources: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-news/) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/live/google-io-2026-live)*

### Gemini Omni Takes Aim at the AI Video Market
Google introduced Gemini Omni at I/O 2026, a new model series that generates video grounded in real-world knowledge from combinations of text, images, audio, and existing video. DeepMind head Demis Hassabis announced the model alongside the other keynote reveals. It puts Google in direct competition with Sora and Runway in a fast-crowding generative video market.

*Sources: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-news/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/19/google-ai-youtube-gemini)*

### Samsung's Android XR Smart Glasses Finally Revealed
Google revealed Samsung's long-awaited Android XR smart glasses at I/O 2026. The glasses run on Gemini and mark Google's most serious wearable AI push since Google Glass. No price or ship date was announced.

*Sources: [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/live/google-io-2026-live) · [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/live/google-i-o-2026-live-blog-android-17-android-xr-glasses-and-all-the-gemini-ai-news)*

### OpenAI, Eleven Labs, and Kakao Adopt SynthID
Google announced at I/O that OpenAI, Eleven Labs, and Kakao are joining SynthID, its imperceptible AI content watermarking standard. SynthID embeds signals that survive cropping, compression, and screenshots — unlike fragile metadata. Over 100 billion files have already been watermarked since SynthID's launch, and OpenAI is layering it on top of its existing C2PA content credentials.

*Sources: [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/05/google-synthid-c2pa-content-credentials-pixel-instagram-ai-detection.html) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-synthid-watermarking-ai-detection/)*

## Deals: Talent and On-Prem Access

### Google DeepMind Hires Contextual AI's Team for ~$100M
Google DeepMind is bringing in more than 20 researchers from Bezos-backed Contextual AI, paying roughly $100 million for talent and technology licenses. Douwe Kiela, Contextual's co-founder and CEO, is among those joining DeepMind. It's Google's second major acqui-hire in two years, following its $2.4 billion Windsurf deal in 2025.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-19/google-hires-staff-from-bezos-backed-contextual-ai-in-licensing-deal) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/google-deepmind-hires-staff-contextual-194051475.html)*

### OpenAI and Dell Take Codex On-Premises for Regulated Industries
OpenAI and Dell Technologies announced a multi-year deal to deploy Codex across hybrid and on-premises enterprise environments via Dell's AI Factory. Codex now has more than 4 million weekly developer users, and the on-prem option directly targets financial services, healthcare, and government buyers that can't route data through public cloud. It's OpenAI's clearest move yet to compete with established enterprise AI vendors.

*Sources: [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/openai-and-dell-technologies-announce-codex-partnership-to-bring-ai-agents-to-hybrid-and-on-premises-enterprise-environments/) · [Channel Life](https://channellife.com.au/story/openai-dell-partner-on-codex-for-on-premises-firms)*

## Capital at the Frontier

### Anthropic Eyes $30B Raise at a $900B Valuation
Anthropic is in early talks to raise $30 billion at a valuation above $900 billion — which would rank it among the most valuable private companies ever. Its Q1 2026 ARR topped $44 billion, up 80x year-over-year, with over 1,000 enterprise clients each spending more than $1 million annually, including PwC, Blackstone, and Goldman Sachs.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/anthropic-in-talks-to-raise-30-billion-at-900-billion-valuation)*

### Q1 2026 VC Hit $300B — AI Companies Took 80%
Global venture funding hit an all-time record of $300 billion in Q1 2026, with $242 billion — 80% — going to AI companies. Four of the five largest VC rounds ever closed in that quarter: OpenAI ($122B), Anthropic ($30B), xAI ($20B), and Waymo ($16B) together absorbed 65% of all global venture capital.

*Sources: [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/record-breaking-funding-ai-global-q1-2026/) · [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/05/278941-ai-venture-funding-reaches-new-highs-in-q1-2026-report-reveals/)*
