Google reshuffled its entire AI leadership this week, and four of its most senior researchers walked out the door to start a rival lab on the same day. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last two days.

## Google's AI Leadership Earthquake

### Hassabis Steps Down as DeepMind CEO in Alphabet AI Shakeup

Demis Hassabis is out as CEO of Google DeepMind. He's moving up to chair of DeepMind and chief scientist for all of Alphabet, while DeepMind CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu becomes SVP and takes over Gemini model development, frontier research, and the Gemini app team, reporting straight to Sundar Pichai. Google is also centralizing its AI work in Mountain View; UK-based coding lead Sebastian Borgeaud relocated as part of the move. Alphabet stock dropped nearly 4% on the news.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/google-s-deepmind-shakeup-weakens-uk-bid-to-stay-in-ai-race) · [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/google-deepmind-announces-major-leadership-changes-demis-hassabis-steps-down-as-ceo-all-details-547553-2026-08-06)*

### Jeff Dean Leaves Google After 27 Years to Found Discovery Loop

Jeff Dean and fellow Google veteran Sanjay Ghemawat quit on August 5, taking Gemini co-lead Oriol Vinyals and researcher Quoc Le with them. The four are launching Discovery Loop, a public benefit corporation built to automate the loop of scientific discovery: hypothesis, experiment, evaluation. They're starting with machine learning research itself before moving into hardware design, drug discovery, and clean energy. Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures are co-leading a seed round that hasn't closed yet, and Alphabet is staying in as both an investor and a cloud partner.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/) · [Radical Ventures](https://radical.vc/our-investment-in-discovery-loop/)*

## Anthropic Doubles Down on Compute and Policy

### Anthropic Locks In $10B Compute Deal With Norway-Based Volta Infra

Anthropic signed a six-year, $10 billion deal for 121 megawatts of Nvidia Vera Rubin chip capacity, all from a hydropower-fed data center in Tydal, Norway. The supplier, Volta Infra, is only seven months old. Volta leases the site from bitcoin miner turned data-center builder Bitdeer under a 16-year, roughly $4.7 billion contract, and JPMorgan arranged $1.3 billion in letters of credit to backstop the deal. Capacity comes online in two phases, by December 2026 and March 2027.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/04/anthropic-signs-10-billion-deal-with-ai-cloud-startup-volta/) · [DataCenterDynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/volta-launches-out-of-stealth-to-lease-capacity-in-norway-from-bitdeer-for-leading-ai-lab/)*

### Anthropic Hires Its First Global Affairs Chief as Trump Tensions Simmer

Anthropic named Mariano-Florentino "Tino" Cuéllar, a former California Supreme Court justice and past president of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, as its first chief global affairs officer. He'll report to President Daniela Amodei and take a leave from Stanford Law to run policy and government relations worldwide. The hire lands as Anthropic navigates a Pentagon lawsuit and export-control friction with the Trump administration.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/anthropic-names-global-affairs-chief-as-trump-tensions-persist.html) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tino-cuellar)*

## The Model Race Keeps Moving

### xAI Says Grok 4.6 Is Days Away, Ships New Voice Model

Elon Musk said on August 4 that Grok 4.6 should land within about a week, putting a release right around now. It follows Grok 4.5, which xAI calls its strongest model yet for coding and agentic work. xAI already shipped Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 on August 5, a speech-to-speech model with faster reasoning and better transcription that now powers the default voice mode. Musk says Grok 4.7 follows in about four weeks.

*Sources: [Roic News](https://www.roic.ai/news/musk-grok-46-coming-out-likely-next-week-08-04-2026) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/xai)*

### DeepSeek Quietly Upgrades Its V4-Flash API for Agents

DeepSeek pushed its V4-Flash API into public beta on August 1 with stronger agent capabilities, native support for the Responses API, and Codex-focused compatibility, all without changing how developers call it. The upgrade only touches V4-Flash; V4-Pro and the consumer app and web models are unchanged for now. It's a small release, but it keeps DeepSeek's API on the same fast cadence rivals have set this year.

*Sources: [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/deepseek)*

## AI Transparency Laws Take Effect on Both Sides of the Atlantic

### EU's AI Act Transparency Rules Go Live, Deepfakes Must Be Labeled

Article 50 of the EU AI Act kicked in on August 2. Any AI system that talks directly with people now has to make clear it's a bot, unless that's already obvious. Generated images, audio, and video need machine-readable marking, and deepfakes need explicit labels. AI-written text on public-interest topics needs disclosure too, unless a human editor takes responsibility for it. Fines run up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover.

*Sources: [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august) · [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026)*

### California's AI Transparency Act Becomes Operative the Same Week

California's AI Transparency Act also became operative on August 2, after lawmakers pushed its start date back seven months to line up with the EU's rules. Any generative AI provider with over a million monthly California users now needs a free AI-content detection tool and has to embed a hidden disclosure in AI-generated images, audio, and video. Violations cost $5,000 per day.

*Sources: [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/08/new-california-ai-disclosure-rules-become-operative) · [KALW](https://www.kalw.org/bay-area-news/2026-08-04/landmark-ai-transparency-law-takes-effect-in-california)*
