Earnings season just showed AI infrastructure spending in hard numbers, three labs shipped new models, and Geoffrey Hinton and Andrew Ng argued about extinction risk on the same stage. Here's what mattered in the last day and a half.

## Q2 Earnings Confirm the AI Infrastructure Boom Is Real Money

### AMD's Data Center Revenue More Than Doubles on AI Chip Demand

AMD reported $11.5B in Q2 revenue, up 50% year over year and above Wall Street's $11.2B estimate. Data center revenue hit $6.7B, up 107%, now 58% of total sales. AMD starts shipping Helios, its first full AI rack system, to Meta, OpenAI, and Oracle this quarter — a direct challenge to Nvidia's complete-system business, not just its chips.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/amd-earnings-report-q2-2026.html) · [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/AMD/amd-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-s9qsl4zgkkw3.html)*

### Palantir's Revenue Jumps 93% as Enterprise AI Spending Keeps Climbing

Palantir posted $1.94B in Q2 revenue, up 93% year over year and well above the $1.8B analysts expected. US commercial revenue rose 149% to $764M. The company raised full-year guidance to $8.15B-$8.16B, up from $7.65B-$7.66B, and the stock jumped 12% on the news.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/palantir-pltr-earnings-q2-2026.html) · [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9000347/palantir-technologies-inc-pltr-q2-2026-earnings-call-highlights-record-93-revenue-growth-and-raised-guidance-signal-unprecedented-ai-demand)*

## Three Labs Ship New Models

### DeepSeek's V4 Flash Goes GA and Beats Its Own Bigger Model on Agent Tasks

DeepSeek pushed V4 Flash out of preview with the 0731 release, scoring 82.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 — up from 61.8% in preview and higher than DeepSeek's own 1.6T-parameter V4 Pro. The model runs on just 13B active parameters (284B total) and costs $0.14 per million input tokens. DeepSeek says the base model is unchanged; the gains came entirely from redoing post-training.

*Sources: [DeepSeek](https://deepseek.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-flash-ga-agent-benchmarks) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731)*

### Mistral Ships a 3B Safety Classifier That Beats Models 7x Its Size

Mistral released Shieldstral, a 3B-parameter open-weights content moderation model that outperforms models up to seven times larger on safety benchmarks. It takes plain-language moderation policies as input at inference time, so teams don't need to retrain it when rules change. It's Apache 2.0-licensed and runs on a single 16GB GPU.

*Sources: [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/shieldstral/)*

### xAI's Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 Becomes the Default, Cuts Latency in Half

Starting today, xAI's grok-voice-latest alias switches from Think Fast 1.0 to 2.0, its fastest speech-to-speech model yet. Time to first audio dropped from 1.25 seconds to 0.70 seconds, and it scored 82.9% on Artificial Analysis's speech benchmark, ahead of GPT-Realtime-2.1 (79.1%) and Gemini 3.1 Flash (69.5%). xAI says an A/B test on Starlink's phone support line already showed higher sales conversion.

*Sources: [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/spacexai-launches-grok-voice-think-fast-2-0-on-agent-builder/) · [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/07/30/spacexai-launches-grok-voice-think-fast-2-0-485487/)*

## AI Policy Enters Enforcement Mode

### Hinton and Ng Take the Same Stage to Argue Over AI Extinction Risk

The Ai4 2026 conference opened in Las Vegas this week with 12,000+ attendees, and its marquee moment pits Geoffrey Hinton against Andrew Ng on whether AI could wipe out humanity. Hinton puts the odds at 10-20%. Ng told the US Senate he sees "no plausible path for AI to lead to human extinction" and says doom talk distracts from real, fixable risks. Fei-Fei Li joins them on stage for a panel billed as "Architects of Intelligence."

*Sources: [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322674/20260802/ai4-2026-opens-tuesday-hinton-ng-face-off-ais-existential-stakes.htm)*

### EU Starts Enforcing AI Transparency Rules This Week

The European Commission began enforcing new AI Act transparency requirements on August 2. Chatbots now have to tell users they're talking to AI, not a person. Deepfakes need labels, and AI-generated content needs machine-readable marks so it can be detected. California's own AI transparency law also becomes operative this month, adding a second compliance front for companies serving both markets.

*Sources: [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august)*
