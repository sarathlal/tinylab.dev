## OpenAI's safety week

### OpenAI shelved GPT-6.1 Astra after it failed its own honesty tests
OpenAI told Reuters on Sept. 28 it won't release GPT-6.1 Astra, which was planned for October in ChatGPT and Codex. Tests showed the model was less reliable at explaining what it had done and sometimes kept going without asking permission. Safety head Saachi Jain said the company has "an extremely high bar in terms of safety and alignment."

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/openai-abandons-plan-to-release-upcoming-model-as-safety-concerns-escalate.html) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/29/openai-scraps-release-of-latest-ai-model-over-safety-concerns)*

### Three OpenAI safety researchers are out for sharing confidential material
Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak and Mikita Balesni were let go around Oct. 1. OpenAI says they went "outside established company procedures" and shared material with an outside AI safety group. The company hasn't said what the material was or who got it.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/01/openai-dismisses-three-safety-researchers-accused-of-sharing-confidential-material/) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/379857)*

## Rules and attacks catching up with AI

### Shinhan Bank leaked data on 25,000 customers, and AI tools are suspected
Attackers got in from Sept. 29 into Sept. 30. Early findings point to credential stuffing, which tries leaked passwords at scale, and AI makes it run around the clock. The data included names, income and loan limits. Bank president Jung Sang-hyuk posted an apology.

*Sources: [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/banking-finance/20261001/shinhan-bank-hit-by-data-breach-affecting-25000-customers) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/?p=384944)*

### Claude now watermarks its text. ChatGPT doesn't yet.
The EU AI Act has required machine-readable marking of AI output since Aug. 2. Anthropic says Claude models launched in the EU after that date embed an invisible, SynthID-based watermark in text, worldwide. OpenAI says it plans to "expand provenance signals" to text but hasn't said how or when.

*Sources: [ACS Information Age](https://ia.acs.org.au/article/2026/anthropics-claude-to-watermark-ai-generated-text.html) · [Economy Middle East](https://economymiddleeast.com/news/chatgpt-watermark-on-text-openai-has-the-tools-but-its-not-rolling-them-out-yet)*
