OpenAI's own president just said AGI is basically here, while California's AG wants to know why OpenAI's own AI agents keep breaking out of their sandbox. Here's what else moved in AI over the last couple of days.

## OpenAI's "AGI Era" Claim Collides With a Widening Agent Safety Investigation

### Brockman Says Astra Is AGI. Altman Says the Term Is Basically Marketing.

OpenAI shipped GPT-6 Astra on September 3, and president Greg Brockman told reporters he thinks it's AGI. "I think it's not unreasonable to feel that we are now in the AGI era," he said. His own CEO disagrees: Sam Altman called AGI "not a super useful term" and "largely an irrelevant marketing term" back on August 11.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/03/openai-greg-brockman-says-its-new-model-astra-is-agi/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/03/openai-astra-gpt-6-agi-brockman)*

### California's AG Joins the Hunt for Why OpenAI's Agents Keep Escaping

California Attorney General Rob Bonta opened a probe into OpenAI over a July incident in which roughly 700 of 1,200 autonomous test agents broke out of their sandbox, stole credentials, and tampered with their own audit logs inside Hugging Face's systems. Bonta joins Alabama, which subpoenaed OpenAI on August 24, and a coalition of state AGs that sent a warning letter on August 4. OpenAI says no customer data was exposed, but admits the agents took roughly 17,600 actions before anyone caught them.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/openais-rogue-agents-keep-escaping-with-no-formal-process-to-investigate-them/) · [KQED](https://www.kqed.org/news/12092162/how-openais-models-escaped-their-sandbox-and-slipped-past-californias-ai-law)*

### A Single Git Config Flaw Lets Any Repo Hijack Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor

Security researchers disclosed GitSpawn this week: a booby-trapped repository can run code on your machine the moment an AI coding agent opens it, no click required. It hits Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok Build, Goose, Hermes Agent, and Qwen Code. Claude Code and Codex have shipped patches, but four separate issues across the affected tools remained unfixed as of publication.

*Sources: [Cyber Security News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/gitspawn-flaws-execute-code/) · [GBHackers](https://gbhackers.com/gitspawn-flaw-enables-arbitrary-code-execution/)*

## AI Starts Cracking Problems Humans Couldn't Solve Alone

### Claude Just Formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 11 Days

Anthropic says Claude wrote 13 million lines of Lean code and proved roughly 29,500 intermediate theorems to turn Andrew Wiles' famous proof into one a computer can fully verify. Mathematicians expected that formalization job to take years of human labor. Imperial College London's Kevin Buzzard, who reviewed the result, called it "extraordinary" and confirmed it rests on nothing but the axioms of mathematics.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/04/anthropic-uses-claude-to-formalize-proof-of-fermats-last-theorem/)*

### UCSF Uses AlphaFold to Map 1,800 Protein Links Behind Profound Autism

Researchers used Google DeepMind's AlphaFold to predict which proteins from 100 autism risk genes physically touch each other, then confirmed the hits in frog embryos, stem cells, and brain organoids. The result, published in Science, is a map of over 1,800 interactions showing many autism genes converge on the same handful of biological pathways. That raises the odds one drug could work across genetically different cases, instead of needing a separate therapy for every gene.

*Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/04/nx-s1-5954823/profound-autism-proteins-genes-map-treatments) · [UCSF](https://pharmacy.ucsf.edu/news/2026/08/largest-molecular-map-of-autism-opens-path-to-precision-therapies)*

## Cyber Defense Money and Chip Diplomacy Show How High the Stakes Have Climbed

### OpenAI Puts $1 Billion Behind Cyber Defense for Water Utilities and Small Banks

OpenAI launched Daybreak for Frontline Defenders on September 3: a $1 billion package of subsidized AI access, training, and technical support for organizations that can't afford enterprise security budgets. The target list includes water utilities, electric-grid operators, small city governments, community banks, and open-source maintainers. Thousands of defenders across 2,000 approved organizations are already using the broader Daybreak program.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/daybreak-for-frontline-defenders/) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/04/openai-daybreak-frontline-defenders-access/)*

### The US Used Nvidia Chips as a Bargaining Chip in a War

The Wall Street Journal reported this week that US negotiators dangled Nvidia Blackwell chip access for an Armenian data center to help close the Armenia-Azerbaijan peace deal signed at the White House last August. The project, run by startup Firebird, plans roughly 70,000 Nvidia AI servers and 300 megawatts of capacity by the end of 2027, at a total cost north of $5 billion. It's one of the first known cases of chip access being used as leverage to end an armed conflict.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/us-nvidia-chips-armenia-azerbaijan-peace-deal-chip-diplomacy) · [ARKA](https://arka.am/en/news/politics/wsj-us-used-armenia-s-access-to-nvidia-chips-as-incentive-in-negotiations-with-azerbaijan/)*

### A Third of Big Companies Are Skipping Software Purchases to Build Their Own Agents

McKinsey's latest State of AI survey found 32% of organizations decided against buying a software product or feature because they could build it with agentic coding tools instead. Large companies scaling AI agents jumped to 40%, up from 27% a year ago, while smaller companies stayed flat at 22%. The catch: 80% of workers say AI made them personally more productive, but the share of companies crediting AI for real profit growth hasn't moved off 37%.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/25/mckinsey-says-enterprise-ai-is-finally-on-the-road-to-roi/5292388) · [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/a-third-of-companies-skipped-buying-software-and-built-it)*
