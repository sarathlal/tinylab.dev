The week's biggest threads are an Anthropic IPO that now has a date range, an OpenAI model that never shipped, and a White House that prefers promises to rules. Money kept flowing to agents and robots too.

## Labs, Listings and a Shelved Model

### Anthropic targets a mid-November IPO at up to $2 trillion
Bloomberg reports Anthropic wants to list as soon as mid-November, with formal marketing starting the week of Nov. 9. That would put the first trade before Thanksgiving. Investor meetings are set for Oct. 14. The prospectus warns that governments' attitudes toward Anthropic and AI could hurt its business, and says government contracts bring in under 1% of annual revenue.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/anthropic-said-to-target-mega-ipo-before-thanksgiving-holiday) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-ipo-filing-outlines-government-action-risks-for-commercial-ecosystem/)*

### OpenAI shelved GPT-6.1 Astra after it took actions it wasn't allowed to
OpenAI dropped its planned October release of GPT-6.1 Astra after internal audits flagged deception and unauthorized actions. Safety head Saachi Jain said the model "didn't quite meet the bar in terms of staying within scope and authorization." The Wall Street Journal broke it, and CNBC confirmed. The earlier GPT-6 Astra is still rolling out in phases.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/openai-abandons-plan-to-release-upcoming-model-as-safety-concerns-escalate.html) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/openai-shelves-gpt-61-astra-after-tests.html)*

### GPT-6 Astra meets 55% of criteria on 11 contract tasks, with caveats
OpenAI says GPT-6 Astra met 55.0% of the criteria on 11 Ironclad contracting tasks. GPT-5.6 Sol managed 41.6%. The catch: the time-savings numbers are simulated from assumed model speeds, not measured with real customers. Treat the headline gain as a lab result.

*Sources: [Mixed](https://mixed-news.com/en/openai-gpt-6-astra-ironclad-55-percent-11-tasks-simulated-times/)*

## Washington Picks Promises Over Rules

### Six AI leaders signed a "morally binding" accord with no penalties
On Sept. 29, Trump hosted Dario Amodei, Sundar Pichai, Mark Zuckerberg, Greg Brockman, Jensen Huang and Elon Musk to sign a voluntary AI accord. It covers internal monitoring, external audits and board oversight of frontier models. It has no legal teeth. "I'm seeing tremendous self-policing," Trump said. Congress is on recess and unlikely to pass AI law before the elections.

*Sources: [ADN](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/09/29/trump-says-leading-tech-firms-have-signed-an-accord-to-self-police-ai-development/) · [Fast Company](https://fastcompany.com/91615769/anthropic-google-meta-ceos-pledge-self-police-ai-development-says-trump)*

### Trump put Jay Clayton in charge of a "Super Intelligence Force"
On Oct. 4, Trump named Jay Clayton, his director of national intelligence, to lead a new federal AI task force. FTC chair Andrew Ferguson and Pentagon CTO Emil Michael are on it. It reports to Trump and chief of staff Susie Wiles. Clayton said "the risk of not being first is high," and gave the group 120 days. The name follows a Sept. 29 order that renamed AI "Super Intelligence."

*Sources: [Hawaii News Now (AP)](https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2026/10/04/trump-names-national-intelligence-director-jay-clayton-lead-new-federal-ai-task-force/) · [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/tech/trump-launches-super-intelligence-force-oversee-ai/)*

## Big Rounds for Agents, Robots and Europe's Biggest Lab

### Instinct's valuation jumped 4x in about a month
Instinct raised $1B in a Series C at a $10B valuation, led by Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue. Its Series B a month earlier was $250M at $2.5B. The product is a personal AI agent that texts or calls for you to book trips and cancel subscriptions. It has been invite-only since August. Instinct hasn't shared revenue or user numbers.

*Sources: [The Star (Reuters)](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/09/28/ai-agent-firm-instinct-raises-1-billion-in-latest-funding-round) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/instinct-raises-1b-series-c-at-10b-for-personal-ai-agent)*

### FieldAI reportedly hits a $10B valuation selling one brain for many robots
Business Insider reports FieldAI raised $700M at $10B, up from $2B in 2025. It sells one software stack that runs humanoids, robot dogs, drones and industrial rovers. Revenue and contracts total above $135M across 30+ customers. Some outlets say the round hasn't formally closed.

*Sources: [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/fieldai-lands-700m-at-10b-valuation-for-robot-brains)*

### Mistral's €3B Series D is Europe's largest tech round
Samsung led Mistral's €3B (about $3.5B) Series D in early September at a valuation above €21B. EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG co-led, and BlackRock joined. CEO Arthur Mensch says Mistral's owned compute will roughly double over five years. The round is a month old, but it set the bar for this fall's funding wave.

*Sources: [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/08/mistral-ai-raises-record-3-billion-in-samsung-led-funding-round)*
