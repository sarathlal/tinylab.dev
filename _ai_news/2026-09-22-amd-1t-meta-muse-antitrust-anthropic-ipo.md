Chip stocks had their best run of the year, Washington and Beijing traded proposals days before Trump and Xi sit down, and four of the biggest AI labs got hit with an antitrust suit over their own safety pledges. Here's what mattered in AI over the last day.

## Meta's Muse Agent Sets Off a Trillion-Dollar Chip Rally

### AMD Joins the $1 Trillion Club as Agentic AI Rewrites Chip Demand

AMD closed at $619.89 on Monday, pushing its market cap past $1 trillion for the first time — only the fourth US chipmaker to get there, after Nvidia, Broadcom and Micron. The stock capped a five-day, 24% run built on one bet: AI agents burn far more CPU power than a single chatbot prompt ever did. Intel jumped 12%, Arm surged 17%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index notched its fifth straight winning session.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/amd-stock-1-trillion-value.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/amd-set-to-top-1-trillion-in-market-value-as-chip-stocks-soar)*

### Meta's Muse Hits 448,000 Daily Users in 10 Days, and Chipmakers Cash In

Meta's Muse AI agent hit 448,000 daily active users on September 18, just 10 days after launch. Unlike a chatbot that answers once and stops, Muse plans, browses and acts across many steps, which burns through CPU cycles a single prompt never touched. Intel's CEO says demand is now so high the company can fill only about half of customer orders, and Arm pegs the server-CPU market at $120 billion by 2030.

*Sources: [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/intel-arm-holdings-stocks-skyrocket-over-11-as-metas-muse-ai-app-drives-big-growth-for-server-processors) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/meta-ai-agent-muse-triggers-massive-tech-rally-as-chips-and-wall-street-soar-4909393)*

## Washington's AI Reckoning: Diplomacy, Chips and a New Antitrust Fight

### Bessent Pitches China an AI Incident Alert System Ahead of the Trump-Xi Summit

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent met China's Vice Premier He Lifeng at JPMorgan's New York headquarters on September 20, four days before Trump and Xi sit down in Washington. The two sides discussed a new "US-China AI Dialogue" that would set up a notification system for AI incidents serious enough to touch national security. "Moving from opaque to more transparency between the number one and the number two AI powers in the world is very important," Bessent said.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/20/bessent-he-lifeng-trump-xi-summit.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/09/21/bessent-touts-ai-dialogue-with-china-and-notification-mechanism-for-incidents/)*

### A China Hawk in Congress Wants Tighter Chip Controls Days Before Xi Lands

House Select Committee on China chair John Moolenaar wrote Trump urging him to slow China's AI progress "as much as possible" by tightening chip and chipmaking-equipment export rules. He specifically wants Washington to close "cloud services loopholes" that let Chinese firms rent access to restricted compute overseas. US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer has already said chip export controls are off the table for the AI-notification talks, exposing a split inside Trump's own team.

*Sources: [Export Compliance Daily](https://exportcompliancedaily.com/article/2026/09/21/lawmaker-export-controls-key-to-slowing-chinas-ai-development-2609180046) · [Daily Caller](https://dailycaller.com/2026/09/21/congress-artificial-intelligence-chips-china/)*

### Four Users Sue Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI and Google Over an Alleged Slowdown Pact

Four ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini subscribers sued Anthropic, OpenAI, Musk's SpaceXAI and Google in federal court in San Francisco on September 18. The complaint centers on September 12, when Anthropic's Dario Amodei published an essay calling for the industry to slow down on safety grounds, and Sam Altman, Elon Musk and Demis Hassabis each publicly agreed the same day. The suit claims that public unity was really a Sherman Act violation, an illegal agreement to trade individual competition for "collective restraint" that devalued their paid subscriptions.

*Sources: [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-slowdown-lawsuit-openai-anthropic-google/) · [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/lawsuit-says-anthropic-openai-spacexai-and-google-made-illegal-agreement-on-ai-slowdown)*

## Anthropic and CoreWeave Keep AI's Financing Machine Running

### Anthropic Pushes Its IPO to November, Now Targeting a $2 Trillion Valuation

Anthropic is now targeting a November IPO, pushed back a month so it can show investors fresh third-quarter numbers, at a valuation near $2 trillion. That figure is double the company's last private mark of $965 billion from May, and rests on a bet that revenue will hit $190-200 billion by 2028. Annualized revenue already topped $65 billion at the end of July and is expected to clear $110 billion by year-end.

*Sources: [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/anthropic-targets-november-ipo/) · [Unusual Whales](https://unusualwhales.com/news/anthropic-november-ipo-two-trillion-valuation)*

### CoreWeave Prices $3.7 Billion in Convertible Notes to Fund Its Buildout

CoreWeave priced $3.7 billion in convertible senior notes due 2033 on September 18, upsized from an initial $3 billion target, with an option for $500 million more. The notes carry a 2.875% coupon and convert at about $97.85 a share, a 22.5% premium over CoreWeave's stock price the day before pricing. Settlement lands September 22, and most of the roughly $3.6 billion in net proceeds go toward funding its AI data center buildout.

*Sources: [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9088103/coreweave-crwv-pricing-37b-convertible-notes-amid-high-ps-valuation-and-mixed-guru-sentiment) · [CoreWeave Investor Relations](https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Prices-Upsized-3-7-Billion-Convertible-Senior-Notes-Offering/default.aspx)*
