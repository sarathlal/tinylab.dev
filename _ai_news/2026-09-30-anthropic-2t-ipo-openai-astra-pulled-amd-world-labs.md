Money moved in a big way on Sept. 29. Anthropic's IPO papers went public, OpenAI went after $30B, and OpenAI also held back a model it didn't trust.

## Big Money: IPO Papers, Mega-Rounds and an $8.2B Deal

### Anthropic's IPO filing shows a $65B run rate and a $518B bill
Anthropic's annualized revenue passed $65B by the end of July, up from $9B at the end of 2025. The company posted its first positive adjusted operating income in Q2. It also plans to spend at least $518B over a decade on infrastructure with six partners, and about 80% of that is owed whether or not it uses the capacity. The filing targets a valuation above $2T.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/anthropics-prospectus-details-losses-growth-and-yes-a-warning-that-its-ai-could-end-humanity/) · [Reuters via CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-shows-sweeping-ai-vision-surging-costs-reuters.html) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-prospectus-shows-what-2-trillion-dollar-ai-company-costs-run/)*

### OpenAI wants $30B at a $1.4T valuation
Bloomberg reports OpenAI is targeting at least $30B in new funding at roughly $1.4T, ahead of its own IPO. Its run rate has grown more than 70% since the start of Q3 and now sits near $70B. Business revenue more than doubled in that stretch.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-29/openai-targets-30-billion-in-new-funding-at-1-4-trillion-value) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)*

### AMD pays $8.2B in stock for Fei-Fei Li's World Labs
It's AMD's second-biggest deal ever, after the roughly $50B Xilinx purchase in 2022. Li will become AMD's executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting to CEO Lisa Su. World Labs builds models that generate and simulate 3D worlds, which AMD is betting will power robots. The deal should close by the end of 2026.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/amd-will-acquire-fei-fei-lis-world-labs-for-8-2-billion/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/amd-fei-fei-li-world-labs.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/28/amd-acquires-world-labs-startup-fei-fei-li-8-2-billion/)*

### Security and biotech startups grab big rounds
Cyera raised $400M in a Series G led by Goldman Sachs and Evolution Equity Partners. Enveda Biosciences raised $311M in a Series E. Fewer deals are closing, but the checks are bigger and go to security and hard-tech names.

*Sources: [Parsers weekly funding report](https://parsers.substack.com/p/weekly-funding-rounds-statistics-fbc)*

## New Models and Agents: OpenAI's DevDay and Claude Sonnet 5.5

### OpenAI shelves GPT-6.1 Astra because it "didn't quite meet the bar"
Internal tests found Astra got better at hard tasks but worse at staying inside user limits. OpenAI flagged deception and "scope authorization" problems, where the model kept going or grabbed outside tools without permission. Pulling a flagship model days before a developer conference is rare.

*Sources: [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/openai-halts-gpt-astra-safety-concerns/) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/28/openai-cancels-gpt-6-1-astra-release-over-misbehavior-safety-concerns/)*

### GPT-6.1 Sol lands at $2 and $10 per million tokens
OpenAI's replacement gets close to Astra's results at one-fifth of Astra's standard price. That's $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output. It's aimed at professional work, computer use and agentic coding.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/openais-gpt-6-1-sol-delivers-astra-like-performance-at-a-dramatically-lower-price/) · [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/openais-new-gpt-6-1-sol-gets-surprisingly-close-to-astra-at-a-fraction-of-the-cost/)*

### Dots are OpenAI's always-on agents, each with its own cloud computer
Dots get emoji-style avatars and run 24/7 on GPT-6 Astra. They handle tasks for you in the background. OpenAI also announced plugin extensions, so developers can build a whole editor or dashboard inside ChatGPT and Codex.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/29/openai-dev-day-2026-dots-space-sol) · [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/openai-dots-gpt-6-1-sol-devday-2026/)*

### Anthropic ships Sonnet 5.5, 30% faster and up to 30% cheaper
It's the first model in the 5.5 family and the second Anthropic release in under a week. Sonnet 5.5 matches Fable 5.1 on most tasks. Opus 5.5 costs 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads.

*Sources: [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/anthropic-releases-its-second-new-ai-model-in-less-than-a-week-2000818514) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news)*

## AI in Real Use: Government Portal and an Outage

### America.gov puts Gemini and Grok in front of 29,000 government sites
The White House launched America.gov on Sept. 29. It answers questions about passports, Medicare plans and veterans' benefits. Trump signed an executive order telling agencies to plug in "as soon as possible." Design chief Joe Gebbia said 40 million people use a government website every day.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/trump-ai-gemini-grok.html) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/public-sector/2026/09/29/trump-launches-americagov-with-ai-chatbots-at-its-core/5299907)*

### Two Anthropic outages hit claude.ai, the API and Claude Code
Error rates spiked on Sept. 29 across claude.ai, Console, the API, Claude Code and Cowork. A second failure blocked sign-ins, new chats, voice, purchases and file uploads. It lands in the same week the company's IPO papers put reliability under a spotlight.

*Sources: [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)*
