A Chinese model got so popular its maker had to shut off new signups, Brussels forced Google to open Android's doors to rival AI assistants, and Shanghai closed out its AI summit with a governance body that leaves out Washington. Here's what actually moved the needle in AI over the last day and a half.

## China's Open Models Surge, Rattling Nvidia's Grip on the Chip Trade

### Moonshot AI Pauses Kimi K3 Signups as Demand Overwhelms Its Servers

Moonshot AI stopped accepting new subscribers for Kimi K3 on July 20, just a week after launching the 2.8-trillion-parameter model. Demand pushed the company's compute close to its limits over 48 hours. Existing subscribers keep their access, and Moonshot says new signups will reopen "in batches" as it adds capacity; it won't release K3's weights until July 27.

*Sources: [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/20/moonshot-ai-kimi-k3-subscribers-halted-servers/) · [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3361172/kimi-k3-developer-suspends-new-subscriptions-amid-compute-constraints)*

### Alibaba Ships Qwen3.8 Max as Nvidia Stock Slides Toward $200

Alibaba released Qwen3.8 Max on July 20, its latest open-weight model aimed at squeezing more capability out of less compute. The launch landed the same day Nvidia shares tested $200 support, down roughly 14% from their 2026 high, as investors weighed China's open-model wave against a broader semiconductor pullback.

*Sources: [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/20/top-tech-news-today-july-20-2026-alibaba-bezos-blackstone-google-moonshot-ai-nvidia-samsung-more/) · [FX Leaders](https://www.fxleaders.com/news/2026/07/20/nvidia-stock-nvda-tests-200-support-as-chinese-ai-competition-shakes-the-chip-rally/)*

## Brussels Tightens the Screws on Google and AI Transparency

### EU Orders Google to Open Android and Search Data to Rival AI Assistants

The European Commission issued binding decisions requiring Google to let rival AI assistants use 11 Android features, from booking taxis to reading out messages, and to share anonymized search data with competing chatbots starting January 2027. Google must roll out the Android changes by August 2027. Android chief Sameer Samat called the Commission "on the wrong track," warning the changes could weaken privacy and security. Google faces fines of up to 10% of its global annual turnover for non-compliance.

*Sources: [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/eu-android-ai-google-search-mandates-3688186/) · [Digital Markets Act](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/commission-provides-guidance-google-ai-interoperability-android-and-sharing-google-search-data-under-2026-07-16_en)*

### EU Publishes AI Act Transparency Guidelines Before August 2 Deadline

The European Commission published guidelines on July 20 spelling out how AI providers must comply with the AI Act's transparency rules, which take effect August 2. Companies will have to tell users when they're talking to an AI system and add machine-readable marks to AI-generated content, including deepfakes, so it can be detected as synthetic.

*Sources: [eunews.it](https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/07/20/greater-transparency-from-ai-providers-and-deployers-the-eu-commissions-new-guidelines/) · [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-transparency-ai-generated-content)*

## Shanghai's WAIC Closes With a Governance Body That Leaves Out Washington

### World AI Conference Wraps in Shanghai With a Declaration on Global Governance

The 2026 World AI Conference and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance closed in Shanghai on July 20, drawing more than 1,400 guests in its biggest turnout yet. Xi Jinping attended, and the summit issued a Shanghai Declaration on Global AI Governance covering system safety, fairness, infrastructure access, and AI's effect on jobs.

*Sources: [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260720/e2550c55ecfd413188eb965e304c8be2/c.html) · [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260717/fdc7646bcf8c491c92123f095aaaa670/c.html)*

### 29 Countries Launch a Shanghai-Based World AI Cooperation Organization

Twenty-nine countries, including Russia, Brazil, Indonesia, and Pakistan, signed the agreement founding the World AI Cooperation Organization on the eve of the conference. UN Secretary-General António Guterres attended the signing. The body will be headquartered in Shanghai, giving China a governance forum for AI that doesn't run through the US or its allies.

*Sources: [US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-07-16/twenty-nine-countries-sign-agreement-to-establish-global-ai-cooperation-body) · [Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-07-17/china-launches-shanghai-based-ai-governance-body-with-29-founding-nations-102465524.html)*

## AI's Physical Bottlenecks Attract Big Money

### Bezos Backs CuspAI's $450M Round to Hunt for Chipmaking Materials

Cambridge-based CuspAI raised $450 million (£335 million) in a Series B on July 20, valuing the company at $2.6 billion, five times what it was worth nine months ago. Jeff Bezos and the UK's sovereign AI fund co-led the round alongside Kleiner Perkins and NEA. CuspAI uses AI and lab automation to search for new materials for semiconductors and energy systems, and it's now teaming up with Nvidia on the hunt for chip materials.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/bezos-cuspai-new-chip-materials-nvidia.html) · [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/cuspai-400m-series-b-2-6b-valuation-bezos-materials-search/)*

### AMD Stock Jumps 5% After Microsoft Expands Its Azure AI Chip Order

AMD shares rose 5% on July 20 after Microsoft expanded their partnership to put more AMD silicon inside Azure's AI infrastructure. AMD's first rack-scale AI system, Helios, ships to customers including Microsoft later this year. The news lands two days before AMD's Advancing AI event on July 22, where analysts expect more customer announcements.

*Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/amd-stock-surges-5-as-microsoft-locks-in-massive-ai-infrastructure-deal-4801074) · [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/20/amd-just-got-a-potential-ai-heavyweight-customer-heres-the-new-price-target/)*
