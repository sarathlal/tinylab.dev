OpenAI's newest model just crossed a cybersecurity line no AI has crossed before, and Washington can't decide if it trusts Anthropic or wants it gone. Here's what else moved in AI in the last day.

## AI Models Are Crossing Into "Critical" Danger Territory

### OpenAI's Astra Becomes the First Model to Hit a 'Critical' Cyber Rating

Astra scored a perfect result on ExploitBench, OpenAI's benchmark for turning known vulnerabilities into working exploits. In a separate test, it found two zero-day flaws on its own, with no human pointing it there. That's enough to trip the top cybersecurity tier of OpenAI's Preparedness Framework for the first time ever, forcing extra safeguards before release. Access to Astra's sharpest cyber skills will start out limited to testers and paying "Daybreak Blue" partners.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/open-ai-astra-cyber-model.html) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/)*

### A Startup Just Raised $30M to Stop Agents Like Astra From Going Rogue

Lasso Security closed a $30 million round led by ClearSky, days after Astra's rating made headlines. Its new product, LEAP, screens AI agent behavior on ordinary CPUs — no GPU needed — in under five milliseconds per check. Customers already include BMW, eToro, Fiverr, and the Department of Homeland Security, covering tens of thousands of agents and billions of actions a month.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/02/lasso-security-launches-cpu-only-ai-guardrail-raises-30m/)*

## Anthropic's Whiplash Week in Washington and the Courts

### Commerce Secretary Says 'We Trust Anthropic' Despite the Pentagon Fight

Howard Lutnick told Axios on September 1 that the Trump administration trusts Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei again: "They've done what we asked. They're back on the right side." The comment marks a thaw after months of conflict with the Pentagon over Claude's refusal to allow mass surveillance or autonomous weapons use — a fight still playing out in court even as Lutnick extends an olive branch.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/02/lutnick-anthropic-trump) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/lutnick-declares-administrations-trust-in-anthropic-at-g20-meeting/)*

### Sony and Warner Sue Anthropic's Founders by Name Over 'Tens of Thousands' of Songs

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell filed suit accusing Anthropic of training Claude on pirated lyrics and sheet music pulled from sites like Library Genesis, then scraping licensed lyric databases Musixmatch and LyricFind on top of that. Unlike most AI copyright suits, this one names co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann personally. The publishers want up to $150,000 per song, for songs including "Eye of the Tiger" and Taylor Swift's "Paper Rings."

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/sony-music-warner-sue-anthropic-alleging-a-brazen-campaign-of-intellectual-property-theft/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/01/anthropic-warner-sony-music-songs-lawsuit/)*

## Frontier Labs Keep Shipping Faster Than Anyone Can Track

### Google Ships Its Fourth Flash Model in Four Months

Gemini 3.8 Flash landed Wednesday in two flavors: a general coding and reasoning model, and a specialized "Cyber" version. Google says it hits 73.7% on the DeepSWE v1.1 long-horizon coding benchmark, closing in on pricier frontier models. It's live now for Gemini app subscribers at an introductory $0.75 per million input tokens, good through the end of the year.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/02/with-gemini-38-flash-google-reminds-everyone-its-still-in-the-race/5294049) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/02/gemini-3-8-flash-launch/)*

### Alibaba's Qwen Update Jumps 22 Points on Coding Without a Version Bump

Qwen3.8-Max-0902 keeps the same 2.4 trillion parameters and 1-million-token context as its predecessor, but Alibaba retrained it hard on coding and multi-step agent work. Its score on the CodeArena leaderboard climbed 22 points to 1,691, putting it at the top of the board. Pricing stays put at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output.

*Sources: [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/09/02/alibaba-upgrades-qwen38-max-with-new-0902-snapshot/)*

## Investors Keep Betting Big on AI Infrastructure and Enterprise Software

### Dell's AI Server Backlog Hits a Record $95 Billion

Dell booked $60.9 billion in new AI server orders last quarter alone, pushing its total backlog to $95 billion. Revenue hit $47 billion, up 58% year-over-year, and AI-optimized servers alone brought in $16.4 billion — double what they did a year ago. Dell raised its full-year revenue forecast by $25 billion to $192 billion on the back of it.

*Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/dell-q2-fy27-slides-ai-server-backlog-hits-95b-revenue-up-58-93CH-4884718) · [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/09/02/dell-surges-9-on-record-95b-ai-backlog-hewlett-packard-enterprise-climbs-4-super-micro-ticks-up/)*

### Enterprise AI Startup Wonderful Doubles Its Valuation to $5B in Six Months

Wonderful closed a $550 million Series C led by Insight Partners, with Salesforce joining as a new investor. That's up from a $2 billion valuation just six months ago at its Series B. The Amsterdam-based startup, founded in early 2025, sells an "AI operating system" that lets enterprises automate workflows and coordinate agents — it's now in 35+ markets with 650 employees.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/wonderful-550m-series-c-5bn-valuation-amsterdam-eu-data-act-lock-in) · [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/02/wonderful-raises-550m-at-5-billion-valuation-just-20-months-after-launch/)*
