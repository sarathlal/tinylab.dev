Nvidia just bought its way into open-source AI's biggest hub, and Anthropic is buying itself another month before Wall Street gets a look at its books. Here's everything else that moved in AI this week.

## Nvidia and Anthropic Are Redrawing Who Controls AI's Infrastructure

### Nvidia Buys Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion

Nvidia is paying $12.93 billion for Hugging Face, the platform that hosts 3 million AI models and 500,000 datasets for more than 18 million developers. CEO Jensen Huang says it stays open: "Nvidia compute will not be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face." Hugging Face reportedly turned down a $500 million Nvidia offer last year — this time it said yes, making it Nvidia's second-largest deal ever, after December's $20 billion Groq asset buy.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-agrees-to-buy-hugging-face-for-almost-13-billion-ai-expansion.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/)*

### Anthropic Pushes Its IPO Back to Mid-October

Anthropic is delaying the marketing launch of its IPO by about a month, to mid-October, with the prospectus not going public until late September. Investors are hoping for a $75 billion-plus raise at a $2 trillion-plus valuation, landing the listing just before the US midterm elections. Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Citi are also arranging a $15 billion revolving credit facility alongside it.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/05/anthropic-ipo-launch-shifts-toward-mid-october-reuters.html) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-said-to-postpone-ipo-to-just-before-midterms/)*

### Nscale's Pre-IPO War Chest Leans on One $45B Anthropic Deal

Nscale is raising $3.5 billion ahead of its own IPO: $2 billion from Nvidia and $1.5 billion in convertible notes led by Dan Loeb's Third Point. The London cloud firm's contracted backlog nearly doubled to $103 billion in a single month, and almost half that jump came from one six-year, $45 billion deal to host Anthropic's workloads at its West Virginia campus. Goldman Sachs is running the raise, priced at a discount to whatever Nscale's IPO ends up costing.

*Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nscale-3-5-billion-pre-ipo-financing-nvidia-third-point-103bn-contracts-narvik-norway-new-york-listing) · [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nscale-seeks-35bn-in-pre-ipo-funding-report/)*

## Neoclouds That Don't Own a Single Chip Are Worth Billions

### Crusoe Raises $3B at $30B, Nine Months After Its Last Round

Crusoe just raised more than $3 billion at a $30 billion valuation, triple what it was worth ten months ago. The round follows a five-year, $13 billion deal to supply trading firm Jane Street with GPUs and AI infrastructure. Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners co-led, with Abu Dhabi's Mubadala Capital joining — Crusoe already counts Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI as customers.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/crusoe-reportedly-raises-3b-at-a-30b-valuation/) · [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/crusoe-raises-3b-at-30b-valuation-after-landing-13b-jane-street-deal/)*

### Fluidstack Doubles Its Valuation to $18B Without Owning a Single GPU

Fluidstack closed a $1.5 billion round led by Jane Street's capital arm, doubling its valuation from $7.5 billion in July to $18 billion on projected revenue jumping from $1.8 million to $660 million. Its pitch: build and run the data centers and software, and leave the actual chips to customers. Anthropic and Google have each backstopped more than a billion dollars of Fluidstack's lease obligations as anchor tenants for its buildout.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2026/09/03/a-tiny-startup-helping-google-take-on-nvidia-is-now-worth-18-billion/) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326746/20260905/fluidstack-closes-15b-revenue-soars-18m-660m-projected-while-owning-zero-chips.htm)*

## OpenAI Pulls Ahead in the Model and Research Race

### GPT-6 Astra Takes the Top Coding Benchmark From Claude

GPT-6 Astra scored 1,797 on Code Arena's WebDev leaderboard, 35 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.1's 1,762, based on more than 650,000 crowdsourced votes across 126 models. It's OpenAI's first clear lead on that board in months, and it matches Fable 5.1's $40-per-million-token price to get there. Astra had already made headlines this month for tripping OpenAI's top "Critical" cybersecurity tier, which forced extra safeguards before its wider release.

*Sources: [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-gpt6-astra-tops-code-arena/) · [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gpt-6-astra-vs-claude-fable-5-1)*

### OpenAI Says Its Coding Agents Now Outwork Humans Three-to-One

OpenAI's research organization now logs 3.1 "agent-workdays" of coding-agent effort for every one day of human work, up from below parity as recently as June. The typical researcher burns through $600 a day in inference costs; the top 10% spend more than $7,000 a day. Output per researcher is up 124-fold since December, but jobs that would take a person four to eight hours still need a human to step in more than half the time.

*Sources: [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/6/research-acceleration-the-view-inside-openai/) · [Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/09/07/openai-ai-research-acceleration/)*

### Grok 4.7 Is Coming September 12, Trained on SpaceX's Own Engineering Data

Elon Musk says Grok 4.7 lands in ten days at 2.1 trillion parameters, a 40% jump from Grok 4.6. Its standout feature is a training pass on decades of SpaceX telemetry, manufacturing records, and Starlink satellite data that Musk claims no competitor can match. xAI also just brought Grok 4.6 to Microsoft Foundry with a 500K-token context window, positioning it for long-running enterprise agent work ahead of the bigger launch.

*Sources: [Big Hat Group](https://www.bighatgroup.com/blog/xai-weekly-2026-09-06/) · [explainX](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/grok-4-7-spacex-data-2-1-trillion-parameters-2026)*
