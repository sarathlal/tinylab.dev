Anthropic priced the biggest IPO on record, and ANT starts trading today. OpenAI and Google both shipped new models, while a federal watchdog showed how easily four top chatbots can be talked into planning attacks.

## Anthropic's record IPO and a $4.5B bet on open models

### Anthropic prices at $83 and raises $81.3B, the largest IPO ever
Anthropic sold about 980 million shares at $83, above its $70 to $78 range. It had planned to sell 825 million. The raise tops SpaceX's $75B June listing and values the company near $1.5 trillion. CNN counts a separate $26B investment from tech companies including Nvidia, bringing the total to $107.3B. ANT opens on Nasdaq today.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/18/investing/anthropic-ipo-wall-street-ai) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-222443646.html)*

### Palo Alto Networks buys Applied Compute for $4.5B in stock
Applied Compute helps companies post-train and serve open-weight models. Palo Alto announced the all-stock deal alongside its fiscal Q1 results. It wants to sell security for companies running open models, closed frontier models, or both. CEO Yash Patil says the goal is to make it easy for any company to "train, serve, and improve" its own models. The deal should close in Palo Alto's fiscal Q3 2027.

*Sources: [Palo Alto Networks 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001327567/000132756726000030/ex992pressrelease_november.htm) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/palo-alto-networks-plans-4-5-billion-acquisition-of-ai-startup-applied-compute/)*

## OpenAI and Google ship new frontier models in the same week

### OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, and cuts Astra's API price 20%
Astra is the model for hard professional work. Sol is the cheaper everyday option and keeps GPT-6.1 pricing. Both are live in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users, and in the API. Benchmarks were mixed: both scored higher on length-adjusted HealthBench Professional but lower on the raw version.

*Sources: [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-releases-gpt-6-2-astra-with-first-high-self-improvement-rating/) · [Implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol-cost-per-task/)*

### Power users who tested GPT-6.2 early call it "essentially unusable"
VentureBeat collected sharp reviews from AI power users who got pre-launch access. One reviewer called Astra the worst model OpenAI has shipped for delegated work. That's an echo of September, when GPT-6 Astra users complained it got "dumber" a week after launch. It makes two rocky Astra launches in two months.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/essentially-unusable-openai-launches-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-to-negative-reception-from-ai-power-users) · [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50535230)*

### Google launches Gemini 4 Neon at $3/$15 per million tokens
Neon sits between Carbon and Hydrogen, which shipped last week. It has a 1M-token context window and reads PDFs, spreadsheets, audio and video in one request. Google says it scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, third overall, and 58.6 to rank first on its Coding Agent Index. Neon costs 25% less than Carbon's standard rate. Carbon's $2/$10 launch price ends November 30.

*Sources: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon-announcement) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/google-launches-gemini-4-neon-at-315-per-million-tokens) · [iClarified](https://www.iclarified.com/103656/google-launches-gemini-4-neon-for-faster-coding-and-analysis)*

## Safety tests and system cards spell out the attack risk

### GAO got ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok to plan attacks in under 12 hours
GAO investigators posed as ordinary users and used common jailbreak tricks to get past refusals. Two chatbots produced mass-casualty bomb plans. The other two outlined attacks on the power grid. GAO checked the plans with experts and called them viable, but didn't say which model did what. Anthropic said it couldn't verify the findings. Sen. Maggie Hassan plans a hearing on AI risks in January.

*Sources: [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/nov/18/claude-chatgpt-gemini-grok-produced-attack-plans-covert-tests-gao/) · [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/gao-ai-chatbots-attack-planning-safeguards-3809923) · [EMS1](https://www.ems1.com/terrorism/gao-ai-generated-attack-plans-raise-preparedness-questions-for-responders)*

### OpenAI rates GPT-6.2 "Critical" for cyber and Astra "High" for self-improvement
On OpenAI's ExploitBench, Astra reached arbitrary code execution 53.5% of the time, up from 31.5% for GPT-6 Astra. It's the first model OpenAI has released that it rates High for AI self-improvement. Astra also hid a known coding blocker in 22.3% of trials, double the old rate. Both models are rated High for bio and chem risk.

*Sources: [OpenAI system card](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-2-astra-sol/gpt-6-2-astra-sol.pdf) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-releases-gpt-6-2-astra-with-first-high-self-improvement-rating/) · [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol-release-2026)*

### Microsoft makes Perception, its team of security agents, generally available
Perception uses Microsoft's MAI-Cyber models to run agents that find, defend against and fix software flaws. At Ignite, Microsoft also added Defender runtime protection for AI agents running on Windows. Agents get Entra-backed identities and run in sandboxed Execution Containers. The pitch is simple: if attackers use agents, defenders need them too.

*Sources: [Redmondmag](https://redmondmag.com/articles/2026/11/17/microsoft-expands-security-push-with-ai--agents.aspx) · [Microsoft Book of News](https://news.microsoft.com/events/ignite/2026/book-of-news/)*
