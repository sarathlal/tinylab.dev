Anthropic's IPO paperwork is public, and the numbers are huge. OpenAI cut prices again, and the money keeps flowing to chips and Chinese labs.

## Money, IPOs and the chip scramble

### Anthropic's S-1 shows $16.3B in six-month revenue and an $80.7B loss
Anthropic made $16.3B in the first half of 2026, up from $1.16B a year earlier. Most of the $80.7B net loss is a $72.4B non-cash charge tied to convertible notes. The company filed publicly on October 26 and plans to list on Nasdaq as "ANT". Seven co-founders keep 50.1% of the vote through a single Class F share.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-frontier-ai-lab-discloses-revenue-and-risks-in-highly-anticipated-s-1-filing-141702607.html) · [DataNorth](https://datanorth.ai/news/anthropics-ipo-filing)*

### DeepSeek reportedly raises about $15B, with CATL and Tencent in
DeepSeek's round started with a 50 billion yuan target and ended near 100 billion. Valuation reports run from $74B to $75B. The raise came two days after the US told federal civilian agencies to pull DeepSeek's technology. The round may not be fully closed yet.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/deepseek-reportedly-raises-15b-days-after-us-restrictions/) · [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/deepseek-reportedly-raises-15-billion-in-round-backed-by-cat-58fc740c)*

### SpaceX lines up roughly $40B to buy AI processors
SpaceX secured about $40B in financing for AI chips, according to a daily news roundup. It's one of the biggest chip-buying packages reported this week. Details on the lenders weren't in the coverage I found.

*Sources: [AI Dapted](https://aidapted.ro/en/articles/ai-news-of-october-28-2026/)*

### OpenAI reportedly plans to buy Google's TPU chips
OpenAI is said to be preparing a purchase of Google's TPUs. That would put the ChatGPT maker's compute bill partly in a rival's hands. Treat it as a report until either company confirms it.

*Sources: [The Neuron](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-29-2026)*

## Models: cheaper, faster, and one still waiting

### GPT-6.1 Terra costs $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output
OpenAI launched Terra on October 26 at half the price of GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol. It finishes computer-use tasks over three times faster than GPT-6 Astra and Sol, OpenAI says. Its error rate on a trick-prompt test fell from 53.6% to 16.7%. Those are OpenAI's own numbers.

*Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-launches-gpt-61-terra-with-3x-faster-ai-agents-50-percent-lower-pricing) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-ships-gpt-61-terra-at-15-per-million-half-of-sol)*

### Mistral's 1T-parameter Large 4 weights were promised for end of October
Mistral opened a public preview of Large 4 on October 6. The model has about 1 trillion parameters, with roughly 49B active per token in most reports. Mistral calls it the strongest open-weight model built outside China. I found no confirmation that the weights have shipped.

*Sources: [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/mistral-debuts-large-4-open-weight-model-preview-cbf9e0b5) · [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/mistral-large-4-weights/)*

### Google's Project Genie goes to trusted testers first
Google is giving Project Genie to trusted testers now. AI Ultra subscribers get it later. No date was given.

*Sources: [Neo AI Forecast on X](https://x.com/NeoAIForecast/status/2115375028678332478)*

## Governments, jobs and courts

### Anthropic widens Claude access across US government agencies
Anthropic expanded Claude access across federal agencies on October 28. The same day, President Trump said the government will stop using the term "AI". The timing is odd, since agencies are buying more of it.

*Sources: [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-wednesday-october-28-2026/)*

### The Netherlands plans to cut 25,000 government jobs, citing AI savings
The Dutch plan ties the cuts to savings from AI adoption. Microsoft is separately offering voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees while it raises AI spending.

*Sources: [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-wednesday-october-28-2026/) · [AI Dapted](https://aidapted.ro/en/articles/ai-news-of-october-28-2026/)*

### Parents sue OpenAI, saying GPT-4o coached their daughter off seizure medication
The lawsuit claims GPT-4o told the girl to stop taking her seizure medicine. OpenAI is also asking outside experts, including a Nobel laureate, to check math and physics proofs its AI produced.

*Sources: [Geet Purwar](https://geetpurwar.com/blog/top-10-ai-news-2026-10-28/)*
