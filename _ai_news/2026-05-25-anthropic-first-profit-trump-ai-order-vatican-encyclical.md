AI's busiest week in months peaks today: Anthropic's first-ever profit projection, a papal encyclical on machine intelligence, and a White House regulatory U-turn all landed within 72 hours of each other.

## Anthropic's Profitability Milestone — and the SpaceX Bill Behind It

### Anthropic Projects $10.9B in Q2 Revenue, Its First Profitable Quarter

Anthropic told investors it expects $10.9 billion in Q2 2026 revenue — up 130% from Q1's $4.8 billion — with roughly $559 million in operating profit. That would make Q2 Anthropic's first profitable quarter ever, two years ahead of internal projections. Enterprise API and cloud-provider deals account for the bulk of revenue, though skeptics note Q2 is also when Anthropic's SpaceX compute costs are suppressed during a ramp-up period.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html) · [Analytics Drift](https://analyticsdrift.com/anthropic-first-profitable-quarter-q2-2026/) · [Where's Your Ed At](https://www.wheresyoured.at/anthropics-profitability-swindle/)*

### Anthropic Pays SpaceX $1.25 Billion a Month for Compute

Anthropic's contract with SpaceX gives it access to the Colossus supercomputer in Memphis for $1.25 billion per month through May 2029 — roughly $15 billion per year at full rate. The ramp-up discount that applies in Q2 is why the profitability projection is hard to extend into Q3. Once full billing kicks in, the math changes.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/anthropic-nears-first-quarterly-profit-120712552.html) · [ResultSense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-05-21-anthropic-q2-profit-spacex-compute-deal/)*

## AI Governance Pulls in Opposite Directions

### Trump Scraps AI Oversight Order After Last-Minute Calls from Musk, Zuckerberg, and Sacks

Hours before the planned Oval Office signing on May 21, President Trump canceled an executive order that would have created a voluntary 90-day pre-release security review for frontier AI models. Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, and AI adviser David Sacks called Trump between the evening of May 20 and the morning of May 21. Trump's stated reason: "We're leading China, we're leading everybody, and I didn't want to do anything to get in the way of that lead." No replacement order is in sight.

*Sources: [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/05/21/2026/elon-musk-mark-zuckerberg-derail-trump-ai-order) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/trump-pulls-ai-safety-order-after-last-minute-calls-from-musk-zuckerberg-and-sacks/) · [US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-05-21/white-house-postpones-trumps-ai-signing-ceremony-says-axios)*

### Pope Leo XIV Publishes First Papal AI Encyclical, With Anthropic's Christopher Olah at His Side

Pope Leo XIV released "Magnifica Humanitas" today — the Catholic Church's first encyclical specifically on artificial intelligence, focused on "the protection of the human person in the age of AI." He signed it on May 15, exactly 135 years after Leo XIII signed Rerum Novarum, which addressed workers' rights during industrialization. Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah co-presented the document at the Vatican — a pairing that drew immediate attention from both the tech and theology worlds.

*Sources: [Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-05/pope-leo-xiv-first-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas.html) · [National Catholic Reporter](https://www.ncronline.org/vatican/vatican-news/pope-leo-present-his-encyclical-ai-alongside-anthropic-co-founder) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/anthropic-s-co-founder-to-launch-encyclical-on-ai-with-pope-leo)*

## The Infrastructure Spending Spiral

### Meta Commits $115–135 Billion in Capex for 2026

Meta announced it will spend $115–135 billion on AI infrastructure this year, nearly double its 2025 capex. The budget covers data centers, compute hardware, and the new Superintelligence Labs led by Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang. Wang's team also launched Muse Spark, Meta's first flagship multimodal model under the new structure, targeting reasoning, health, and agentic use cases.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/google-ai-ultra-gemini-spark-omni.html) · [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)*

### NextEra Buys Dominion Energy for $67 Billion — AI Power Demand Is the Driver

NextEra Energy announced an all-stock deal to acquire Dominion Energy for $67 billion, creating the largest regulated utility in the US. The deal joins two major players racing to meet electricity demand from AI data centers, which is growing at a pace not seen since the post-WWII era. The combined company will serve about 10 million customer accounts across Florida, Virginia, North Carolina, and South Carolina, with 110 gigawatts of generation capacity.

*Sources: [SEC Filing (Dominion Form 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000715957/000119312526227930/d158175dex991.htm)*

## Model Updates Reshape Consumer AI

### Google Flips Search to Gemini 3.5 Flash; AI Mode Hits 1 Billion Monthly Users

Starting today, Google runs Gemini 3.5 Flash as the default model in Search's AI Mode globally. One year after launch, AI Mode passed one billion monthly users, with query volume more than doubling every quarter. Google called it the biggest update to the Search box in 25 years. The shift follows last week's I/O announcements, which also introduced Gemini Spark (a reasoning agent) and Omni (a world model for simulating physical environments).

*Sources: [Google Blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/google-search-as-you-know-it-is-over/)*

### OpenAI Makes GPT-5.5 Instant the Default ChatGPT

OpenAI rolled out GPT-5.5 Instant as the new default model in ChatGPT, with memory sources and spreadsheet integration for Excel and Google Sheets. Three new real-time voice models also went live in the API. The move pushes OpenAI further into enterprise software territory, competing more directly with Microsoft 365 Copilot and Google Workspace.

*Sources: [CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/4167787/openai-anthropic-expand-services-push-signaling-new-phase-in-enterprise-ai-race.html) · [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)*
