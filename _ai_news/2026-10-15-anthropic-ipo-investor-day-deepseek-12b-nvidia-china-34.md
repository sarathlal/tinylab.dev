Anthropic met IPO investors in person, and DeepSeek is close to a giant raise. Meanwhile OpenAI had a rough week and Nvidia keeps losing China. Some details below come from single reports, so treat them with care.

## The IPO and funding race

### Anthropic pitches institutional investors at a private IPO day
Anthropic invited select institutional investors to its San Francisco headquarters on October 14 to question senior executives. Prospective buyers reportedly peg its value at $1.8 trillion to $2 trillion, against $965 billion at the last private round in May. Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan lead the planned offering, and the public S-1 hasn't appeared yet.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-01/anthropic-is-said-to-plan-pre-ipo-investor-day-as-listing-nears) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/ipo/2026/anthropic-begin-investor-meetings-potential-october-ipo/) · [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/anthropics-14-october-investor-day-nears-but-its-s-1-still-isnt-public-the-ipo-timeline-explained/)*

### Anthropic's run rate topped $65B in July, up from $47B in May
Anthropic told investors its annualized revenue run rate passed $65 billion by the end of July. Bloomberg also saw documents showing more than $11.5 billion in revenue last quarter and a positive adjusted operating income. The figure is a run rate from an unnamed source, not published revenue.

*Sources: [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-surpasses-65-billion-ahead-of-ipo) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-65-billion)*

### DeepSeek nears a $12B round backed by Tencent and CATL
DeepSeek is close to raising at least 80 billion yuan, about $12 billion, Bloomberg reported on October 6. It first sought around 50 billion yuan. The round reportedly values it near 500 billion yuan, and an IPO could follow in early 2027.

*Sources: [The Star](https://thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/06/deepseek-to-raise-at-least-12-billion-in-tencent-backed-funding-bloomberg-news-reports) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/deepseek-12bn-funding-round-tencent-catl)*

## OpenAI's legal and security problems

### A former OpenAI engineer is charged with planting a recording device
Federal prosecutors charged a former OpenAI engineer, a Saudi national, with hiding a recording device in an executive conference room at the San Francisco office. He had worked there since July 2025. The arrest was reported on October 14.

*Sources: [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/openai-saudi-engineer-arrested-10-14-26)*

### State attorneys general keep piling onto OpenAI over child safety
Florida filed the first state suit in June with 10 counts, including negligence and public nuisance. Platformer now reports suits in eight state courts. Forty-two states led by New York are also investigating safety and privacy. OpenAI denies responsibility.

*Sources: [Platformer](https://platformer.news/openai-states-lawsuit-liability) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/florida-becomes-first-state-sue-172332329.html)*

### A report says OpenAI's revenue is about $20B below expectations
Futurism, citing the Financial Times, says OpenAI's annualized revenue is roughly $20 billion lower than anyone expected. We couldn't check the original FT story, so this is secondhand. It lands as Anthropic courts IPO buyers.

*Sources: [Futurism](https://futurism.com/future-society/ai-financial-bubble-openai-billions-failure)*

## Chips and new models

### Nvidia's China AI chip share fell to 34% while Huawei hit 28%
An IDC report seen by Reuters says Nvidia shipped 678,000 AI accelerators in China in the first half, down 44% from a year earlier. Its share of the data-center market there dropped to 34%. Huawei is six points behind at 28%.

*Sources: [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/10/14/nvidias-china-ai-chip-share-falls-to-34-as-huawei-reaches-28) · [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/1156d043-8753-41ec-9a86-a28fd9ed14c2)*

### Google opens Gemini 4 Argon on the paid API
Google first gave Gemini 4 Argon to a small group of cyber-defense testers on September 30. One tracker says it reached the paid Gemini API on October 13. Dates vary by source, so check Google's own post.

*Sources: [LLM Gateway](https://llmgateway.io/timeline) · [Local AI Zone](https://local-ai-zone.github.io/blog/October_2026_AI_Model_Updates.html)*

### xAI ships Grok Voice 3.0
xAI's news page lists Grok Voice 3.0 on October 14, a voice model. A bigger Grok 4.8 has been teased with no date or price.

*Sources: [xAI](https://x.ai/news) · [Medium](https://medium.com/@shtse8/whats-coming-next-in-ai-grok-4-8-gemini-4-deepseek-v4-1-pro-qwen-4-wait-or-pay-1942d3ef5952)*
