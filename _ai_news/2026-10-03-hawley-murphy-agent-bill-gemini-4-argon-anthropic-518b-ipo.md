Washington wants someone to blame when an AI agent hacks a system. Meanwhile, Google, OpenAI and Anthropic all shipped big news.

## Who Pays When AI Agents Break Things

### Two senators want criminal liability for AI agent hacking
Senators Josh Hawley and Chris Murphy introduced the AI Agent Accountability Act on October 1. It would make operators liable under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act if they knowingly run an agent that causes damage. Developers would also be on the hook if they skipped reasonable safeguards. Hawley's line: AI companies shouldn't escape blame because a system, not a person, did the attack.

*Sources: [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/liable-ai-goes-rogue-senators-010524805.html) · [CDO Magazine](https://www.cdomagazine.tech/aiml/ai-agent-liability-bill-puts-data-access-and-accountability-in-focus) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328503/20261002/ai-agent-accountability-act-rogue-agent-hacks-now-carry-criminal-risk-executives.htm)*

### OpenAI parts ways with three safety researchers
OpenAI let go of three safety-team researchers after an internal review. The company says they "mishandled sensitive information outside established company procedures." The timing is awkward. The FTC opened a probe into OpenAI, Anthropic and other labs about rogue agent behavior about a week ago.

*Sources: [The Neuron](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328503/20261002/ai-agent-accountability-act-rogue-agent-hacks-now-carry-criminal-risk-executives.htm)*

### Connecticut's AI law took effect October 1
Connecticut's CART Act went live on October 1. The first wave covers frontier-developer duties and generative-AI provenance. Employment-decision rules bind deployments made on or after October 1, 2027. Separately, 26 state attorneys general wrote Congress on September 23 asking for federal AI rules.

*Sources: [CT Public](https://ctpublic.org/2026-09-29/ai-artificial-intelligence-data-privacy-new-laws-ct-october) · [Origin Brief](https://www.originbrief.app/en/reports/ai-regulation-policy/2026-10-01/monthly)*

## New Models From Google and OpenAI

### Google gives Gemini 4 Argon to defenders first
Google released Gemini 4 Argon to vetted cyber defenders through its Fairwind Program before any public launch. It scored 68% on CWE-bench v1 and can find, validate and patch critical bugs on its own. It already found a flaw in healthcare software used by hospitals worldwide. Fairwind users get a version without cyber guardrails.

*Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-argon-to.html) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/01/google-gemini-4-argon/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/google-launches-gemini-4-argon-with-guardrail-free-access-for-vetted-defenders/)*

### OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol costs one-fifth of Astra
GPT-6.1 Sol costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. That's a fifth of GPT-6 Astra's price. OpenAI says it comes close to Astra on coding and computer use evals. It launched at DevDay with more than 20 other updates.

*Sources: [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/devday-2026-announcements-and-developer-resources/1402006) · [The Neuron](https://theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-thursday-october-1-2026)*

### OpenAI's "Dots" are always-on agents with a second model watching
Dots keep monitoring your work in the background. Consequential actions need your confirmation. A second model also checks each action against your rules. DevDay also brought computer use for the Agents API, a Decisions API and cloud Codex environments.

*Sources: [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/devday-2026-announcements-and-developer-resources/1402006)*

## Anthropic's IPO Numbers and Hiring Push

### Leaked prospectus shows $518B in compute commitments
Anthropic's leaked IPO filing lists $518B in infrastructure commitments over about a decade. Roughly 80% can't be cancelled. Revenue hit $4.6B in 2025, up 12x, against an $8.06B operating loss. Reuters says the listing could value the company above $2T.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/leaked-anthropic-ipo-filing-reveals-8b-operating-loss-rapid-revenue-growth/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/29/anthropic-leaked-ipo-prospectus-losses-growth-ai-end-humanity/) · [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/equities/anthropics-landmark-ipo-filing-shows-12-fold-revenue-jump-518b-compute-bill/268391)*

### Anthropic pledges $100M to train 10,000 deployment engineers
Claude Frontier Academy launched October 2. It's a medical-residency-style program: multi-day training, then a 12-week on-the-job rotation. First cohorts run in San Francisco, New York and London, with people from Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey and Morgan Stanley. The target is 10,000 engineers by the end of 2027.

*Sources: [Anthropic Release Notes (Releasebot)](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)*

## Inference Startups Keep Raising Money

### Fireworks lands a $1.5B Series D
Index Ventures co-led Fireworks' $1.5B Series D. The same week, a16z put $300M into Gimlet Labs. Both companies sell inference, the cost of actually running models. Coatue pegs 2026 hyperscaler AI capex at $733B.

*Sources: [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/ai-is-the-only-growth-budget-ramp-supabase-and-alphasense-headline-a-month-of-mega-rounds-0d5b56) · [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-energy-biotech-joulent/)*

### Wonderful raises $550M for AI customer agents
Wonderful closed a $550M Series C for its agent platform, which runs across 35 markets. Cognition is also landing big names like Goldman Sachs, Mercedes-Benz and Nubank. Agents are now moving from pilots into paid deployments.

*Sources: [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/ai-is-the-only-growth-budget-ramp-supabase-and-alphasense-headline-a-month-of-mega-rounds-0d5b56) · [Crescendo](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-vc-investment-deals-in-ai-startups)*
