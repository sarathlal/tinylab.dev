The biggest AI story today isn't a launch. It's an absence. Anthropic's two best models are still dark on day 10 of a US export ban, while the rest of the field keeps shipping.

## Washington Keeps Frontier Models on a Leash

### Fable 5 Stays Offline on Day 10 as the Free Trial Quietly Ends

Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are still offline for every user on Earth, ten days after a US export-control order pulled them on June 12. The free trial Anthropic gave paid subscribers ends today, June 22, with usage credits starting tomorrow for models nobody can run. An exec told reporters they're "very confident" access returns "in the coming days," and prediction markets put 57% odds on restoration before July 1.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) · [explainx](https://explainx.ai/blog/is-fable-5-back-2026)*

### A New White House Order Shapes How Frontier Models Ship

Trump's June 2 executive order tells federal agencies to build a voluntary framework that lets AI developers hand the government early access to frontier models for up to 30 days before release. It also sets up an "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse" run through Treasury, the NSA, and CISA to coordinate vulnerability scanning. The order bans any mandatory licensing, so it leans on voluntary cooperation, not permits.

*Sources: [The White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/) · [Covington / Inside Privacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/white-house-releases-executive-order-on-advanced-ai-innovation-and-security/)*

## The Assistant Race Reshuffles

### ChatGPT Falls Below 50% of the Assistant Market for the First Time

ChatGPT held 46.4% of global AI-assistant users by the end of May, under half for the first time, per Sensor Tower's State of AI 2026 report. It still leads with 1.1 billion monthly users, but Gemini (662M, 27.7%) and Claude (245M, 10.3%) keep eating into the gap. Share was 65.3% in December 2024, so the slide is steep.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/) · [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/chatgpt-market-share-slips-below-50-google-gemini-anthropic-claude-gain-ground-126061700765_1.html)*

### Gemini 3.5 Pro Is Days From a Deadline It Hasn't Hit

Google promised Gemini 3.5 Pro for June, and it's still limited to select Vertex AI enterprise customers. The specs are the draw: a 2-million-token context window, the largest of any announced frontier model, plus a "Deep Think" reasoning mode. Pricing is expected near $15 and $60 per million input and output tokens, roughly 10x Flash.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317919/20260606/google-gemini-35-pro-nears-launch-2-million-token-context-deep-think-reasoning.htm) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/gemini-35-pro-eyes-june-ga-with-2m-context-and-deep-think)*

### Google's First Smart Speaker in Six Years Runs Gemini

The new Google Home Speaker ships June 25 at $99.99, Google's first audio device built for Gemini. It handles multi-step conversations and 360-degree sound, and comes in four colors. A $10-a-month Home Premium tier adds Gemini Live and "Home Briefs" that summarize what happened while you were out.

*Sources: [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/google-home-speaker-gemini-features/) · [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/accessories/smart-home/google-home-speaker-launched)*

## Big Integrators Bet on Claude

### TCS Will Train 50,000 Staff on Claude and Stand Up an AI Unit

Tata Consultancy Services signed on June 11 as a Global Premier Partner in Anthropic's Claude network, committing to train 50,000 employees and launch a dedicated AI business unit. The deal targets regulated industries like financial services, healthcare, aviation, and telecom, where TCS already runs core systems. It's Anthropic's play to reach enterprises through the integrators that already hold the contracts.

*Sources: [TCS](https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-anthropic-launch-global-premier-partnership-drive-enterprise-ai-scaling) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/anthropic-taps-tcs-to-scale-its-enterprise-ai-deployments/)*

### DXC Says Claude Now Writes 95% of Its Software Before Review

DXC Technology's multi-year alliance with Anthropic, also June 11, will certify tens of thousands of engineers on Claude for banks, airlines, and insurers. The headline number: DXC says Claude sped up delivery of its OASIS platform roughly 10x, generating more than 95% of the code before a human looks at it. That's the kind of figure that makes CIOs rethink headcount.

*Sources: [DXC](https://dxc.com/newsroom/06112026-dxc-and-anthropic-announce-multi-year-global-alliance-to-bring-ai-into-mission-critical-enterprise-systems) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/dxc-anthropic-alliance)*

## The Security Bill for AI Agents

### A Fake Bug Report Can Hijack Your Coding Agent

Security firm Tenet showed that a crafted Sentry error report can turn Claude Code or Cursor into a command runner. When the agent reads the report through a Sentry MCP server, it treats attacker text as instructions. Tenet hit an 85% success rate for full execution and found 2,388 orgs with exposed error endpoints. They call it "agentjacking," and it works because agents trust the diagnostic data they're told to read.

*Sources: [SC Media](https://www.scworld.com/brief/agentjacking-attack-exploits-ai-coding-tools-with-fake-error-reports) · [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/agentjacking-attacks-hijack-ai/)*

### 97% of Developers Code With AI, but a Third Have Rules for It

Black Duck's June 9 report found AI coding tools at 97% adoption across 800-plus teams, saving developers about eight hours a week. Only a third have full governance over that code. GitHub Copilot (83%) and Claude Code (63%) lead, and 90% of teams hit problems with AI output, most often slow manual review and security testing.

*Sources: [SD Times](https://sdtimes.com/ai/ai-coding-adoption-rate-hits-97-black-duck-study-reveals/) · [Black Duck](https://news.blackduck.com/2026-06-09-AI-Coding-Hits-97-Enterprise-Adoption-New-Black-Duck-Study-Shows-Governance-Is-the-ROI-Multiplier)*

### Google DeepMind Publishes a Playbook for AI That Might Go Rogue

DeepMind released an "AI Control Roadmap" on June 18 for handling capable but possibly misaligned AI inside its own walls. The approach is defense-in-depth: assume alignment can fail, then wrap models in system-level security so a bad action gets caught anyway. It treats internal AI agents as insider threats, not just tools.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/securing-the-future-of-ai-agents/)*
