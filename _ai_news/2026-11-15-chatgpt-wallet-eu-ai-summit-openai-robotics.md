OpenAI handed its agents a credit card and folded its robotics group into the main lab. Europe's leaders spent a day on AI for the first time. It was a quiet Saturday, so this brief also picks up a few mid-week launches we hadn't covered yet.

## OpenAI bets on agents that act, not robots of its own

### ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's dots agents pay for things
OpenAI announced ChatGPT Wallet on Friday. It lets its dots agents finish purchases for you. It's rolling out first to US Pro users aged 18 and up with eligible American Express, Chase, Mastercard or Visa cards. The agent gets a tokenized card credential, not your real card number. That piece comes from a deal Visa and OpenAI announced earlier.

*Sources: [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/chatgpt_wallet_lets_openais_agents_buy_things_for_you-news-75481.php) · [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/exclusive-openai-is-building-a-chatgpt-wallet-for-agentic-purchases) · [ABC News (AP)](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/visa-plugs-payment-network-chatgpt-letting-ai-agents-shop-and-pay-for-users-133757718)*

### OpenAI breaks up its stand-alone robotics team
Fortune reports that OpenAI dissolved its separate robotics group. Robotics researchers now sit in the main research team under Chief Research Officer Mark Chen. Applied robotics and hardware staff move to Greg Brockman's Applied group. The reason, per Fortune: OpenAI's general-purpose models have started running robots themselves. This is the second time OpenAI has shut a dedicated robotics team. The first time was in 2021.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/13/openai-disbands-robotics-team-as-its-general-models-start-running-robots/) · [VentureBeat (2021)](https://venturebeat.com/2021/07/16/openai-disbands-its-robotics-research-team/)*

## Europe and Washington rethink how to police AI

### EU leaders spend a full summit on AI for the first time
All 27 EU leaders met in Dublin on Friday with AI as the only formal topic. António Costa chaired, and Taoiseach Micheál Martin hosted. Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch and Geoffrey Hinton briefed them. Hinton warned that humanity could be "toast" if AI can't be controlled. A fight over who negotiates for Europe on Ukraine took most of the headlines. Leaders agreed on a seven-member team for those talks. The meeting was informal, so it produced no binding AI decisions. Costa wants leaders to come back to AI in 2027.

*Sources: [European Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/european-council/2026/11/13/) · [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/arid-41937552.html) · [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/13/ukraine-envoy-row-hijacks-eu-leaders-first-ai-summit) · [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261113/26b371bf08db450ca108e6366e5c9f6a/c.html)*

### White House advisers want lighter liability so labs report AI incidents
Officials on President Trump's AI task force told agencies to ease their cyber liability policies, Bloomberg reports. The idea is simple. If reporting a critical incident invites penalties, labs will stay quiet. The officials say older rules for acting after cyber incidents may now do more harm than good. The task force still hasn't defined what counts as an incident or how fast labs must report one.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-14/white-house-urges-easing-liability-to-encourage-ai-disclosure) · [Semafor](https://semafor.com/article/10/15/2026/white-houses-ai-task-force-gets-to-work)*

## The race for office work and cheap agent models

### Anthropic launches Claude Sheets and Whiteboards, and Microsoft stock dips
Anthropic launched Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards in beta on Tuesday. Claude Data, the renamed Claude Dashboards, is now generally available. Claude Meetings, a bot that joins calls and takes notes, is coming for Team and Enterprise plans. Microsoft shares fell as much as 3% that day. It's an awkward spot: Microsoft is an Anthropic investor and partner, and now a direct rival in spreadsheets.

*Sources: [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/microsoft-shares-fall-anthropic-office-ai-tools-3809631) · [Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/digital-workplace/anthropic-turns-claude-into-an-ai-office-suite/) · [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/microsoft-stock-drops-3-after-anthropic-announces-claude-sheets-and-claude-data/)*

### Meta prices Muse Ember at $0.10 per million input tokens
Muse Ember costs $0.10 in and $0.50 out per million tokens, with a 1M-token context window. It's Meta's smallest Muse model. Meta pitches it as the worker that carries out tasks while the bigger Muse Wonder does the planning. Meta claims it matches Claude Haiku 5.5's best score at about a third of the cost. Nobody has checked that claim independently yet, and the weights stay private.

*Sources: [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/meta-muse-ember-launch/) · [Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/computing/meta-launches-muse-ember-a-cheaper-ai-model-built-for-repetitive-tasks)*

### Alibaba opens a two-week Qwen4-Max preview with a hard stop
Qwen4-Max-Preview has a 1M-token context window and can write up to 393,216 output tokens. The catch is the deadline. The preview ends November 25, and the model gets retired then, so anything built on it stops working. Alibaba published no benchmarks, parameter count or weights.

*Sources: [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-alibaba-opens-a-350-hour-qwen4-max-preview-with-1m-context/) · [Magica](https://magica.com/news/alibaba-qwen4-max-preview-token-plan)*

## Money keeps chasing AI, from retail investors to data center factories

### Anthropic courts retail buyers before Wednesday's IPO pricing
Anthropic set up a livestream with Dario Amodei and JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon, aimed at retail investors. Ordinary buyers are expected to take 15% to 20% of the deal. The book is already several times oversubscribed at $70 to $78 a share. Pricing is set for November 18, with Nasdaq trading as "ANT" the next day.

*Sources: [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/anthropic-ipo-livestream-amodei-dimon-jpmorgan) · [StockAnalysis (Bloomberg)](https://stockanalysis.com/news/anthropic-ipo-multiple-times-oversubscribed/)*

### Mavria hits a $1B valuation to build data centers in a factory
Paradigm led a round that values Mavria at $1B. The Houston-area startup says it has raised $130M to mass-produce modular AI data centers. Its first demo unit, "Terrain 01," is still being built. One analysis points out that an October SEC filing shows only $75M sold. Mavria hasn't announced any customer deployments.

*Sources: [DatacenterDynamics](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/former-us-doe-staffers-launch-modular-data-center-firm-mavria) · [Kirkland & Ellis](https://www.kirkland.com/news/press-release/2026/11/kirkland-advises-mavria-on-fundraising) · [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-mavria-hits-1b-valuation-sec-filing-shows-75m-sold/)*
