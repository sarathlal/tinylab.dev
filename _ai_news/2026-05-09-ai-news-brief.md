Anthropic just eclipsed OpenAI in revenue for the first time, a model too dangerous to ship is rewriting US policy, and OpenAI started charging advertisers to reach ChatGPT's free users. Here's what mattered in the last 24-36 hours.

## Anthropic Overtakes OpenAI on Revenue

### Anthropic's ARR Hits $30B, Passing OpenAI for the First Time

Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate crossed $30 billion in April — past OpenAI's $24B for the first time since ChatGPT launched. About 80% comes from enterprise customers; more than 1,000 businesses now each spend over $1 million annually with Anthropic, double the count from two months ago. OpenAI disputes the figure, arguing it overstates actual revenue by roughly $8 billion due to gross-versus-net accounting differences.

*Sources: [The AI Corner](https://www.the-ai-corner.com/p/anthropic-30b-arr-passed-openai-revenue-2026) · [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/anthropic-just-passed-openai-in-revenue-while-spending-4x-less-to-train-their-models/)*

### Anthropic Rents SpaceX's Data Center and Signs $1.8B Akamai Deal to Handle the Surge

Dario Amodei told staff that Q1 revenue grew 80-fold on an annualized basis — a rate he called "just crazy" and "too hard to handle." To keep up, Anthropic signed a deal for all compute at SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center in Memphis (over 300 megawatts) and separately inked a $1.8 billion contract with Akamai for cloud inference capacity.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/08/anthropic-80fold-growth-quarter-renting-elon-musk-data-center/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-08/anthropic-inks-1-8-billion-computing-deal-with-akamai) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/06/anthropic-spacex-data-center-capacity.html)*

## OpenAI's Push to Monetize ChatGPT

### GPT-5.5 Instant Is Now the Default ChatGPT Model

OpenAI swapped in GPT-5.5 Instant as ChatGPT's default on May 5. It produces 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims than its predecessor on high-stakes prompts covering medicine, law, and finance. The model also pulls context from users' Gmail accounts and past conversations to personalize responses.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-5-instant/) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai)*

### ChatGPT Ads Expand to Five More Countries

After early tests in the US, Canada, and Australia, OpenAI is rolling out sponsored results to the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea. Ads run only in the free and Go tiers, clearly labeled and visually separated from answers — Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers won't see them. Target, Adobe, Williams-Sonoma, and Albertsons are among the first advertisers on the platform.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/testing-ads-in-chatgpt/) · [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/openai-taps-retail-giants-for-first-wave-of-chatgpt-ad-testing)*

## A Restricted Model Reshapes AI Policy

### Claude Mythos Can Chain Linux Kernel Exploits to Own Any Linux Machine — and Anthropic Won't Release It

Anthropic's Mythos model finds "tens of thousands" of vulnerabilities that advanced human security researchers would miss. A specific internal test had it discovering several Linux kernel flaws, then autonomously chaining them into an exploit that grants full control of any Linux system. Anthropic has released Mythos only to a handful of vetted cybersecurity partners through Project Glasswing. In April, an unauthorized contractor accessed the restricted model before Anthropic caught the breach.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/07/anthropic-mythos-preview-cybersecurity-risks) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/21/unauthorized-group-has-gained-access-to-anthropics-exclusive-cyber-tool-mythos-report-claims/) · [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/138011/too-dangerous-anthropic-mythos/)*

### Trump Administration Reverses on AI Oversight After Mythos Raises National Security Alarms

The White House is now considering mandatory rules for the most capable frontier models — a sharp reversal from its earlier stance of blocking AI regulation to protect US competitiveness. Officials cited Mythos's autonomous hacking capabilities as a national security concern. The administration had previously rejected similar proposals from both Democrats and safety-focused industry groups.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/trump-administration-embraces-ai-oversight-policies-it-once-rejected-anthropic-mythos-caisi/)*

### Connecticut Passes the Most Comprehensive US AI Law Yet

Connecticut's House voted 131-17 for SB 5 — the AI Responsibility and Transparency Act — following a 32-4 Senate vote. The law requires employers using AI in hiring, promotion, or firing to notify affected workers. It bans chatbots from forming romantic or emotionally dependent relationships with minors. Synthetic content must be labeled as such by October 2027. Governor Lamont said he looks forward to signing it.

*Sources: [CT Mirror](https://ctmirror.org/2026/05/01/artificial-intelligence-house-regulation-passage-ct/) · [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/05/unpacking-connecticuts-new-ai-law)*

## Enterprise AI Funding Keeps Accelerating

### Sierra Raises $950M at $15.8B as AI Captures 66% of April's Global VC

Bret Taylor's enterprise AI agent startup Sierra closed a $950 million Series E led by Tiger Global and Google's GV, valuing the company at $15.8 billion. Sierra hit $150M in ARR in eight quarters — a pace Taylor calls unprecedented in traditional software. Its customers include Cigna, Prudential, Rocket Mortgage, and one in three of the world's largest banks. The deal fits a broader pattern: global VC funding hit $56 billion in April, with $37 billion of that going to AI companies.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/) · [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/global-startup-funding-april-2026-anthropic-jeff-bezos-project-prometheus-biggest-deals/)*

### Google DeepMind Takes a Stake in Eve Online's Studio to Research AI in Complex Systems

Google DeepMind bought a minority stake in Fenris Creations (formerly CCP Games, maker of Eve Online) and signed a research deal to study AI behavior in complex player-driven environments. The work focuses on long-horizon planning, memory, and continual learning, using an offline version of the game on a local server. Fenris simultaneously bought itself back from Korean publisher Pearl Abyss for $120 million. More details on the research program will come at Eve Fanfest on May 14.

*Sources: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/06/google-deepmind-is-partnering-with-eve-online-to-research-player-driven-systems/) · [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/ccp-games-is-no-more-eve-online-studio-changes-its-name-as-it-goes-independent-and-enters-an-ai-research-partnership-with-google-deepmind/)*
