Washington accused a Chinese lab of stealing Anthropic's model, OpenAI's spending plans got even bigger, and Wall Street started punishing AI capex instead of rewarding it. Here's what mattered in the last day and a half.

## Washington vs. Moonshot: A Distillation Fight With Sanctions on the Table

### White House Says Moonshot Distilled Anthropic's Fable to Build Kimi K3

Michael Kratsios, director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, said on July 22 the administration has "information that Moonshot AI distilled Anthropic's Fable for the development of its K3 model." He said Moonshot built "a sophisticated internal platform to conduct large-scale distillation against U.S. models," letting it "quickly switch between multiple methods of access to avoid detection." Kratsios also alleged Moonshot acquired banned Nvidia GB300 servers and accessed them from Thailand.

*Sources: [HNGN](https://www.hngn.com/articles/272292/20260723/white-house-accuses-chinas-moonshot-ai-stealing-anthropic-model-banned-chips.htm) · [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/white-house-accuses-moonshot-ai-anthropic-model-distillation/)*

### Treasury Puts Sanctions and Entity List on the Table

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent followed up by warning that sanctions and Entity List designations are on the table for Chinese AI firms found running "covert, industrial-scale distillation attacks that cross into IP theft." The government says it will now examine open-weight Chinese models for signs of stolen IP. Whether Treasury actually moves against Moonshot will be the first real test of how the administration enforces the policy.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/treasury-threatens-sanctions-after-white-house-claims-moonshot-distilled-anthropics-fable/) · [Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/07/23/us-treasury-ai-sanctions/)*

### Researchers Say Two Weeks Isn't Enough Time to Pull Off That Distillation

Kimi K3 is a 2.8-trillion-parameter model that scored 1,679 in Arena's Frontend Code benchmark, ahead of Claude Fable 5 in blind testing. Braden Hancock, a researcher at the Laude Institute and co-founder of Snorkel AI, pushed back on the distillation story: "I don't think you get a model this strong and this quickly on the heels of Fable doing strictly distillation... You can't distill that much data, train a model, and release it in two weeks." Moonshot's weights go public July 27, when outside researchers can finally check the claims themselves.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/experts-say-exploiting-anthropics-fable-isnt-how-kimi-k3-got-so-good/) · [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-war/article/3361625/global-ai-experts-push-back-us-distillation-claims-against-moonshots-kimi-k3-model)*

## Big Tech's AI Bill Keeps Climbing, and Wall Street Is Getting Nervous

### OpenAI Lifts Its 2030 Compute Budget to $750B, Breaks Ground on a $30B Georgia Campus

The Wall Street Journal reported July 22 that OpenAI raised its compute and cloud spending forecast through 2030 to roughly $750 billion, up 25% from the $600 billion figure it had floated earlier this year. Alongside that, OpenAI committed $20 billion to start Project Camellia, a 3.2-gigawatt, 1,400-acre data center campus in Effingham County, Georgia — its first self-built site, with early capacity online in 2028 and full buildout through 2032. The company also launched Presence, an enterprise platform that plugs AI agents into internal company systems with shared permissions and guardrails.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance/WSJ](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-lifts-planned-compute-spending-144917731.html) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/openai-plans-to-spend-over-30-billion-on-georgia-data-center)*

### Alphabet Beats on Revenue, Then Investors Punish It for Spending More

Alphabet posted Q2 revenue of $119.8 billion, up 23% year over year, with cloud revenue jumping 82% to $24.8 billion. But the stock fell as much as 4.6% after Google raised its 2026 AI capex forecast to $195-205 billion, well above the $187.1 billion Wall Street expected. Investors are done cheering bigger AI budgets and started treating them as a warning sign.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/google-earnings-q2-goog-live-updates.html) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/23/ai-alphabet-google-earnings)*

### Tesla's Profit Miss and Doubled Capex Trigger Its Worst Drop Since March 2025

Tesla beat on revenue ($28.24 billion versus a $25.55 billion estimate) but missed badly on profit, with adjusted EPS of $0.33 against a $0.49 forecast. Capital expenditures more than doubled to $5.79 billion, pushing free cash flow negative $1.09 billion for the quarter. The stock tumbled 15% the next day, its steepest one-day drop since March 2025, extending its 2026 decline to 29%.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/tesla-second-quarter-earnings-miss-wall-street-s-expectations) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/tesla-tsla-alphabet-googl-stock-today.html)*

## AMD Crashes the AI Chip Party

### AMD Launches Zen 6 EPYC Venice and $5.5M Helios Racks

At its Advancing AI 2026 event in San Francisco, AMD launched EPYC "Venice," the first x86 server chip in volume production on TSMC's 2-nanometer node, alongside Instinct MI450-series GPUs built on its CDNA 5 architecture. The rack-scale Helios system, powered by the flagship MI455X, is priced at $5.5 million per rack and is already backed by commitments from Meta, Microsoft, Oracle, and OpenAI.

*Sources: [AMD](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1294/aai-2026-amd-delivers-full-stack-compute-for-the-agentic-ai-era) · [ServeTheHome](https://www.servethehome.com/amd-advancing-ai-2026-keynote-live-coverage/)*

### Anthropic Signs Up for 2 Gigawatts of AMD's New Chips

Anthropic and AMD announced a strategic partnership to deploy up to 2 gigawatts of Instinct MI450X GPUs in Helios racks, one of the largest single commitments to AMD's new hardware disclosed so far. The deal gives AMD a marquee customer to point to just as it tries to prove Nvidia has real competition at the rack level, not just the chip level.

*Sources: [IT Pro](https://www.itpro.com/infrastructure/amd-advancing-ai-2026-helios-on-the-rise-with-launch-of-new-instinct-gpus) · [AMD](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1294/aai-2026-amd-delivers-full-stack-compute-for-the-agentic-ai-era)*
