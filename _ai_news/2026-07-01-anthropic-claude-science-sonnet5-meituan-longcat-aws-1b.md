Anthropic had its busiest single day of the year on Tuesday, China proved it can train frontier-scale models without a single Nvidia chip, and the fight over who controls frontier AI access kept splitting along national lines.

## Anthropic's Biggest Day Since the Export Ban

### Claude Science Launches to Chase Drug Discovery

Anthropic rolled out Claude Science on Tuesday, an AI workbench pre-loaded with more than 60 scientific databases for biology and chemistry work. It renders 3D protein structures and genome browser tracks, and keeps an auditable history of every output. Dario Amodei said humans have only ever wrestled with biology's complexity "with their minds" — Claude Science is his bet that AI changes that math the way Claude Code changed programming.

*Sources: [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-release-claude-science-ceo-dario-amodei/) · [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-launches-claude-science-ai-research-workbench-85428f54) · [The Deep View](https://www.thedeepview.com/articles/new-claude-science-tool-chases-ai-s-biggest-promise)*

### Claude Sonnet 5 Becomes the New Free Default, Priced Like a Budget Model

Sonnet 5 launched the same day, with a full 1M-token context window at no extra charge and introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through August 31. It's now the default model for Free and Pro users. Anthropic says its agentic performance — tool use, coding, autonomous browser and terminal work — comes close to Opus 4.8 at a fraction of the cost.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-sonnet-5-model-details-here/) · [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/claude-sonnet-5-launch/)*

### Claude Goes Fully Native on Azure, Adds a Gateway for Bedrock and Google Cloud

Claude models became generally available in Microsoft Foundry the same day, running inside customers' existing Azure identity, billing, and governance setup. Anthropic paired that with a new self-hosted "apps gateway" for Bedrock and Google Cloud, giving enterprises SSO login, role-based access, and per-user cost tracking for Claude Code. Three product launches, one day — a clear signal Anthropic is racing to lock in enterprise distribution ahead of its IPO.

*Sources: [Claude](https://claude.com/blog/claude-in-microsoft-foundry) · [Microsoft Azure Blog](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/claude-in-microsoft-foundry-is-now-generally-available/) · [Claude Code Docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-apps-gateway)*

## Washington and Beijing Draw Different Lines Around Frontier AI

### Commerce Partially Restores Claude Mythos 5 — But Only for About 100 Vetted Groups

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick eased the June 12 export ban on June 26, clearing Claude Mythos 5 for roughly 100 trusted US critical-infrastructure defenders, including Apple, Google, Cisco, Nvidia, and Microsoft under the Project Glasswing program. Claude Fable 5 stays blocked for everyone else. It's the first crack in a ban that shut down both models globally for two weeks straight.

*Sources: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kateoflahertyuk/2026/06/29/us-government-partially-lifts-anthropic-ai-export-ban-what-it-means/) · [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/06/27/us-lifts-anthropic-ban-allows-mythos-5-access-for-over-100-trusted-organisations/) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319213/20260628/claude-fable-5-still-offline-us-clears-mythos-5-critical-infrastructure.htm)*

### OpenAI Gates GPT-5.6 to About 20 Government-Approved Companies

OpenAI began a limited preview of GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna on June 26, after the Trump administration's cyber and science policy offices asked OpenAI to stagger the rollout over national-security concerns. Sol sets a new state of the art on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and starts at $5 per million input tokens. General availability is still just "coming weeks" away.

*Sources: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-unveils-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-and-luna-models-but-only-accessible-to-limited-preview-partners-for-now-per-us-gov) · [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/openai-gpt-5-6-sol-limited-preview/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/26/openai-gpt-sol-terra-luna-trump)*

### Meituan Open-Sources a 1.6 Trillion-Parameter Model Trained Entirely on Chinese Chips

Meituan released LongCat-2.0 on Tuesday, claiming it's the first trillion-parameter model both pre-trained and run entirely on domestic silicon — a 50,000-chip cluster of homegrown ASICs, not Nvidia GPUs. It ships with a 1M-token context window and an open license. DeepSeek's earlier models used domestic chips only for inference; Meituan says it did the whole job on Chinese hardware.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/30/chinas-meituan-open-sources-massive-longcat-2-0-ai-model-saying-trained-domestic-chips/) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/meituan-trillion-parameter-ai-model-china-chips/) · [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/china-debuts-longcat-ai-model-without-nvidia-chips/)*

## The AI Deployment Arms Race Goes Physical

### AWS Commits $1 Billion to Embed Its Own Engineers Inside Customer Companies

AWS announced a Palantir-style forward-deployed engineer unit on Tuesday, funded entirely from Amazon's own balance sheet with no outside investors. Pods of five or six engineers embed with clients for roughly 45-day sprints; the NFL, NBA, Cox Automotive, and Southwest Airlines are among the early customers. It's the first hyperscaler to build this model in-house, rather than through a joint venture like the ones OpenAI and Anthropic run.

*Sources: [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/aws-1-billion-forward-deployed-ai-engineers) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/aws-amazon-ai-forward-deployed-engineers.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/aws-launches-1-billion-forward-160014306.html)*

### SpaceX Starts Billing Reflection AI $150 Million a Month for Compute Today

The deal signed with Elon Musk's Colossus 2 data center kicks in today: Reflection AI, an open-source lab founded by ex-DeepMind researchers Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou, gets access to Nvidia GB300 chips for $150M a month, up to $6.3B if it runs the full three-year term. It's smaller than SpaceX's compute deals with Anthropic ($1.25B/month) and Google ($920M/month), but it's notable for going to an open-weights lab instead of a closed one.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/spacex-ai-colossus-data-center-reflection.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/spacex-inks-compute-deal-with-reflection-ai-an-open-source-ai-lab/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/22/open-source-ai-gets-more-compute-from-spacex)*
