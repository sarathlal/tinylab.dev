Google I/O's keynote opens this morning at 10 a.m. PT with Gemini and AI agents at the center. It's already a full day: Anthropic secured its biggest compute deal to date, a federal deepfake law takes effect, and ChatGPT is now reading your bank statements.

## Google I/O 2026: Gemini, Glasses, and a New PC Category

### Google I/O Keynote Opens Today with Gemini Model Update and Agentic AI Push

The annual developer keynote starts at 10 a.m. PT in Mountain View. AI is the clear focus. Google is expected to announce a new Gemini model — most tracking points to Gemini 3.5 or a full 4.0 — with agentic capabilities and tighter integration across Search, Workspace, and Android. The current Gemini sits behind Anthropic's Mythos and OpenAI's GPT-5.5 in public benchmarks, giving Google something real to prove today.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/316755/20260517/google-i-o-2026-keynote-opens-tuesday-new-gemini-lands-behind-mythos-gpt-55.htm) · [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/what-to-expect-from-google-io-2026-3664979/)*

### Android XR Glasses Confirmed; Aluminium OS Merges Chrome and Android into 'Googlebooks'

Google confirmed two Android XR glasses: a lighter audio-focused pair for hands-free Gemini access, and a display-equipped version with in-lens navigation and live translation. Google also introduced Aluminium OS — a merged Android-and-ChromeOS platform — under a new laptop category called Googlebooks. Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, and Lenovo will ship the first units this fall. A feature called Magic Pointer uses Gemini to make the cursor context-aware, the first major rethinking of the cursor in over 50 years.

*Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2171038/everything-announced-at-android-show-google-io-2026/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/05/13/googles-ai-enabled-mouse-pointer-understands-this-and-that/5240005)*

## Anthropic Secures Compute and a $200M Mission

### Anthropic Taps SpaceX's 220,000-GPU Colossus to Double Claude Code Limits

Anthropic signed a deal to use all capacity at SpaceX's Colossus 1 supercluster: 220,000+ NVIDIA GPUs across more than 300 megawatts. Claude Code rate limits doubled for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise users, and peak-hour caps are gone. For API Tier 1, max input tokens per minute jumped from 30,000 to 500,000 — a 17x increase. The deal follows months of user complaints about hitting limits far sooner than expected.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/higher-limits-spacex) · [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-spacex-claude-limits/) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2166315/anthropic-is-doubling-claude-code-rate-limits-after-deal-with-spacex/)*

### Anthropic and Gates Foundation Commit $200M to AI for Global Health and Education

Anthropic committed $200 million — in grants, Claude usage credits, and technical support — to the Gates Foundation over four years. The focus starts with global health: 4.6 billion people lack access to basic health services, and the initial work targets polio, HPV, and eclampsia. For education, Claude will power K-12 tutoring in the US and foundational literacy programs in sub-Saharan Africa and India. Anthropic will also train Claude on agricultural data to help roughly two billion smallholder farmers.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/gates-foundation-partnership) · [Gates Foundation](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/ideas/media-center/press-releases/2026/05/ai-anthropic-partnership)*

## AI Enters Finance and Small Business as Deepfake Law Takes Effect

### OpenAI's ChatGPT Connects to 12,000 Banks via Plaid for Pro Personal Finance Tools

OpenAI launched a personal finance experience for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US. Through Plaid, users can link accounts from more than 12,000 institutions — including Chase, Fidelity, Schwab, Robinhood, and American Express — and ask ChatGPT about spending, savings, and planning. It's a direct challenge to dedicated finance apps like Monarch Money and YNAB.

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/)*

### Anthropic Kicks Off Free Small Business AI Workshops, Starting in Chicago

Starting May 14 in Chicago, Anthropic took Claude for Small Business on the road with free half-day workshops for 100 local business leaders per stop. The product targets SMB owners without technical teams, automating payroll, invoice chasing, and marketing campaigns. It's Anthropic's first open bid for the main street market after years of enterprise-first focus.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/13/anthropic-butts-in-to-small-business-promises-help-with-payroll-and-other-core-tasks/5239967)*

### Take It Down Act Compliance Deadline Hits Today; FTC Warns 13 Major Platforms

Today is the one-year compliance deadline for the Take It Down Act, signed by President Trump in May 2025. Starting today, covered platforms must have working removal mechanisms for nonconsensual intimate images — including AI-generated deepfakes. Victims submit a notice and the platform has 48 hours to act. The FTC sent formal warning letters to more than a dozen companies, including Amazon, Apple, Meta, TikTok, X, Discord, Reddit, Snapchat, and Bumble.

*Sources: [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/ftc-reminds-tech-platforms-of-deadline-to-comply-with-take-it-down-act) · [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/146) · [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2025/06/take-it-down-act)*
