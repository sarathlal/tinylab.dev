Nvidia passed its first test without Jensen Huang, and Anthropic's IPO book is so full it may price above range tonight. Meanwhile Microsoft used Ignite to argue Copilot is now the place where work happens.

## Nvidia and Anthropic face Wall Street in the same week

### Nvidia beats by $7B in Colette Kress's first quarter as interim CEO
Revenue hit $115.8B, up 103% from a year ago and about $7B above the $108.7B estimate. Data center alone brought in a record $107.6B. Nvidia guided to $130B next quarter, ahead of the roughly $125B Wall Street wanted. Shares rose 2% to 5% after hours, less than the 8% swing options had priced in.

*Sources: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/nvidia-q3-fy27-slides-revenue-doubles-to-1158b-on-ai-demand-93CH-5034317) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-earnings-jensen-huang-colette-kress) · [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/nvidia-results-boost-confidence-in-the-post-huang-era-202611172358)*

### Nvidia writes China out of its forecast and says Huang isn't coming back
The $130B guide assumes zero data center revenue from China. Nvidia shipped $2.4B of licensed Hopper chips there this quarter, and Kress said those shipments are ending. She also said Q4 should "mark the bottom" for gross margins. CNN reports the company ruled out a Huang return, less than a week after he resigned.

*Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/17/tech/nvidia-earnings-jensen-huang-exit) · [Livewire Markets](https://www.livewiremarkets.com/wires/live-blog-nvidia-earnings-a-nervous-market-puts-the-ai-trade-to-the-test) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/earnings/nvidia-quarterly-results-beat-estimates-5033943)*

### Anthropic will likely price its IPO above the $70 to $78 range
Investors have asked for about 5.7 billion shares, nearly seven times the 825 million on offer. CNBC sources say the price will likely land above $78, and the WSJ reports advisers have discussed more than $80. Monday's SEC filing still showed the old range. Pricing comes Wednesday, and ANT starts trading on Nasdaq Thursday at a valuation near $1.5 trillion.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/anthropic-ipo-price.html) · [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/business/60016) · [Coinpaper](https://coinpaper.com/38977/anthropic-ipo-could-price-above-78-as-investors-push-valuation-toward-1-4-trillion)*

## Microsoft and Hugging Face rewire where agents and models run

### Microsoft opens Ignite by calling Copilot "the new OS for work"
Satya Nadella split Copilot into four modes: Chat, Cowork, Code and Autopilot. Autopilot is a long-running agent that keeps working after you step away. Copilot Workspaces, a shared space for teams and their agents, replaces Copilot Notebooks for Frontier program users. The pitch lands days after a Microsoft 365 outage knocked Copilot offline for more than 20 hours.

*Sources: [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/ignite-2026-microsoft-makes-case-be-your-work-operating-system-intelligence-layer) · [Microsoft Book of News](https://news.microsoft.com/events/ignite/2026/book-of-news/) · [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/127120/microsoft-upgrades-ai-agent-strategy-deploys-copilot-as-work-operating-system)*

### Nadella says Agent 365 already tracks 50 million agents
He didn't say whether that counts active agents or just registered ones. Microsoft also made Microsoft IQ generally available. It bundles Work IQ, Fabric IQ, Foundry IQ and Web IQ with 350 third-party connectors, so agents can pull company knowledge. Nadella said more than 4,000 customers have used it.

*Sources: [The Letter Two](https://thelettertwo.com/2026/11/16/microsoft-ignite-2026-keynote-live-blog/) · [Microsoft Source EMEA](https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/2026/11/microsoft-ignite-2026-empowering-human-ambition-intelligence-and-trust/) · [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/ignite-2026-microsoft-makes-case-be-your-work-operating-system-intelligence-layer)*

### Hugging Face hands its language-model routing to OpenRouter
Chat and text requests on Hugging Face Inference Providers now run through OpenRouter. That kills custom provider API keys for language models, which is a breaking change for some teams. You pay the provider's price on your Hugging Face bill with no markup. Image, video and audio models still skip OpenRouter.

*Sources: [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/openrouter) · [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/hugging-face-moves-inference-providers-routing-to-openrouter/)*

## Investors pay up for robot hands and banker agents

### Genesis AI raises $500M to build robots with human-level dexterity
The French robotics startup is now worth $3.5B, up from a reported $3B pre-money in July talks. Premji Invest, Khosla Ventures and Eclipse co-led the Series A, and Nvidia joined. Genesis came out of stealth only in July 2025 with a $105M seed. Its GENE-26.5 model and its own robotic hand are aimed at long, multi-step physical tasks.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/nvidia-backed-genesis-ai-raises-funds-at-3-5-billion-valuation) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/genesis-ai-raises-500m-series-a-at-3-5b-valuation-to-master-human-level-dexterity-in-general-purpose-robots-and-deploy-at-scale-302941249.html)*

### Model ML raises $250M at a $1.5B valuation for banker agents
A year ago the London startup raised a $75M Series A. Now General Atlantic and QED Investors co-lead a $250M Series B. Model ML builds agents that do junior-banker grunt work, like pulling data and building pitch decks. It shows how fast finance is paying for agents that touch real deliverables.

*Sources: [MLQ.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/model-ml-raises-250-million-at-a-15-billion-valuation-for-ai-agents-built-for-bankers-and-consultants) · [WOWTALE](https://en.wowtale.net/2026/11/18/236073/)*
