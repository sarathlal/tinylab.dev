Next week decides a lot: Nvidia reports Tuesday and Anthropic prices its IPO Wednesday. Meanwhile, Google cut the price of a Gemini 4 model, and the backlash against AI agents moved into courts and airline contracts.

## Nvidia and Anthropic head into the week that tests the AI trade

### Anthropic's IPO stays on schedule despite the Nvidia shake-up
Anthropic still plans to price on November 18 at $70 to $78 a share, aiming for a valuation as high as $1.4 trillion. Trading under "ANT" starts the next day. The open question is Nvidia's roughly $10B piece of a $26B private placement that closes with the IPO. As of Friday, nobody has said that pledge is cut or cancelled.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/13/anthropic-ipo-nvidia-jensen-huang) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/nvidia-huang-resigns-anthropic-ipo-pledge-111226) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html)*

### Nvidia's first earnings without Jensen Huang land Tuesday
Nvidia guided to $108B in revenue for the quarter, give or take 2%. Analysts now expect about $108.8B. Options traders are pricing a 10.6% move either way, above the stock's usual 7.8% earnings-day swing. Interim CEO Colette Kress will run the call. Nvidia told CNBC it's picking a search firm for the permanent job.

*Sources: [MarketPulse](https://www.marketpulse.com/markets/nvidia-nvda-q3-earnings-preview-ai-spending-risks-and-governance-concerns-threaten-major-uptrend/) · [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-nvidias-q3-guide-is-108b-12-above-q2-what-17-nov-tests/) · [Kitco (Reuters)](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-11-13/wall-st-week-ahead-nvidia-results-anthropic-ipo-pose-tests-tumultuous)*

## The pushback against AI agents gets serious

### Four nonprofits sue five AI labs to freeze frontier development
Public Citizen, the Heat Initiative, the Irreplaceable Education Fund and the Alliance for Secure AI filed in San Francisco Superior Court on Thursday. They want an injunction stopping Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX's AI unit from building anything stronger than their current best models. Former New Jersey Attorney General Matthew Platkin filed the complaint. It leans on California's unfair competition law and public nuisance doctrine, citing agents that allegedly escaped test environments. No judge has ruled yet.

*Sources: [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/164494/nonprofits-lawsuit-freeze-frontier-ai-development/) · [Global Banking & Finance](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/openai-google-anthropic-hit-lawsuit-seeking-halt-frontier-ai/) · [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/nonprofits-sue-openai-google-anthropic-meta-and-spacexs-ai-unit-to-freeze-frontier-ai-development/)*

### United Airlines bans AI agents from booking its flights
United rewrote its contract of carriage to bar AI agents from making, changing or paying for tickets. The rule applies even when a person watches and approves each step, and it covers travel agencies too. Agents can still show United fares, but a human has to click buy. CEO Scott Kirby says he doesn't want "another middleman."

*Sources: [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/11/11/united-airlines-moves-to-block-personal-ai-agents-from-booking-flights/) · [AirlineGeeks](https://airlinegeeks.com/2026/11/11/united-bans-ai-agents-from-booking-flights-for-customers/) · [View from the Wing](https://viewfromthewing.com/united-airlines-ceo-scott-kirby-bans-ai-agents-from-booking-tickets-doesnt-want-another-middleman/)*

### 35,578 Grok Build apps opened without a sign-in
Security firm Claroc found 37,571 live apps built with SpaceXSI's Grok Build agent. Almost all of them opened for anyone with the link. It estimates 650 to 800 held private data, including medical exams. Twenty-four came from SpaceXSI staff, one with a sales map of 594 accounts. SpaceXSI pulled its own apps and says sharing takes several deliberate steps.

*Sources: [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/claroc-finds-grok-build-shared-apps-exposing-confidential-da-af9abf6d) · [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/grok-build-apps-exposed-sensitive-data-without-sign-1831677) · [Runtime Wire](https://runtimewire.com/article/grok-build-share-links-35578-apps-open-claroc)*

## Cheaper models and new agent deals for business

### Google ships Gemini 4 Hydrogen at $1.50 per million input tokens
Hydrogen costs $1.50 in and $7.50 out per million tokens, flat across its full 1M-token context. It replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash and is live in the Gemini app, Search, the API and AI Studio. Google says it edges out its bigger sibling Argon on the Artificial Analysis index, 51 to 50. The catch: 3.8 Flash's price doubles to match Hydrogen on January 1.

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/12/google-releases-gemini-4-hydrogen-a-lower-cost-model-said-to-outscore-argon/) · [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/11/13/google-gemini-4-hydrogen-replaces-gemini-3-8-flash-pricing/) · [Data Studios](https://www.datastudios.org/post/google-gemini-4-hydrogen-launch-api-pricing-gemini-app-search)*

### OpenAI and Parloa team up to sell customer service agents
Berlin-based Parloa joins OpenAI's alpha program to test new models before release. The two will pitch enterprises together, mostly in North America. Parloa's platform is also headed for the OpenAI Marketplace. Parloa is valued at $3B and has signed similar deals with SAP and Microsoft this year.

*Sources: [Parloa](https://www.parloa.com/parloa-in-the-press/parloa-open-ai-expand-partnership/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/parloa-turns-its-350-million-war-chest-into-a-partnership-web-spanning-sap-microsoft-and-openai)*

### Commvault plugs its backup data into Nvidia's agent tools
Commvault's Data Activate now works with Nvidia NIM microservices and the Nemotron Retriever. The goal is to let AI agents search company data that already sits in backups, without breaking access rules. It's a small deal, but it shows where enterprise AI spend is going: getting messy internal data into a shape agents can use.

*Sources: [TechTrends KE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/11/13/commvault-nvidia-enterprise-ai-data-partnership/)*
