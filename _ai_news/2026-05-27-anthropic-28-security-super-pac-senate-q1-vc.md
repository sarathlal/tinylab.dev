Claude got a security perimeter. A well-funded super PAC named its Senate targets. And Q1's VC numbers showed AI absorbed 80% of all global venture funding — in three months.

## Anthropic Locks In Enterprise Security

### Claude Now Connects to 28 Corporate Security Platforms

Anthropic announced a Claude Compliance API and integrations with 28 security vendors on May 25. Partners include CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Okta, Zscaler, Netskope, Microsoft Purview, Tenable, and Wiz. The API gives corporate IT and security teams programmatic access to two data streams: Claude conversation content — chats, uploaded files, project data — and event logs covering user logins, admin actions, and configuration changes. That data feeds directly into SIEM, DLP, identity management, and e-discovery platforms companies already run. The announcement removes a specific blocker: security teams who couldn't audit Claude the way they audit other software now can.

*Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anthropic-expands-claudes-enterprise-security-reach-with-28-new-integrations/) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/05/25/anthropic-security-compliance-integrations-claude/)*

### Bristol Myers Squibb Puts Claude in Front of 30,000 Researchers

BMS announced May 20 that Claude Enterprise becomes the company's "shared intelligence platform" globally. The deal covers 30,000 employees across drug discovery, clinical development, and corporate operations. Scientists use it to generate clinical study reports from raw trial data and flag manufacturing quality deviations in real time. Engineers use Claude Code for software development. BMS is connecting Claude to decades of proprietary scientific data that has sat in organizational silos — the bet is that unified AI access compresses drug development timelines. BMS stock rose on the news, signaling that markets are pricing enterprise AI adoption as a drug pipeline asset.

*Sources: [BMS Press Release](https://news.bms.com/news/details/2026/Bristol-Myers-Squibb-Announces-Strategic-Agreement-with-Anthropic-to-Position-Claude-Enterprise-as-the-Shared-Intelligence-Platform-Across-Its-Global-Operations/default.aspx) · [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4595247-bristol-myers-taps-anthropics-claude-for-30000-workers-report) · [Pharma Exec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/roundup-bristol-myers-squibb-strategic-agreement-anthropic-incyte-genesis-expand-strategic-collaboration)*

## AI Money Enters the Ballot Box

### Pro-AI Super PAC Drops $2M Into Three Senate Primaries

"Leading the Future" is deploying $2 million across Senate primaries in Louisiana, Montana, and Oklahoma, and $750,000 in House primaries in California and Washington. The PAC has a $140 million war chest for the 2026 midterms. Backers include OpenAI president Greg Brockman, Marc Andreessen, Ben Horowitz, and Palantir co-founder Joe Lonsdale. In Louisiana's June 27 Senate runoff, it's backing Republican Rep. Julia Letlow against state Treasurer John Fleming. The PAC's stated goal: elect candidates who support federal preemption of state AI laws, preventing a state-by-state regulatory patchwork.

An affiliated dark money group, Build American AI, is paying TikTok and Instagram creators $5,000 per video to run scripted narratives that frame AI safety advocates as threats to US competitiveness against China. Internal documents reviewed by Wired show the campaign's stated goal was to "subtly shift public debate" toward deregulation without disclosing the funding source.

*Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/05/23/ai-industry-super-pacs-are-intervening-midterm-congressional-races/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/22/open-ai-pac-kentucky) · [The Nation](https://www.thenation.com/article/politics/crypto-ai-super-pacs-election-spending-big-tech-dark-money-2026/)*

### Colorado's AI Enforcement Clock Hits 34 Days

Colorado's AI Act starts enforcement June 30 — the first time a US state imposes real compliance requirements on AI making consequential decisions. The law covers AI used in hiring, credit and lending, educational placement, housing, healthcare, and government services. Companies must have risk management programs, consumer disclosures, and bias mitigation steps in place. The White House's National AI Policy Framework recommends federal preemption of state laws, but recommending isn't legislating. Only Congress can override Colorado, and no preemption bill has passed. Companies that counted on federal action clearing the way before June 30 are now scrambling.

*Sources: [Cooley LLP](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-04-24-state-ai-laws-where-are-they-now) · [Gunderson Dettmer](https://www.gunder.com/en/news-insights/insights/2026-ai-laws-update-key-regulations-and-practical-guidance) · [Verifywise](https://verifywise.ai/blog/state-of-ai-governance-regulations-united-states-2026)*

## The Q1 Capital Numbers

### AI Took $242 Billion in Q1 — More Than All of 2025's Global VC

Crunchbase's Q1 2026 report shows AI companies received $242 billion in venture funding, representing 80% of all global VC in the quarter. That single quarter eclipses the $254 billion raised across every sector worldwide in all of 2025. Four rounds drove the bulk of it: OpenAI's $122 billion, Anthropic's $30 billion, xAI's $20 billion, and Waymo's $16 billion — a combined $188 billion, or 65% of total global VC on its own. Outside the mega-rounds, $18.8 billion went to AI companies founded since January 2025, showing that new capital isn't only consolidating around incumbents.

*Sources: [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/record-breaking-funding-ai-global-q1-2026/) · [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/05/278941-ai-venture-funding-reaches-new-highs-in-q1-2026-report-reveals/)*

### EY and Microsoft Commit $1 Billion to Joint Enterprise AI Deployment

The two companies announced May 21 a five-year, $1 billion initiative to embed AI into enterprise operations across EY's global client base. Microsoft's Forward Deployed Engineers will work alongside EY professionals at client sites in healthcare, financial services, and manufacturing. It follows the logic behind OpenAI's Deployment Company: frontier AI doesn't sell itself at scale — it gets installed by teams who understand both the technology and the specific business. The EY–Microsoft footprint covers roughly 150,000 EY employees and clients in more than 150 countries.

*Sources: [Microsoft News](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/05/21/ey-and-microsoft-announce-global-initiative-to-help-clients-scale-ai-enterprisewide-value-creation-and-move-beyond-experimentation/)*
