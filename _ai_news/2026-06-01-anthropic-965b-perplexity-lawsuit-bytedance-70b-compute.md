Three stories collided this weekend: Anthropic became the most valuable private AI company ever, CNN became the first TV network to sue an AI search firm, and ByteDance signaled it will double its AI infrastructure bet. The week's other theme — companies cutting workers to fund AI — kept running quietly in the background.

## Anthropic's Record-Setting Week

### Anthropic Raises $65B at $965B Valuation, Leapfrogging OpenAI

Anthropic closed its Series H on May 29 at a $965 billion valuation — the highest ever for a private AI company, past OpenAI's $852 billion. The company's ARR crossed $47 billion in May, with roughly 80% coming from enterprise API deals and cloud partnerships. The capital will fund compute for next-generation model training and an expanded international push.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/29/anthropic-raises-65-billion-at-record-965-billion-valuation-promises-mythos-ai-model-in-wide-release-in-coming-weeks-releases-claude-opus-4-8/) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/5/29/anthropic-soars-to-965bn-valuation-leapfrogging-openai)*

### Claude Opus 4.8 Ships Faster, Cheaper, and Better at Catching Bugs

Anthropic released Opus 4.8 alongside the funding news. It flags code bugs roughly four times as often as Opus 4.7 — a meaningful gain for developers running agentic coding workflows. Fast mode runs at 2.5× standard inference speed, and pricing landed at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens, about three times cheaper than Opus 4.7's Fast mode.

*Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-opus-release-mythos)*

### Mythos Cybersecurity Model Coming to All Customers "in Weeks"

Anthropic confirmed Mythos-class models will ship to all users "in the coming weeks." The model launched April 7 in a restricted preview for vetted cybersecurity partners under Project Glasswing. It can find software vulnerabilities and chain them into multi-step attacks — a capability Anthropic is holding back until stronger safeguards are in place.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/05/25/anthropic-to-release-mythos-class-models-to-the-public/5245596) · [AndroidHeadlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/05/anthropic-claude-mythos-ai-model-public-release-cybersecurity.html)*

## AI Meets Copyright Law

### CNN Joins Nine Plaintiffs Suing Perplexity Over 17,000 Scraped Stories

CNN filed a copyright and trademark lawsuit against Perplexity AI on May 28, alleging the AI search tool scraped and redistributed more than 17,000 CNN news stories without permission. It's the first lawsuit by any television network against an AI company. The plaintiff list now stands at nine: the New York Times, News Corp, Reddit, the Chicago Tribune, Encyclopedia Britannica, Merriam-Webster, Japan's Yomiuri Shimbun, and CNN. Other publishers — Time, Gannett, and Le Monde — cut licensing deals with Perplexity instead.

*Sources: [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/28/media/cnn-sues-perplexity-ai-copyright) · [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/28/g-s1-124680/cnn-sues-ai-company-perplexity-alleging-it-violates-copyright-protections)*

### OpenAI Publishes Governance Framework Aligned with EU AI Act

OpenAI released its Frontier Governance Framework on May 28, formally aligning its internal Preparedness Framework with the EU AI Act's Code of Practice for General-Purpose AI and California's Transparency in Frontier AI Act. The EU's full transparency rules take effect August 2, 2026. The framework covers model evaluation, risk disclosure, and incident reporting — three areas where U.S. and EU compliance now overlap for the first time.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317461/20260531/ai-regulation-2026-opens-three-fronts-cnn-sues-perplexity-openai-aligns-eu-rules.htm)*

## Infrastructure and Platform Bets

### ByteDance Weighs Up to $70B in AI Infrastructure This Year

ByteDance is discussing capital expenditures as high as $70 billion in 2026 on data centers and AI compute — more than double the roughly $25 billion it spent last year. The TikTok parent generated about $50 billion in profit in 2025, giving it the cash to self-fund most of the expansion. Goldman Sachs estimates China's top internet firms will collectively spend over $70 billion on data centers this year, making ByteDance's push part of a national compute race.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-27/bytedance-weighs-capex-of-as-much-as-70-billion-in-ai-push) · [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/bytedance-70b-ai-capital-spending/)*

### AWS Moves to Put Grok on Bedrock Despite Near-Zero Enterprise Demand

Amazon Web Services is in talks to add SpaceXAI's Grok to its Bedrock platform, which already hosts Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, and Cohere. The obstacle is demand: Netskope data shows Grok peaked at 5 out of every 1,000 enterprise users before falling to 2 out of 1,000, with users spending less than half as long as ChatGPT users. Analysts say the real incentive is locking SpaceXAI into Amazon's Trainium chips ahead of SpaceX's anticipated IPO, not enterprise Grok adoption.

*Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-ml/2026/05/29/aws-reportedly-to-tuck-elon-musks-grok-into-bedrock-despite-zero-enterprise-demand/5248832) · [OpenTools](https://opentools.ai/news/aws-bedrock-grok-spacexai-enterprise-demand)*

## AI and the Workforce

### SentinelOne Cuts 230 Jobs After CEO Says AI Does Months of Work in Weeks

SentinelOne is cutting 8% of its workforce — about 230 people — alongside a weak Q1 earnings report and soft guidance. CEO Tomer Weingarten said AI tools now complete in weeks work that previously took months, and the savings will fund more AI investment. The stock dropped sharply on the combination of earnings miss, weak forecast, and layoff announcement.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/sentinelone-stock-falls-revenue-miss-151016078.html) · [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/sentinelone-cuts-8-percent-of-its-workforce-and-bets-the-proceeds-on-ai-as-investors-punish-the-trade/)*

### Groupon Cuts 400 Jobs — Nearly a Quarter of Staff — to Go AI-Native

Groupon announced up to 400 cuts under "Project Foundry," a multiyear plan to rebuild as an AI-native company by 2027. The reduction covers employees and contractors and is projected to save $20-25 million annually, with one-time severance charges of $7-13 million. CEO Dusan Senkypl framed it as a transformation, not a retreat.

*Sources: [American Bazaar Online](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/05/27/hundreds-of-jobs-cuts-in-groupons-ai-native-pivot-481654/) · [Layoff Hedge](https://layoffhedge.com/company/groupon)*
