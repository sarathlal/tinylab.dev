Anthropic had one of its biggest weeks ever, Google's AI division kept losing people at the top, and DeepSeek reminded everyone that cheap AI doesn't stay cheap forever. Here's what actually mattered in AI over the last day or two.

## Anthropic's Record Week

### Anthropic's Q2 Revenue Hits $11.5B, Its First-Ever Operating Profit

Anthropic's preliminary Q2 2026 revenue came in above $11.5 billion, Bloomberg reported August 15, up from $787 million in the same quarter last year. That's roughly 14.6x growth in twelve months, and 2.4x more than the $4.73 billion the company booked in Q1. Anthropic also posted its first positive adjusted operating profit, a milestone it wasn't expected to hit for another two years.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-revenue-jumps-to-over-11point5-billion-in-q2-report.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-revenue-q2-11-5-billion-ipo-investors/)*

### Anthropic in Talks to Buy Decart for $6B, Its Biggest Deal Yet

Anthropic is negotiating to acquire Israeli AI infrastructure startup Decart for about $6 billion, Bloomberg reported August 13. Decart builds software that makes chips run AI workloads more efficiently, plus "world models" used for things like virtual apparel try-on and autonomous driving simulation. If it closes, it would be Anthropic's largest acquisition ever, aimed at squeezing more capacity out of its existing compute as demand keeps outrunning supply.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-ai-startup-decart-for-6-billion) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-startup-decart-for-6-billion/)*

### An Unreleased Claude Model Pushed a Century-Old Math Bound From 41.6% to 67.2%

Anthropic disclosed that an unreleased research version of Claude improved the proven lower bound on how many Riemann zeta zeros sit on the critical line, from 41.6% to 67.2%. The model burned through 31 million output tokens across two sessions in Claude Code, coordinating dozens of specialized agents and checking its own work with formal proof software. It didn't prove the Riemann hypothesis, but MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland called it real evidence AI can do original math research, not just ace benchmarks.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta) · [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113472-anthropic-claude-tried-solve-riemann-hypothesis-found-something.html)*

## Google's AI Program Shows Cracks

### DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis Steps Down as Jeff Dean and Other Veterans Exit

Hassabis stepped down as Google DeepMind CEO on August 5, moving to chairman and chief scientist of Alphabet. Days later, longtime Google AI lead Jeff Dean announced he's leaving to co-found a new startup, Discovery Loop, joined by fellow veterans Oriol Vinyals, Quoc Le, and Sanjay Ghemawat. The reshuffle follows repeated delays to Gemini 3.5 Pro and a wave of senior researchers leaving for Anthropic, including AlphaFold co-creator John Jumper.

*Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/10/how-stalled-models-missed-deadlines-and-staff-burnout-lead-to-the-unraveling-of-googles-deepmind/) · [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/08/12/what-google-deepminds-departures-say-about-the-ai-talent-war/)*

### Google Ships Gemini 3.7 Flash as a Stopgap While Its Flagship Model Stays Delayed

Google launched Gemini 3.7 Flash on August 13, just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash, built by refining the prior model rather than training from scratch. It scores 65.3% on the DeepSWE coding benchmark, up from 49.0% for its predecessor, and costs half as much: 75 cents per million input tokens. The bigger story is what's missing: flagship model Gemini 3.5 Pro, promised for June, still hasn't shipped.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/google-debuts-new-gemini-flash-while-top-ai-model-still-delayed) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/13/google-gemini-37-flash)*

## China's AI Push Advances on Two Fronts

### DeepSeek Ships V4 Pro, Then Hikes API Prices Up to 1,100%

DeepSeek's official V4 Pro release scored 53 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, up from 40 for V4 Flash, a real jump from its preview build. Starting August 17, DeepSeek raised API prices across the board, with peak-hour rates for V4 Pro jumping from 3 yuan to 9 yuan per million input tokens and 6 yuan to 27 yuan for output. The company says it's doubling staff and needs to cover surging compute costs, and it's now splitting pricing into peak and off-peak windows for the first time.

*Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/deepseek-raises-api-pricing-v4-115712902.html) · [US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-08-13/deepseek-raises-api-pricing-for-its-v4-models)*

### US Tells 35 Pax Silica Countries: Don't Hedge With China's Rival AI Bloc

The State Department is preparing letters to 35 countries signed on to its Pax Silica AI partnership, warning that joining China's rival World AI Cooperation Organization will get them cut off, Bloomberg-linked reporting showed August 14. The message, according to one account: "To be part of everything is to be part of nothing." Kazakhstan is currently the only country trying to straddle both blocs, and the warning looks designed to force it to choose.

*Sources: [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/pax-silica-us-letter-35-countries-china-ai-coalition) · [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/08/15/us-warns-korea-34-others-join-chinas-ai-bloc-lose-supply)*

## AI's Two-Sided Security Story

### OpenAI's Purpose-Built Cyber Model Found Two Real Chrome Zero-Days

OpenAI's new GPT-5.6-Cyber model, built for vetted security researchers under its expanded Daybreak program, discovered two previously unknown vulnerabilities in Chrome's V8 engine that could be chained to corrupt memory and escape the browser's sandbox. Google patched the flaw as CVE-2026-15903 after coordinated disclosure. OpenAI now offers two access tiers, Daybreak Blue and the more restricted Daybreak Red, gated by identity checks for legitimate defenders.

*Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows/) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/openais-new-cyber-ai-finds-hundreds-of-bugs-before-launch/)*

### Grok Tied to 87% of Deepfake Attacks Tracked in New Threat Report

Resemble AI's H1 2026 Deepfake Threat Report, published August 12, found Grok was responsible for 87% of the synthetic files behind documented deepfake attacks in the first half of the year. Researchers counted 821 verified attacks and 15,736 victims across 1,760 public news reports, with political disinformation cases alone reaching a potential audience of over a billion people each. Corporate victims mostly stay quiet: only 15 firms reported incidents publicly all year.

*Sources: [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324250/20260813/grok-deepfakes-hit-15736-victims-six-months-physics-based-detection-arrives-late.htm)*
