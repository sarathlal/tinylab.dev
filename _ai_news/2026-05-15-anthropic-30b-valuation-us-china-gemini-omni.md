Anthropic made two of the biggest AI headlines of the week — a potential $900 billion valuation and a $200 million global health deal. The US and China agreed to formal AI safety talks for the first time after Trump and Xi met in Beijing. And Google is showing what's coming at I/O next Tuesday.

## Anthropic Stacks Up Billions and Partnerships

### Anthropic in Talks to Raise $30B at $900B Valuation — Topping OpenAI

Anthropic is in early talks to raise at least $30 billion at a valuation above $900 billion, which would put it ahead of OpenAI's $852 billion post-money mark from February. The company's ARR hit $30 billion in March 2026, up from $9 billion at the end of 2025 — more than tripling in roughly three months. No term sheet is signed yet, but sources say the deal could close by end of May. An IPO as early as October is also in discussion.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-12/anthropic-in-talks-to-raise-30-billion-at-900-billion-valuation) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/anthropic-potential-900b-valuation-round-could-happen-within-two-weeks/)*

### Anthropic and Gates Foundation Commit $200M to AI in Health, Education, and Agriculture

The two organizations announced a four-year, $200 million commitment covering grant funding, Claude usage credits, and technical support. The biggest slice goes to global health — accelerating vaccine and therapy development for diseases like HPV, polio, and eclampsia, targeting a world where 4.6 billion people lack access to essential health services. Education funding covers K-12 AI tutoring in the US and foundational literacy programs in sub-Saharan Africa and India.

*Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/gates-foundation-partnership) · [Gates Foundation](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/ideas/media-center/press-releases/2026/05/ai-anthropic-partnership)*

## AI Goes Global — Diplomacy and Governance

### US and China Agree to Formal AI Safety Talks After Trump-Xi Summit in Beijing

After Trump and Xi met in Beijing, Treasury Secretary Bessent confirmed May 14 that the two countries will launch formal AI safety discussions. The goal is a shared framework on best practices and guardrails to keep advanced models from being misused. Bessent's framing was direct: "We can hold these discussions because we are in the lead." He added he wouldn't be having the same conversation if China were this far ahead.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/14/us-china-ai-rules-bessent-us-lead.html) · [Daily Signal](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/05/14/us-china-ai-models-guardrails/)*

### OpenAI Opens GPT-5.5-Cyber to EU Teams; Anthropic Keeps Mythos Locked

OpenAI said May 11 it would give EU vetted cybersecurity teams limited preview access to GPT-5.5-Cyber, its advanced security model. Anthropic took the opposite approach: Claude Mythos — which identified zero-days in every major OS and solved 73% of expert-level CTF challenges — stays gated under Project Glasswing, available only to screened research partners. The split shows two labs reaching opposite conclusions on how to handle AI capable of real harm.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/11/openai-eu-cyber-model-anthropic-mythos-gpt.html)*

## Google Loads Up for I/O Week

### DeepMind Reimagines the Mouse Cursor With Gemini — First Redesign in 50 Years

Google DeepMind published research May 12-13 on an AI-enabled pointer that understands both what you're pointing at and what you intend to do. Users can say "this" or "that" while pointing and Gemini takes contextual action — no prompt typing required. Two demos are live in Google AI Studio now, and a Chrome integration is rolling out this week. DeepMind describes it as the first major rethink of the pointer since the cursor was invented in the 1960s.

*Sources: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/ai-pointer/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/05/13/googles-ai-enabled-mouse-pointer-understands-this-and-that/5240005)*

### Google Retires Chromebook, Launches AI-Native "Googlebook" This Fall

Starting fall 2026, Google replaces its Chromebook lineup with Googlebook — laptops built around Gemini Intelligence rather than Chrome OS. Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, and Lenovo are all manufacturing partners, with US and UK availability confirmed first. Every Googlebook ships with the Magic Pointer built in, so the AI-native cursor is the default from first boot.

*Sources: [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/12/deepmind-googlebook-magic-pointer/) · [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/12/google-unveils-googlebook/)*

### Gemini Omni Surfaces in Google's UI — Unified Text, Image, and Video Model Spotted Before I/O

A UI string spotted in the Gemini interface on May 2 read "Powered by Omni," pointing to a new model that generates text, images, and video in a single pipeline. Early evidence includes video remixing, in-chat editing, and text-to-video — potentially replacing Veo 3.1. The most likely reveal window is Google I/O 2026, which kicks off May 19 at Shoreline Amphitheatre in Mountain View.

*Sources: [WaveSpeed Blog](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/google-omni-video-model-leak-i-o-2026/) · [iMini AI](https://imini.com/blogs/gemini-omni-google-io-2026)*
