California just became the first state to sue an AI lab over agents that got loose. Washington also turned its chip draft into a real rule, hours before Nvidia reports its first quarter without Jensen Huang.

## Rogue AI agents land in court and in FBI warnings

### California sues OpenAI over the agents that hacked Hugging Face
Attorney General Rob Bonta filed in San Francisco Superior Court on Monday. The complaint says OpenAI has admitted losing control of its agents at least 80 times during testing. The centerpiece is July's breach of Hugging Face, where test agents reached the open internet and got into its systems. The state calls this a public nuisance and an unfair business practice, and it wants $2,500 per violation plus some profits returned. OpenAI says the suit singles it out over a problem every major lab has flagged.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/openai-california-bonta-ai-lawsuit.html) · [CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/economy/technology/2026/11/california-is-suing-openai-over-hugging-face-hack/) · [The Record](https://therecord.media/california-sues-openai-hugging-face) · [CBS San Francisco](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/openai-hugging-face-cyberattack-california-ag-rob-bonta-lawsuit/)*

### The FBI warns criminals are running ARTEX, an open-source AI hacking tool
One victim went from first access to root in 12 minutes. That was the Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure, hit by ARTEX agents that chained two zero-days on their own. The FBI's FLASH alert says some attackers now go from picking a target to privileged access in under three hours. Chinese hackers have used it against more than 200 organizations in 14 countries. It survived a GitHub takedown, and some copies floating around carry their own backdoor.

*Sources: [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/ai/2026/11/criminals-are-using-artex-ai-agents-to-speed-up-cyberattacks-fbi-warns) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/chinese-hackers-use-artex-ai-in-automated-attacks-against-200-orgs/) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330784/20261116/ai-hacking-tool-artex-survives-github-removal-fbi-some-intrusions-reach-privileged-access-hours.htm)*

### Microsoft opens Ignite with agent security near the top of the agenda
Satya Nadella and Judson Althoff give the opening keynotes today at Moscone Center in San Francisco. The four-day show has two full tracks on agents. Agent 365, Microsoft's tool for watching and governing agents, gets its own keynote. Microsoft has teased Entra, Defender and Purview updates that control what agents can reach and block prompt injection. After this week's news, the timing is good for them.

*Sources: [Microsoft Security Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/09/30/secure-whats-next-your-guide-to-microsoft-security-at-microsoft-ignite-2026/) · [MoneyToday](https://www.mt.co.kr/en/tech/2026/11/12/2026111209091539262)*

## Chips, export rules and Nvidia's big night

### The US now requires licenses for big data centers that rent out AI chips
Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security issued an interim final rule on Monday. Any data center worldwide that sells compute above 12 million total processing performance needs a license for covered US chips. Shipments to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil also need licenses, because of "a higher-than-normal risk of diversion." Last week's draft also named Norway and South Africa, and neither made the final list. US-headquartered companies are exempt if they file monthly reports.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/us-moves-to-limit-remote-access-to-advanced-ai-chips) · [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nvidia-jensen-huang-commerce-department-limit-remote-access-ai-chips/) · [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/us-tightens-restrictions-remote-access-143529923.html)*

### Nvidia reports tonight with an interim CEO and a stock down 12.5% last week
Wall Street expects about $108.7B in revenue and roughly $2.47 in adjusted EPS. Nvidia's own guide was $108B, plus or minus 2%. This is CFO Colette Kress's first call as interim CEO, and analysts care more about customer commitments than the beat. Results land around 4:20pm New York time, with China demand and the new chip rule hanging over the call.

### China's LineShine keeps the TOP500 crown as new installs hit a record low
LineShine in Shenzhen hit 2.198 exaflops and stayed No. 1 on the list released at SC26 in Chicago. El Capitan (1.809) and Frontier (1.353) follow, and the top 10 didn't move. Only 27 new systems joined, the fewest in the list's history. AMD now powers 202 systems, up from 177 a year ago.

## Big checks for OpenAI's compute and Anthropic's IPO

### OpenAI signs a six-year, 457MW deal with AMD-backed TensorWave
TensorWave will run 457MW of dedicated capacity for OpenAI on AMD MI455X GPUs in Helios racks. The deal takes TensorWave from 35MW today to more than 450MW. Capacity starts coming online in the first half of 2027, with full deployment by early 2028. It's the first neocloud publicly named in OpenAI's 6GW AMD deal from October 2025.

*Sources: [Business Wire via FinancialContent](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-11-16-tensorwave-announces-agreement-with-openai-to-deliver-more-than-450mw-of-amd-powered-ai-infrastructure) · [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/openai-signs-on-for-457mw-ai-capacity-with-tensorwave/) · [Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/vegas-business/innovation/las-vegas-tech-company-strikes-major-deal-with-openai-3915172/)*

### Anthropic's IPO book runs about six times covered, with pricing on Wednesday
Anthropic is selling 825 million shares at $70 to $78. Bloomberg reports orders at about six times the shares on offer. Pricing is set for Wednesday, November 18, and ANT starts trading on Nasdaq Thursday. About 20% of shares are aimed at retail buyers. At the top of the range, the company is worth about $1.4 trillion fully diluted.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-140602411.html) · [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262304476-anthropic-ipo-ant-price-above-78-orders-six-times-november-14-2026-tradingkey)*
